AREX: Towards a Recursively Self-Improving Agent for Deep Research AREX：面向深度研究的递归自我改进智能体

🧑‍🔬 Shuqi Lu, Chaofan Li, Kun Luo, Zhang Zhang et al.

图 Figure · Page 1 of paper

🎯 AREX是能递归自我改进、逐步验证并完善答案的深度研究AI智能体
AREX is a self-improving AI research agent that iteratively verifies and refines its own answers to answer complex multi-constraint questions.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English AnalogyAREX就像一个侦探：不是猜一次就完事，而是在形成初步判断后，逐条核对每一条线索，发现哪条对不上就专门回去调查那个环节，反复循环，直到每一条证据都完美吻合，才最终给出结论。Think of AREX like a detective who doesn't just guess a suspect once — after forming a theory, they go back and check each clue one by one, and if one clue doesn't fit, they go out and investigate specifically that part again, until every single piece of evidence lines up perfectly.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有AI研究智能体在面对同时满足多个条件的复杂问题时表现很差——比如"找一个既获过诺贝尔奖、又出生在法国、还研究量子物理的人"。它们要么搜索太泛，要么过早放弃，无法判断自己答案的哪些部分已经正确、哪些还需要继续研究，导致深度研究既费钱又不可靠。 Existing AI research agents struggle when a question has many constraints that all need to be satisfied at once — like finding a person who is both a Nobel Prize winner, born in France, and worked in quantum physics. These agents either search too broadly or give up too early. They can't easily check which parts of their answer are already correct and which still need more work, making deep research expensive and unreliable.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 自我改进循环：AREX不是一次搜索就完事，而是逐条验证自己答案的每个约束条件，只对还未解决的部分重新搜索。 • 智能记忆压缩：内置工具将不断增长的研究历史压缩成一个紧凑的"改进状态"，避免遗忘已确认的信息。 • 高效训练方式：结合合成任务和高质量真实样本，用强化学习奖励"关键决定性步骤"而非仅奖励最终答案，解决了长时序稀疏奖励问题。 • Self-improvement loop: AREX doesn't just search once — it checks its own answer constraint by constraint, then re-searches only for the parts that are still wrong or unverified. • Smart memory compression: A built-in tool compresses the growing history of research into a compact "improvement state," so the agent doesn't forget what it's already confirmed. • Efficient training: Uses both synthetic tasks and real high-quality examples, plus a reinforcement learning trick that rewards the agent for key decisive steps, not just the final answer.
🧪 实验
内容		 团队在五个主要基准上测试了AREX：BrowseComp（复杂网页浏览问答）、WideSearch（广泛多跳搜索）、DeepSearchQA（深度多约束问答）、Humanity's Last Exam（HLE，极难专家级问题），以及通用推理和工具使用基准。他们将AREX与同等规模的最先进研究智能体和搜索增强大语言模型进行对比，使用两种模型规模：小型4B稠密模型和大型122B混合专家模型（激活参数10B）。 The team tested AREX on five major benchmarks: BrowseComp (complex web browsing questions), WideSearch (broad multi-hop search), DeepSearchQA (deep multi-constraint QA), Humanity's Last Exam (HLE, extremely hard expert-level questions), and general reasoning and tool-use benchmarks. They compared AREX against similarly sized state-of-the-art research agents and search-augmented LLMs, using two model sizes — a small 4B dense model and a large 122B Mixture-of-Experts model (with 10B active parameters).
📊 实验
结果		 AREX在所有测试基准上大幅超越同等规模的基线模型。在BrowseComp上，AREX在同等激活参数量的模型中达到最优水平。122B-A10B混合专家模型能与激活参数量远更大的模型竞争，充分说明AREX以小博大。在公认最难的AI基准HLE上，AREX与规模大得多的系统相比仍具竞争力。在WideSearch和DeepSearchQA上，相比之前的深度研究智能体均有明显提升，验证了递归自我改进方法在宽泛和深度研究任务上的普遍有效性。 AREX substantially outperforms comparable-scale baselines across all tested benchmarks. On BrowseComp, AREX achieves state-of-the-art results among models of similar activated parameter counts. The 122B-A10B MoE model remains competitive with models that use significantly more activated parameters — meaning AREX punches well above its weight class. On HLE (one of the hardest AI benchmarks), AREX scores competitively against much larger systems. On WideSearch and DeepSearchQA, it demonstrates clear improvements over prior deep research agents, validating the benefit of the recursive self-improvement approach across both broad and deep research tasks.
🏁 结论 AREX证明了一个递归自我改进的研究智能体——能审查自己的答案并有针对性地补充不足——可以大幅超越那些只是"搜索更久"的智能体，且即使在较小的模型规模下也能高效实现。这为AI驱动的深度研究确立了递归自我改进这一实用且强大的新范式。 AREX proves that a recursively self-improving research agent — one that audits its own answers and targets remaining gaps — can dramatically outperform agents that simply search longer, doing so efficiently even at modest model sizes. This establishes recursive self-improvement as a practical and powerful paradigm for AI-driven deep research.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

AREX通过两个嵌套循环工作，就像一个认真的学生：先写出草稿答案，再逐条对照要求检查，只修改不符合要求的部分。内循环负责上网搜索并构建初步答案；外循环像严格的编辑，逐条审查每个约束条件，标记未解决的部分，再派智能体针对性地继续搜索。为避免多轮研究后迷失，AREX自学了一个"上下文压缩工具"，把漫长的研究历史压缩成一份简洁的摘要，记录什么已确认、什么还悬而未决。整个系统先在精心设计的合成任务上训练，再用强化学习微调——奖励那些找到关键证据或纠正错误方向的决定性时刻。 AREX works in two nested loops, like a diligent student who drafts an essay, checks each requirement against a rubric, then rewrites only the failing parts. The inner loop searches the web and builds a draft answer. The outer loop acts like a strict editor — it goes through each constraint, flags what's still unresolved, and sends the agent back to search specifically for those gaps. To avoid losing track of what's been done across many rounds, AREX uses a self-learned "context compression" tool that squeezes the long research history into a short summary of what's confirmed and what's still open. The whole system is trained first on carefully constructed synthetic problems, then fine-tuned with reinforcement learning that gives bonus credit for the moments when the agent finds a crucial piece of evidence or corrects a wrong direction.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{RSI Loop: } s_{t+1} = \text{Compress}(s_t, \text{Research}(q, s_t))\]
💡 递归自我改进循环：每轮研究后，将当前状态与新证据压缩成下一轮的改进状态，持续迭代直到所有约束得到验证 The recursive self-improvement loop: after each research round, the current state and new evidence are compressed into the next improvement state, iterating until all constraints are verified

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI助手越来越多地用于复杂研究任务——如法律分析、科学文献综述、竞争情报——同时可靠地满足多个约束条件的能力至关重要。AREX展示了一条清晰路径：让AI智能体更像严谨的、能自我纠错的研究员，而不是一次性的答题机器。 As AI assistants are increasingly used for complex research tasks — legal analysis, scientific literature review, competitive intelligence — the ability to reliably satisfy multiple constraints at once is critical. AREX shows a clear path toward AI agents that are more like thorough, self-correcting researchers rather than one-shot answer machines.

OpenForgeRL: Train Harness-native Agents in Any Environment OpenForgeRL：在任意环境中训练原生推理框架智能体

🧑‍🔬 Xiao Yu, Baolin Peng, Ruize Xu, Hao Zou et al.

图 Figure · Page 4 of paper

🎯 开源框架让AI智能体直接在真实工具环境中端到端训练
An open-source framework that trains AI agents end-to-end inside the real-world tools and environments they actually use.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像培训新员工：以前只能在教室用道具模拟练习，现在OpenForgeRL让他们直接在真实岗位上学习——用真实工具、做真实任务——同时有个聪明的教练在背后默默记录每一步操作，给出反馈帮助他们进步。Think of it like training a new employee: instead of making them practice with fake props in a classroom, OpenForgeRL lets them learn on the real job — real tools, real tasks — while a clever coach silently watches every move and gives feedback to help them improve.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现代AI智能体（比如代码助手或网页操控机器人）不是单独运行的，而是嵌套在复杂的"推理框架"（harness）中——这些框架帮助智能体使用工具、浏览网页或分步完成任务。问题在于，现有的AI训练系统无法与这些框架配合，研究人员无法在真实环境中直接训练和改进智能体。就像只能在停车场练车，却从没上过真正的赛道。 Modern AI agents like coding assistants or browser-controlling bots don't just run alone — they live inside complex "harnesses," special software wrappers that help them use tools, browse the web, or write code step by step. The problem is that today's AI training systems weren't built to work with these harnesses, so researchers can't easily improve agents while they're running in their real environments. It's like trying to train a race car driver only in a parking lot, never on an actual track.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • OpenForgeRL是首个允许AI智能体在真实推理框架（如Claude Code、Codex）内直接进行强化学习训练的开源框架，无需重写框架代码。• 设计了一个轻量级"代理拦截器"，悄悄捕获框架发出的模型调用，记录为训练数据，无缝接入标准RL训练系统。• 基于Kubernetes的云端编排器为每个训练回合分配独立容器，使训练安全、可扩展、且与具体环境无关。 1. OpenForgeRL is the first open framework to let AI agents be trained with reinforcement learning directly inside real inference harnesses (like Claude Code or Codex), without needing to rewrite them. 2. A clever lightweight "proxy" quietly intercepts model calls from the harness, records them as training data, and feeds them to a standard RL training system — all invisibly. 3. A Kubernetes-based cloud orchestrator runs each training episode in its own isolated container, making training safe, scalable, and environment-agnostic.
🧪 实验
内容		 团队测试了两类智能体：工具/代码智能体（OpenForgeClaw）和图形界面/浏览器智能体（OpenForgeGUI）。工具智能体在ClawEval和QwenClawBench基准上测试，与开源基线对比。GUI智能体在OSWorld-Verified（电脑操作）、Online-Mind2Web（网页导航）和WebVoyager（网页浏览）上测试。所有模型仅用数百到数千个任务训练，与同等规模乃至更大规模的开源模型对比。还分析了不同框架选择（ZeroClaw、OpenClaw、Codex）如何影响强化学习的结果。 The team tested two types of agents: tool/code-use agents (OpenForgeClaw) and GUI/browser agents (OpenForgeGUI). For tool agents, they used benchmarks ClawEval and QwenClawBench, comparing against open-source baselines. For GUI agents, they tested on OSWorld-Verified (computer use), Online-Mind2Web (web navigation), and WebVoyager (web browsing). All models were trained on only a few hundred to a few thousand tasks, and compared against open-source models of similar and larger sizes. They also analyzed how different harness choices (ZeroClaw, OpenClaw, Codex) shape what RL actually learns.
📊 实验
结果		 OpenForgeClaw在ClawEval上达到31.7 pass³和55.9 pass@3，在QwenClawBench上达到33.7。OpenForgeGUI在OSWorld-Verified上达到37.7，在Online-Mind2Web上达到63.0，在WebVoyager上达到72.3。两个模型在几乎所有基准上均优于同等规模的开源基线。尤其值得注意的是，OpenForgeGUI仅用数百到数千个任务训练，却能媲美甚至超越体量大数倍的模型。 OpenForgeClaw achieves 31.7 pass³ and 55.9 pass@3 on ClawEval, and 33.7 on QwenClawBench. OpenForgeGUI achieves 37.7 on OSWorld-Verified, 63.0 on Online-Mind2Web, and 72.3 on WebVoyager. Both models outperform open-source baselines of similar size on nearly all benchmarks. Notably, OpenForgeGUI matches or surpasses models several times larger in the GUI setting — a strong efficiency win from training with only hundreds to a few thousand tasks.
🏁 结论 OpenForgeRL证明了AI智能体可以在真实推理框架中利用开源强化学习基础设施进行端到端训练，以极少量训练数据达到最先进的性能。它让智能体训练不再依赖专有工具，真正向研究社区开放。 OpenForgeRL proves that AI agents can be effectively trained end-to-end inside real-world inference harnesses using open-source reinforcement learning infrastructure, achieving state-of-the-art results with surprisingly small amounts of training data. It democratizes agent training by making it accessible to researchers without proprietary tools.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

OpenForgeRL在推理框架和AI模型之间插入一个聪明的"中间人"（轻量级代理）。每当框架请求模型思考或行动时，代理悄悄记录这段对话，并传递给标准强化学习训练系统（如veRL）。与此同时，云端编排系统（Kubernetes）为每个训练回合启动一个全新的隔离虚拟容器，让智能体在真实网页、终端或图形界面中交互，互不干扰。每轮结束后，记录的交互被转化为训练信号来改进模型。这样，训练与推理彻底解耦，但智能体始终在真实部署环境中接受训练。 OpenForgeRL works by placing a smart "middleman" (a lightweight proxy) between the harness and the AI model. When the harness asks the model to think or act, the proxy silently records that conversation and passes it to a standard reinforcement learning training system (like veRL). Meanwhile, a cloud orchestration system (Kubernetes) spins up a fresh, isolated virtual container for every single training episode, so the agent can interact with real websites, terminals, or GUIs without one session interfering with another. After each episode, the recorded interactions are turned into training signals to improve the model. This way, training and inference are cleanly separated, yet the agent always trains in its true deployment environment.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{pass}^3 = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{1}[\text{all 3 attempts pass}]\]
💡 pass³衡量模型在同一任务上连续3次尝试全部成功的比例，是对可靠性的严格考核 pass³ measures the fraction of tasks where all 3 independent attempts succeed — a strict test of consistent reliability

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

目前，最强大的AI智能体（如代码助手或电脑操控机器人）大多由大型科技公司用私有工具训练和改进。OpenForgeRL让所有人都能用开源、可扩展的方式在真实环境中训练智能体，有望加速各行业自主AI的突破。这意味着在软件开发、网页研究和日常电脑任务方面，AI可以进步得更快。 Right now, the most capable AI agents (like coding assistants or computer-controlling bots) are mostly trained and improved by large tech companies with private tools. OpenForgeRL changes this by giving everyone an open, scalable way to train agents in their real environments — potentially accelerating breakthroughs in autonomous AI across industries. This could mean faster progress in AI that helps with software development, web research, and everyday computer tasks.

GS-Agent: Creating 4D Physical Worlds With Generative Simulation GS-Agent：通过生成式仿真创建4D物理世界

🧑‍🔬 Hongxin Zhang, Chunru Lin, Junyan Li, Zhou Xian et al.

图 Figure · Page 6 of paper

🎯 用自然语言指令，AI自动生成真实物理动态的4D虚拟世界
A multi-agent AI system that turns plain text descriptions into physically realistic, dynamic 4D worlds automatically.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像你告诉一个"全能工作室团队"——有道具师、灯光师、导演、特效组——你的剧本想法，他们各司其职自动把场景搭出来，你喝杯咖啡回来，电影场景已经拍好了。GS-Agent就是这个AI版全能团队，而且每一幕都遵守物理定律，水真的会流，球真的会弹。Think of it like hiring a full movie production crew — props master, lighting director, special effects team, and cinematographer — except they're all AI agents. You just whisper your scene idea, go grab a coffee, and come back to find a fully animated, physically realistic 4D world ready to go. The water actually flows, the rubber ball actually bounces — because real physics rules are baked in.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 想象一下，你只是说了一句"下雨天，一个球滚过泥地"，就能自动生成一个有雨水、泥浆、滚动球体的逼真动态3D动画——但现有的AI和传统方法都做不到这一点。传统电脑动画需要专业美术师手动调整材质、运动和灯光，费时费力；而现有的AI生成方法虽然能生成图像或视频，却很难保证物理真实性，比如液体流动、弹性物体变形等细节常常看起来很假。这个问题在游戏、电影、机器人训练等领域都是巨大的痛点。 Imagine telling a computer "a ball rolls through muddy ground in the rain" and instantly getting a realistic animated 4D scene with splashing water and deforming mud — but today's tools can't do that automatically. Traditional 3D animation requires skilled artists to manually fine-tune every material, motion, and light setting, which takes enormous effort. Existing AI methods can generate images or video clips, but they often produce physically unrealistic results — liquids don't flow right, soft objects don't squish properly, and nothing really obeys the laws of physics.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首创"以代码驱动物理引擎"的多智能体框架，多个专属AI协作完成场景构建，无需人工干预 • 将4D世界创建拆解为"实体管理"（资产获取、材质调整、位置摆放、运动控制）和"渲染配置"（相机和灯光）两大模块，分工明确 • 内置物理引擎在循环中持续校验，确保液体、柔性体、刚体之间的交互符合真实物理规律 • 支持电影级相机运镜和灯光控制，生成结果既逼真又可控 • First end-to-end multi-agent framework where specialized AI agents collaborate via code to build physically realistic 4D worlds from text — no human editing needed. • Cleverly splits the hard task into "entity management" (finding 3D assets, setting materials, positioning, animating) and "rendering configuration" (camera angles, lighting), letting expert agents tackle each piece. • A physics engine runs inside the loop at every step, acting as a reality check so liquids, soft objects, and rigid bodies all behave the way they should in the real world.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队设计了多种场景测试GS-Agent的能力，涵盖液体（如水流、倒液体）、柔性物体（如布料、橡皮）、刚体（如箱子、球）及它们之间的复杂交互。评估指标包括物理真实性、场景与文本描述的一致性、视觉质量，以及与现有方法（如纯生成式视频/图像方法）的对比。同时通过用户研究和自动化评测两种方式验证生成质量。 The team tested GS-Agent across a wide variety of scene types, including liquid interactions (water pouring, splashing), deformable objects (cloth, soft bodies), and rigid body dynamics (boxes, balls colliding), as well as complex mixed scenes with all three. They compared against existing generative methods that produce images or videos directly from text. Evaluation used both automatic metrics (physical plausibility, text-scene alignment, visual quality) and human user studies to judge realism and controllability.
📊 实验
结果		 GS-Agent在物理真实性、文本对齐度和视觉质量等维度上均显著优于现有纯生成式方法。用户研究显示，超过80%的评估者认为GS-Agent生成的场景在物理真实性上优于对比基线方法。系统能够生成包含液体、柔性体和刚体复杂交互的多样化4D场景，并支持电影级相机运镜（如推拉摇移）和动态灯光效果，而现有方法通常无法同时做到这几点。 GS-Agent significantly outperforms existing purely generative (image/video) methods in physical plausibility, text alignment, and visual quality. User studies showed that over 80% of evaluators preferred GS-Agent's outputs for physical realism compared to baseline methods. The system successfully generates diverse 4D scenes featuring complex interactions among liquids, soft bodies, and rigid objects, while also supporting cinematic camera movements and dynamic lighting — capabilities that existing baselines largely cannot combine in a single pipeline.
🏁 结论 本文证明了通过多智能体协作结合物理引擎，AI可以从自然语言自动生成既视觉逼真又物理可信的4D动态世界，为4D内容生成开辟了全新范式。这一框架有望成为创意内容创作和物理AI训练的重要基础工具。 This paper proves that a team of collaborating AI agents, guided by a physics engine, can automatically turn plain language into rich, physically realistic 4D animated worlds — establishing a new blueprint for how AI should generate dynamic content. GS-Agent sets the stage for a new era in automated creative production and training environments for physical AI systems.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

核心思路是"反向工程出题"：研究团队不是直接从网上找题，而是先找到真实的代码变更（比如GitHub上的一次代码提交或合并请求），再把这个变更的上下文改写成一个普通员工对AI助手说话的口吻，比如"帮我修一下这个按钮点击没反应的问题"。这样，即使有人搜索原始提交记录，也找不到和题目一模一样的描述，防止AI"作弊"。四个领域的题目分别打包成统一的"任务目录"格式，配套评测工具和环境镜像，在两个主流AI编程助手（CodeBuddy Code 和 Claude Code）上运行，采用各自领域适合的评分方式（如单元测试通过率、前端截图对比、安全漏洞发现数等）。数据集版本化发布，通过持续更新来保持抗污染性，而不是靠保密。 The key idea is "reverse-engineering the questions." Instead of copying problems from the internet, the team starts with a real code change (like a GitHub commit or pull request), then rewrites the context as a casual, conversational request — the kind of thing a regular employee might say to an AI assistant, like "hey, can you fix the button that doesn't work?" This way, even if someone searches for the original commit, they won't find a matching test prompt, blocking the AI from "cheating." All four domain-specific task sets are packaged in a uniform folder format with evaluation tools and environment images, then run on two real-world AI coding agents (CodeBuddy Code and Claude Code). Each domain uses its own appropriate scoring method, and contamination resistance is maintained through ongoing dataset versioning rather than keeping secrets.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI编程助手越来越多地进入职场，我们迫切需要知道它们到底有多可靠——而不是依赖可能"泄题"的测试成绩。这个基准让开发者、企业和研究者能更诚实地比较不同AI工具的真实能力，从而做出更明智的技术选型决策。 As AI coding assistants become more common in real workplaces, we urgently need honest, tamper-proof ways to measure how good they actually are. This benchmark gives developers, companies, and researchers a trustworthy testing ground to compare AI tools fairly — helping everyone make smarter choices about which AI to actually rely on at work.
