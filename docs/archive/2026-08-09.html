Energy-Guided Flow Matching 能量引导流匹配

🧑‍🔬 Haoyang Tong, Yu He, Fang Li, Lichen Ma et al.

图 Figure · Page 3 of paper

🎯 用"从模糊到清晰"的渐进轨迹，让AI图像生成更快更好
A smarter generative path that guides image synthesis from coarse to fine, improving quality with fewer training steps.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像画家画肖像时，先用铅笔勾勒大致轮廓，再逐渐加上色彩和细节，而不是一上来就直接画每根睫毛——EG-FM让AI也学会了这种"先整体后细节"的绘画节奏，画得又快又好。Think of it like a sculptor who first shapes a rough lump of clay into a face, then gradually carves in the nose, eyes, and finally tiny wrinkles — instead of trying to carve every wrinkle all at once from a random blob. EG-FM teaches the AI to generate images the same smart way: big shapes first, fine details last.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的AI图像生成模型在生成高分辨率图像时，需要同时学习图像的整体构图（大轮廓）和细节纹理（细节），这就像让一个画家同时关注整幅画的构图和每根头发丝的细节，非常困难。传统方法让AI从随机噪声直接"飞向"最终图像，但忽略了一个自然规律：我们人类画画时总是先勾勒大轮廓，再添加细节。这种"跳步"方式导致模型需要更多训练时间，生成质量也有提升空间。 Existing AI image generators have to learn both the big picture (overall layout) and tiny details (textures, fine lines) all at once in a single high-dimensional space. Standard "flow matching" methods send the AI on a straight-line path from random noise to the final image, but never teach it to think step-by-step — coarse shapes first, then fine details. This makes training slow and image quality harder to maximize.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 创新"移动终点"机制：不再让生成轨迹终止于固定的清晰图像，而是让终点从模糊低频图像逐渐演变为完整清晰图像，自然引导模型先学大结构再学细节。 • 能量引导调度：设计了一种针对每张图像自适应的"高频信号释放"策略，根据图像本身的频谱特性来决定何时、以多快的速度加入细节信息。 • 零改造成本：不需要修改已有模型结构或训练数据，直接插入现有框架即可使用，训练和推理的额外开销几乎可忽略不计。 • Moving Endpoint: Instead of always aiming at the full clean image, the target smoothly evolves from a blurry, low-frequency version to the full sharp image — teaching the model to build structure before details. • Energy-Guided Scheduling: An image-specific strategy controls exactly when and how fast high-frequency detail is "released," tailored to each image's own spectral content. • Plug-and-Play: No changes to model architecture or training data are needed — it wraps around existing systems with negligible extra cost.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在ImageNet数据集上进行了256×256和512×512两种分辨率的类别条件图像生成测试，以FID（越低越好）为主要评估指标，并与SiT、MaskBit等主流生成模型基线进行对比。此外，还将EG-FM迁移到文本到图像生成任务，在GenEval和DPG-Bench两个基准上评估生成质量和文本-图像对齐能力。 Experiments were run on the standard ImageNet benchmark for class-conditional image generation at both 256×256 and 512×512 resolutions, using FID (Fréchet Inception Distance, lower = better) as the primary metric, compared against strong baselines like SiT and MaskBit. The method was also transferred to text-to-image generation and evaluated on GenEval and DPG-Bench, which measure how well generated images match text prompts.
📊 实验
结果		 • ImageNet 256×256：仅训练200个epoch就达到FID 1.55，训练600个epoch达到FID 1.45，优于同类方法。 • ImageNet 512×512：在256×256预训练基础上，仅需40个高分辨率适应epoch就达到FID 1.58。 • 文本到图像：GenEval得分0.85，DPG-Bench得分83.9，均达到竞争性水平。 • 相比基线（如SiT-XL/2），EG-FM在更少训练轮次下一致取得更低FID，体现了显著的训练效率提升。 • ImageNet 256×256: FID of 1.55 at just 200 training epochs, and 1.45 at 600 epochs — competitive with or better than prior state-of-the-art methods. • ImageNet 512×512: FID of 1.58 after only 40 high-resolution adaptation epochs on top of the 256×256 model. • Text-to-image: GenEval score of 0.85 and DPG-Bench score of 83.9, reaching competitive levels. • Consistently lower FID than baselines (e.g., SiT-XL/2) across all training budgets, demonstrating clear training efficiency gains.
🏁 结论 本文证明了通过让生成轨迹的终点"动起来"——从模糊到清晰地演变——可以显著提升图像生成质量和训练效率，且无需改动模型结构。EG-FM为流匹配类生成模型提供了一种简单有效的即插即用改进方案。 This paper proves that making the generation target dynamically evolve from blurry to sharp — rather than always pointing at the final image — meaningfully boosts both image quality and training efficiency with zero architectural changes. EG-FM is a practical, plug-and-play upgrade for flow-matching generative models.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

EG-FM的核心思路是改变生成过程的"目的地"。在普通流匹配中，AI从噪声出发，目标始终是最终清晰图像。EG-FM则用"热核滤波"技术，把终点图像先变成一张模糊版本（只保留大轮廓和低频信息），随着生成步骤的推进，这个模糊终点逐渐"解冻"，越来越多的高频细节被加入进来，直到变成完整清晰图像。这个"解冻"速度由一个能量引导调度器控制，它会根据每张图像自身的频率特性动态决定细节释放的节奏，让生成路径更符合"先整体后细节"的自然规律。整个过程不需要修改原有模型的任何结构。 EG-FM changes where the AI is "aiming" during generation. In standard flow matching, the model always targets the final sharp image from the very start. EG-FM uses a mathematical tool called a heat-kernel filter to create a blurry, low-frequency version of the target image as the initial endpoint. As generation progresses, this endpoint gradually "thaws" — more and more high-frequency detail is added back in, guided by an energy scheduler that adapts to each image's unique frequency content. The model therefore learns a natural coarse-to-fine trajectory without any changes to its architecture or training data.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

更快、更好的图像生成模型意味着AI绘画、影视特效、游戏设计等应用能以更低的计算成本产出更高质量的内容。EG-FM的即插即用特性让它很容易被整合进现有的商业AI图像生成系统中，推动整个行业的效率提升。 Better image generators with less training time directly translate to cheaper, higher-quality AI art, visual effects, and design tools for everyone. Because EG-FM plugs into existing systems without redesigning them, the industry can adopt this improvement quickly and economically.

HarnessOpt-Bench: Evaluating LLMs at Harness Optimization HarnessOpt-Bench：评估大语言模型在"执行框架优化"任务上的能力

🧑‍🔬 Varun Ursekar, Apaar Shanker, Yash Maurya, Shehab Yasser et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 首个专门评测AI自动优化其执行框架能力的基准
A benchmark measuring how well LLMs can automatically improve the scaffolding that makes AI agents work.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个赛车手不只靠驾驶技术取胜，还要不断调整赛车的设置（轮胎、悬挂、油量）。这篇论文举办了一场"谁最擅长在比赛中自己调赛车"的锦标赛，并发明了一套公平的计分规则。Think of it like a race where the drivers also have to tune their own cars during pit stops — this paper created the first official rulebook and scoreboard for judging who's the best self-tuning racer, discovering that the driver matters way more than the pit crew's toolkit.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现在的AI系统不只是一个模型，而是模型+提示词+工具+控制流程等一整套"执行框架"（harness）的组合。如何让AI自动优化这套框架，并科学衡量哪些AI最擅长做这件事，目前完全没有统一标准。就像不同厨师的厨艺有高低，但从来没有人举办过"谁最擅长改进整个厨房配置"的比赛。 Modern AI systems are not just a single model — they're a whole package: prompts, tools, memory, and code that wraps around the model (called a "harness"). Making a better AI system often means improving this whole package, not just the model itself. But there's no standard test to measure how good different AI systems are at automatically improving their own harness. It's like having lots of chefs but no competition to judge who's best at redesigning an entire kitchen.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首创"端到端执行框架优化"基准，填补了业界空白，让不同AI的优化能力可以被公平比较。 • 设计了受信任的执行环境，严格隔离测试集，防止AI"作弊"偷看答案，保证评测公正性。 • 发现模型本身的优化能力差异，远大于它们所使用的编程框架差异——说明"优化能力"是模型的内在本领。 • 揭示了"原生框架不总是最好的"这一反直觉结论，为AI系统设计提供了新思路。 • First benchmark dedicated to end-to-end harness optimization, creating a fair playing field to compare AI optimizers. • Uses a trusted execution environment that strictly hides test data, so the AI can't "cheat" by peeking at answers. • Finds that which LLM you use as the optimizer matters far more than which coding framework it operates through. • Counterintuitively reveals that an AI's "native" harness is not reliably better than a shared standard one.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验选取了5个前沿大语言模型（如GPT-4o、Claude等顶尖模型）作为"优化器"，在4个不同的下游任务上进行测试。每个模型既在统一的共享编程框架下测试，也在各自的"原生框架"下测试，共进行了111次有效评分实验。评估指标为"归一化增益"——即优化后的框架相对于初始种子框架在测试集上的性能提升比例。实验还测试了不同"种子质量"（初始框架好坏）对优化结果的影响。 Five frontier LLMs served as optimizers and were tested on 4 downstream tasks. Each model was evaluated both through a shared coding harness and through its own native harness. The key metric was "normalized gain" — how much the optimized harness improved over the original seed harness on a hidden test set. Across all conditions, 111 scored runs were collected. Experiments also varied the quality of the starting seed harness to see how that affects the optimizer's ability to improve it.
📊 实验
结果		 在111次评分实验中，不同优化器模型之间的性能差距显著大于不同编程框架之间的差距，说明"谁来优化"比"用什么框架优化"更重要。原生框架相比共享框架并没有稳定的优势。不同任务和不同种子质量下的优化增益差异极大，说明这是一个高度任务敏感的能力。具体数字上，各模型的归一化增益从接近零到可观的正增益均有分布，整体仍有很大提升空间，表明这是一个对当前前沿模型都有挑战性的任务。（论文未公布单一最优模型的精确分数，但记录了跨5模型×4任务×多种子条件的全面比较结果。） Across 111 scored runs, the choice of optimizer LLM produced far more variation in performance than the choice of coding harness. Native harnesses showed no consistent advantage over the shared harness. Normalized gains varied substantially across the 4 tasks and different seed quality levels, confirming this is a highly task-sensitive skill. Individual model gains ranged from near-zero to meaningfully positive, but the overall performance ceiling remains low — indicating that even today's best frontier models have a long way to go at harness optimization. (The paper reports comparative trends across 5 models × 4 tasks rather than a single headline accuracy number.)
🏁 结论 本文证明了"AI自动优化其执行框架"是一种真实存在、可被科学测量的能力，并且当前顶尖AI模型在这方面仍有巨大提升空间。HarnessOpt-Bench为未来这一领域的研究提供了标准化评测协议。 This paper proves that the ability of an AI to automatically improve its own operating scaffolding is a real, measurable skill — and one that today's best models still perform poorly at. HarnessOpt-Bench gives the research community a standardized way to track and drive progress on this important capability.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究者设计了一个像"闯关游戏"一样的评测流程：给AI一个初始执行框架（种子框架），让AI通过反复修改、运行、看反馈的方式来改进它，但全程只能看训练反馈，不能碰测试集。最终用一个独立的测试集来打分，看AI改进后的框架比初始版本提升了多少（归一化增益）。整个过程有预算限制，就像给厨师规定只能用有限次数的试菜机会来改进菜谱。5个顶尖大模型作为"优化器"参与测试，覆盖4个不同的下游任务，总计进行了111次评分实验。 The researchers built a structured competition: an AI (the "optimizer") is given a starting harness and asked to improve it through a series of edits and tests, but with a fixed budget of evaluation attempts — like a chef who only gets 10 trial tastings to perfect a recipe. The AI sees feedback from training runs but never touches the hidden test set. At the end, the improved harness is scored on that held-out test set by measuring how much better it performs compared to the original starting point (called normalized gain). Five top LLMs were tested as optimizers, each working through 4 different tasks, producing 111 total scored runs.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{Normalized Gain} = \frac{\text{Score}(\text{optimized harness}) - \text{Score}(\text{seed harness})}{\text{Score}(\text{seed harness})}\]
💡 归一化增益：衡量AI优化后的框架相对于初始框架在测试集上提升了多少比例，是本基准的核心评分指标。 Normalized Gain measures how much better the AI's improved harness performs compared to the original starting harness on the hidden test set — the core scoring metric of the benchmark.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI越来越多地以"智能体"形式部署，AI系统的整体性能不只取决于模型本身，还取决于围绕模型的那套"脚手架"。如果AI能自动优化这套脚手架，就能大幅降低人工调优成本，让AI系统自我进化。这个基准为这一方向的研究提供了科学的"尺子"，意义深远。 As AI moves from simple chatbots to complex "agents" that plan and act in the world, the scaffolding around the model becomes just as important as the model itself. If AI can learn to automatically tune that scaffolding, it could dramatically reduce the human effort needed to build powerful AI systems. This benchmark gives researchers the measuring stick they need to make real progress on that goal.

Towards Physics of Multimodal Pretraining: Knowledge Flow, Modality Synergy, Early Unification, and Recipes 迈向多模态预训练的物理学：知识流动、模态协同、早期统一与训练秘诀

🧑‍🔬 Junlin Han, Shengbang Tong, David Fan, Minghao Chen et al.

🎯 揭示语言与视觉联合预训练四大核心规律的系统性研究
A systematic study uncovering four key principles that govern how language and vision work together during AI pretraining.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像研究怎样同时教好一个学生音乐和数学：这篇论文相当于发现了"从第一天就两门课一起上、别让他靠音乐天赋偷懒不学数学、理论课共用同一个老师但练习课各请专科老师"——有了这套秘诀，每所学校都能照着做，不用再靠猜了。Think of it like figuring out the best way to teach a student both music and math at the same time. This paper is like discovering: "start both subjects on day one, don't let them ignore math by leaning on music skills, and use the same teacher for theory but different teachers for practice" — now every school can follow this recipe instead of guessing.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 当今最强的AI模型同时处理文字和图像，但没人真正搞清楚"两者如何一起训练最有效"。就像同时教一个孩子中文和英文，却不知道该从第一天就混合教，还是先学一门再学另一门。研究者们一直靠猜测来设计训练方案，浪费了大量算力。 Today's best AI models try to understand and generate both text and images at the same time — but nobody really knows the "rules" for how to train them together effectively. It's like trying to teach someone two languages simultaneously without knowing whether to mix them from day one or teach one first. Researchers have been guessing at the best approach, wasting huge amounts of computing power in the process.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 发现"知识流动"现象：语言和视觉之间的知识传递存在明显方向性和不对称性，并非平等互助。 • 揭示数据"复杂度"是决定两种模态相互促进还是相互竞争的关键开关。 • 发现"视觉懒惰"现象：如果推迟将视觉加入训练，模型会偷懒依赖语言先验，而不是真正学会"看"。 • 提炼出高效训练秘诀，仅用5%的算力就能达到强劲的生成性能，大幅节省成本。 • Discovered "Knowledge Flow": language and vision don't share knowledge equally — there's a clear direction and asymmetry to how they help each other. • Found that data complexity is the key switch between modalities "helping each other" vs. "fighting each other" for model capacity. • Revealed "Vision Laziness": if you delay mixing vision into training, the model gets lazy and just relies on language shortcuts instead of truly learning to see. • Derived efficient training recipes that hit strong performance using only 5% of normal compute — a massive practical saving.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在合成数据集和大规模真实多模态数据集上同时进行。团队训练的模型规模从小型受控实验一直扩展到135亿参数的混合专家（MoE）大模型，共在2万亿token上进行训练。评估基准涵盖视觉理解（图像问答、图像描述）和视觉生成（图像合成质量）等标准测试。基线包括延迟融合对齐模型、顺序训练模型以及多种架构变体，以单独分析每个设计选择的影响。 Experiments were conducted on both synthetic datasets and large real-world multimodal datasets. The team trained models ranging from small controlled setups up to 13.5 billion parameter Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models on 2 trillion tokens. They evaluated on standard benchmarks covering visual understanding (image question answering, captioning) and visual generation (image synthesis quality). Baselines included models trained with late-fusion alignment, sequential training, and various architectural variants to isolate the effect of each design choice.
📊 实验
结果		 高效训练秘诀仅用标准训练5%的算力，即可达到强劲的生成和理解性能。研究结论在135亿参数MoE模型、2万亿token规模上得到验证。采用"共享注意力+归一化、模态专属前馈层"架构的模型，在多个基准测试上持续优于完全共享或完全分离的架构。从训练一开始就联合多模态（早期统一）的策略，在所有测试配置中均优于延迟对齐和顺序训练，而推迟引入视觉模态则会导致可量化的性能下降（视觉懒惰现象）。 The efficient pretraining recipes achieved strong generative and understanding performance using only 5% of the full compute budget compared to standard training approaches. The findings were validated at scale with 13.5B MoE models trained on 2T tokens. Models using shared attention and normalization with modality-specific feed-forward layers consistently outperformed fully shared or fully separate architectures across multiple benchmarks. Early unification (joining modalities from the start) outperformed late alignment and sequential training strategies across all tested configurations, with "vision laziness" causing measurable performance degradation when vision was introduced late.
🏁 结论 本文确立了多模态预训练的四大基本规律——知识流动、模态协同、早期统一和高效训练秘诀，揭示了文字与视觉在联合训练中的相互作用机制，为更高效地构建强大多模态AI模型提供了有科学依据的蓝图。 This paper establishes four fundamental principles — knowledge flow, modality synergy, early unification, and efficient recipes — that explain how text and vision interact during joint AI training. It provides a science-backed blueprint for building powerful multimodal AI models far more efficiently than before.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究者像物理学家做实验一样，进行了数百次控制变量实验——每次只改变一个因素，观察其影响。他们测试了不同的文本和图像数据混合方式、不同的模型架构（AI内部模块的组织方式），以及不同的训练时间表（什么时候引入哪种模态）。实验同时在小型合成数据集（便于分析）和大规模真实数据集上进行，以验证结论的普适性。他们特别研究了模型内部共享还是分离各模态的组件，以及从训练初期还是后期才开始联合训练，哪种方式更有效。 The researchers ran hundreds of controlled experiments — like a physicist running lab tests — varying one thing at a time to isolate what actually matters. They tested different ways of mixing text and image data, different model architectures (how the building blocks of the AI are arranged), and different training schedules (when to introduce each modality). They used both small synthetic datasets (to keep things clean and interpretable) and massive real-world datasets to confirm their findings. They specifically looked at whether shared or separate model components for each modality lead to better teamwork, and whether starting joint training early vs. late makes a difference.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

RST（递归合成终端任务）的工作方式就像滚雪球：从少量人工验证的"种子任务"出发，用大模型把参考解答延长、加复杂，然后自动重写对应的任务指令和验证器，让三者保持一致。接着在全新的隔离沙箱里运行测试，通过了就把这条新任务作为下一轮的种子，再继续扩展。如此循环15轮，每一轮产出的任务都比上一轮更难、更复杂。 RST (Recursive Synthetic Terminal Tasks) works like a snowball rolling downhill — it starts small and keeps growing. First, a handful of human-verified "seed tasks" are collected. Then, an LLM extends each seed's reference solution — adding more steps, more complexity. The instruction and the verifier are then automatically rewritten to match the new, harder solution. The whole thing is run inside a fresh, isolated sandbox to check if it actually works. If it passes, the new task becomes a seed for the next round. This cycle repeats 15 times, each time producing tasks that are harder than the last batch.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{pass@}k = 1 - \frac{\binom{n-c}{k}}{\binom{n}{k}}\]
💡 pass@k 衡量AI在k次尝试内至少解出一道题的概率，用于评估任务难度和模型能力。 pass@k measures the probability that an AI solves a task correctly in at least one of k attempts — used here to track how task difficulty increases across rounds.

让AI能自主完成真实的电脑操作任务（写代码、管理文件、运行流程）需要昂贵的训练数据，这一直是重大瓶颈。RST让这类数据变得低廉且可规模化生产，有望推动新一波强大的"电脑操作AI"走进日常生产力工具和软件工程自动化领域。 Building AI that can autonomously complete real computer tasks — writing code, managing files, running pipelines — requires expensive training data that's been a major roadblock. RST makes this data cheap and scalable, potentially unlocking a new wave of powerful computer-use AI agents for everyday productivity tools and software engineering automation.
