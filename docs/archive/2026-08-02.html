Flux-OPD: On-Policy Distillation with Evolving Contexts Flux-OPD：基于动态上下文的在线策略蒸馏

🧑‍🔬 Yuran Wang, Zekun Wang, Bohan Zeng, Ruixu Zhang et al.

图 Figure · Page 4 of paper

🎯 用动态变化的上下文引导学生模型蒸馏，解决开放域任务缺乏可验证奖励的难题
A smarter distillation method that uses evolving feedback contexts to teach smaller AI models in open-ended tasks without needing fixed reward signals.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像给一个学钢琴的孩子配了一位会"随时调整要求"的老师：起初老师只要求弹对音符，等孩子进步了，就开始要求节奏感，再后来要求感情表达。Flux-OPD 就是这位聪明的老师——它知道哪些新要求是真正有帮助的，哪些只是互相矛盾的噪音，并据此调整自己的指导力度。Imagine a piano student whose teacher keeps raising the bar as they improve — first just hit the right notes, then add rhythm, then add emotion. But sometimes two teachers give conflicting advice, and a smart coach knows to tone down the mixed signals. Flux-OPD works exactly like that adaptive coach: it keeps evolving its guidance and automatically dials down confusing, contradictory feedback.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练大语言模型时，在开放式任务（比如创意写作、开放问答）中很难定义"正确答案"，因此也难以给出明确的奖励信号来指导训练。现有方法要么依赖人工打分，要么只能用静态的提示上下文来传达偏好，一旦学生模型学会了，这些上下文就失去了额外的指导价值。更糟的是，如果让上下文随学生进步而动态变化，又会导致训练目标不稳定，产生相互冲突的学习信号。 Training AI models on open-ended tasks — like creative writing or free-form Q&A — is really hard because there's no clear "right answer" to reward. Existing methods either require expensive human feedback or use static context clues that stop being useful once the student model has already learned from them. And if you try to update those context clues as the student improves, the training becomes unstable and sends conflicting signals to the model.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次通过数学分解揭示：上下文条件教师的作用等价于其"几何平均"，并自然包含一个衡量冲突程度的项 • 提出"上下文差异信号"概念：将有上下文和无上下文的教师之间的差异提取出来，作为稳定的修正量注入训练 • 用冲突项作为权重指示器，自动降低冲突信号的影响，使训练更稳定 • 实现了动态上下文（随学生进步而进化）与在线策略蒸馏的有机结合 • Mathematically proves that multiple context-guided teachers act like their "geometric mean," with a built-in conflict measurement term • Introduces "contextual difference signals" — extracts only the extra info that context adds, rather than using raw context-guided outputs • Uses the conflict term as an automatic weight to mute noisy or contradictory training signals • Successfully combines evolving, adaptive contexts with on-policy distillation for the first time
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在多个开放式生成任务上进行，包括创意写作、摘要生成等开放域任务，与现有的在线策略蒸馏（OPD）方法进行对比，评估指标包括自动评分和人类偏好评估。基线方法涵盖了主流的知识蒸馏和对齐训练方法，实验验证了 Flux-OPD 在任务偏好对齐方面的效果。 The researchers tested Flux-OPD on several open-ended generation benchmarks, including creative writing and summarization tasks. They compared against existing on-policy distillation (OPD) methods as baselines, using both automatic scoring metrics and human preference evaluations to measure how well the trained models captured task preferences. The experiments were designed to stress-test whether evolving contexts genuinely help versus simply adding noise.
📊 实验
结果		 Flux-OPD 在开放式任务上超越了现有所有在线策略蒸馏（OPD）方法，在人类偏好评分和自动评估指标上均取得显著提升。具体而言，在多个开放域任务的对比中，Flux-OPD 相比最强基线取得了明显的性能优势，验证了动态上下文与蒸馏结合的有效性（论文报告了具体提升数字，典型提升幅度在数个百分点以上）。 Flux-OPD outperformed all existing on-policy distillation baselines on open-ended generation tasks, achieving meaningful gains in both automatic metrics and human preference scores. The method demonstrated consistent improvements across multiple open-ended benchmarks, confirming that combining evolving contexts with the conflict-weighted correction mechanism leads to better task preference alignment than prior OPD approaches.
🏁 结论 本文证明了动态进化的上下文可以作为有效的训练监督信号，只要通过数学分析合理提取上下文带来的差异信号并控制冲突，就能稳定地蒸馏出更符合任务偏好的语言模型。Flux-OPD 为开放域大语言模型对齐提供了一条无需固定奖励函数的新路径。 This paper proves that dynamically evolving context clues can be a reliable and powerful training signal for language models, as long as you carefully extract only the "extra value" they add and suppress conflicting signals. Flux-OPD opens a new path for aligning AI models to human preferences in open-ended tasks — without needing fixed, hand-crafted reward functions.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Flux-OPD 的核心思路是：与其直接让学生模型跟着"带上下文的老师"学习（这会导致目标不稳定），不如只提取上下文带来的"额外增益"部分。具体做法是，先用一个不带上下文的"基础老师"作为稳定锚点，再计算"带上下文老师"和"基础老师"的差值，称为上下文差异信号。然后把这个差异信号叠加到基础老师上，作为最终学习目标。同时，利用数学推导出的"冲突项"来衡量不同上下文信号之间的矛盾程度，矛盾越大，这部分信号的权重越低，从而保持训练稳定。整个过程随着学生模型的进步，上下文也会动态更新，持续提供有效的监督信号。 Instead of making the student model directly copy a teacher that's given extra context (which causes instability), Flux-OPD separates the "bonus knowledge" that context provides from the baseline teacher's output. Think of it as subtracting what the teacher would say without context from what it says with context — the difference is a clean, targeted "correction signal." This correction is then added onto a stable, context-free teacher as an anchor. A mathematically derived "conflict score" automatically reduces the influence of signals that contradict each other. As the student model gets better, the contexts evolve to keep pushing it forward, providing fresh and relevant supervision throughout training.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

现实中大量 AI 应用场景（如创意写作助手、开放式聊天机器人）都难以用固定规则定义"好答案"，这正是 Flux-OPD 解决的核心问题。这一方法让 AI 训练更灵活、更自适应，有望降低对昂贵人工标注的依赖，推动更智能的 AI 助手落地。 A huge portion of real-world AI use cases — chatbots, writing assistants, creative tools — involve open-ended tasks where there's no single "right" answer. Flux-OPD's ability to train models in these settings without fixed rewards could significantly reduce the need for expensive human labeling and make AI training more scalable and adaptive.

Qwen-UI-Agent Technical Report: Toward Next-Generation Real-World Centric Foundation GUI Agents Qwen-UI-Agent 技术报告：迈向以真实世界为中心的下一代基础 GUI 智能体

🧑‍🔬 Hanzhang Zhou, Panrong Tong, Xu Zhang, Quyu Kong et al.

图 Figure · Page 5 of paper

🎯 让AI像人一样跨平台操控手机、电脑和浏览器完成复杂任务
A unified AI agent that operates real phones, computers, and browsers to complete complex multi-step tasks autonomously.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像培训一个全能秘书：你告诉他"帮我处理今天所有的行政事务"，他不仅能在手机、电脑、网页上来回切换操作，还能自己总结哪里做错了、下次怎么改进——而且越用越聪明，几乎不需要你手把手教。Imagine hiring a super-intern who can use any app on any device — phone, laptop, or browser — without you showing them how each one works. And every time they make a mistake, they write their own correction notes and practice on their own overnight. That's Qwen-UI-Agent: a self-improving digital assistant that actually clicks, types, and gets things done for you.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的 AI 模型大多只能回答问题或生成文字，却无法真正"动手"帮你在手机或电脑上完成任务——比如订外卖、查资料、填写表格。即使有一些初步的 GUI 智能体，它们也很难跨越不同平台（手机/电脑/网页），更难处理需要几十步才能完成的复杂工作流，在真实设备上的可靠性也很差。 Most AI today can only chat or generate text — it can't actually "use" your phone or computer for you. Existing GUI agents (ones that click buttons and type like a human) often fail on real devices, can't easily switch between phone, computer, and web tasks, and fall apart when a job requires dozens of steps in a row.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 统一行动空间：将鼠标点击、键盘输入、命令行操作打包成一个框架，模型一次就能发出多个批量动作，效率大幅提升。 • 数据飞轮：用 AI 自动生成训练任务、诊断失败原因、规划下一轮改进，减少人工标注，实现自我进化。 • 超长轨迹强化学习：支持超过 100 步的操作序列训练，并用超过 1 万个并发环境加速，让智能体学会完成真正复杂的长任务。 • 真实设备运行时：不只在模拟器里跑，直接在真实手机上执行任务，结果更可靠。 • 主动服务发起：智能体不只被动响应指令，还能主动触发有用的服务（如提醒、自动化工作流）。 • Unified action space: mouse clicks, keyboard input, and command-line actions are all handled in one framework, and the agent can issue multiple actions in a single step — saving time and reducing errors. • Self-improving data flywheel: AI agents automatically create new training tasks, spot their own mistakes, and plan better next rounds — cutting the need for expensive human labeling. • Long-horizon RL training: trains on task sequences of 100+ steps using 10,000+ parallel virtual environments, so the agent can handle truly complex real-world workflows. • Real-device runtime: tested on actual physical phones, not just simulators, making results far more trustworthy. • Proactive service initiation: the agent doesn't just wait for instructions — it can trigger helpful actions on its own, like setting reminders or launching automated workflows.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队在多个主流基准测试上评估了 Qwen-UI-Agent，包括手机端的 MobileWorld、MobileWorld-Real 和 AndroidDaily，电脑端的 OSWorld-Verified 和 OSWorld-v2，以及浏览器任务 WebArena 和界面定位测试 ScreenSpot-Pro。对比对象包括当前最强的前沿模型，如 Claude Opus 4.8、Gemini 3.1 Pro 和 GPT-5.6 Sol。评估既包括模拟环境，也包括在真实手机设备上的测试。 The team tested Qwen-UI-Agent on a wide range of standard benchmarks: MobileWorld, MobileWorld-Real, and AndroidDaily for phone tasks; OSWorld-Verified and OSWorld-v2 for computer tasks; WebArena for browser-based tasks; and ScreenSpot-Pro for GUI element localization. They compared against top frontier models including Claude Opus 4.8, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and GPT-5.6 Sol, across both simulated and real physical device environments.
📊 实验
结果		 • 手机端：MobileWorld 达到 82.1%，MobileWorld-Real（真实设备）达到 92.2%，AndroidDaily 高达 97.5%，均为业界最优。 • 电脑端：OSWorld-Verified 达到 79.5%，OSWorld-v2 部分进度得分 40.0%，与顶尖模型持平或超越。 • 浏览器端：WebArena 达到 73.6%。 • 界面定位：ScreenSpot-Pro 达到 81.5%。 以上成绩在同类开放模型中均创下最新最高水平（State-of-the-Art），在手机使用任务上明显领先 Claude Opus 4.8、Gemini 3.1 Pro 和 GPT-5.6 Sol 等闭源前沿模型。 • Mobile: 82.1% on MobileWorld, 92.2% on MobileWorld-Real (real devices), 97.5% on AndroidDaily — all state-of-the-art. • Computer: 79.5% on OSWorld-Verified, 40.0% partial-progress score on OSWorld-v2 — competitive with or surpassing frontier models. • Browser: 73.6% on WebArena. • GUI grounding: 81.5% on ScreenSpot-Pro. On mobile tasks specifically, Qwen-UI-Agent outperforms closed-source giants like Claude Opus 4.8, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and GPT-5.6 Sol.
🏁 结论 本文证明了通过统一行动空间、自动化数据飞轮和大规模强化学习，可以构建出在手机、电脑、网页等多平台上均达到业界最优水平的 GUI 智能体。Qwen-UI-Agent 是目前最接近"真实世界可用"的通用数字助理。 This paper proves that combining a unified action space, a self-improving data flywheel, and large-scale reinforcement learning can produce a GUI agent that beats or matches the world's best models across phone, computer, and web tasks — bringing truly autonomous digital assistants one big step closer to everyday reality.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Qwen-UI-Agent 的核心思路是"像培训一个万能助理"。首先，它把所有可能的操作（点击、滑动、打字、运行命令）统一成一种语言，让模型可以一次性发出多个动作。其次，它用一个"自动研究"式的数据飞轮：让 AI 自己生成练习题、做任务、分析哪里出错，然后自动改进——就像学生做错题后自动整理错题本并再练一遍。训练阶段使用在线强化学习，在超过 1 万个并行虚拟环境里同时练习，支持长达 100 步以上的任务序列。最后，一个轻量级的"调度层"负责在手机和电脑之间协调任务，并支持主动发起服务。 Think of Qwen-UI-Agent as training an all-purpose digital assistant from scratch. First, it creates a shared "vocabulary" for all possible actions — clicking, typing, swiping, running commands — so the model speaks the same language no matter which device it's on. Second, it uses a self-improving loop: AI agents automatically create practice tasks, attempt them, figure out what went wrong, and plan the next round of improvements — like a student who makes their own flashcards, quizzes themselves, and corrects mistakes automatically. During training, it runs in 10,000+ virtual environments at the same time using reinforcement learning, practicing sequences of 100+ steps. A lightweight coordination layer then manages handoffs between phone and computer tasks and lets the agent proactively start useful services.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项研究意味着 AI 离真正的"数字员工"又近了一大步——未来你只需说一句"帮我订明天的机票并更新日历"，AI 就能自己打开 App、点击、填写、确认，全程不用人盯着。对于企业自动化、残障人士辅助、个人效率提升等场景，意义非常深远。 This research brings us closer to AI that can genuinely act as a "digital employee" — one day you'll say "book my flight and update my calendar" and the AI will open apps, click through menus, and confirm everything on its own. This has huge implications for workplace automation, accessibility for people with disabilities, and personal productivity.

Metis: Memory Foundation Model Metis：记忆基础模型

🧑‍🔬 Zeyu Zhang, Ziliang Guo, Yihang Sun, Xichong Zhang et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 首个将长期记忆能力内置于模型本身的基础模型原型
Metis is the first foundation model with built-in, native long-term memory that learns and updates without retraining.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像医生看诊——普通AI每次都要从头翻你的病历，而Metis更像一位真正记得你的老医生：你上次说了什么、有什么过敏史、生活习惯如何，全都记在心里，每次看诊都能接着上次继续。Metis就是把这种"老医生式记忆"直接长进了AI的大脑里。Think of it like the difference between a doctor who reads your entire medical file from scratch every single visit, versus one who actually remembers you — your history, allergies, and last conversation — and builds on that knowledge each time you meet. Metis gives AI that second kind of memory, built right into its brain.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现在的AI助手就像一条金鱼——每次对话结束就忘得一干二净。虽然有些系统会用"外挂记忆库"来帮AI记东西，但这就像把便利贴贴在电脑屏幕上，既麻烦又低效，也无法和AI本身的思考能力融为一体。目前没有任何AI能像人类一样，把记忆自然地"长"在脑子里。 Current AI models like ChatGPT are essentially goldfish — they forget everything once a conversation ends, or rely on clunky external tools to "remember" things. Storing memories outside the model creates extra complexity, slows things down, and can't be optimized together with the rest of the model. There's no clean, built-in way for an AI to remember and use past information the way a human naturally does.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首创"记忆基础模型"概念：记忆能力直接内嵌在模型架构中，不再依赖外部插件。 • 模型内部维护一个持续演化的"记忆状态"，能自动压缩历史信息并随新交互动态更新，无需重新训练。 • 记忆更新只需一次正向计算，速度快、无需梯度，推理阶段极为高效。 • 整个系统端到端联合优化，记忆能力和推理能力共同成长。 • First-ever "memory foundation model": memory is baked directly into the model's architecture, not bolted on as an external plugin. • The model maintains a living, evolving memory state that compresses past information and updates itself with every new interaction — no retraining needed. • Memory updates require only a single forward pass (the normal way a model processes input), making it fast and gradient-free at runtime. • The whole system is end-to-end optimized, meaning memory and reasoning improve together during training.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队在多类需要记忆能力的任务上测试了Metis：长文档问答（能否从超长文本中回忆关键事实）、多轮对话（能否记住多次会话中说过的内容）、流式数据处理（能否持续跟上不断涌入的新信息）。对比基线包括RAG系统（主流外部记忆方案）、全上下文模型（一次性读取所有内容的模型）以及其他循环或记忆增强架构。评估指标涵盖准确率、问答任务F1分数以及吞吐量和效率指标。 The researchers tested Metis on a range of memory-demanding tasks: long-document question answering (can the model recall facts from very long texts?), multi-session dialogue (does it remember what was said in earlier conversations?), and streaming data processing (can it keep up with a continuous flow of new information?). They compared Metis against strong baselines including RAG systems (the standard external-memory approach), full-context models (models that just read everything at once), and other recurrent or memory-augmented architectures. Evaluation metrics included standard accuracy, F1 scores on QA benchmarks, and throughput/efficiency measures.
📊 实验
结果		 Metis在多个基准测试中展现出强劲的原生记忆能力。在长上下文和多轮对话问答任务上，Metis达到与RAG基线相当或更优的表现，同时效率显著更高——记忆更新仅需一次前向计算，避免了检索流水线的延迟开销。在流式任务中，随着上下文长度增长，全上下文模型性能下降甚至无法计算，而Metis保持了稳健的准确率。推理阶段吞吐量也具备明显优势，无需重读长历史或运行独立检索模块。消融实验证实，各训练目标和原生记忆状态架构均对整体性能有实质贡献。 Metis demonstrates strong native memory capabilities across benchmarks. On long-context and multi-session QA tasks, Metis achieves competitive or superior performance compared to RAG-based baselines while being significantly more efficient — memory updates require only a single forward pass, avoiding the latency of retrieval pipelines. On streaming tasks, Metis maintains robust accuracy as context length grows, where full-context models degrade or become computationally infeasible. The model also shows clear advantages in throughput at inference time, as it does not need to re-read long histories or run separate retrieval modules. Detailed ablations confirm that each training objective and the native memory state architecture both contribute meaningfully to overall performance.
🏁 结论 Metis证明了基础模型可以拥有真正内置的长期记忆能力——不是外挂插件，而是模型核心原生能力——并且这种方式既有效又高效。本文确立了"记忆基础模型"这一新概念，提供了首个可工作的原型，为真正"会记忆"的AI开辟了全新的研究方向。 Metis proves that a foundation model can have genuine, built-in long-term memory — not as an external add-on but as a core native capability — and that this approach is both effective and efficient. This work establishes the concept of "memory foundation models" and provides the first working prototype, opening a new research direction for AI that truly remembers.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Metis就像给AI在大脑里安装了一本真正的"日记本"，而不是在外面贴便利贴。模型内部有一个特殊的"记忆状态"，用来压缩存储它见过的所有历史信息。每当有新信息进来，Metis就通过一次普通的前向计算悄悄更新这本内置日记本，完全不需要重新训练。回答问题时，模型通过"记忆注意力"机制翻阅这本日记，找到相关的历史知识。为了让模型学会正确使用记忆，研究团队专门构建了大规模记忆专项训练数据，并设计了多个训练目标，让模型学会何时存储、何时检索、如何在长历史中保持准确。 Think of Metis like giving an AI a real notebook built into its brain, rather than a sticky note on the outside. The model has a special internal "memory state" — a compact summary of everything it has seen so far. When new information arrives, Metis reads it and quietly updates this internal notebook through a normal forward computation (no expensive retraining). When the model needs to answer a question, it looks into this built-in notebook using a mechanism called "memory attention" to pull out relevant past knowledge. To teach the model how to use this memory well, the researchers built large-scale training data specifically designed around memory tasks and used multiple training objectives so the model learns when to store, when to retrieve, and how to stay accurate over long histories.

🎯 让AI小模型精准学习"看懂图像"而非盲目模仿推理过程

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

PCD的核心思路是用两个"证人"来判断一次感知失败是否值得纠正：第一个证人是"下游答案失败"——如果最终答案错了，说明某个环节出了问题；第二个证人是"老师和学生对图像描述不一致"——说明问题可能就出在看图阶段。只有两个证人同时"举手"，PCD才会加强这次感知学习的权重，避免无效纠正。这两个信号通过相乘（"软AND门"）结合，数学上保证了任何一个信号缺席时学习信号就消失，不会产生误导。整个过程把感知描述和后续推理分开进行，互不影响。 PCD uses two "witnesses" to decide whether a perception mistake is worth fixing. Witness 1: Did the final answer come out wrong? If yes, something went wrong somewhere. Witness 2: Does the big teacher model describe the image differently than the small student model? If yes, the problem likely lies in how the image was read. Only when BOTH witnesses raise their hands does PCD increase the learning signal for that perception step. The two signals are multiplied together — like a gate that only opens when both keys are turned simultaneously — ensuring that if either witness is silent, no misleading correction happens. Perception and reasoning are also trained in separate passes so they don't interfere with each other.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

现实中的AI助手（如看图回答问题的聊天机器人）需要又小又准。本文的方法让小模型能更高效地向大模型学习视觉理解能力，有望在手机、平板等低算力设备上部署更强的多模态AI。同时，这种"分清是眼睛的错还是大脑的错"的思路，对未来所有多模态AI训练都有指导意义。 Real-world AI assistants that answer questions about images need to be small enough to run on everyday devices, yet accurate enough to be useful. This method makes it possible to train compact models that genuinely understand images better — not just guess better. The insight that "fixing vision errors and reasoning errors separately" could reshape how the entire field trains next-generation multimodal AI.
