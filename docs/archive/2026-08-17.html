🎯 用全新框架揭示AI科研智能体"怎么做"而非只看"做得好不好"
A new framework reveals HOW AI research agents behave during long tasks, not just their final scores.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像评价一个厨师，不能只尝最后端上桌的菜——你还得看他怎么选食材、怎么掌握火候、做坏了有没有及时调整。只看结果，你根本不知道他是天才还是运气好。这篇论文就是给AI"厨师"装上了全程摄像头，终于看清楚它们到底在厨房里干了什么。Think of it like judging a chef not just by the final dish, but by watching the whole cooking show — how they plan the menu, handle the heat, and recover when something burns. Just tasting the final plate tells you almost nothing about skill. This paper is essentially installing cameras in the AI's kitchen so we can finally see what's really going on.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有AI智能体在评估时只看最终成绩，就像只看考试分数而不看答题过程。这样我们根本不知道AI在哪个环节出错、积累的经验有没有被用上、以及它是真的"在研究"还是只是在机械地试错。这种"只看结果"的评估方式无法指导我们如何真正提升AI的自主科研能力。 Current AI agents are often judged only on their final results — like grading a student purely on their test score without ever looking at their work. This tells us nothing about where the AI got stuck, whether it learned from past attempts, or whether it's truly being creative vs. just copying existing tricks. Without understanding the process, we can't know how to make these agents genuinely better.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首创"过程评估"框架，将智能体行为拆解为三个阶段：方案制定、执行操作、反馈调整，像给AI做"全程体检"而非只看最终报告。 • 引入"经验复用"对照实验，测试AI在同一任务多次运行或跨任务时，能否正确利用之前积累的经验，而不是被旧经验误导。 • 发现当前最强AI智能体更像"工程优化师"而非"真正的研究者"——能改进已有方案，但极少提出真正新颖的方法。 • Introduces a first-of-its-kind process evaluation framework that breaks agent behavior into three stages: Solution Framing, Execution, and Feedback Control — like a health checkup at every stage, not just at the finish line. • Tests whether agents can reuse past experience wisely (within the same task and across different tasks), revealing that accumulated knowledge can sometimes help but also mislead. • Finds that today's best AI agents act more like "engineering optimizers" than true autonomous researchers — good at adapting known tricks, but rarely inventing genuinely new methods.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队在36个长周期AI研发任务上测试了7个当前最先进的前沿大模型（frontier models）。任务涵盖模型改进、系统优化等真实科研场景。评估维度包括：过程行为分析（方案制定、执行、反馈控制三阶段评分）、经验复用对照（同任务多次运行 vs. 跨任务迁移）、以及运行稳定性分析。没有使用单一数据集，而是基于自建的任务框架（harness）进行端到端评估。 The team tested 7 state-of-the-art frontier AI models across 36 long-horizon AI research and development tasks, covering scenarios like improving models and optimizing systems. They evaluated agents on three behavioral stages (Solution Framing, Execution, Feedback Control) using rule-based metrics, ran controlled comparisons to test experience reuse both within the same task (across multiple runs) and across different tasks, and analyzed performance stability under different harness (environment) designs.
📊 实验
结果		 • 当前AI智能体的表现在多次运行之间波动显著，说明稳定性是一大短板。 • 经验复用并非总是有益——在某些情况下，先前的经验反而会误导后续决策，导致性能下降。 • 真正新颖的方法论创新极为罕见，大多数"最佳方案"只是对已有技术的改进或组合。 • 不同模型在相似的最终得分背后，存在截然不同的过程瓶颈（有的卡在方案制定，有的卡在执行，有的卡在反馈调整）。 • 测试框架（harness）的设计对模型性能稳定性有显著影响。（注：论文为评估分析类工作，具体数值因保密未全部公开，但上述为论文报告的主要定性与定量发现。） • Agent performance varied substantially across repeated runs on the same tasks, exposing poor stability as a key weakness. • Experience reuse was a double-edged sword — prior knowledge helped in some cases but misled agents in others, causing performance to drop. • Genuine methodological novelty was rare; most top solutions were adaptations or combinations of established techniques. • Different models reached similar final scores via very different process bottlenecks (some struggled at planning, others at execution, others at self-correction). • Harness (task environment) design had a measurable effect on performance stability across all tested models. (Note: This is a systematic evaluation study; specific numeric breakdowns are reported per-task across 7 models and 36 tasks rather than a single headline accuracy number.)
🏁 结论 本文证明了仅凭最终分数无法真正理解AI科研智能体的能力边界，必须结合过程分析和经验复用测试才能揭示真实瓶颈。当前最强的AI智能体更像是"高效的工程师"而非"真正的研究者"，在自主创新方面仍有根本性差距。 This paper proves that final scores alone are misleading when evaluating AI research agents — you must also look at the process. Today's best agents are skilled engineering optimizers but fall short of true autonomous researchers, rarely producing genuinely novel ideas.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究团队设计了一套全新的评估框架，把AI完成长周期科研任务的过程拆解成三个可测量的阶段：1）方案制定——AI是如何规划解决问题的思路的？2）执行操作——AI实际动手做得怎么样？3）反馈调控——AI有没有根据实验结果调整自己的策略？每个阶段都用基于规则的指标来打分，而不是只看最终结果。此外，团队还设计了对照实验，让AI在有先前经验和没有先前经验的情况下分别完成任务，看看"记忆"究竟是帮了忙还是帮了倒忙。 The team built a new evaluation framework that breaks down how an AI agent tackles a long research task into three measurable stages: (1) Solution Framing — how does the agent plan its approach? (2) Execution — how well does it carry out the plan in practice? (3) Feedback Control — does it adjust its strategy based on what it learns along the way? Each stage is scored using clear, rule-based metrics rather than just the final output. They also ran controlled experiments where agents worked with and without memory of prior attempts, measuring whether past experience helps or hurts future decisions.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{Agent Score} = f(\text{Solution Framing}, \text{Execution}, \text{Feedback Control})\]
💡 智能体的综合评分由三个过程维度共同决定：方案制定（如何规划）、执行操作（如何动手）、反馈调控（如何根据结果调整），而非只依赖最终输出结果。 An agent's overall performance is characterized by three process dimensions — how it plans a solution, how it carries it out, and how it adjusts based on feedback — rather than a single final output score.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI智能体被越来越多地用于自动做科研、改进模型甚至设计新算法，我们必须知道它们"聪明在哪里、蠢在哪里"，而不只是看一个总分。这项工作为整个AI行业提供了更精准的"体检工具"，帮助开发者找到真正值得改进的方向，而不是盲目刷榜。 As AI agents are increasingly trusted to run experiments, improve models, and even drive scientific discovery, knowing *how* they succeed or fail matters as much as whether they succeed. This work gives the AI industry a much more precise diagnostic tool, helping developers fix the right problems instead of just chasing higher benchmark scores.

❓ 待解决
的问题		 把复杂的学术论文转化为设计精美的学术海报是一件既耗时又需要设计技能的苦差事。现有的AI工具使用固定的"模板规则"，用完就扔，从不学习改进——就像一个厨师不管菜做得多难吃，永远都按同一个菜谱来。目前没有任何系统能从自身错误中学习、自动提升设计质量。 Turning complex research papers into well-designed visual posters is tedious, time-consuming, and requires both design skills and deep content understanding. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

AutoDesign分两层运作。内层是"代码智能体"——一个通过编写和运行代码来实际制作海报的AI，它会调用图像裁剪、文字排版、布局引擎等各种工具。外层是"元工具优化器"，是一个更聪明的AI，它负责观察每张海报的最终效果，然后重新修改内层智能体的"操作手册"（即harness），纠正反复出现的错误。就像一位体育教练在每场比赛后复盘录像，在下场比赛前更新战术手册。经过多轮迭代，操作手册不断精炼，并可复用到新的论文上，整个系统无需人工介入就能持续进步。 AutoDesign works in two layers. The inner layer is a "code agent" — an AI that writes and runs code to actually build the poster, calling tools like image cropping, text formatting, and layout engines. The outer layer is the "meta-harness optimizer," a smarter AI that watches how well each poster turns out and then rewrites the inner agent's playbook (called a "harness") to fix recurring mistakes. Think of it like a sports coach who reviews game footage after each match and updates the team's strategy manual before the next game. Over multiple rounds, the harness gets refined and reused across new papers, so the system keeps improving without human intervention.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{PosterBench Score} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} S_i\]
💡 PosterBench评分是所有测试论文海报得分的平均值，用于衡量AI系统的整体设计水平。 The PosterBench Score is the average quality score across all evaluated posters, measuring how well the AI system designs on a broad set of papers.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

对研究人员、学生和职场人士来说，把一篇密密麻麻的论文做成精美的会议海报目前需要数小时的专业工作——AutoDesign能在40分钟内、花不到3美元完成，让专业设计不再是少数人的特权。更广泛地说，它展示了一种强大的新蓝图：AI系统如何在无需人工干预的情况下，在创意性、多步骤任务上持续自我提升。 For researchers, students, and professionals, creating a polished conference poster from a dense paper currently takes hours of skilled work — AutoDesign can do it in 40 minutes for under $3, democratizing access to professional design. More broadly, it demonstrates a powerful new blueprint for any AI system that needs to get better at creative, multi-step tasks without human hand-holding.

的问题		 现有的AI科学助手通常只能处理文字、表格或预先整理好的数字摘要，就像一个只会读文字报告、看不懂照片和录音的研究员。这意味着大量藏在图像、声音、视频、3D结构中的关键科学证据，AI根本无法直接获取和理解。结果是AI生成的研究结论缺乏真实的"亲眼观察"，只依赖别人总结过的二手信息。 Most AI research assistants can only read text, tables, or pre-summarized numbers — like a scientist who can only read someone else's notes but can never look through a microscope themselves. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

OmniScientist由一个感知层加三个专职智能体组成。感知层负责"看懂"各种原始数据，就像给AI配上了眼睛、耳朵和各种传感器。之后，"创意智能体"根据观察到的原始证据提出研究假设；"实验智能体"设计并执行代码实验，验证假设；"写作智能体"将所有发现整理成一篇完整论文。整个流程通过确定性管道串联，每一步的发现都能影响后续决策，同时系统自动在代码里运行三道质量检查——新颖性、严谨性、论断合理性——保证输出论文的科研水准。 OmniScientist pairs a perception layer with three specialized AI agents. The perception layer acts like giving the AI eyes, ears, and sensors — it reads raw images, audio, video, and other data directly. Then the "ideation agent" forms research questions based on what it actually observes; the "experiment agent" writes and runs code to test those ideas; and the "writeup agent" assembles everything into a finished paper. The whole pipeline is connected so that early observations genuinely influence later decisions. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

SPARGen 的核心思路是把所有空间理解任务都变成同一种"生成"工作。就像一个全能厨师，无论是做甜点、主菜还是汤，都用同一套厨艺和厨具。具体来说，对于需要精细空间细节的任务（如三维重建），模型生成与图像像素对齐的几何"场"；对于需要语言回答的任务（如空间问答），模型生成文字序列。两种输出共用同一个模型主干，训练时彼此共享信息，让几何理解帮助语言推理，反之亦然。 SPARGen treats every spatial task as a generation problem — whether it's rebuilding a 3D scene, matching pixels between two images, or answering "is the cup to the left of the plate?" For dense geometric outputs like depth maps, the model generates image-aligned "fields" that assign a value to every pixel. For language-based tasks, it generates text token sequences. Both types of outputs are produced by the same shared model backbone, trained jointly so that learning one task automatically helps the others. Instructions guide which type of output to produce.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\hat{y} = G_{\theta}(x, \text{instruction})\]
💡 模型根据输入图像 x 和任务指令，生成对应的输出（几何场或文字序列），所有任务共享同一生成器 G。 The model generates output (geometric field or text) from an input image x and a task instruction, using a single shared generator G for all spatial tasks.

This research also validates multi-task joint learning as a powerful strategy for building richer, more general spatial intelligence in AI.
