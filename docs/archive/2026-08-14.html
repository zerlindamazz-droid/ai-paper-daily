DreamX-Phi 1.0: Action-Conditioned Video World Model for Robotic Manipulation DreamX-Phi 1.0：用于机器人操控的动作条件视频世界模型

🧑‍🔬 DreamX Team, Rui Chen, Xiangxiang Chu, Geng Li et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 让机器人在"脑中演练"动作后，精准预测真实操控画面的世界模型
A video world model that predicts what a robot arm will see after executing a sequence of actions — with high fidelity and physical accuracy.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像飞行员用模拟器练习降落，不用冒险开真飞机一样——机器人可以先在一个"虚拟世界"里反复演练抓取动作，这个虚拟世界看起来和真实环境一模一样，练好了再去动真实物体。Think of it like a flight simulator for robot arms — instead of pilots practicing landings without risking a real plane, robots can "rehearse" picking up objects in a virtual world that looks and behaves exactly like reality, before ever touching anything physical.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练机器人需要大量真实视频数据，成本高昂。研究人员希望用"世界模型"来预测机器人执行动作后摄像头会看到什么画面。但现有模型生成的视频虽然看起来逼真，却常常出错：比如搞错了哪只机械臂在动，或者夹住的物体在视频中途凭空消失。 When training robots to manipulate objects, it's expensive and slow to collect real-world video data. AI researchers want to build "world models" — programs that can predict what a robot's camera would see after it performs a series of moves. The trouble is, existing models often produce realistic-looking but physically wrong videos: the wrong arm moves, or a grasped object suddenly disappears mid-clip.
💡 解决
的亮点		 - 用"PRoPE几何编码"将每条机械臂的三维位置和朝向注入模型的注意力机制，彻底解决"搞错机械臂"的问题。 - 引入轻量级深度分支，帮助模型理解整个场景的立体结构，而不只是表面颜色。 - 结合SAM3物体遮罩和V-JEPA教师模型，确保被夹持的小物体在整段视频中保持一致、不消失。 - 通过蒸馏技术将慢速多步生成器压缩为快速少步版本，满足实际部署需求。 - PRoPE-style geometric encoding injects each arm's 3D position and orientation into the model's attention mechanism, so it always knows which arm is which and how it moves in space. - A lightweight depth branch helps the model understand the 3D structure of the whole scene, not just colors and textures. - SAM3 object masks + a frozen V-JEPA teacher keep small manipulated objects visually consistent throughout the video clip. - A distillation step compresses the slow multi-step generator into a fast few-step version for real-world deployment.
🧪 实验
内容		 该模型在WorldArena 2.0挑战赛上接受评测，这是专为机器人操控视频世界模型设计的公开基准测试。挑战赛分为两个赛道：赛道1聚焦单臂操控场景，赛道2涵盖更复杂的双臂或多物体场景。参赛系统在视频真实度、动作忠实度和物体一致性等指标上相互比较。 The model was evaluated on the WorldArena 2.0 Challenge, a public benchmark specifically designed to test robotic manipulation video world models. The challenge contains two tracks: Track 1 focuses on single-arm manipulation scenarios, and Track 2 covers more complex bi-manual or multi-object settings. Competing systems are compared on video fidelity, action faithfulness, and object consistency metrics.
📊 实验
结果		 截至论文撰写时，DreamX-Phi 1.0在WorldArena 2.0挑战赛中取得赛道1第1名、赛道2第2名的成绩，是机器人操控世界模型领域的顶级公开榜单。摘要未披露具体数值，但两个赛道均跻身前2，证明其达到当前最优水平。 At the time of writing, DreamX-Phi 1.0 achieves 1st place on Track 1 and 2nd place on Track 2 of the WorldArena 2.0 Challenge — the leading public benchmark for robotic manipulation world models. Specific numeric scores are not disclosed in the abstract, but the top-2 finish across both tracks demonstrates state-of-the-art performance in action-conditioned video prediction for robotics.
🏁 结论 DreamX-Phi 1.0证明了：将几何臂编码、场景深度感知和物体一致性监督结合起来，可以构建既逼真又物理正确的机器人操控世界模型，并在主流机器人基准测试中斩获第一或第二名。 DreamX-Phi 1.0 proves that combining geometric arm encoding, depth-aware scene modeling, and object-consistency supervision yields a video world model that is both visually realistic and physically faithful — winning or placing top-2 on a major robotics benchmark.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

DreamX-Phi 1.0 接受一帧观测画面、一条文字指令（如"拿起红色杯子"）和一系列规划好的机械臂姿态作为输入。它将每条机械臂的三维刚体变换（SE(3)位置+旋转）通过PRoPE风格的编码直接注入视频扩散模型的注意力层——就像给每条臂的每个动作打上精确的"GPS坐标"，模型再也不会搞混左右臂。与此同时，一个轻量深度分支帮助模型感知场景的立体结构。对于被夹持的小物体，SAM3分割遮罩逐帧追踪，并由V-JEPA特征教师模型提供一致性监督，防止物体"凭空消失"。最后，通过分布匹配蒸馏将慢速扩散模型压缩为更快的学生模型。 DreamX-Phi 1.0 starts with a single observed camera frame, a text instruction (e.g., "pick up the red cup"), and a sequence of planned arm poses. It encodes each arm's 3D rigid-body transformation (position + rotation in SE(3)) directly into the attention layers of a video diffusion model using PRoPE-style encoding — think of it like GPS coordinates stamped onto each arm's movement so the model never confuses left and right arms. A separate depth estimation branch runs alongside to give the model a sense of depth and 3D scene layout. For small objects being grasped, SAM3 segmentation masks are tracked through frames, and a V-JEPA feature teacher provides consistency supervision so the object doesn't "hallucinate" away. Finally, the slow diffusion model is distilled into a faster student model using distribution-matching, cutting inference time for deployment.

💡 SE(3)变换表示三维空间中的刚体运动，包含旋转和平移，用于精确描述每条机械臂的位置和朝向。 SE(3) transformation captures a rigid body's full 3D motion — both rotation and translation — used here to precisely encode each arm's pose in space.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

能够精准模拟机器人动作的世界模型，可以大幅减少对昂贵真实机器人训练数据的依赖，加速家用和工业机器人的研发。本文在物体追踪和机械臂身份识别上的突破，是让机器人在行动前能在"脑中预演"任务的关键一步。 World models that accurately simulate robot actions could dramatically reduce the need for expensive real-world robot training data, speeding up the development of capable household and industrial robots. This paper's advances in keeping track of objects and arm identity are key steps toward robots that can reliably plan and practice tasks "in their heads" before acting.

🎯 专为科学研究设计的全能AI智能体基础模型

维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 真正的科学研究对AI来说非常困难——它需要同时读论文、看图表、使用工具、做实验，还要长时间持续追踪进展。现有AI就像一个只会看文字、不会操作仪器或看显微镜的实验室助手，而且科学覆盖生物、物理、化学、时间序列信号等众多领域，没有一个AI能全部搞定。 Doing real science is hard for AI — it needs to read papers, look at charts and images, run experiments, and keep track of progress over a long time. Science also spans many fields — biology, physics, chemistry, time-series signals — and no single AI handles all of them well.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 构建了一个统一的"科学家AI"，能同时处理文本、图像、时间序列数据和工具调用，而不是为每个任务分别训练不同模型。• 采用聪明的多阶段训练方案：先大量吸收科学知识，再通过强化学习让模型"在做中学"，就像学生先读教材再做实验。• 新增轻量级"记忆解码器"插件，无需修改3970亿参数的主模型就能提升专业生物学任务表现，就像给手机装新APP而无需重装系统。 • Builds a unified "scientist AI" that handles text, images, time-series data, and tool use all together — not separate models for each task. • Uses a smart multi-stage training recipe: first soak up science knowledge, then fine-tune with reinforcement learning so the model learns by doing, like a student who first reads textbooks then does lab practicals. • Adds a lightweight "Memory Decoder" plug-in that upgrades the giant 397-billion-parameter model for specialized biology tasks without touching or retraining the main model — like adding a new app to a phone without rebuilding the operating system.

内容		 团队在涵盖科学理解、多模态推理、智能体任务完成和通用语言能力的多个基准测试上评估了Intern-S2-Preview-397B。测试集包括SciTS（科学时间序列理解与预测）、Biology-Instructions（生物学问答），以及标准多模态和智能体基准。以主流基础模型为基线进行对比，并专门测试了将Intern-MemDec-4B记忆解码器插入冻结397B主模型前后的生物学任务表现变化。 The team evaluated Intern-S2-Preview-397B on a wide range of benchmarks covering scientific understanding, multimodal reasoning, agentic task completion, and general-purpose language tasks. They tested on SciTS (scientific time-series understanding and forecasting), Biology-Instructions (biology question answering), as well as standard multimodal and agentic benchmarks. The model was compared against leading foundation models as baselines, and the Memory Decoder (Intern-MemDec-4B) was specifically tested by plugging into the frozen 397B backbone and measuring biology task performance before and after.
📊 实验
结果		 Intern-S2-Preview-397B在科学、多模态、智能体和通用基准测试上均达到竞争性或领先水平。在SciTS基准上，时间序列模块显著提升了科学信号理解和数值预测能力。记忆解码器Intern-MemDec-4B在不修改冻结397B主模型的前提下，将Biology-Instructions平均得分从56.92提升至60.32，提升+3.4分，展示了高效专业化适配能力。 Intern-S2-Preview-397B achieves competitive or leading results across scientific, multimodal, agentic, and general-purpose benchmarks. On the SciTS benchmark, the time-series modules improve both scientific signal understanding and numerical forecasting. The Memory Decoder (Intern-MemDec-4B) boosts the Biology-Instructions average score from 56.92 to 60.32 — a gain of +3.4 points — without modifying the frozen 397B backbone, demonstrating efficient specialized adaptation.
🏁 结论 本文证明了一个统一的AI系统可以被训练成应对科学研究全部复杂性——读取多模态数据、推理证据、使用工具并专业化到特定领域——同时仍能媲美甚至超越专用模型。模块化记忆解码器方案提供了一种低成本升级超大模型以服务小众科学领域的实用路径。 This paper proves that a single unified AI system can be trained to handle the full complexity of scientific work — reading multimodal data, reasoning over evidence, using tools, and specializing in specific fields — and still match or beat specialized models. The modular Memory Decoder approach offers a practical recipe for cheaply upgrading giant models for niche scientific domains.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

团队分两大阶段构建Intern-S2-Preview。第一阶段：在海量科学文档、图文交错数据和科学语料库上进行预训练，就像让学生把所有学科的教科书都读一遍，打下广泛基础。第二阶段：应用分层后训练流程——先做监督微调（从例题学习），再做多任务强化学习（通过对错奖励信号提升推理），最后做"智能体强化学习"让模型学会使用真实工具和环境交互。多项工程技巧（如部分展开加离线策略校正、自适应长度正则化、在线推测解码）保证训练稳定高效。针对生物专业任务，挂载一个40亿参数的"记忆解码器"模块到冻结的3970亿参数主模型上，实现快速专业化而无需昂贵的重新训练。 The team built Intern-S2-Preview in two big stages. First, they pre-trained it on a massive mix of scientific documents, images embedded in text, and scientific datasets — giving it a broad foundation like a student who read every textbook in every subject. Then they applied a layered post-training pipeline: supervised fine-tuning (learning from examples), multi-task reinforcement learning (getting reward signals for correct reasoning), and "agentic RL" where the model learns to interact with real tools and environments. Several clever engineering tricks kept training stable and fast, such as partial rollout with off-policy correction, adaptive length control, and online speculative decoding. For specialized biology tasks, a small 4-billion-parameter Memory Decoder module is attached to the frozen 397B backbone, enabling quick specialization without expensive retraining.

🎯 将电影转化为可编辑知识图谱，让AI智能体能理解并重建电影级视频。
AVA-Encoder turns films into editable knowledge graphs so AI agents can understand and recreate cinematic video.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像把一部电影拆解成一本超详细的乐高说明书——每个场景、声音和镜头角度都变成一块有标签的积木。AI不仅能"读懂"这本说明书，还能用它从头重建这部电影。每次重建效果不好，就有人用大白话写下哪里出了问题，AI因此越来越厉害。Think of it like converting a movie into a super-detailed LEGO instruction manual — every scene, sound, and camera angle becomes a labeled brick. Now an AI can not only "read" the manual but also rebuild the movie from scratch, and each time it rebuilds badly, someone writes a note in plain English saying what went wrong, so it gets better and better.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 AI创意智能体目前还无法有效地从高质量人类电影中学习，因此难以生成真正具有电影质感的视频。核心问题在于，没有一种结构化的方式让AI既能"读懂"视频内容，又能直接对其进行推理和编辑——就像看菜谱和看菜肴照片之间的差距。 AI creative agents still can't learn well from high-quality human films, so they struggle to produce truly cinematic videos. The core issue is that there's no good way to represent a video in a structured format that an AI agent can both understand and directly work with — like reading and editing a recipe, not just looking at a photo of the dish.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 将视频转换为知识图谱（KG）——包含文本、图像、音频和视频片段的结构化网络，智能体可像查数据库一样查询和编辑。 • 采用"文本梯度"训练方法：用自然语言反馈（如"让场景描述更生动"）代替数字梯度来优化编码策略，无需传统反向传播。 • 双循环架构——外循环训练与数据无关的编码策略，内循环在测试时针对特定视频精化知识图谱。 • Converts videos into knowledge graphs (KGs) — structured networks of text, images, audio, and video clips — that AI agents can query and edit like a database. • Uses a "textual gradient" training trick: instead of number-based gradients, it gives the AI natural-language feedback ("make the scene description more vivid") to improve its encoding policy. • Works in two loops — a fast outer loop that trains the encoding policy without needing specific data, and an optional inner loop that refines the KG for a specific video at test time.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队构建了一个全新的智能体视频重建基准测试，核心任务是测试从知识图谱重建原始视频的质量。他们将AVA-Encoder与多个强外部基线方法（其他先进的视频理解和编码方法）进行了比较。同时还做了对照实验，将伪训练的镜头级编码策略与经过精细人工调优的策略进行比较，评估重建质量和系统提示词的使用效率。 The team built a new benchmark for agentic video reconstruction — essentially testing how well a knowledge graph can be used to rebuild the original video. They compared AVA-Encoder against several strong external baselines (other state-of-the-art video understanding and encoding methods). They also ran a controlled experiment comparing their pseudo-trained shot-level encoding policy against a carefully hand-tuned human policy, measuring reconstruction quality and system-prompt token efficiency.
📊 实验
结果		 在智能体视频重建基准测试中，AVA-Encoder比最强外部基线方法提升了20.7个百分点。在仅使用策略的对照实验中，伪训练的镜头级编码策略在性能上超过了精细人工调优策略，同时系统提示词token数量减少了74.3%——既更准确又更高效。 AVA-Encoder improves by 20.7 percentage points over the strongest external baseline on the agentic video reconstruction benchmark. In the policy-only controlled setting, the pseudo-trained shot-level encoder policy outperforms the carefully human-tuned policy while using 74.3% fewer system-prompt tokens — meaning it's both more accurate and far more efficient.
🏁 结论 AVA-Encoder证明了将视频转化为结构化知识图谱，并使用自然语言反馈优化转化过程，是帮助AI智能体理解和重建电影级视频的有效方法。该工作建立了新的基准，并开放了数据集和框架，推动整个领域发展。 AVA-Encoder proves that converting videos into structured knowledge graphs — and using natural-language feedback to optimize the conversion — is a powerful approach for enabling AI agents to understand and recreate cinematic-quality video. It sets a new benchmark and releases open resources to help the whole field advance.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

AVA-Encoder的工作方式就像一个超级详细的剧本编写者加上一个视频重建机器。首先，它读取一段视频，将其转化为知识图谱——想象成一个为这部电影专门建立的、内容极其丰富的维基百科页面，包含场景、镜头、角色等节点，以及链接的图像、音频、视频片段。然后，系统尝试从这个知识图谱重建原始视频。原视频和重建视频之间的差异会变成反馈，但不是数学数字，而是自然语言（如"光线描述太模糊了"）。这种自然语言反馈训练AI策略生成更好的知识图谱，所需的系统提示词比人工调优少74.3%。 AVA-Encoder works like a super-detailed film script writer and then a film reconstructor. First, it reads a video and converts it into a knowledge graph — think of it as a richly linked Wikipedia page for that film, with nodes for scenes, shots, characters, and linked media assets (images, audio, video clips). Then it tries to reconstruct the original video from that knowledge graph. The difference between the original and the reconstructed video becomes feedback — but instead of math numbers, that feedback is written in plain language ("the lighting description was too vague"). This natural-language feedback trains an AI policy to write better knowledge graphs, using far fewer instructions than a human-crafted system prompt.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L}_{\text{recon}} = d(V, \hat{V}_{\text{KG}})\]
💡 重建损失：衡量原始视频V与从知识图谱重建的视频V̂之间的差距，这个差距以自然语言形式反馈给AI进行优化。 Reconstruction loss: measures the gap between the original video V and the video reconstructed from the knowledge graph. This gap is expressed as natural-language feedback to guide the AI's improvement.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项工作以一种AI工具真正可用的方式弥合了原始视频与AI"理解"之间的鸿沟——想象更智能的视频剪辑工具、自动化电影制作助手或AI导演。通过开源框架、基准和电影知识图谱数据集，为整个研究社区提供了重要的基础资源。 This work bridges the gap between raw video and AI "understanding" in a way that's actually usable for creative AI tools — think smarter video editors, automated film production assistants, or AI directors. By open-sourcing the framework, benchmark, and film knowledge graph dataset, it gives the whole research community a foundation to build on.

V-RAE: Rethinking Video Latent Spaces for Generation V-RAE：重新思考视频生成的潜在空间

🧑‍🔬 Minghui Guo, Shengqiong Wu, Hao Fei

图 Figure · Page 4 of paper

🎯 用冻结视觉基础模型构建更智能的视频潜在空间，加速生成并提升质量
V-RAE builds smarter video generation latent spaces using frozen vision models, achieving faster and higher-quality video synthesis.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像请一位经验丰富的电影剪辑师，而不是从头培训新手。现有系统从零训练新手，只教他们复制视频；V-RAE则直接聘请一位已经深懂叙事和运动规律的老手——当你让他帮忙"创作"新视频时，他上手更快、效果更好。Think of it like hiring an experienced film editor versus training a brand-new one. Current systems train a rookie editor from scratch, teaching them only to make exact copies of films. V-RAE instead hands the job to a seasoned pro who already *understands* storytelling and motion — so when you ask them to help create a *new* film, they're far faster and more creative right from the start.

❓ 待解决
的问题		 AI视频生成需要先把视频"压缩"成紧凑的编码，再让生成模型在这个压缩空间里工作。但现有的压缩系统只追求像素级的精确还原（就像复印机），并没有把视频的语义和动态内容组织得对生成"友好"。这种错位导致视频生成训练慢、质量有限。 AI video generation systems need a "compression room" (called a latent space) where videos are squeezed into compact codes before a generative model works on them. The problem is that current compression systems are only trained to reconstruct pixels perfectly — like a photocopier optimized for exact copies — but they don't organize visual meaning in a way that helps AI *create* new videos. This mismatch makes video generation slower to train and lower in quality.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • V-RAE不从头训练编码器，而是"借用"已训练好的强大视觉基础模型（冻结参数），直接利用其丰富的语义特征作为潜在空间的骨架。 • 设计了轻量级时间池化模块，在去除冗余帧的同时保留运动结构，压缩更高效。 • 提出新指标tFVD，专门衡量视频的时序连贯性，能更可靠地预测下游生成质量，弥补了现有指标的不足。 • Instead of training an encoder from scratch, V-RAE "borrows" powerful, already-trained vision models (kept frozen) to provide rich semantic features as the backbone of its latent space. • A lightweight temporal pooling module cleverly removes redundant frames while keeping the motion structure intact, making the compressed representation much more efficient. • The paper introduces a new diagnostic metric called tFVD, which measures temporal coherence and better predicts how good a generative model will actually be — filling a gap where standard metrics fall short.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者在三个任务上测试了V-RAE：视频重建（能多准确地还原原始视频）、语义探测（潜在编码包含多少高层语义信息）、以及类别条件视频生成（基于V-RAE潜在空间训练的生成模型能否生成真实多样的视频）。使用了Kinetics-600（K600）和UCF101等标准数据集，与多个大规模预训练视频VAE基线对比。重建质量用rFVD衡量，生成质量用gFVD，并引入新指标tFVD衡量时序连贯性。此外还在Cityscapes自动驾驶数据集上与Wan 2.2 VAE基线对比了未来视频预测任务。 The researchers tested V-RAE across three tasks: video reconstruction (how faithfully can it rebuild original videos?), semantic probing (how much meaningful high-level information do its latents contain?), and class-conditional video generation (can a generative model trained on V-RAE latents produce realistic, diverse videos?). They used benchmark datasets including Kinetics-600 (K600) and UCF101, and compared against multiple large-scale pretrained video VAEs as baselines. They measured reconstruction quality with rFVD, generation quality with gFVD, and introduced tFVD as a new temporal diagnostic. They also tested future video prediction on the Cityscapes driving dataset against the Wan 2.2 VAE baseline.
📊 实验
结果		 V-RAE在K600视频重建上取得rFVD 2.13，超越所有评测的大规模预训练视频VAE。在生成任务中，最佳变体在UCF101上gFVD为117.86，在K600上为19.16，与基线相比具有竞争力甚至更优。最关键的是，生成模型训练收敛速度最高提升6倍。V-RAE潜在编码保留的语义信息也远多于传统视频分词器（通过线性探测准确率验证）。在Cityscapes未来视频预测任务中，V-RAE潜在空间同样优于Wan 2.2 VAE。 V-RAE achieves an rFVD of 2.13 on K600 for reconstruction, outperforming all evaluated large-scale pretrained video VAEs. For generation, the best V-RAE variant scores gFVD of 117.86 on UCF101 and 19.16 on K600 — competitive with or better than baselines. Crucially, V-RAE converges up to 6× faster during generative model training compared to conventional video tokenizer latents. Its latents also retain substantially more semantic information than conventional video tokenizer latents (measured via linear probing accuracy). On Cityscapes future video prediction, V-RAE latents outperform Wan 2.2 VAE latents under matched settings.
🏁 结论 V-RAE证明：以冻结的语义丰富视觉基础模型作为视频潜在空间的骨架，而非训练纯重建编码器，能够实现更好的视频生成质量、更快的收敛速度以及更具语义信息量的潜在表示。这重新定义了视频AI潜在空间的设计思路。 V-RAE proves that using frozen, semantically rich vision foundation models as the backbone for video latent spaces — rather than training pure reconstruction encoders — leads to better video generation quality, much faster convergence, and latents that carry more meaningful information. This reframes how the field should think about designing latent spaces for video AI.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

V-RAE的核心思路是：编码阶段"不重复造轮子"。它直接使用已经在海量数据上训练好的强大视觉基础模型，并把其参数锁定（冻结），让它保持聪明而无需重新训练。在这些冻结特征之上，一个小型时间池化模块将视频的多帧压缩成紧凑的时序编码，去掉冗余信息、保留运动主线。最后，视频解码器学会从这些压缩编码中"还原"出流畅的连续动作。整个系统端到端训练，但只有轻量级的池化和解码部分需要从头学习，大大节省了计算资源。 V-RAE's key idea is: don't reinvent the wheel for encoding. It takes a powerful vision model (like those used for image understanding) that's already been trained on massive data, and locks its weights — so it stays smart without needing retraining. On top of these frozen features, a small temporal pooling module squishes a video's many frames into a compact set of time-stepped codes, removing duplicate information while keeping the storyline of motion. Finally, a video decoder learns to "play back" smooth continuous motion from these compressed codes. The whole system is trained end-to-end, but only the lightweight pooling and decoder parts need to be learned from scratch — saving enormous compute.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{tFVD}(p, q) = \text{FVD}(\{v_t\}_{t=1}^{T}, \{\hat{v}_t\}_{t=1}^{T})\]
💡 tFVD（时序FVD）衡量视频帧序列之间的时序连贯性差异，比标准FVD更能反映生成视频的时间流畅程度。 tFVD measures how temporally coherent generated video sequences are compared to real ones — a new diagnostic that better predicts real-world generation quality than standard FVD.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

训练速度最高提升6倍，意味着开发视频生成AI（如各类AI视频工具）的成本大幅降低，让更多研究者和公司受益。本文揭示"重建好"不等于"生成好"，有望重新指导整个领域如何设计视频AI的基础模块。 Faster convergence (up to 6×) means building video generation AI systems — like those behind AI video tools — becomes dramatically cheaper and quicker, lowering the barrier for researchers and companies. By showing that "good for reconstruction" doesn't equal "good for generation," this paper could redirect how the whole field designs the foundational building blocks of video AI.

Reinforcement learning improves autonomous-driving vision-language-action (VLA) models by evaluating trajectories sampled from the current policy. Group relative policy optimization (GRPO) learns from reward differences within each rollout group. When all sampled trajectories are poor, this relative signal can rank failures without identifying behavior outside the failed region. We introduce FIRE-VLA, a failure-informed self-evolution framework that converts such unresolved failures into privileged supervision for the next policy. Low-reward, low-diversity groups trigger self-distillation from a frozen round-start copy of the same model. Teacher and student have the same parameter scale, but only the teacher observes the hidden future trajectory. Supervision follows the student's generated prefix and is restricted to answer tokens, while GRPO remains active for every group. The updated policy supplies the teacher for the next round, allowing the routed failure distribution to change with the policy without requiring a larger external teacher. Starting from the same Qwen2.5-VL-3B SFT checkpoint, the comparison matches student rollout and policy-update counts. On 6,019 examples from 150 held-out nuScenes scenes, FIRE-VLA retains comparable single-sample planning, reduces G=4 mean L2 from 1.848 to 1.500 m, and lowers evaluation-persistent failure prevalence from 13.03% to 11.20%. The reduction in mean error arises mainly from rare severe rollouts rather than uniform improvement across ordinary trajectories.

Contact-rich manipulation failures are often detected only after the robot has committed to contact. This is especially limiting in wrist-camera setups: close gripper--object views help observe contact, but a poor approach may already push, miss, slip, or disturb the object before conventional detectors react. We introduce \emph{ContactGuard}, a pre-contact execution monitor for chunked visuomotor policies. Given the policy's planned action chunk, ContactGuard predicts its short-horizon consequence in latent visual space and aborts if the predicted future latent indicates likely failure. Its latent world model is trained from unlabelled robot trajectories to predict compact multi-view visual embeddings under planned actions, avoiding pixel-level video prediction. A lightweight failure probe is then trained from a small labelled set of pre-contact clips. At deployment, ContactGuard anchors prediction before an imminent contact event, rolls the model forward under the policy's own actions, and verifies the predicted post-contact latent. Across real-world contact-rich manipulation tasks, ContactGuard predicts failure more accurately than direct and corrupted-action ablations, and transfers to live robot as a pre-contact abort signal without modifying the underlying policy.
