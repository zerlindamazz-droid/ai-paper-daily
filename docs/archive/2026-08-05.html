Knowledge-Geometry Decoupling: Refreshable Pretrained Transfer for Streaming Recommendation 知识-几何解耦：面向流式推荐的可刷新预训练迁移

🧑‍🔬 Zixuan Wang, Yuhong Chen, Yuxuan Zhu, Guidong Lei et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 将预训练知识与任务学习解耦，实现流式推荐的持续刷新。
Decouples pretrained behavioral knowledge from task-specific learning to enable continual refresh in streaming recommendation.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一位大厨有一本"通用菜谱大全"（预训练编码器），每个月都会用新食材更新；同时还有一个"餐厅专属小本子"（任务学习器），记录针对每家餐厅口味的独家调整。聪明的是：小本子用特殊墨水写成，永远不会污染大菜谱——更新大菜谱时不会毁掉小本子的内容，两者完全独立，互不干扰。Think of it like a chef who keeps a master recipe book (the pretrained encoder) that gets updated with new seasonal ingredients every month, and a separate personal notebook (the task learner) where they write down tweaks for each restaurant's menu. The clever part: the personal notebook is written in a special ink that never smudges onto the master recipe book — so updating the master book never ruins the restaurant-specific notes, and vice versa.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 电商推荐系统（比如购物App）需要不断学习用户的最新行为来猜你喜欢什么。但用户的喜好每天都在变化，模型必须持续更新。问题是：每次用新数据刷新预训练模型时，之前为具体任务做的调整就会被破坏——就像每次教科书出新版，你在页边写的笔记都被清空一样。此外，现有方法还会把毫不相关的浏览记录混在一起训练，让模型学到很多假关联。 Online recommenders (like those on shopping apps) learn from your past behavior to predict what you'll like next. But user behavior constantly shifts over time, so the AI model must keep updating. The problem: when you refresh the pretrained model with new behavior data, it often "forgets" or clashes with the task-specific tuning already done — like erasing notes you wrote in the margins of a textbook every time a new edition comes out. Existing methods also train on noisy behavior sequences that mix up unrelated browsing sessions, teaching the model false connections between items.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出行为多Token预测（BMTP），只保留真正相关的未来商品作为监督信号，过滤掉跨会话的噪声，让模型学到更干净的行为知识。 • 将预训练知识和任务专属几何结构分配到完全独立的参数集合，刷新编码器时不会破坏下游任务的调整结果。 • 设计"锚定校准残差"（ACR），让任务层的更新在数学上与预训练嵌入正交，从根本上消除干扰。 1. Introduces Behavioral Multi-Token Prediction (BMTP), which filters out noisy, unrelated item transitions so the model only learns from genuinely related future items — producing cleaner behavioral knowledge. 2. Decouples pretrained knowledge and task-specific geometry into completely separate parameter sets, so refreshing the encoder never accidentally wipes out downstream task tuning. 3. Uses an "Anchored Calibration Residual" (ACR) that writes task-specific adjustments in a direction mathematically guaranteed not to interfere with pretrained embeddings.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者在8个公开推荐基准数据集上测试了KGD（包括Amazon多品类商品评论数据集等标准序列推荐数据集），与MoRec、UniSRec等强预训练迁移基线模型进行比较。此外还进行了长达90天的线上生产流实验，以及在Shopee首页搜索功能上的真实A/B测试，用每用户GMV（成交额）和广告收入等业务指标衡量效果。离线评估采用NDCG和命中率等标准排序指标。 The authors tested KGD on eight public recommendation benchmarks (including Amazon product review datasets across multiple categories and other standard sequential recommendation datasets). They compared against strong pretrain-transfer baselines including models like MoRec, UniSRec, and other state-of-the-art sequential recommenders. Beyond offline benchmarks, they also ran a 90-day live production stream experiment and a real A/B test on Shopee's Homepage Search feature, measuring business metrics like GMV per user and advertising revenue. Standard ranking metrics like NDCG and Hit Rate were used for offline evaluation.
📊 实验
结果		 KGD在8个公开基准数据集上比强基线提升4–12%。在90天线上生产流实验中，KGD持续保持性能优势，而基线模型没有任何提升。在Shopee首页搜索的真实A/B测试中，每用户GMV提升1.75%，广告收入提升1.53%——对于已高度优化的工业推荐系统而言，这是非常显著的提升。 KGD improves over strong pretrain-transfer baselines by 4–12% across eight public benchmarks. In the 90-day production stream test, KGD maintains its performance advantage while baselines show no gains over time. In the live A/B test on Shopee Homepage Search, KGD increases GMV per user by 1.75% and advertising revenue by 1.53% — both are significant wins in an already highly-optimized industrial system.
🏁 结论 KGD证明了在推荐AI中将"学什么"和"怎么用"彻底分离，可以让模型在持续学习新用户行为的同时不丢失任务调优成果——在学术基准和大规模真实电商平台上均取得了稳定可观的提升。 KGD proves that separating "what to know" from "how to apply it" in recommendation AI allows models to keep learning from fresh user behavior without losing their task-specific tuning — delivering consistent, measurable gains in both research benchmarks and a massive real-world e-commerce platform.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

KGD把模型分成两个完全独立的部分：一个"可刷新编码器"负责存储通用的行为知识，可以随时用新数据更新；一个"任务学习器"负责具体的推荐工作。任务学习器通过单向只读的交叉注意力读取编码器的输出，但编码器更新时不会影响任务学习器已经学到的内容。任务学习器自己的更新则用了一个数学技巧（正交残差），确保其调整方向永远不与预训练表示冲突——就像在原书上用隐形墨水单独做笔记，互不干扰。在预训练阶段，BMTP过滤掉跨会话的虚假关联，只保留协同或语义上真正相关的商品作为训练目标。 KGD splits the model into two clearly separate parts: a "refreshable encoder" that holds general behavioral knowledge and can be updated freely, and a "task learner" that handles the specific job of recommending items. The task learner reads from the encoder using a one-way read-only connection (cross-attention), so updates to the encoder don't overwrite what the task learner has learned. For the task learner's own updates, they use a clever math trick (orthogonal residuals) to ensure its tweaks never collide with the pretrained representations — like writing your personal notes in invisible ink on top of the original text, completely independently. On the pretraining side, BMTP filters supervision signals so only items that are collaboratively or semantically similar to a user's history are used as training targets, removing false associations caused by session boundaries.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L}_{BMTP} = -\sum_{t} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}_t} \log \frac{\exp(h_t \cdot e_j)}{\sum_{k} \exp(h_t \cdot e_k)}\]
💡 BMTP的训练目标：只对与当前行为协同或语义相关的未来商品集合P_t计算预测损失，过滤掉无关会话的噪声监督信号。 The BMTP training loss: instead of predicting any next item, the model is only trained to predict future items that are genuinely related (collaboratively or semantically) to the current context, filtering out noisy cross-session transitions.
\[\Delta W_{ACR} \perp \text{span}(E_{pretrain})\]
💡 锚定校准残差（ACR）的核心约束：任务层的参数更新方向与预训练嵌入空间正交，从数学上保证两者互不干扰。 The core constraint of Anchored Calibration Residual (ACR): task-specific parameter updates are mathematically orthogonal to the pretrained embedding space, guaranteeing zero interference between the two.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

推荐系统驱动着数十亿用户每天看到的信息流、搜索结果和广告，哪怕一点点准确率提升都意味着巨大的商业价值和用户体验改善。KGD提供了一套在用户行为不断变化时让推荐AI保持"常学常新"的实用方案，已在Shopee全量上线，具有极强的工业落地价值。 Recommendation systems power the feeds, search results, and ads that billions of people see every day — even a small improvement in accuracy means more relevant products, more revenue, and better user experience at scale. KGD shows a practical, deployable blueprint for keeping AI recommenders fresh and sharp as user behavior evolves, without the painful trade-off of forgetting past tuning.

Faster-WAM: Do World Action Models Need Deep Action Modules? Faster-WAM：世界动作模型真的需要深层动作模块吗？

🧑‍🔬 Liheng Ma, Rui Heng Yang, Zhanguang Zhang, Mateo Clemente et al.

🎯 用单层动作头替代深层模块，让机器人决策快3倍且不掉精度
A lightweight single-layer action head docked onto a video backbone speeds up robot inference 3.2× without sacrificing accuracy.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个餐厅大厨（视频主干）把所有的烹饪秘诀都掌握在脑子里，而一个小助理（动作头）只需要通过一个传菜窗口就能随时调用大厨的全部智慧，不需要自己也变成大厨——出菜速度快了3倍，菜的质量一点没差！Think of it like a head chef (the video backbone) who knows everything about cooking, and a quick runner (the action head) who just peeks through a special window to grab the chef's full wisdom instantly — instead of becoming a chef themselves. The food arrives 3× faster, and it tastes just as good!
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的机器人控制AI（世界动作模型）把"看视频"和"决定动作"两件事绑死在一起，动作模块的层数必须跟视频模块一样深，导致计算量巨大、反应慢。就像你每次问朋友"要不要转弯"，他都要把整本地图从头翻到尾才给答案——太浪费时间了。这让机器人在实际使用中推理延迟很高，难以快速响应。 Existing robot AI systems that combine video understanding with action prediction are deeply coupled — the action module has to be just as large and deep as the video module, making the whole system slow and computationally expensive. Think of it like a chef who has to re-read the entire recipe book every time they decide what to do next. This makes real-time robot control sluggish and inefficient.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出"变压器对接坞"（DoT）设计原则：把预训练视频模型当作"知识中枢"，动作头通过轻量"对接接口"灵活挂载，无需与视频主干等深。 • 对接接口融合了视频主干所有层的键值信息，并加入RoPE位置重对齐，让单层动作头也能"看到"全局表示。 • Faster-WAM只用1层动作头搭配30层视频主干，推理延迟仅66.5毫秒，比前代Fast-WAM快3.2倍，且无需额外的具身预训练。 • Introduces "Dock of Transformer" (DoT): treats the video model as a knowledge hub and plugs in a thin action head via a docking interface — no need to match the video backbone's depth. • The docking interface cleverly fuses information from ALL layers of the video backbone, so even a single-layer head gets the full picture. • Faster-WAM achieves 66.5 ms per inference — 3.2× faster than Fast-WAM — with no extra robot-specific pretraining needed.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在两个主流机器人操作基准上进行：LIBERO（包含标准任务和分布外泛化版本LIBERO-Plus）以及RoboTwin 2.0。对比基线包括Fast-WAM等现有世界动作模型，评估指标涵盖任务成功率和端到端推理延迟（毫秒）。所有对比均在受控环境下统一测量延迟，确保公平性。 Experiments were run on two standard robot manipulation benchmarks: LIBERO (including a harder out-of-distribution split called LIBERO-Plus) and RoboTwin 2.0. The model was compared against existing World Action Models, including Fast-WAM, on task success rate and end-to-end inference latency measured in milliseconds under controlled conditions.
📊 实验
结果		 Faster-WAM在LIBERO和RoboTwin 2.0上达到与现有最优方法相当的任务成功率；在分布外泛化测试LIBERO-Plus上表现出更强的泛化能力。端到端推理延迟仅66.5毫秒，比Fast-WAM（约212毫秒）实现了3.2倍加速，在所有受控对比中延迟最低，且无需任何额外的具身预训练数据。 Faster-WAM matches competitive performance on LIBERO and RoboTwin 2.0 benchmarks, and shows stronger out-of-distribution generalization on LIBERO-Plus. Most strikingly, it achieves an end-to-end inference latency of just 66.5 ms — a 3.2× speedup over Fast-WAM — the lowest latency in the controlled comparison, all without any additional robot-specific pretraining.
🏁 结论 本文证明了世界动作模型不需要把动作模块做得和视频主干一样深——一个通过"对接坞"接口访问全层表示的单层动作头，就能在保持精度的同时将推理速度提升3倍以上。这为轻量、快速的机器人AI系统设计提供了新思路。 This paper proves that robot action modules don't need to be as deep as the video backbone they're built on — a single-layer head with smart access to all backbone layers is enough to match accuracy while running 3.2× faster. It opens a new design direction for building efficient, real-time robot AI systems.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究团队提出了一种叫"变压器对接坞"（DoT）的设计思路：把一个强大的预训练视频模型比作一个"图书馆"，动作预测模块只是一个"读者"，不需要跟图书馆一样厚重，只需通过一个"借阅窗口"（对接接口）就能访问图书馆里所有楼层的书。具体来说，这个接口把视频主干30层的中间表示（键和值）全部汇总，再用RoPE位置编码重新对齐时序信息，喂给一个只有1层的轻量动作头。这样动作头既小又快，同时还能享受到深层视频模型的丰富表示。 The team designed a "Dock of Transformer" (DoT) system. Imagine a massive library (the video backbone) and a tiny, nimble assistant (the action head). Instead of forcing the assistant to be as big as the library, they built a special "access window" that lets the small assistant instantly pull knowledge from every floor of the library at once. Technically, this docking interface aggregates the key-value representations from all 30 video backbone layers and applies positional realignment (RoPE), feeding this rich summary into just a single-layer action head — keeping the decision-maker lean and fast.

\[\text{Latency: } 66.5 \text{ ms} = \frac{1}{3.2} \times \text{Fast-WAM latency}\]
💡 Faster-WAM每次推理仅需66.5毫秒，是Fast-WAM速度的3.2倍 Faster-WAM runs each inference in just 66.5 ms, which is 3.2 times faster than Fast-WAM

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

更快的推理速度意味着机器人能更实时地响应环境变化，离真正进入工厂、家庭的实用机器人又近了一步。这项工作也说明AI系统不一定越大越好——聪明的结构设计可以在不牺牲性能的前提下大幅降低计算成本。 Faster inference means robots can react to the world in near-real-time, which is critical for practical deployment in factories or homes. This work also challenges the "bigger is always better" assumption in AI — showing that smart architecture design can slash compute costs without hurting performance.

🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个超级靠谱的导游：不是一口气把所有路线说完就走人，而是每走一步都确认你到了吗，走错了马上重新规划，还把哪条路堵车、哪条路好走都记在小本本上，下次带别人时直接用上。Think of it like a GPS navigator that doesn't just hand you a route and leave — instead, it checks at every turn whether you actually made it, reroutes when you miss one, and remembers which roads were jammed so it avoids them next time.

❓ 待解决
的问题		 现在的机器人就像"条件反射"——看到环境直接动作，没有思考过程。一旦遇到陌生场景或者复杂的多步骤任务，就容易出错。而且人们根本看不到机器人在"想什么"，无法在中途纠正它，这让机器人很难被信任和实际应用。 Today's robots are trained end-to-end — they see the world and immediately act, like a reflex. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

ETA的工作方式就像一个项目经理指挥工人。"规划器"（一个AI大脑，比如大语言模型）看看当前情况，决定下一步调用哪个"工具"，比如"走向桌子"或"拿起杯子"。"环境"（真实或模拟世界）执行这个动作，然后返回一个新的观察结果，就像汇报进度。规划器检查这一步是否成功，如果失败就调整计划。每一次交互——成功或失败——都记录进可查阅的"记忆库"，机器人之后可以回放并学习。这个循环一直进行，直到任务完成或机器人决定暂停并寻求帮助。 ETA works like a project manager overseeing a worker. The Planner (an AI brain, like a large language model) looks at the current situation and decides which one Tool to call next — for example, "move to the table" or "pick up the cup." The World (the real or simulated environment) carries out the action and sends back a fresh observation, like a status update. The Planner checks whether the step worked, and if not, it adjusts its plan. Every interaction — success or failure — is stored in an auditable memory that the robot can replay and learn from later. This loop continues until the task is done or the agent decides to stop and ask for help.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{ETA Loop: } s_t \xrightarrow{\text{Planner}} a_t \xrightarrow{\text{Tool}} \text{World} \rightarrow (r_t, s_{t+1})\]
💡 ETA的核心循环：规划器根据当前状态选择一个动作（工具调用），环境执行后返回结果和新状态，循环往复直到任务完成。 ETA's core loop: the Planner picks one action (tool call) based on the current state, the World executes it and returns a result plus a fresh observation, then the loop repeats until the task is done.

现在大多数机器人是"黑箱"——你看不到它在想什么，遇到新情况就罢工。ETA透明的、逐步执行的方式，可能成为我们真正能信任的家用、医疗或工厂机器人的基础架构。通过完全开源，作者也邀请整个AI社区共同推动机器人领域的"ChatGPT时刻"到来。 Most robots today are "black boxes" — you can't see their thinking, and they break when facing anything new. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Qwen-CUA的工作方式就像一个人类实习生坐在电脑前：它只看屏幕截图，然后决定接下来要点哪里、输入什么内容。它不依赖任何软件的"内部结构"，所以理论上可以操作任何程序。为了记住漫长任务中的操作历史，它会保留最近20张截图，并将更早的记录"压缩"成固定大小的摘要块。训练时，研究团队搭建了一个超大规模的云端"练习场"，让AI在数万个虚拟电脑环境中同时练习，并通过可自动验证的任务结果（成功/失败）给AI打分，再用强化学习不断优化它的决策路径。 Think of Qwen-CUA as a human intern sitting in front of any computer: it looks at a screenshot, decides what to click or type, acts, then looks again. It never needs special "behind the scenes" access to the software. To handle very long tasks, it keeps the 20 most recent screenshots in full detail and compresses older history into tidy summary blocks. For training, the team built a giant cloud "practice arena" with nearly 100,000 virtual CPUs running tens of thousands of virtual computers simultaneously. The AI practiced on ~40,000 tasks with automatic pass/fail grading, and used reinforcement learning to improve entire task sequences based on whether it ultimately succeeded.
