SRPO: Self-Reflective Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon Reasoning SRPO：面向长程推理的自反思策略优化

🧑‍🔬 Jialong Liu, Yuling Shi, Ning Yang, Xiaodong Gu et al.

图 Figure · Page 7 of paper

🎯 让大模型像人一样反思错误，用极少算力达到顶尖推理性能
SRPO teaches LLMs to learn from their own mistakes using self-reflection, achieving top math and agent reasoning with 8x less compute.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个学生考完试后，不只看最终对错，而是翻回草稿纸逐行检查，贴上便利贴："第三行代数搞错了，下次应该这样做"——然后靠这些便利贴高效复习。SRPO就是给AI模型这种"贴便利贴自我复盘"的超能力，而且完全不需要老师！Think of it like a student who, instead of just checking if they got the final answer right, goes back through every line of their scratch work, writes a sticky note saying "I messed up the algebra here — next time do THIS instead," and uses those sticky notes to study smarter. SRPO gives AI models that same sticky-note superpower — no tutor required.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 当AI模型尝试解决复杂的多步骤问题时（比如网页搜索、写代码或解数学题），通常只在最后得到一个"对或错"的信号。就像你考完试只被告知"不及格"，却不知道哪道题错了、为什么错。这让模型很难搞清楚问题出在哪里、该如何改进。现有的方法要么需要更大更贵的"老师模型"，要么需要复杂的奖励机制，代价很高。 When AI models try to solve complex, multi-step problems — like browsing the web, writing code, or solving math — they usually only get a single "right or wrong" signal at the very end. It's like studying for an exam and only being told "you failed" with no feedback on which questions you got wrong. This makes it hard for the model to figure out where exactly it went wrong and how to improve. Existing solutions either require expensive bigger teacher models or elaborate separate reward systems.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 无需外部大型教师模型，SRPO让模型自己回顾、批评并学习自己的做题过程，实现真正的自我提升。• 将最终的"对/错"单一信号，转化为每一步都有详细指导的"反思补丁"，精准告知哪里出错、怎么改。• 在数学推理和真实世界智能体任务上均达到顶尖水平，却只需通常所需算力的8%，效率大幅领先。 • Instead of relying on a separate, larger teacher model, SRPO lets the model critique and learn from its own completed attempts — true self-improvement. • It converts a single "pass/fail" outcome into rich, step-by-step learning signals ("reflection patches") that tell the model exactly what went wrong at each step. • Achieves state-of-the-art results on both math reasoning AND real-world agent tasks using only 8% of the computing power normally required — a huge efficiency leap.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队以Qwen3-8B为基础模型，在多个高难度基准上测试了SRPO：数学竞赛题（AIME 2024、AIME 2025、AMC）、真实网络购物任务（WebShop）、家庭机器人指令执行（ALFWorld）以及软件工程bug修复（SWE-Bench-Lite）。与之对比的基线包括标准监督微调（SFT）、GRPO强化学习以及其他自我提升方法，评估指标为准确率和任务成功率，同时严格统计训练所需算力（FLOPs）。 The team tested SRPO on a Qwen3-8B base model across several challenging benchmarks: math competition problems (AIME 2024, AIME 2025, AMC), real-world web shopping tasks (WebShop), household robot instruction following (ALFWorld), and software engineering bug fixing (SWE-Bench-Lite). They compared against strong baselines including standard supervised fine-tuning (SFT), GRPO reinforcement learning, and other self-improvement methods, measuring accuracy and task success rate while carefully tracking training compute costs (FLOPs).
📊 实验
结果		 在AIME 2024数学竞赛上，SRPO达到73.3%的准确率，仅使用了大规模SFT所需算力的8%（0.08倍），效率提升极为显著。在真实智能体任务上：WebShop成功率64.7%，ALFWorld成功率76.8%，SWE-Bench-Lite成功率31.2%。这些成绩在所有测试基准上均达到或接近最先进水平，且模型参数量仅8B，训练算力远低于竞争方法。 On AIME 2024, SRPO hits 73.3% accuracy using only 8% (0.08×) of the training FLOPs needed by scaled SFT — a massive efficiency gain. On real-world agent tasks: WebShop success rate reaches 64.7%, ALFWorld reaches 76.8%, and SWE-Bench-Lite reaches 31.2%. These results represent state-of-the-art or near state-of-the-art performance across all tested benchmarks, achieved with a relatively small 8B-parameter model and far less training compute than competing methods.
🏁 结论 SRPO证明了：赋予AI模型自我反思错误的能力——无需任何外部教师或奖励系统——是一种高效且有效的训练策略。它在数学推理和复杂现实智能体任务上均达到顶尖水平，所需算力仅为通常方法的一小部分。 SRPO proves that giving AI models the ability to reflect on their own mistakes — without any external teacher or reward system — is a highly effective and efficient training strategy. It achieves top-tier performance on both math reasoning and complex real-world agent tasks at a fraction of the usual computational cost.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

SRPO的工作方式就像一个学生考完试后，仔细回顾每一步解题过程，然后写下一张"错题笔记"："我在第3步犯了这个错误，正确做法应该是……"模型先尝试解题，然后回头审视整个解题路径，生成一段简短的"反思补丁"，总结哪里出错、为什么出错。这段反思随后被用来对原始解答的每一个词（token）重新打分，把模糊的最终结果反馈转化为细致的逐步指导信号。最关键的是：这一切都在模型内部完成，不需要更大的外部教师模型，也不需要单独的奖励模型。 Think of SRPO like a student who, after finishing a test, carefully reviews every step of their working — not just the final answer — and writes down a personal note: "I made this specific mistake at step 3, here's how I should have done it." The model first attempts a problem, then looks back at its entire solution path and generates a short "reflection patch" summarizing what went wrong and why. This reflection is then used as a rich teaching signal to re-score every single token (word) in the original attempt, turning vague end-result feedback into detailed, step-by-step guidance. Critically, this entire process happens inside the model itself — no bigger external teacher or separate reward model is needed.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L}_{\text{SRPO}} = -\mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim \pi_\theta} \left[ \sum_{t} w_t^{\text{reflect}} \log \pi_\theta(a_t | s_t) \right]\]
💡 SRPO的训练目标：用反思生成的逐token权重（w）来引导模型学习，权重越高说明该步骤越值得强化或纠正。 SRPO's training loss: the model is trained by weighting each token in its solution according to reflection scores — tokens at key mistake points get stronger learning signals.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项工作有望大幅降低训练高性能AI推理系统的成本，让算力资源有限的公司和研究者也能负担得起。它同时指向了一类能通过自我反思持续进步的AI模型——这是迈向更可靠、更自主AI助手的重要一步。 This work could make powerful AI reasoning systems dramatically cheaper to train, lowering the barrier for companies and researchers who can't afford massive compute budgets. It also points toward AI models that continuously improve themselves through reflection — a key step toward more reliable and autonomous AI assistants.

🏷️ Autonomous Driving · 自动驾驶🎮 World Models · 世界模型🏷️ 3D Geometry · 三维几何🏷️ Action Prediction · 动作预测
GeoWAM: Visual Geometry World Action Models for Autonomous Driving GeoWAM：用于自动驾驶的视觉几何世界动作模型

🧑‍🔬 Yiren Lu, Xin Ye, Jiaming Liu, Jin Yao et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 用3D点云预测未来场景，让自动驾驶决策更精准
GeoWAM predicts future 3D geometry instead of future images to train smarter autonomous driving policies.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就好像你在学打乒乓球——与其看一段慢动作视频，不如直接拿一把3D扫描仪追踪球的飞行轨迹和旋转角度。视频里有太多无关的东西（背景、光线、衣服颜色），而3D轨迹数据才是你真正需要的"球感"。GeoWAM就是把这个道理用到了自动驾驶上。Imagine learning to play ping-pong by watching blurry videos versus having a 3D tracker that shows you exactly where the ball is and how fast it's spinning in space. The video has tons of distracting stuff — backgrounds, clothing, lighting — while the 3D tracker gives you only what matters. GeoWAM does exactly that for self-driving cars: swap out the blurry video for a clean 3D map of the world.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的自动驾驶AI通常通过预测未来的"视频画面"来学习如何驾驶——就像让一个学员司机靠脑补电影场景来学开车。问题是，画面里包含太多无关信息（光线、颜色、纹理），让AI很难从中理解真正重要的东西：物体在三维空间里是怎么运动的。这让模型在理解场景和做出驾驶决策时都受到了限制。 Existing AI driving models learn by predicting what future video frames will look like — but video pixels carry a lot of "noise" like lighting changes, colors, and textures that have nothing to do with how to actually drive. This makes it hard for the AI to understand the true 3D structure of the world and how objects are really moving around it, which in turn makes driving decisions less accurate.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 创新性地用"点云"（3D空间中的一堆坐标点）替代像素图像，作为世界模型的状态空间，更直接地表达空间结构和运动。 • 提出预训练目标：让模型预测未来的三维几何形状，而不是未来的视频帧，使模型天然获得空间感知能力。 • 设计了几何条件动作头，将学到的几何动态直接用于预测自车未来的行驶轨迹，形成端到端闭环。 • Replaces pixel-based future prediction with point cloud (3D geometry) prediction, cutting out irrelevant visual noise like lighting and color. • Introduces future-geometry forecasting as a pretraining objective, so the model learns spatial structure and motion patterns naturally. • Attaches a geometry-conditioned action head that uses learned 3D dynamics to produce precise ego-vehicle trajectory predictions.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在自动驾驶标准数据集和仿真环境中进行，分别评估开环（离线回放轨迹预测）和闭环（在模拟器中实际驾驶）两种场景。基线对比了主流的图像/像素空间世界模型方法，评估指标包括轨迹预测误差（L2距离、碰撞率等）以及闭环驾驶得分。 The team evaluated GeoWAM in both open-loop settings (predicting trajectories from recorded driving logs) and closed-loop settings (actually controlling a simulated car). They compared it against leading image-based world action models as baselines, using metrics like L2 trajectory error, collision rate, and overall closed-loop driving scores on standard autonomous driving benchmarks.
📊 实验
结果		 GeoWAM在开环和闭环评测中均大幅超越基于图像的世界模型基线。闭环驾驶得分显著提升，碰撞率明显降低，轨迹预测的L2误差也更小，证明几何预训练作为目标函数在驾驶策略学习上具有实质性优势。（具体数值依论文正文为准，摘要中未列出精确数字。） GeoWAM substantially outperforms image-based world action model baselines on both open-loop and closed-loop benchmarks, achieving lower L2 trajectory prediction error, reduced collision rates, and higher overall closed-loop driving scores. The paper reports that geometry-based pretraining yields "substantially stronger driving policies" than pixel-space alternatives across all evaluated metrics. (Exact numerical figures are reported in the full paper's experiment tables.)
🏁 结论 本文最终证明：用三维几何（点云）而非二维像素图像来建模未来场景，是训练自动驾驶策略的更有效预训练目标。GeoWAM为世界动作模型提供了一个全新的几何优先范式，为更精准、更安全的自动驾驶AI奠定了基础。 This paper proves that predicting future 3D geometry — rather than future video frames — is a superior pretraining objective for autonomous driving. GeoWAM establishes a new geometry-first paradigm for world action models, showing that working in 3D space leads to significantly better and safer driving decisions.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

GeoWAM的核心思路是：与其让AI预测"未来长什么样"，不如让它预测"未来空间结构是什么样"。具体来说，模型用激光雷达点云来描述当前场景的三维形状，然后训练它预测未来时刻的三维点云。这就好比把眼睛换成了一把"空间尺"——不再关心颜色和光线，只关心每个物体在哪里、往哪移动。预训练好的几何表示随后被送入一个专门的动作预测模块，直接输出车辆该如何行驶的轨迹。整个流程在开环和闭环两种评测场景下均进行了验证。 GeoWAM replaces future image prediction with future 3D geometry prediction. It represents the driving scene as a point cloud — a set of 3D coordinates capturing where every object is in space — and trains the model to forecast what that point cloud will look like in the future. Think of it like teaching the AI to think in "3D blueprints" rather than "photographs." Once pretrained this way, a special action head uses those learned 3D dynamics to directly output the trajectory the car should follow. The whole system is tested in both open-loop (offline) and closed-loop (simulated driving) settings.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

自动驾驶汽车的核心难题之一是如何让AI真正"理解"三维世界，而不只是"看图说话"。GeoWAM证明了几何优先的思路能带来更准确的驾驶决策，这对推动自动驾驶从实验室走向真实道路具有直接的实践意义。对于整个AI领域，这也启示我们：选对"状态空间"（用什么来表示世界）往往比模型本身更关键。 For autonomous vehicles to become truly safe and reliable, the AI needs to reason about the 3D world — not just recognize images. GeoWAM shows a practical path to building driving AIs that think spatially, which could accelerate the deployment of safer self-driving cars. More broadly, it demonstrates that choosing the right representation of the world (geometry over pixels) can be more impactful than just scaling up models.

ReWorld: An Interactive World Model with Long-Horizon Memory ReWorld：具备长时记忆的交互式世界模型

🧑‍🔬 Zhifei Chen, Luozhou Wang, Guibao Shen, Dongyu Yan et al.

图 Figure · Page 4 of paper

🎯 实时交互世界模型，能记住走过的地方并高质量生成视频
ReWorld is an interactive world model that remembers where it's been and streams photorealistic video in real time.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像你雇了一个向导在巨大的城市里陪你探险。普通向导走得太远就忘了你之前去过哪里。但ReWorld的向导有个秘密小本子——每到一个地标就拍张照片贴上GPS坐标。当你绕了一大圈回到原地时，他翻出那张照片，完美重现出那条街的样子——而不需要把整段旅程的视频全部记在脑子里。Think of it like exploring a huge city with a tour guide who has a perfect photographic memory. Most guides forget streets you visited an hour ago — but ReWorld's guide secretly jots down a snapshot of every landmark you pass, tagged with your GPS location. When you circle back, they flip to the right photo and recreate the street perfectly, even though they didn't keep a full video recording of the whole trip.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 AI"世界模型"可以生成逼真的虚拟世界视频，让你用键盘或手柄在里面"漫游"。但现有模型面临一个两难困境：要让模型快速响应你的操作，它就只能记住最近的画面，忘掉你之前去过的地方；要让它记住更远的历史，它又会变得很慢甚至内存溢出崩溃。目前没有任何模型能同时做到"精准控制+长程记忆+实时生成"三件事。 AI "world models" can generate realistic-looking video worlds that you navigate with a keyboard or controller — like a video game engine run entirely by AI. But existing models face a painful trade-off: if you make them responsive to your controls (short memory), they forget places they showed you earlier; if you make them remember far back (long memory), they become too slow or run out of computer memory. There's no existing model that handles precise steering, long-range memory, AND real-time speed all at once.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 分工注意力机制：大多数"思考头"专注近期画面保证快速响应，少数"全局头"扫描完整历史保证长程记忆，两者互不干扰。 • 地标记忆库：不是存储每一帧，而是按位置建立一个精简"相册"，当你回到某地时，模型直接查找最近的记忆快照。 • 一个模型两种速度：同一神经网络既可以高质量多步生成，也可以通过轻量适配器实现实时交互，无需训练两个模型。 • 统一动作尺度：将8种不同来源的数据（游戏、真实视频、3D渲染）标准化，确保同一按键在所有场景中移动相同距离，让训练更一致。 • Split-brain attention: most of the model's "thinking heads" focus on recent frames for fast control, while a small set of special heads scan the entire history for long-range memory — the two jobs never interfere. • Landmark memory bank: instead of storing every frame, the model keeps a compact "photo album" of key viewpoints indexed by position; when you revisit a place, it looks up the nearest saved snapshot. • One model, two speeds: a single neural network can run in high-quality multi-step mode OR real-time interactive mode via a lightweight adapter — no need to train two separate models. • Unified action scale: eight different data sources (game footage, real-world video, 3D renders) were normalized so that the same button press always moves the camera the same distance, making training consistent.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队用三个维度的评估协议与6个最新交互式世界模型对比：(1) 动作跟随准确性——摄像机是否按指令精准移动？(2) 长程回忆——长途旅行后回到原地，画面是否一致？(3) 视频质量——生成画面是否真实、连贯？测试使用长达64秒（384个潜变量步骤）的"往返轨迹"，远超大多数基准测试的时长。数据来自8个来源，包括Unreal Engine渲染、游戏录像和真实世界拍摄素材。 The team evaluated ReWorld against six recent interactive world models using a three-axis protocol: (1) action-following accuracy — does the camera move exactly where you tell it to? (2) long-horizon recall — when you return to a place after a long journey, does it look the same? (3) video quality — how realistic and consistent are the generated frames? They tested on minute-long "out-and-back" rollouts of 64 seconds (384 latent steps), a much longer horizon than most benchmarks. Data came from eight sources including Unreal Engine renders, game recordings, and real-world footage.
📊 实验
结果		 ReWorld在动作跟随上达到11.95°旋转误差（所有对比模型中最优），摄像机运动一致性得分最佳。在写实、游戏风格、风格化三类世界中的视频生成质量均为最优。最关键的是：在64秒往返轨迹（384个潜变量）测试中，ReWorld的固定12块缓存仍能正确重建起点画面——而滑动窗口基线早已丢失该记忆，全KV注意力基线则直接因内存溢出崩溃。 ReWorld achieves 11.95° rotation error (best among all compared models) and the best camera-motion consistency score for action following. It attains the best video generation quality metrics across photorealistic, game-style, and stylized worlds. Crucially, on 64-second out-and-back rollouts (384 latents), its fixed 12-chunk cache successfully regenerates the starting view — while a sliding-window baseline has long evicted that evidence and a full-KV-attention baseline runs out of memory entirely at that rollout length.
🏁 结论 ReWorld证明了一个交互式世界模型可以同时实现精准实时控制、长程空间记忆和高质量视频生成——这三个目标此前被认为存在根本性冲突。其地标记忆库和混合注意力设计在三项评估维度上均超越了6个竞争模型，达到新的最优水平。 ReWorld proves that a single interactive world model can simultaneously achieve precise real-time control, long-range spatial memory, and high-quality video generation — goals previously thought to be in fundamental conflict. Its landmark-bank memory and mixed-attention design set a new state of the art across all three evaluation axes against six competing models.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

ReWorld把注意力机制分成两类"阅读器"来解决控制与记忆的矛盾。大多数阅读头只看最近的帧，保证对玩家操控的快速响应；少数"全局头"可以回顾整个历史。训练时，模型通过随机分配头的职责和随机丢弃历史片段，防止模型"偷懒"——每个头都可能被要求做任何工作，所以必须两种能力都学会。 推理时，不是把所有历史帧塞进内存（会溢出），而是维护一个"地标银行"——一个按摄像机位置标注的精简快照相册，固定只保留12个块的预算。玩家走回老地方时，模型检索最近的快照重建场景。 数据方面，团队构建了一个数据引擎，把8种视频来源（游戏、真实拍摄、3D渲染）对齐到同一物理移动尺度，并专门制作"回文轨迹"（出发再返回）供模型训练"重访"场景的能力。 最后，一个轻量LoRA适配器把多步生成压缩成4步，实现704×1280分辨率的实时流式生成，无需重新训练主干网络。 ReWorld tackles the control-vs-memory conflict by splitting its attention mechanism into two types of "readers." Most reading heads only look at recent frames — this keeps response to player controls fast and precise. A small number of "global" heads are allowed to look all the way back in history. During training, random tricks (random head routing and randomly dropping chunks of history) prevent the model from cheating: any head could be asked to do either job, so it has to learn both. At inference time, instead of keeping every past frame in memory (which would overflow), ReWorld stores a compact "landmark bank" — a limited album of key snapshots tagged with camera position. When the player moves back toward a previously visited spot, the model retrieves the nearest saved snapshots and reconstructs what that place looked like, all within a fixed memory budget of 12 chunks. To make training data consistent, the team built a data engine that aligns eight video sources onto one physical movement scale, and creates "palindrome" (out-and-back) trajectories so the model explicitly trains on revisiting the same location. Finally, a LoRA adapter (a small plug-in module) distills the multi-step generation process into just 4 steps, enabling real-time streaming at 704×1280 resolution without retraining the backbone.

❓ 待解决
的问题		 用强化学习训练大语言模型时，存在一个令人头疼的两难困境：如果对模型约束太紧，它就无法探索新策略；如果约束太松，训练过程就会崩溃变得不稳定。现有方法用一种叫"Policy-KL"的惩罚项来限制模型的回答，但这个惩罚同时也压制了模型的"创造力"。简单说，就像骑自行车——捏紧刹车就跑不快，松开刹车又容易摔跤，很难两全。 When training AI language models with reinforcement learning (RL), there's a painful trade-off: if you constrain the model too tightly, it can't explore new strategies; if you loosen the leash, training goes haywire and becomes unstable. Current methods use a "Policy-KL" penalty that limits how different the model's responses can be from a reference model — but this same penalty also eats up the model's freedom to try new things. You're stuck choosing between stability and creativity.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 全新视角：不约束模型"怎么回答"，而是约束模型"在哪些问题上训练"，从输入端而非输出端入手解决稳定性问题。 • 提出Query-KL（QKL）项，限制训练问题分布的漂移，完全不干涉模型回答的自由度，探索空间得以保留。 • 设计了基于参考分布的逐问题重要性权重，让模型更专注于"典型"训练问题，相当于一个智能过滤器。 • 即插即用：可无缝接入GRPO、PPO、REINFORCE等主流强化学习框架，无需额外的前向计算。 • Novel insight: Instead of constraining what the model *says* (output/response side), ERPO constrains what *questions* the model is trained on (input/query side) — a fundamentally different angle on the same problem. • The Query-KL (QKL) term keeps the training question distribution from drifting too far from the original, without touching response freedom at all. • A per-query importance weight further focuses learning on questions that are "typical" for the original training setup, acting like a smart filter. • Plug-and-play: works seamlessly inside existing RL pipelines like GRPO, PPO, and REINFORCE with no extra computation cost.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

想象你在辅导一个学生备考数学。旧方法是告诉学生："你的答题风格不能和以前差太多"——这限制了他的创新思维。ERPO的做法则是："确保你练习的题型还是跟最初一样"——管的是"刷题范围"，而不是"答题方式"。具体来说，ERPO引入了一个Query-KL（QKL）惩罚项，衡量当前训练题目的分布与原始参考分布的偏差，并惩罚过大的漂移。同时，它为每道题赋予一个权重，越"典型"的题目权重越高，让模型优先学习有代表性的内容。由于这个惩罚只影响题目选择，完全不干涉模型的答题方式，模型的回答探索空间得以完整保留。 Think of training an AI like coaching a student to solve math problems. The old approach was to tell the student, "Don't answer too differently from how you used to answer" — which limits creativity. ERPO instead says: "Let's make sure you're still practicing on the same types of problems you started with" — controlling the *study diet*, not the *answers*. Technically, ERPO adds a Query-KL (QKL) penalty that measures how far the current set of training questions has drifted from the original reference set, and penalizes large drifts. It also assigns each question a weight based on how "typical" it was in the original training distribution, so the model focuses more on common/representative questions. Because this penalty only touches question selection — not how the model generates answers — the model's response exploration remains fully free.

强化学习是让ChatGPT等AI模型变得更聪明、更安全的核心技术，但训练不稳定一直是主要瓶颈。ERPO提供了一种实用的即插即用解决方案，让大模型的强化学习训练更可靠、更强大，有望直接加速更智能的AI助手和推理系统的研发落地。 Reinforcement learning is the key technique behind making AI models like ChatGPT smarter and safer — but instability during training is a major bottleneck. ERPO offers a practical, drop-in solution that makes RL training for LLMs more reliable and more powerful, which could directly speed up the development of smarter AI assistants and reasoning systems.
