⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Crucially, it's connected to real lab equipment (like a chemical reactor) and can send instructions, receive results, and loop back to refine its approach — all automatically, without a human in the loop for every step.

🎯 机器人看一段人类视频就能学会新任务，无需重新训练。

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Zero-WAM的工作方式就像一个学生在动手之前先看示范视频。给机器人一个新任务时，先播放一段人类完成该任务的视频。模型将这段"上下文"视频与机器人当前的摄像头画面一起处理，逐步生成对应的动作指令。为了解决人类-机器人配对数据稀缺的问题，团队构建了自动化流水线：先获取机器人仿真轨迹，再用视频生成工具将其转换为逼真的人类操作视频。此外，特殊的IFP训练目标在训练时对模型隐藏任务身份，强迫它真正从视频中读取任务信息，而非记忆捷径。 Zero-WAM works like a student who learns by watching a demonstration video before attempting a task. When given a new task, the robot is shown a short video of a human performing it. A causal video-action model then processes this "in-context" video prompt alongside the robot's current camera view and generates the appropriate actions step by step. To train this model without mountains of rare human-robot paired data, the team built an automatic pipeline that takes existing robot simulation trajectories and converts them into realistic-looking human videos using video generation tools. Finally, a special training objective (IFP) was added that hides the task identity from the model during training, forcing it to genuinely extract task guidance from the video instead of memorizing shortcuts.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L}_{\text{IFP}} = -\sum_{t} \log p(a_{t+1:t+k} \mid o_{\leq t},\, v_{\text{human}},\, \text{mask}(\tau_{\text{seen}}))\]
💡 上下文未来块预测（IFP）目标：训练模型在屏蔽已见任务信息的情况下，利用人类视频提示预测未来k步的动作序列，强迫模型从视频中提取任务信息而非走捷径。 The In-context Future chunk Prediction (IFP) loss: trains the model to predict the next k robot actions using the human video prompt, while masking out seen-task shortcuts — forcing genuine video-guided reasoning.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项研究让机器人距离真正的通用助手又近了一步：不再需要为每项家务或工厂任务单独训练机器人，只需给它看一段示范视频即可。无论是工业场景还是家庭应用，这都能大幅降低机器人部署新任务的成本与时间。 This research moves robots one step closer to being truly general-purpose: instead of training a separate robot for every household chore or factory task, you could simply show it a video of what you want done. For industries and homes alike, this could dramatically reduce the cost and time needed to deploy robots for new jobs.

🧑‍🔬 Lukas Kuhn, Lucas Maes, Giuseppe Serra, Quentin Le Lidec et al.

图 Figure · Page 3 of paper

🎯 用极少计算量让AI从视频中高效学习视觉表征
A simpler, faster video pretraining method that matches or beats state-of-the-art at up to 20x less compute.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个孩子学认字，以前需要两个老师互相核对答案才能防止偷懒，现在只需要一个老师加一套聪明的数学规则，孩子还可以"蒙着眼"只看部分内容就能学得更扎实——LeVJEPA就是这套新方法，让AI从视频"上课"的成本大幅降低。Imagine learning to recognize animals by watching nature documentaries — but instead of needing two TVs playing simultaneously and a complicated remote control to prevent you from zoning out, you just need one TV and a smart rule that says "pay attention!" LeVJEPA does exactly that for AI: one simple setup, fewer frames to watch, yet the AI learns just as well — or even better.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有AI视觉模型要么依赖大量图片数据训练，要么从视频学习时计算代价极其高昂。从视频学习理论上更好——因为视频包含物体如何运动、世界如何随时间变化的信息——但现有方法需要复杂的"防崩溃"技巧（比如用两个网络互相约束），这让训练变得又慢又贵，还有很多需要手动调节的参数。 Teaching AI to understand video is expensive and complicated. Current self-supervised methods need elaborate tricks to prevent the model from "cheating" (collapsing to a trivial solution where everything looks the same). These tricks — like using two separate networks, stop-gradients, or pixel reconstruction — are computationally heavy and require lots of hand-tuning. The goal is to make video pretraining as cheap and simple as image pretraining, while still capturing how things move over time.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次将LeJEPA的"无崩溃"目标函数应用于视频领域，彻底去掉了动量编码器、停止梯度等复杂技巧，架构极度简洁。 • 引入随机丢弃token（视频帧的小块）策略，训练速度大幅加快，同时下游任务准确率反而提升——少看反而学得更好。 • 编码器本身支持块因果注意力，使模型天然理解时间顺序，无需额外的架构非对称性设计。 • First video model trained with a collapse-free objective (SIGReg) that requires no momentum encoder, stop-gradient, or pixel reconstruction — just one encoder and one projector. • Random token dropping dramatically cuts compute while actually improving accuracy — the model learns better by seeing less. • The encoder natively learns temporal order via block-causal attention, without any asymmetric network tricks.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在大规模视频数据集上训练ViT-S、ViT-B、ViT-L三种规模的模型，与V-JEPA 2等强基线对比。下游评估涵盖ImageNet-1K图像分类（测外观理解）、Something-Something v2和Kinetics等视频动作识别基准（测运动理解），以及与仅用图片训练的DINOv2进行对比，全面考察外观和运动两类能力。 Models of three sizes (ViT-S, ViT-B, ViT-L) were pretrained on the same large video dataset used by V-JEPA 2. They were evaluated on ImageNet-1K (appearance/image understanding), Something-Something v2 and Kinetics (motion/action recognition), comparing against V-JEPA 2 and other strong video SSL baselines. A compute-matched DINOv2 (image-only baseline) was also trained on frames from the same videos to isolate the benefit of using video vs. images.
📊 实验
结果		 • 在相同训练轮次和数据下，LeVJEPA以5.6到20.8倍更少的预训练计算量，达到或超越V-JEPA 2（跨ViT-S/B/L三个规模）。 • 在相同总FLOPs预算下，LeVJEPA在ImageNet-1K上超越最强视频基线7.6个百分点。 • 与计算量匹配的DINOv2（图片训练）相比，LeVJEPA在运动中心基准上准确率接近翻倍，同时在外观类评估上也接近图片训练模型的水平。 • At matched epochs on identical data, LeVJEPA matches or surpasses V-JEPA 2 across ViT-S/B/L at 5.6× to 20.8× less pretraining compute. • At matched total FLOPs, LeVJEPA exceeds the strongest video baseline by 7.6 points on ImageNet-1K. • Against a compute-matched DINOv2 trained on frames of the same videos, LeVJEPA nearly doubles motion-centric accuracy while approaching image-pretrained performance on appearance benchmarks.
🏁 结论 本文证明：通过用数学可保证的正则化替代复杂的架构技巧，并结合随机token丢弃，视频预训练可以做到既便宜又有效。一旦去掉计算开销，视频是比图片更优越的通用视觉预训练数据来源。 LeVJEPA proves that video pretraining can be made as simple and efficient as image pretraining — and that once the computational overhead is removed, training on video produces better visual representations than training on images alone, especially for understanding motion and temporal dynamics.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

LeVJEPA的核心思路是：让同一段视频的"全局视图"和"局部视图"经过同一个编码器后，产生相似的特征表示（不变性损失）。为了防止模型偷懒（把所有视频都映射到同一个点），引入了SIGReg正则化，它从数学上保证表征不会崩溃，且只有一个超参数需要调节。训练时，模型随机丢弃大量视频token（视频帧切割成的小块），大幅减少计算量。整个架构只有一个编码器加一个投影头，比以前的双编码器方案简单得多。 LeVJEPA trains a single encoder to produce similar representations for different "views" (crops) of the same video clip — a global view and several local views. To prevent the model from collapsing (mapping everything to the same vector), it uses SIGReg regularization, which mathematically guarantees representations stay spread out, with just one tunable knob. During training, most video tokens (small patches of frames) are randomly dropped, so the encoder only processes a small fraction of the video — this cuts compute drastically while acting as a useful data augmentation. The result is a minimal architecture: one encoder + one projector, no second network needed.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{inv}}(z_{\text{global}}, z_{\text{local}}) + \lambda \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{SIGReg}}\]
💡 总损失 = 全局视图与局部视图表征的不变性损失 + SIGReg正则化项（防止崩溃），λ是唯一需要调节的超参数 Total loss = invariance between global and local view representations + SIGReg regularization to prevent collapse, with λ as the single hyperparameter

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项研究大幅降低了从视频中训练强大视觉AI的门槛，意味着未来的机器人、自动驾驶和视频理解应用可以用更少的资源获得更好的视觉感知能力。它也说明视频比图片更适合作为通用视觉AI的训练素材，这可能推动整个领域从图片预训练转向视频预训练。 This work makes powerful video-based AI training accessible at a fraction of the current cost, which is great news for robots, self-driving cars, and any system that needs to understand the physical world. It also makes a strong case that the field should shift from image-based to video-based pretraining as the default, since video teaches AI about motion and causality that static images simply can't capture.

🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像备考时你不需要把所有笔记都重读一遍——只需要重点复习那几道"似是而非"的易错题，就能避免考试时张冠李戴。记忆锚点就是这些最容易混淆的"易错题"。Think of it like studying for an exam: you don't need to re-read every single note — you just need to review the tricky questions where two similar-looking problems have completely different answers. Memory Anchors are exactly those "confusing questions" for a robot's brain.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 机器人学了新任务之后，往往会把旧任务忘得一干二净——这叫做"灾难性遗忘"。就像你刚学会一门新乐器，却突然忘了怎么骑自行车一样。现有的解决方案是把过去的经验随机存储起来、反复复习，但这种"眉毛胡子一把抓"的做法效率很低，大量珍贵资源被浪费在并不重要的记忆上。 When robots learn new tasks, they often completely forget how to do old ones — a problem called "catastrophic forgetting." Existing fixes involve storing past experiences and replaying them randomly during new training, but this wastes resources on unimportant memories and still fails to protect the most critical ones.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 发现"记忆锚点"：仅仅10%的特殊历史经验，就对防止遗忘起到了决定性作用 • 精准定位冲突区域：当新旧任务的"感知输入"相似、但所需"动作"完全相反时，这类经验最关键 • 双向验证：去掉锚点会让遗忘程度暴增4.5倍；加入锚点则让遗忘率下降63% • 真实机器人验证：在实体机器人上成功完成了多任务连续学习 • Identifies "Memory Anchors" — just ~10% of past experiences that are disproportionately responsible for preventing forgetting • Pinpoints the cause: forgetting peaks when new-task and old-task observations look similar but require conflicting actions • Proven both ways: removing anchors causes 4.5x more forgetting; adding them reduces it by 63% • Validated on a real physical robot, not just simulations
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在LIBERO机器人仿真基准上进行，涵盖多个连续任务序列，测试了随机采样、去掉锚点、加入锚点等不同缓冲区策略，并与标准的随机回放基线对比。评估指标为各旧任务的成功率（任务遗忘程度）。此外，还在真实物理机器人上验证了方法的实用性。 Experiments were run on the LIBERO robot simulation benchmark suite across multiple sequential task learning scenarios. The team compared random replay buffers, buffers with memory anchors removed, and buffers enriched with memory anchors. Success rate on previously learned tasks was the main metric for measuring forgetting. They also tested the approach on a real physical robot arm doing two consecutive task sequences.
📊 实验
结果		 • 仅去除10%的记忆锚点，灾难性遗忘程度增加超过4.5倍 • 在高冲突任务序列上，引入记忆锚点使遗忘率下降63% • 在真实机器人上，成功实现了两个任务序列的持续学习（基线方法失败） • Removing just 10% of Memory Anchors from the replay buffer causes more than a 4.5x increase in catastrophic forgetting on LIBERO benchmarks • Enriching the buffer with Memory Anchors reduces forgetting by 63% on high-conflict task sequences • Successfully enabled continual learning of two real-world robot task sequences where standard baselines failed
🏁 结论 本文证明了并非所有历史经验对防止遗忘同等重要——少量处于"感知相似、动作冲突"区域的记忆锚点才是关键。合理识别并利用这些锚点，能大幅提升机器人的持续学习能力。 The paper proves that a tiny fraction of past experiences — those where old and new tasks look alike but demand opposite actions — are the true guardians against forgetting. Identifying and prioritizing these Memory Anchors is a simple yet powerful way to enable robots to keep learning without losing what they already know.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究者发现，遗忘的根源在于：新任务的"感知画面"和旧任务很像，但要求的动作却完全相反——比如"用新方式抓同一个杯子"。这会导致AI的内部表示混乱，把旧知识覆盖掉。他们通过分析神经网络的内部特征空间，找出新旧任务"表示重叠但动作冲突"的经验数据点，将其定义为记忆锚点。只要在复习缓冲区中优先保留并重放这些锚点，AI就能有效记住旧技能，同时学习新技能。 The researchers traced forgetting to a specific trigger: when a robot sees something familiar (like a known object) but needs to do something different with it, its internal representations get scrambled, overwriting old knowledge. They analyze the robot's internal feature space to find moments where old and new task observations overlap but demand conflicting actions — these are the Memory Anchors. By making sure these anchor experiences are always included in the replay buffer during new training, the robot keeps its old skills intact while still learning new ones.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

现实中的机器人需要不断学习新任务（比如在工厂换产品线），但又不能忘掉原有技能，这一直是部署AI机器人的巨大障碍。本研究提供了一个高效、轻量的解决思路，有望让家用机器人、工业机器人真正做到"终身学习"。 Real-world robots need to keep learning new tasks over time — like a factory robot adapting to new products — without forgetting old skills, which has been a major barrier to deployment. This research offers a practical, low-cost solution that could make lifelong-learning robots a reality in homes and factories.
