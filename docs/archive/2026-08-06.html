🎯 系统揭示视觉与语言在联合预训练中如何最优协同学习
A scientific study uncovering how vision and language best learn together during multimodal AI pretraining.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像同时教一个学生弹钢琴和学外语——是从第一天就两样一起练，还是先学好一样再学另一样？这篇论文就像一项严谨的研究发现：从第一天就同时练两样、用同一双手（共享注意力机制）但用不同的练习册（模态专用层），学得最快——而且只需要通常5%的学习时间！Think of it like teaching a student to play piano AND speak a new language at the same time. 维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现代AI模型需要同时处理图像和文字（比如能看图又能聊天的GPT-4o），但训练这类模型时，视觉和语言数据究竟如何在模型内部相互影响，几乎无人真正搞清楚。语言会帮助视觉理解吗？视觉会拖语言的后腿吗？什么时候该把两者结合？这些问题一直靠"猜"，导致大量计算资源被浪费，模型效果也不够好。 Modern AI models that handle both images and text (like GPT-4o) need to be trained on both modalities together — but nobody really understands *how* the two types of data interact inside the model. Does vision help language? Does language hurt vision? When should you combine them? These questions have been guesses until now, which leads to wasted compute and suboptimal models.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 发现"知识流动"规律：语言、视觉理解、视觉生成三类信息在模态间的传递方式各不相同，呈现出明显的不对称性。 • 找到视觉与语言"协作"还是"竞争"的关键：数据复杂度是决定因素，使用共享注意力+模态专用前馈层的架构设计能有效促进协同。 • 证明从训练一开始就融合视觉和语言远优于后期对齐——延迟融合会导致"视觉惰性"，模型变得过度依赖语言捷径。 • 推导出高效训练配方：仅用5%的算力就能达到强劲的生成性能，并在135亿参数的MoE模型上用2万亿token验证。 • Discovered "Knowledge Flow" — language, visual understanding, and visual generation each transfer knowledge in different, asymmetric ways across modalities. • Identified when vision and language *cooperate* vs. *compete*: data complexity is the key factor, and certain model designs (shared attention + modality-specific feed-forward layers) promote cooperation. • Found that merging vision and language from the very start of training is far better than adding vision later — delayed integration causes "vision laziness," where the model leans too heavily on language shortcuts. • Derived efficient training recipes that hit strong performance using only 5% of normal compute, validated on massive 13.5B MoE models trained on 2 trillion tokens.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在合成数据集（用于干净的因果分析）和大规模真实图文数据集上进行。团队训练了从小型对照模型到135亿参数MoE模型（在2万亿token上训练）的一系列模型。对比了不同架构选择（注意力层/归一化/前馈层的共享与分离）、训练策略（早期与晚期模态统一、顺序训练与联合训练），以及不同数据复杂度水平。评估涵盖视觉理解基准测试和视觉生成质量指标，并在多种视觉分词器设计上验证了结论的普适性。 Experiments were conducted on synthetic datasets (for clean causal analysis) and large-scale real-world image-text datasets. The team trained models ranging from small controlled setups to 13.5B MoE models on 2T tokens. They compared different architectural choices (shared vs. separate attention/normalization/FFN layers), training schedules (early vs. late modality unification, sequential vs. joint training), and data complexity levels. Evaluations covered both visual understanding benchmarks and visual generation quality metrics, with multiple visual tokenizer designs tested to check generalizability.
📊 实验
结果		 高效预训练配方仅用标准算力的5%就达到了强劲的生成性能。早期模态统一在所有测试场景中均显著优于晚期对齐和顺序训练策略。共享注意力层+归一化、但使用模态专用前馈层的架构设计展现出最佳协同效果。四项核心发现（知识流动、协同/竞争、早期统一、高效配方）均在135亿参数MoE模型（2万亿token训练）上得到验证，证明结论可从小规模推广到真实大规模场景。 Efficient pretraining recipes achieved strong generative performance using only 5% of the standard compute budget. Early unification of modalities consistently outperformed late-stage alignment and sequential training strategies across all tested settings. Architectural designs with shared attention and normalization but modality-specific feed-forward layers showed the best synergy scores. All four core findings (knowledge flow, synergy/competition, early unification, efficient recipes) were validated at scale with 13.5B MoE models trained on 2T tokens, confirming the insights generalize beyond small-scale experiments.
🏁 结论 本文首次以科学实验的方式系统研究了视觉与语言在联合预训练中的交互机制，提炼出四条可操作的原则——最重要的是，早期融合加上正确的架构设计可在不损失性能的前提下将算力成本削减95%。这为构建下一代多模态AI系统奠定了有理论依据的基础。 This paper provides the first systematic, science-like study of how vision and language interact during joint AI pretraining, yielding four actionable principles — most notably that early fusion and the right architecture design can slash compute costs by 95% without sacrificing quality. It lays a principled foundation for building the next generation of multimodal AI systems.

🎯 让AI像艺术家一样思考、查资料、再作画的全流程智能体图像生成模型
A fully agentic model that reasons, uses external tools, and generates images all within one unified policy.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个真正厉害的插画师接到订单时，不会闭着眼睛乱画，而是先看看参考资料、想想构图，再拿起画笔——ToolArtist就是让AI学会这套"查资料→想一想→再动笔"的完整工作流，而不是只会"听到词就画"。Think of it like the difference between a skilled illustrator and a vending machine: a vending machine just spits out whatever button you press, but a skilled illustrator looks up references, thinks about your needs, and then draws something that truly fits. 维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的文字转图片AI（比如Stable Diffusion、DALL-E）只会按照你输入的文字直接画图，但遇到需要查找真实世界信息、多步推理或复杂语义理解的任务时就傻眼了。比如你说"画一张今年最新款iPhone旁边放一杯咖啡的图"，它不知道最新款iPhone长什么样，也不会主动去查。现有的"加了智能体能力"的方案，要么流程固定死板，要么只让AI参与部分步骤，推理、调用工具、画图三件事没法由同一个模型统一协调完成。 Current text-to-image AI models can draw pretty pictures, but they fail at tasks that need real-world knowledge, like drawing "the latest iPhone next to a coffee cup" — they don't know what the newest iPhone looks like and can't look it up. Existing attempts to add "agent" smarts to image generation either follow a rigid fixed script or only let the AI control part of the process. The result: reasoning, searching for information, and actually drawing the image are never coordinated by a single brain.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首个将推理、外部工具调用和图像生成完全统一在单一策略下的智能体图像生成框架，无需固定流程 • 提出"隐藏图像生成工具"的SFT数据构建技巧，让模型学会把画图当作自己的原生能力，而非外部调用 • 提出RAD-GRPO强化学习算法，同时用"意图奖励"（有没有按对方要求做）和"质量奖励"（图画得好不好）双信号优化模型 • 开源全套训练数据和训练基础设施，大幅降低同类研究门槛 • First framework to unify reasoning, external tool use, and image generation under one single agent policy — no fixed pipelines • Clever SFT trick: hide the image-generation tool so the model treats drawing as a native skill, not an external call • New RL algorithm RAD-GRPO uses two complementary reward signals — intent (did it follow instructions?) and quality (does the image look good?) • Fully open-source training data and infrastructure, lowering the barrier for future research
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在开放世界图像生成任务上进行，对比了固定流程的pipeline方法、只有部分智能体控制的方法以及纯文字转图片基线模型。评估指标涵盖图像质量、指令遵循准确率和整体用户偏好，同时测试了模型在需要外部知识（如查询最新信息）的场景下的表现。消融实验逐一验证了SFT数据构建策略、RAD-GRPO中意图奖励和质量奖励各自的贡献。 Experiments were run on open-world image generation benchmarks that require external knowledge and multi-step reasoning. The team compared ToolArtist against fixed-pipeline agents, partially-agentic systems, and plain text-to-image baselines. Metrics included image quality scores, instruction-following accuracy, and human preference ratings. Ablation studies were conducted to test each design choice — especially whether hiding the image-generation tool and each of the two reward signals actually helped.
📊 实验
结果		 ToolArtist在开放世界图像生成任务上全面超越固定流程和部分智能体控制的方法。完全智能体控制策略相比固定流程基线在指令遵循和图像质量综合评分上取得一致性提升；RAD-GRPO的意图奖励和质量奖励缺一不可，单独使用任一奖励均导致性能下降；SFT阶段隐藏图像生成工具的设计也被消融实验证明显著有效。（注：论文摘要中未披露具体数字，以上为论文报告的定性和消融结论。） ToolArtist consistently outperforms fixed-pipeline and partially-agentic baselines on open-world image generation tasks in both instruction-following and image quality. The SFT trick of hiding the image-generation tool also proves significantly effective in ablations. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

ToolArtist的训练分两个阶段。第一阶段（监督微调SFT）：先让一个"教师智能体"带着搜索工具和画图工具去完成各种图像生成任务，记录下完整的"思考→搜索→画图"轨迹。然后在整理数据时，把"画图工具调用"这个步骤藏起来，只保留画出来的图，让学生模型以为画图是自己天生就会的能力。第二阶段（强化学习RL）：用全新设计的RAD-GRPO算法继续训练，同时给模型两种反馈信号——"意图奖励"衡量模型有没有理解并执行用户的真实需求，"质量奖励"衡量生成的图好不好看、合不合要求。两个信号共同引导模型越来越聪明地决定什么时候思考、什么时候查资料、什么时候直接画图。 ToolArtist is trained in two stages. First, a "teacher agent" equipped with search and drawing tools is sent on many image-generation tasks, and all its step-by-step actions (thinking → searching → drawing) are recorded. When packaging this data for the student model, the "call drawing tool" step is hidden, so the student learns to think of drawing as something it naturally does itself. Second, a new reinforcement learning algorithm called RAD-GRPO fine-tunes the model further using two feedback signals: an "intent reward" (did it understand and follow the user's actual goal?) and a "quality reward" (does the image look good and meet the requirements?). Together these two signals teach the model when to reason, when to search the web, and when to just draw.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项研究意味着未来的AI绘图助手可以真正理解复杂需求、主动查资料、再高质量作画，而不只是"按词索图"。对于设计师、内容创作者和普通用户来说，AI将能处理更贴近真实世界的复杂图像生成需求，大幅提升实用价值。 This means future AI art tools could actually look up information about the world, reason about your request, and then draw — making them far more useful for real creative tasks. For designers, content creators, and everyday users, this moves AI image generation from a "fancy autocomplete" to a genuine intelligent creative assistant.

🎯 单张GPU可训练的轻量级机器人世界-动作模型
A lightweight robot brain that predicts the future and acts — all trainable on a single consumer GPU.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个下棋高手，不需要死记硬背棋盘上每一个细节，而是在脑子里保留一张"关键局势草图"——同时练习预判对手下一步棋，又规划自己的最佳应对。LiLa-WAM对机器人做的正是这件事：用一张紧凑的"心理草图"想象未来，而不是完整的高清画面，既省力又精准地抓住了控制所需的关键信息。Think of it like a chess player who doesn't memorize every detail of the board, but instead keeps a mental "summary" of key positions — and simultaneously learns both to predict the opponent's next move AND to plan their own best response. 维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 让机器人学会执行复杂任务非常困难。目前最先进的"世界-动作模型"（能预测未来场景变化并做出动作的AI）训练成本极高，需要多张昂贵的GPU，普通实验室根本用不起。现有方法要么把大量算力浪费在与控制无关的视觉细节上，要么需要复杂的多阶段训练流程，门槛极高。 Teaching robots to do complex tasks is hard. Most cutting-edge AI robot "brains" that can predict what will happen next — called world-action models — are extremely expensive to train, requiring many powerful GPUs and huge amounts of memory. This makes them out of reach for most researchers and labs. Existing approaches either waste compute on unimportant visual details, or need complicated multi-step training pipelines.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 只需一张24GB显存的GPU即可端到端训练，大幅降低了机器人学习研究的门槛。• 设计了紧凑的"潜在推理空间"，同时服务于两个目标：预测未来场景 + 生成控制动作，让模型保持轻量且高度专注于控制相关信息。• 提出"视觉过渡Token（VTT）"——一种无需文字描述的任务表达方式，直接在视觉特征空间里用"方向"来告诉机器人该做什么。 • Trains end-to-end on a single 24GB GPU — dramatically lowering the cost barrier for robot learning research. • A compact "latent reasoning space" is jointly shaped by two goals at once: predicting future scenes AND generating actions, keeping everything lean and focused. • Introduces Visual Transition Token (VTT) — a clever language-free way to tell the robot what task to do by encoding the task as a direction in visual feature space, no text prompts needed.
🧪 实验
内容		 团队在三个平台上测试了LiLa-WAM：①RoboTwin 2.0模拟基准，包含50种不同的机器人操作任务；②LIBERO，另一个标准机器人模拟基准套件；③真实物理机器人实验。主要评估指标为任务成功率，并与其他世界-动作模型及机器人策略方法进行对比。所有训练均在单张24GB GPU上完成。 The team tested LiLa-WAM on three evaluation settings: (1) RoboTwin 2.0, a simulation benchmark with 50 different robotic manipulation tasks; (2) LIBERO, another standard robot simulation benchmark suite; and (3) real physical robot experiments. They compared against other world-action models and robot policy methods, measuring task success rate as the main metric. All training was done on a single 24GB GPU.
📊 实验
结果		 在RoboTwin 2.0的50个任务上，LiLa-WAM实现了90.48%的平均成功率，且仅用单张GPU训练完成。在LIBERO基准上，其性能与需要更多算力的方法相当甚至更优。真实机器人实验进一步验证了其在物理世界中的有效性，不只是在模拟环境中表现良好。 On RoboTwin 2.0 (50 tasks), LiLa-WAM achieves a 90.48% average success rate — trained on a single GPU. On LIBERO benchmarks, it achieves competitive results compared to methods requiring far more compute. Real-robot experiments further confirm it works in the physical world, not just simulation.
🏁 结论 LiLa-WAM证明了构建强大的机器人大脑并不需要超级计算机——精心设计的紧凑潜在推理空间，通过预测与动作联合训练，在单张消费级GPU上就能达到顶尖性能。这让先进的机器人学习研究变得更加触手可及。 LiLa-WAM proves that you don't need a supercomputer to build a powerful robot brain — a well-designed compact latent reasoning space, jointly trained for prediction and action, achieves top-tier performance on a single consumer-grade GPU. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

LiLa-WAM在压缩后的"想象空间"中工作，而不是直接处理原始像素图像。就像一个人不需要记住照片的每个像素，只需记住场景的关键要点。模型同时用两个任务来训练：①想象执行某个动作后场景会变成什么样，②决定该采取什么动作。这两个目标互相约束，使表示空间既紧凑又与控制高度相关。在任务指定方面，他们发明了视觉过渡Token（VTT）：通过对比任务执行前后的图像特征，计算其差值方向作为任务信号，机器人完全靠视觉理解目标，无需任何语言模型。 LiLa-WAM works in a compressed "imagination space" instead of raw pixel images. Think of it like a robot that doesn't need to remember every pixel of a photo — it just remembers the key ideas about a scene. The model is trained with two tasks at the same time: imagining what the scene will look like after an action, and figuring out what action to take. These two goals keep each other honest, producing a compact and control-relevant representation. For telling the robot what to do, instead of giving it text instructions, the team invented the Visual Transition Token (VTT): they look at a "before" image and an "after" image of a task, compute the difference in feature space, and use that direction as the task signal. This means the robot understands goals purely from visual context, no language model needed.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

DreamWAM 的核心思路是：训练时，让模型同时预测未来的四种"视角"——彩色图像（外观）、光流（运动方向）、深度图（空间几何）和语义分割图（物体类别）。这四种预测通过共享的注意力层互相交流，尤其让"动作决策"模块能从运动、几何、语义里汲取知识。训练完成后，那些额外的预测分支被"摘掉"，机器人上线时只需要输入普通摄像头拍到的RGB图像，完全无需改变硬件。 During training, DreamWAM asks the model to simultaneously predict four complementary views of the future: normal RGB frames (appearance), optical flow (how pixels move), depth maps (how far things are), and semantic segmentation (what objects are where). These four streams share attention layers, so the action-decision module absorbs all that richer knowledge. Think of it like a student who studies from textbooks, flashcards, diagrams, AND practice problems — but at exam time only needs a pencil. At inference, only RGB input is used; the extra branches are cleanly removed, so there's no added cost or hardware requirement.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

真实世界的机器人需要在光线不同、背景杂乱的环境中稳定工作，现有模型对这些变化非常脆弱。DreamWAM 让机器人在陌生场景下的成功率提升近 20%，向家用、工业机器人的实际落地迈出了重要一步。 Real-world robots must work reliably under constantly changing lighting, backgrounds, and clutter — conditions that break most current AI models. DreamWAM's near-20% real-world improvement brings us meaningfully closer to robots that can actually be deployed in homes, warehouses, and factories without constant retraining.
