Data Pyramid for Embodied Manipulation 具身操作的数据金字塔

🧑‍🔬 Yifan Ye, Yankai Fu, Yaoxu Lv, Bohan Hou et al.

图 Figure · Page 7 of paper

🎯 用"数据金字塔"框架系统梳理机器人学习的五类数据来源与配方
A structured "data pyramid" framework organizing five data sources to guide embodied robot learning.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一本厨师的食材指南——不是随手抓冰箱里的东西，这篇论文告诉机器人AI研究者每种"食材"（数据来源）的新鲜度和稀缺程度，以及如何搭配出最好的"食谱"，最终做出一个真正能干活的机器人大脑。Think of it like a chef's guide to ingredients: instead of just grabbing whatever's in the fridge, this paper tells robot AI researchers exactly which "ingredients" (data sources) to use, how fresh or rare each one is, and the best recipes for combining them to cook up a capable robot brain.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练能在真实世界中操作物体的机器人AI，比训练聊天机器人难得多——因为机器人需要"看到什么、做了什么"配对在一起的数据，而这类数据极其稀缺且昂贵。不像语言或图像模型可以从互联网上抓取海量内容，机器人AI没有这条捷径。研究者们缺乏一张清晰的"数据地图"，不知道该去哪里找数据、怎么组合不同来源、每种来源有哪些利弊。 Training AI robots to physically interact with the world is much harder than training chatbots or image recognizers, because robots need data that links what they see with what they do — and that kind of data is extremely scarce and expensive. Unlike language or vision models that can feast on billions of web pages and images, robot AI has no such shortcut. Researchers lack a clear map of where to get good robot data, how to combine different data sources, and what trade-offs each source brings.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出"数据金字塔"——将五类机器人数据来源统一分类，并按可扩展性与物理真实性的权衡关系排列。• 系统分析了当前主流具身AI模型（VLA模型、大脑模型、世界-动作模型）如何选择和混合数据。• 明确指出六大开放挑战，为领域提供了清晰的研究路线图。 • Introduces a "data pyramid" — a unified taxonomy of five complementary robot data sources, ranked by scalability vs. physical realism trade-offs. • Systematically analyzes how leading embodied AI models (VLA models, brain models, world-action models) choose and mix their data. • Identifies six concrete open challenges for the field, serving as a practical research roadmap.
🧪 实验
内容		 这是一篇综述/分析类论文，没有独立的实验设置。作者通过系统文献回顾，分析了来自真实机器人、UMI设备、第一/第三人称视频、仿真环境和通用视觉语言数据集等五类来源的数据特性，并对比了Open X-Embodiment、RT系列、π0、UniSim等数十个具身AI模型的数据配方与能力表现。 This is a survey and analysis paper, not an empirical study with its own experiments. The authors conduct a systematic literature review, examining datasets and models across five data source categories. They analyze prominent embodied AI models — including Open X-Embodiment, RT-2, π0, UniSim, and many others — comparing their data compositions, training strategies, and resulting capabilities across tasks like manipulation, navigation, and dexterous control.
📊 实验
结果		 作为综述论文，作者本身没有产出新的基准数字，但整理了领域内关键数据：真实机器人数据集（如Open X-Embodiment）涵盖22+种机器人、50万+条轨迹；仿真环境可近乎零成本生成数百万条标注样本；第一人称视角数据集Ego4D包含3600+小时视频；据被综述的工作报告，引入视觉-语言预训练数据可使机器人任务成功率提升20–40%。 As a survey paper, there are no new benchmark numbers produced by the authors. However, they document findings such as: real-robot datasets (e.g., Open X-Embodiment) span 22+ robot types and 500K+ trajectories; simulation environments can generate millions of labeled episodes at near-zero cost; egocentric datasets like Ego4D contain 3,600+ hours of video; and mixing vision-language pretraining data can boost robot task success rates by 20–40% in downstream evaluations reported across reviewed works.
🏁 结论 本文提供了具身机器人学习领域首个系统性的"数据金字塔"框架，清晰描绘了各类数据来源的全貌，揭示了数据组合如何影响模型能力，并提出了六大开放挑战，为下一代机器人系统的研发指明了方向。 This paper provides the field's first comprehensive "data pyramid" framework that maps the entire landscape of embodied robot learning data, revealing how data composition shapes model capabilities and laying out a clear roadmap of six open challenges for building next-generation robots.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

作者并没有训练新的机器人或模型，而是做了一件像"编写机器人数据百科全书"的事情。他们把所有与机器人相关的数据来源整理成一个五层金字塔：最底层是稀缺但最真实的真实机器人数据；往上依次是UMI式（可穿戴设备）数据、第一/第三人称视角视频数据、仿真数据，最顶层是数量最多但与机器人关联最弱的视觉-语言数据。每种数据来源在数据质量、多样性、可复用性和物理逼真度四个维度上都有评分。然后，他们系统回顾了数十个近期具身AI模型，梳理每个模型用了哪些数据、这些数据组合如何影响模型的感知、规划和动作生成能力。 The authors don't build a new robot or train a new model — instead, they do something like writing a field guide or encyclopedia for robot data. They organize all known robot-relevant data sources into a five-level pyramid: at the bottom is rare but highly realistic real-robot data; moving up are UMI-style (wearable device) data, egocentric/exocentric video data, simulation data, and at the top, abundant but less robot-specific vision-language data. Each source is rated on four dimensions: data quality, diversity, reusability, and physical fidelity. They then systematically review dozens of recent embodied AI models, mapping out exactly which data sources each model uses and how that affects the model's abilities — like perception, planning, or physical action generation.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

要造出能做饭、打扫卫生或辅助医疗的机器人，首先得解决数据问题——本文为研究者和企业提供了共同的语言和蓝图。通过清晰梳理机器人数据的来源与用途，这项工作有望加速家用和工业机器人走向现实。 Building robots that can cook, clean, or assist in hospitals requires cracking the data problem first — and this paper gives researchers and companies a shared vocabulary and blueprint for doing so. By clearly mapping where robot data comes from and what each source is good for, this work could accelerate progress toward capable household and industrial robots.

The Physics of Multi-Turn Long-Horizon Planning: From Pre-training to Post-training via Single- and Multi-Teacher On-Policy Agentic Distillation 多轮长程规划的物理学：从预训练到后训练的单教师与多教师在线蒸馏

🧑‍🔬 Tianyi Men, Zhuoran Jin, Kang Liu, Jun Zhao

图 Figure · Page 3 of paper

🎯 系统揭示AI智能体如何学会多步长程规划的完整机制
A unified framework revealing how AI agents acquire, shape, and integrate long-horizon planning skills across training stages.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像培养一个厨师：你要先教他基础食谱（预训练），再让他在真实厨房练习并被名厨点评纠正（后训练），最后让他同时跟几位风格不同的大师学习融合菜系（多教师蒸馏）。这篇论文相当于第一次用科学方法搞清楚了：每个阶段到底应该怎么教、教什么、用什么食材，才能培养出真正能应付复杂长宴的顶级厨师。Think of training an AI planner like coaching a chess student: first you teach them openings from textbooks (pre-training), then a grandmaster watches their live games and corrects mistakes in real time (post-training), and finally the student learns from several grandmasters with different styles to become well-rounded (multi-teacher distillation). This paper is the first to scientifically map out exactly what works at each coaching stage — and what backfires badly.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的AI模型在需要"多步长远规划"的任务上表现很差——比如让AI独立完成一项需要几十步骤的复杂任务时，它很容易在中途出错并越错越多。更麻烦的是，这些模型是用海量互联网数据训练的，我们根本搞不清楚它的规划能力是从哪里来的、怎么形成的。就像一个学生考试考了高分，却没人知道他到底是怎么学会的。 Current AI models struggle badly at tasks that require many steps planned in advance — like completing a complex research task or navigating a tricky game over dozens of moves. Worse, these models are trained on uncontrolled internet data, so nobody really knows *how* or *why* they pick up (or fail to pick up) planning skills. It's like a student who aced an exam but neither the student nor the teacher knows which study habits actually helped.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次在受控环境中系统拆解AI规划能力的三个阶段：习得（预训练）→塑造（后训练强化）→融合（多教师蒸馏） • 发现"显式世界模型构建"（让AI用链式思维模拟状态变化）比单纯堆砌技能数据更能泛化到长程任务 • 用信息论（互信息）方法区分"通用规划模式"和"任务专属知识"，精确定位后训练何时有效、何时无效 • 提出多教师在线蒸馏（MOPD）：让AI同时向多个不同专家学习，通过汇聚共同规划模式实现跨环境泛化 • First systematic breakdown of AI planning ability into three controllable stages: acquisition (pre-training) → shaping (post-training) → integration (multi-teacher distillation) • Discovered that having the AI explicitly model world states step-by-step (CoT state transitions) generalizes far better than just practicing isolated skills • Used mutual information to cleanly separate general planning patterns from task-specific knowledge, pinpointing exactly when post-training helps vs. hurts • Proposed Multi-teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD) that merges skills from multiple expert teachers by converging on shared planning patterns
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在自研的受控多轮规划环境中进行，覆盖不同长度（短程到长程）、不同难度和不同数据质量的任务场景。基线包括标准预训练模型、GRPO强化学习后训练、单教师OPD蒸馏等方法。评估指标包括任务完成率、长程泛化能力（随步数增长的性能衰减），以及互信息量化的规划模式迁移效果。还专门测试了"不同知识背景教师"对学生模型的影响，以及多教师场景下的干扰与协同现象。 Experiments were run in a purpose-built controlled multi-turn planning environment, testing scenarios of varying horizon lengths (short to very long), difficulty levels, and data quality tiers. Baselines included standard pre-trained models, GRPO reinforcement learning post-training, and single-teacher on-policy distillation (OPD). Key metrics were task completion rate, long-horizon generalization (how fast performance drops as steps increase), and mutual-information-measured transfer of planning patterns. Special experiments tested what happens when teacher and student have mismatched knowledge, and how multiple teachers interact (cooperate vs. interfere).
📊 实验
结果		 • 显式世界模型（CoT状态转移）比无CoT基线在长程任务上提升显著，随着规划步数增加优势扩大；仅使用原子技能数据在组合泛化任务上接近随机水平，加入少量长程数据后性能大幅跃升。 • 劣质轨迹数据使长程任务性能下降超过50%（误差随步数指数级放大）。 • OPD在低质量数据和长程设置下有效区域明显大于GRPO，更新方向一致性更高。 • MOPD在多环境融合测试中，兼容规划模式场景下实现跨环境零样本泛化；部分冲突场景支持持续学习；完全冲突场景性能严重干扰（下降可达40%以上）。 • Explicit world modeling (CoT state transitions) significantly outperforms no-CoT baselines on long-horizon tasks, with the gap widening as planning steps increase; atomic-skill-only data performs near random on compositional tasks, but adding even a small amount of long-horizon data causes a large performance jump. • Suboptimal trajectory data degrades long-horizon performance by over 50%, with errors compounding exponentially across steps. • OPD achieves a substantially broader "effective region" than GRPO under low-quality data and long-horizon settings, with more consistent gradient update directions. • MOPD achieves cross-environment zero-shot generalization when planning patterns are compatible; partially shared patterns enable continual learning; fully conflicting patterns cause severe interference (performance drops exceeding 40%).
🏁 结论 本文系统证明了：AI智能体的长程规划能力是由预训练数据格式、后训练方法选择和多教师融合策略共同决定的，并非"黑箱"。通过受控实验，研究者精确定位了每个阶段的关键因素，为未来构建更强大、更可靠的AI规划智能体提供了清晰的科学路线图。 This paper systematically proves that an AI agent's long-horizon planning ability is jointly determined by pre-training data format, the choice of post-training method, and how multi-teacher knowledge is merged — none of it is a black box anymore. By running controlled experiments across all three stages, the researchers provide a clear scientific roadmap for building stronger and more reliable AI planning agents.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究者搭建了一个完全受控的多轮交互环境（类似一个可精确调参的"AI训练实验室"），分三步研究规划能力：第一步，在预训练阶段测试不同数据格式、质量和分布的影响，发现让模型用"链式思维"逐步模拟世界状态变化效果最好，且劣质轨迹数据会指数级放大错误。第二步，在后训练阶段用GRPO（强化学习）和OPD（在线策略蒸馏）两种方法"塑造"规划能力，并用互信息量化哪些能力可以被强化、哪些反而会被破坏。第三步，提出MOPD方法，让学生模型同时向多位具有不同知识的"教师模型"学习，通过找到各教师共享的规划模式来实现能力融合，而非简单叠加。 The researchers built a tightly controlled multi-turn environment — think of it as a "training lab" where every variable can be dialed up or down precisely. They studied planning in three phases: First, during pre-training, they tested different data formats and qualities, finding that teaching the model to mentally simulate each step of a world's state (using chain-of-thought reasoning) works far better than just drilling isolated skills. Second, during post-training, they applied both reinforcement learning (GRPO) and a teacher-student distillation method (OPD), using a math tool called mutual information to measure exactly which planning skills get stronger or weaker. Third, they introduced MOPD, where the student AI learns from multiple teacher AIs simultaneously, converging on the planning strategies that all teachers agree on — avoiding confusion when teachers contradict each other.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

对于AI助手、自动编程工具、游戏AI等一切需要"多步规划"的应用来说，这项研究就像一本操作手册，告诉工程师在哪个训练阶段做什么才最有效。它还揭示了"用差数据训练会让AI越用越烂"这一关键风险，对工业界有直接警示价值。 For any AI product that needs to plan ahead — like coding assistants, autonomous agents, or game-playing bots — this research acts like an instruction manual, telling engineers exactly what to fix and when. It also exposes a critical risk: training on low-quality data doesn't just hurt a little; it makes AI catastrophically worse at long tasks, which is a direct warning for industry teams.

🏷️ Video Editing · 视频编辑🏷️ Camera Control · 相机控制🏷️ Generative AI · 生成式AI📷 Computer Vision · 计算机视觉
CameraAnything: Refilming Videos with Arbitrary Camera Control CameraAnything：用任意相机控制重新拍摄视频

🧑‍🔬 Yixuan Li, Yanhong Zeng, Ka Leong Cheng, Jiayi Zhu et al.

图 Figure · Page 5 of paper

🎯 首个统一框架，让你随意改变视频的拍摄角度、焦距和分辨率，无需3D重建。
A unified AI framework that lets you reshoot any video with full camera control — angle, zoom, and resolution — no 3D scanning needed.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像给已经拍好的视频装上一个"魔法遥控器"——拍摄结束后，你还能随时按下按钮切换到俯视角、拉近焦距，或者把横屏影片变成竖屏手机版，完全不需要重新架摄像机。Think of it like having a magic remote control for any movie scene — even after filming is done, you can press a button to instantly switch to a bird's-eye view, zoom in dramatically, or convert it to portrait mode for your phone, all without ever picking up a camera again.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 拍完一段视频后，如果想改变拍摄角度、焦距或分辨率，通常只能重新拍摄，或者用昂贵的3D重建软件。现有AI工具要么需要对场景进行复杂的3D扫描，要么只能调整相机位置而无法同时改变焦距或分辨率。目前没有一个工具能同时、灵活地处理所有这些相机控制需求。 When filmmakers or content creators want to change the camera angle, zoom level, or resolution of an existing video, they usually have to reshoot the entire scene or use expensive 3D reconstruction software. Existing AI tools can only partially help — they either require heavy 3D scanning of the scene, or they can only change the camera position but not the zoom or resolution. There's no single tool that handles all of these camera controls together in a flexible, affordable way.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次在单一系统中同时控制相机的外参（位置/角度）和内参（焦距/缩放）以及原生分辨率。 • 创新的逐像素"光线注入"技术，无需完整3D模型即可精确告诉AI新视角中每个像素从哪里来。 • 设计了一套可扩展的合成数据生成流程，通过多相机录制构建多样化训练数据，解决真实配对数据稀缺问题。 • 正交训练策略，让焦距控制和位置控制互不干扰，各自独立学习。 • First framework to jointly control both camera position/orientation (extrinsic) AND zoom/focal length (intrinsic) plus native resolution in one system. • Clever per-pixel "ray injection" technique that tells the AI exactly where every pixel in the new camera view should come from, without needing a full 3D model. • A smart synthetic data pipeline that creates diverse training videos using multi-camera setups, solving the problem of scarce real-world paired training data. • An orthogonal training strategy that teaches zoom and position controls separately so they don't interfere with each other.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究人员在标准视频相机控制基准上测试了CameraAnything，与MotionCtrl、CameraCtrl、CogVideoX-Camera和ReCapture等现有方法进行对比。评估任务包括视角变换、焦距调整、分辨率适配和多镜头切换。使用的评估指标包括视频质量分数（FID、FVD）、相机姿态精度（旋转误差RotErr、平移误差TransErr）以及感知质量（PSNR、SSIM、LPIPS）。此外还进行了用户研究，从人类视角评估视觉质量和相机可控性。 The researchers tested CameraAnything on standard video camera control benchmarks, comparing it against existing methods including MotionCtrl, CameraCtrl, CogVideoX-Camera, and ReCapture. They evaluated the model on both synthetic and real-world videos across tasks like viewpoint change, focal length adjustment, resolution adaptation, and multi-shot transitions. Metrics included standard video quality scores (FID, FVD), camera pose accuracy (RotErr, TransErr), and perceptual quality measures (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS). User studies were also conducted to assess visual quality and camera controllability from a human perspective.
📊 实验
结果		 CameraAnything在多项指标上达到最优水平。在相机姿态精度上，其旋转误差和平移误差均显著低于CameraCtrl和MotionCtrl等方法。在视频质量基准（FVD、FID）上同样优于竞品。感知质量指标（PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS）超越ReCapture等基线。用户研究显示，受试者在视觉质量和相机可控性两方面均明显偏好CameraAnything，是与竞品比较中被选择最多的方法。它也是唯一一个在不依赖3D重建的前提下，在单一框架内完成视角、焦距、分辨率和多镜头切换四大任务的方法。 CameraAnything achieves state-of-the-art results across multiple metrics. On camera pose accuracy, it significantly outperforms prior methods with lower rotation and translation errors compared to CameraCtrl and MotionCtrl. On video quality benchmarks, it achieves improved FVD and FID scores. In perceptual quality (PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS), it outperforms ReCapture and other baselines. User studies showed a clear preference for CameraAnything in both visual quality and camera controllability, with users preferring it over competing methods in the majority of comparisons. It is also the only method capable of handling all four editing tasks (viewpoint, focal length, resolution, multi-shot) within a single unified framework without 3D reconstruction.
🏁 结论 CameraAnything证明了单一AI模型可以同时处理视频编辑中所有主要的相机控制——位置、焦距和分辨率——而无需昂贵的3D重建，为灵活、实用的电影级视频编辑工具树立了新标准。 CameraAnything proves that a single AI model can jointly handle all major camera controls — position, zoom, and resolution — for video editing without expensive 3D reconstruction, setting a new standard for flexible, practical cinematic video editing tools.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

CameraAnything的核心思路是把目标视频中的每个像素描述为一条"光线"——即3D空间中的一条线，精确表示该像素在新相机视角下的"看向"方向。这些光线描述直接输入AI视频生成器，让它准确知道新相机的位置、方向和焦距。为了自然处理不同分辨率，系统采用了感知分辨率的3D位置编码（3D RoPE），AI无需裁剪或拉伸画面。模型在合成视频上训练，这些视频由多台虚拟相机围绕动态3D场景录制而成。两阶段正交训练策略让模型分别学习焦距控制和相机位置控制，避免两者相互干扰。 CameraAnything works by describing every pixel in the target video as a "ray" — essentially a line in 3D space showing exactly where that pixel is "looking." These ray descriptions are fed directly into the AI video generator, giving it precise information about the new camera's viewpoint and zoom level. To handle different resolutions naturally, the system uses a special positional encoding (3D RoPE) that is aware of the output resolution, so the AI doesn't need to crop or stretch the video. The model is trained on synthetic videos created by placing multiple virtual cameras around dynamic 3D scenes, generating pairs of videos from different angles. A two-stage orthogonal training strategy teaches the model to control zoom and camera position independently, preventing the two controls from getting confused with each other.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

论文首先从数学上分析了带CFG的在线蒸馏，发现只匹配最终引导输出时，内部的"正分支"（响应提示词）和"负分支"（无提示词的基准）都没有被单独约束，误差可以相互抵消。接着研究两种情形：若师生共享负分支则没问题；若教师负分支含有学生无法获取的私有信息，则触发NBA失效。解决方案PDM将训练目标拆分为两部分：①单独匹配正分支预测，②匹配正负分支之间的"条件方向"差异。这样两个分支都被明确监督，无法再相互掩盖错误。 The paper first analyzes the math behind on-policy distillation with CFG and shows that matching only the final guided output leaves the two internal "branches" (a positive branch that uses your prompt, and a negative branch that represents "no prompt") under-constrained. They then study two scenarios: one where the teacher and student share the same negative branch (no problem), and one where the teacher has extra private information in its negative branch that the student can't access — this creates the NBA problem. To fix it, they design PDM: instead of matching the combined guided output, PDM separately matches (1) the positive branch prediction and (2) the directional difference between positive and negative branches. This two-part supervision removes the hiding effect and forces both branches to improve together.
