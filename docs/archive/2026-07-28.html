❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练一个又强大又高效的AI模型极其困难——尤其是当模型规模大到需要数千个GPU协同工作时。现有的开源模型要么能力不够强，要么效率太低，训练成本高得吓人。就像你想造一辆又快又省油的赛车，但大多数方案要么快但费油，要么省油但跑不快。 Building AI models that are both extremely powerful and efficient is incredibly hard. 💡 解决
的亮点		 • **Delta注意力 + 注意力残差**：全新的信息传递机制，让模型在处理超长文本时不会"遗忘"早期内容，就像给AI装了更大的工作记忆 • **Stable LatentMoE**：896个专家中每次只激活16个，既保留了超大模型的知识容量，又大幅降低了计算成本 • **扩展效率提升约2.5倍**：比上一代Kimi K2用同样的算力能学到更多，相当于"事半功倍" • **百万token上下文窗口**：能一次性阅读并理解相当于一本厚书的内容，远超大多数同类模型 • **全权重开源发布**：让全球研究者都能免费使用和研究这个前沿模型 • **Delta Attention + Attention Residuals**: A new way for the model to pass information across very long texts without "forgetting" earlier parts — like giving the AI a bigger, better working memory • **Stable LatentMoE**: Activates only 16 out of 896 specialized "expert" sub-networks per word — massive knowledge capacity at a fraction of the compute cost • **~2.5x scaling efficiency gain** over the previous Kimi K2 — same hardware, dramatically more learning • **1-million-token context window**: Can read and reason over an entire thick novel in one go • **Fully open-sourced weights**: Any researcher worldwide can download and build on this frontier model for free

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Kimi K3采用"混合专家"架构——想象一个拥有896位专家顾问的大公司，每处理一个问题时只调用最合适的16位，这样既保留了所有专家的知识，又不用每次都让所有人同时工作。此外，新设计的"Delta注意力"让模型在阅读长文本时能更好地记住和关联前后内容，就像在书上做标记一样。训练后期大量使用强化学习，让模型在编程、推理、自主任务执行等多个领域反复练习和自我纠错，就像通过大量刷题来提升解题能力。整个训练系统针对万卡GPU集群进行了深度优化，实现了专家均衡分配和高效显存管理。 Kimi K3 uses a "Mixture of Experts" design — imagine a company with 896 specialist consultants where only the 16 best-suited ones are called in for each task, so you get the full knowledge base without paying everyone to work at once. A new "Delta Attention" mechanism helps the model remember and connect information across very long texts, like having color-coded sticky notes throughout a giant document. After initial training, the team used reinforcement learning — essentially having the model practice thousands of real coding, reasoning, and multi-step tasks and learn from its own mistakes. The entire system was engineered for massive GPU clusters with balanced workload distribution and smart memory management.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{Activated Params} = 16 \text{ experts} / 896 \text{ total routed experts, plus shared experts}\]
💡 每处理一个词（token），模型从896个路由专家中只激活16个，加上共享专家部分，合计约1040亿激活参数——保证效率的同时维持超大模型的知识容量 For each word/token processed, only 16 out of 896 specialized sub-networks ('experts') are activated, giving ~104B active parameters — keeping compute costs low while retaining the knowledge of a 2.8T parameter model
\[\text{Scaling Efficiency} \approx 2.5\times \text{ over Kimi K2}\]
💡 与上一代K2相比，K3用同样的计算资源可以训练出约2.5倍更强的模型，这是架构和训练配方综合优化的结果 Kimi K3 gets approximately 2.5 times more 'learning per compute dollar' compared to its predecessor K2, thanks to combined improvements in model architecture and training recipes

顶尖AI模型长期被少数科技巨头垄断，开源社区难以企及。Kimi K3的发布打破了这一格局，让全球开发者和研究者都能免费获得接近最强水平的AI能力，可能加速医疗、教育、科研等领域的AI应用普及。同时，其2.5倍效率提升意味着同样的算力可以做到更多，对降低AI训练成本、推动可持续发展具有现实意义。 For a long time, the most powerful AI models have been locked behind expensive APIs controlled by a handful of tech giants. Kimi K3's open release gives any developer, researcher, or startup free access to near-frontier AI — potentially accelerating breakthroughs in medicine, education, and science. Its 2.5x efficiency improvement also means future AI can do much more with the same energy and hardware, making powerful AI more sustainable and accessible.

🎯 让AI在可编辑画布上持续规划、生成和修改多媒体创意项目的开放框架
JarvisHub is an open AI framework that lets creative agents plan, generate, and revise multimedia projects on an editable canvas — like a smart, collaborative digital whiteboard.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像给AI一块永远不擦的大白板：普通AI就像一台打印机，每次只给你一张纸然后忘掉一切；而JarvisHub让AI像一个真正的创意搭档，把所有草稿、修改记录、版本关系都贴在白板上，你随时可以走过去指着某个地方说"这里改一下"，AI就能接着往下做。Think of it like Google Docs for AI creativity: instead of the AI handing you one drawing and forgetting everything, JarvisHub gives the AI a giant shared whiteboard where it can pin ideas, draw connections, leave sticky notes about what worked or failed, and keep building — while you can grab the marker and redirect it anytime.

的问题		 现在的AI工具很擅长根据一句提示词生成一张图或一段视频，但真正的创意工作——比如做故事板、幻灯片或短片——需要很多步骤、反复修改、多轮反馈，以及各种素材之间的关联。现有AI系统要么会"忘记"项目的历史记录，要么把你困在单一的聊天对话里，要么需要你手动把每个工具连接起来。目前没有一个开放的系统能让AI像真正的协作者一样，参与完整的、持续演进的创意项目。 Today's AI tools are great at generating a single image or video clip from a prompt, but real creative work — like making a storyboard, a slide deck, or a short film — involves many steps, revisions, feedback rounds, and connected pieces. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

JarvisHub由三层协同工作。最底层是"画布状态"——一个实时共享的白板，每个创意素材（图像、片段、草稿、注释）都作为带标签的节点存在，并与相关素材和早期版本保持可见的连接。中间层是"协议桥"，负责将AI智能体想要执行的操作转化为实际的工具调用，并在每次操作后更新画布。最上层是"智能体运行时"——AI的"大脑"，它读取当前画布状态，决定下一步做什么（生成、修改、整理），然后将输出写回画布。因为所有内容都在画布上，人类可以随时查看、修改或引导AI，AI从不在"黑箱"中工作。 JarvisHub organizes itself into three layers working together. The bottom layer is the "canvas state" — a live, shared whiteboard where every creative artifact (image, clip, draft, note) exists as a labeled node with visible connections to related items and earlier versions. The middle layer is a "protocol bridge" that translates between what the AI agent wants to do and actual tool calls, keeping the canvas updated after every action. The top layer is the "agent runtime" — the AI brain that reads the current canvas, decides what to do next (generate, revise, organize), and writes its outputs back onto the canvas. Because everything lives on the canvas, humans can peek in, make changes, or steer the agent at any point — the AI never works in a black box.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

今天大多数AI创意工具就像自动贩卖机——按一下按钮，得到一件东西，然后重新开始。JarvisHub打开了一扇门，让AI更像真正的创意伙伴：记住你已经做了什么，在草稿基础上继续创作，从你的反馈中学习，并将所有内容整理有序。这对电影人、设计师、教育者和营销人员意义重大，可能彻底加速复杂多媒体内容的生产效率。 Most AI art and content tools today are like vending machines — you press a button, get one thing, and start over. JarvisHub opens the door to AI that acts more like a real creative partner: remembering what you've made, building on drafts, learning from your feedback, and keeping everything organized. This matters because it could radically speed up how filmmakers, designers, educators, and marketers produce complex multimedia content.

From Proprietary to Open-Source: Bridging the Distribution Gap via Multi-Agent Protocol Distillation in Agentic Search 从专有到开源：通过多智能体协议蒸馏弥合智能搜索中的分布差距

🎯 用结构化协议让开源小模型学会顶级AI的搜索推理能力
A structured protocol bridges top proprietary AI teachers and small open-source student models for smarter agentic search.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一位顶级厨师不愿公开秘方，但愿意让助手旁观做菜全过程——MAPD就是那个把观察记录整理成标准化菜谱的"翻译官"，让厨艺学徒真正学到烹饪精髓，而不只是学会说"少许盐、适量油"这样的口头禅。Imagine a master chef who won't share their secret recipes, but lets an apprentice watch them cook. MAPD is like a smart translator who watches the chef, strips out all the "hmms" and personal quirks, and writes down a clean, step-by-step recipe card — so the apprentice learns the real cooking logic, not just the chef's catchphrases.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的开源小模型在"边搜索边推理"这类复杂任务上表现很差——它们虽然可以向GPT这样的顶级商业AI"学习"，但传统的学习方式要么需要看到对方内部参数（商业AI不公开），要么只是模仿表面说话风格，学不到真正的推理能力。就像你想学一位大厨做菜，但人家既不告诉你配方，你又只是学着他的口头禅，根本学不到厨艺精髓。 Small open-source AI models struggle to learn from powerful but closed commercial models like GPT-4. Traditional "teacher-student" learning either requires peeking inside the teacher's brain (impossible with proprietary models) or just copying surface writing style without absorbing the real reasoning skills underneath. This means small, affordable models can't easily benefit from the hard-won intelligence of big, expensive ones.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出"多智能体协议蒸馏（MAPD）"：用一个结构化的JSON中间协议作为"翻译桥梁"，让不同来源、不同风格的AI知识都能被小模型有效吸收。 • 用离线多智能体系统自动分解问题、检索证据、修复失败搜索，生成高质量训练数据，无需人工标注。 • 引入"特权分支"训练机制：学生模型的一个分支看到协议内容，另一个不看，两者的差异产生密集的学习信号，比稀疏的强化学习更有效。 • 有效防止模型"风格漂移"和"废话膨胀"——学生只学推理骨架，不学老师的说话口癖。 • Introduces Multi-Agent Protocol Distillation (MAPD): a structured JSON "translation layer" that lets any proprietary AI teacher pass its reasoning skills to a small student model, regardless of style differences. • An offline multi-agent system automatically decomposes questions, retrieves evidence, and repairs failed searches — generating rich training data with no human labeling needed. • A clever "privileged branch" trick: one copy of the student sees the structured protocol, one doesn't — their difference creates a dense learning signal far stronger than sparse reward-only training. • Actively prevents "style drift" (copying the teacher's verbal habits) so the student learns genuine reasoning, not just surface mimicry.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者在七个问答基准数据集上进行了评估，包括PopQA、TriviaQA、HotpotQA、MuSiQue、Bamboogle、FRAMES和GPQA等，覆盖单跳、多跳、开放域等多种难度。基线对比包括纯强化学习方法、原始轨迹模仿蒸馏、以及多种混合方法。学生模型选用Qwen3-1.7B和Qwen3-4B两个规模，教师模型涵盖多个不同来源的专有大模型，以验证框架对不同教师的泛化能力。 The researchers tested their method on seven question-answering benchmarks spanning different difficulty levels: PopQA, TriviaQA, HotpotQA, MuSiQue, Bamboogle, FRAMES, and GPQA — covering simple factual lookups, multi-step reasoning, and expert-level questions. They compared against pure reinforcement learning, raw trajectory imitation, and various hybrid baselines. Two student model sizes were used (Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-4B), and multiple different proprietary teacher models were tested to see if the approach works broadly.
📊 实验
结果		 在七个问答基准上，MAPD让Qwen3-1.7B平均成功率达到39.4%，Qwen3-4B达到44.4%，均显著超过单独使用强化学习或原始蒸馏的竞争基线。框架对多个不同来源的专有教师模型均表现出稳健的泛化能力，同时有效缓解了学生模型的风格漂移和冗余输出问题。 Across seven QA benchmarks, MAPD achieved average success rates of 39.4% on Qwen3-1.7B and 44.4% on Qwen3-4B, consistently outperforming competitive distillation-only and RL-only baselines. The framework proved robust across multiple different proprietary teacher models and successfully reduced style drift and verbosity compared to raw trajectory imitation methods.
🏁 结论 本文证明了通过结构化中间协议，可以有效弥合专有大模型与开源小模型之间的"知识鸿沟"，使小模型在复杂搜索推理任务上获得显著提升，且不受教师风格差异的干扰。 This paper proves that a structured, style-neutral "protocol" layer can effectively transfer deep reasoning skills from closed, proprietary AI giants to small open-source models, delivering significant gains on complex search-and-reasoning tasks without copying superficial stylistic habits.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

MAPD的做法分两步：首先，用一个由多个AI组成的"团队"对每道问题进行深度分析——分解任务、检索资料、修复搜索失败——然后把整个探索过程整理成一份标准化的JSON协议，里面包含任务类型、推理计划和关键事实摘录。这个协议就像一份"去掉老师个人风格的推理说明书"。训练时，学生模型被分成"知情分支"（能看到协议）和"盲测分支"（看不到协议），两个分支的输出差异作为密集的学习信号，同时配合稀疏的强化学习奖励，让小模型既能学到深层推理逻辑，又不会走样。 MAPD works in two stages. First, a team of AI agents collaboratively tackles each question: they break it down, search for evidence, fix any failed retrievals, and distill everything into a clean, style-neutral JSON "protocol" — a structured recipe containing the task type, reasoning plan, and key extracted facts. Think of it as a translator that strips away the teacher's personal quirks and captures only the pure reasoning logic. During training, the student model runs in two parallel "modes": one branch sees the protocol (the hint sheet), and one doesn't. The gap between their outputs creates a rich, detailed learning signal that guides the student far more effectively than just rewarding correct final answers.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L}_{\text{MAPD}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{RL}} + \lambda \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{distill}}\]
💡 总训练损失 = 稀疏强化学习目标 + 加权蒸馏信号（来自特权分支与盲测分支之间的差异），两者联合优化学生模型。 The total training loss combines the sparse RL reward signal with a weighted dense distillation signal derived from the gap between the privileged (protocol-aware) and unprivileged (blind) student branches.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项研究让小型、低成本的开源模型也能享受到顶级商业AI的推理能力，降低了高质量AI应用的门槛。对于希望在本地或低预算环境下部署强大问答系统的开发者和企业来说，这是一个重要的技术突破。 This research means that small, affordable open-source models can now learn to reason as well as expensive proprietary giants — making powerful AI search tools accessible to developers and organizations without massive compute budgets. It's a step toward democratizing high-quality AI reasoning for everyone.

❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的AI电脑操作助手（能帮你点击、打字、操作软件的AI）主要靠"看截图"来决定下一步做什么——就像一个人被蒙住眼睛，只能靠别人描述屏幕上有什么来操作电脑。截图只是程序实际数据的"渲染图"，很多关键信息在截图里根本看不出来，导致AI频繁出错、成本极高。特别是在需要执行几百步操作的复杂任务中，依赖截图的AI往往跑偏、出错或无法完成。 Existing AI computer-use assistants (the kind that can click, type, and operate software for you) mostly work by "looking at screenshots" to decide what to do next — like trying to drive a car by only watching a blurry photo of the road. Screenshots are lossy: they hide a lot of the real data inside programs. This makes AI agents slow, expensive, and error-prone, especially on long, complex tasks requiring hundreds of steps.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 核心创新：提出"状态接地（state-grounding）"理念——让AI主智能体直接用代码读写程序内部状态（文件、数据库、DOM等），而非依赖截图感知 • 分工明确的多智能体架构：主智能体负责代码逻辑，仅在必要时（108个任务中仅28个）调用专门的GUI子智能体处理截图点击 • 内置验证机制：独立的"完成门"子智能体对最终结果做结构性校验，避免输出丢失、未保存、路径错误等常见失败 • 上下文管理：主智能体将子目标分发给新鲜的子智能体，避免长任务中上下文爆炸导致的"跑偏" • Core innovation: "State-grounding" — the main AI agent uses code to directly read and write program internals (files, databases, DOM), bypassing screenshots entirely most of the time • Smart division of labor: A dedicated GUI sub-agent only handles screenshot-and-click tasks when truly necessary (just 28 of 108 tasks; 1.1% of main-agent steps) • Built-in verification: An independent "finish gate" sub-agent double-checks every result for structural failures like missing output or wrong file paths • Context management: The main agent delegates sub-goals to fresh sub-agents, keeping its own memory focused and preventing "drift" over hundreds of steps

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

StateAct的核心思路是：与其让AI盯着屏幕截图猜测程序状态，不如让AI直接用代码去"触摸"程序内部——读取文件、查询数据库、检查DOM结构等。整个系统由三类角色分工合作：主智能体负责用代码完成绝大多数任务；当遇到必须点击界面的情况，主智能体会呼唤GUI子智能体接管；任务完成后，独立的"完成门"智能体再做一轮结构验证，确保结果真的被保存到正确位置。为了防止长任务中AI"记性变差"，主智能体把大任务分解为子目标，交给全新的子智能体处理，保持每个智能体的上下文简洁清晰。 Instead of having the AI stare at screenshots and guess what's happening inside a program, StateAct lets the AI directly use code to touch the program's internals — reading files, querying databases, inspecting web page structure. Three specialized roles work together: the main agent handles most tasks through code; a GUI sub-agent is called in only when clicking on-screen is unavoidable; and a "finish gate" agent independently checks that the final result is actually saved correctly. To prevent "memory overload" on long tasks, the main agent breaks big jobs into sub-goals and hands each one to a fresh sub-agent, so no single agent has to juggle hundreds of steps at once.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项研究意味着AI助手在帮助用户操作电脑时可以更准确、更便宜，未来有望大幅降低部署成本并提升可靠性。将AI的失败从"看不清楚"转向"推理不足"，也为整个领域指明了下一步攻关方向：改进AI的推理能力，而非单纯堆砌更好的视觉模型。 This research means AI computer assistants could become far more reliable and 9x cheaper to run — a big deal for businesses deploying AI automation at scale. By re-framing the key challenge from "better vision" to "better reasoning," it gives the entire field a clearer roadmap for where to focus next.
