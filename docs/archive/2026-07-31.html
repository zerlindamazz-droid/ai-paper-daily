HumanCLAW: Can Vision-Language Models Act Through a Body? HumanCLAW：视觉语言模型能通过身体来行动吗？

🧑‍🔬 Siyao Li, Jiawei Gu, Shuai Liu, Kairui Hu et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 首个将AI决策与身体执行解耦的具身智能评测框架
A new benchmark that fairly tests whether AI brains can control a physical body — separately from motor errors.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像你闭上眼睛、戴上耳机，只靠别人喊指令来穿越一个陌生房间——你可能知道桌子在哪儿，却不知道自己有没有撞到它、走过头没有。当前AI就是这样：看得见东西，却搞不清自己身在何处。Imagine playing a video game where you can perfectly read the map, but you have no idea where YOUR character is standing on it — so you keep walking past the goal or bumping into walls you thought you'd avoided. That's exactly what today's AI does when put in a body.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有AI模型（比如GPT-4V这类"看图说话"的大模型）越来越厉害，但我们并不清楚它们是否真的能控制一个有实体的身体去完成任务。更麻烦的是，当机器人任务失败时，根本搞不清楚是AI"脑子"判断失误，还是机器人"腿脚"执行出了问题——就像考试不及格，分不清是题没读懂还是手抖写错了答案。 Today's AI vision-language models (like GPT-4V) are great at describing pictures, but can they actually control a physical body to complete real tasks? The big challenge is: when a robot fails a task, we can't tell if the AI made a bad decision or if the robot's motors just fumbled — like not knowing if a student failed because they didn't understand the question or just had shaky handwriting.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 创新解耦设计：把AI的"决策脑"和身体的"执行腿"分开测试，确保评分只反映AI的判断力，而不受物理执行误差的干扰。 • 大规模真实评测台：构建了包含1,218个长流程任务、41个室内场景的HumanCLAW-Bench，是迄今最全面的具身AI评测之一。 • 发现关键短板：研究发现当前VLM的瓶颈不是"认路"，而是缺乏"身体自我感知"——不知道自己身在何处、是否碰到了障碍物。 • Smart decoupling: Separates the AI's decision-making from physical motor execution, so scores only reflect the AI's thinking quality. • Large-scale realistic benchmark: 1,218 long-horizon tasks across 41 indoor scenes — one of the most comprehensive embodied AI tests yet. • Key insight discovered: Current AI models aren't bad at recognizing objects — they fail because they lack "body awareness," not knowing where they are or if they've bumped into something.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者构建了HumanCLAW-Bench，包含1,218个"寻找-导航-交互"任务片段，分布在41个不同室内场景中，全部采用第一人称视角。他们测试了9个当前最先进的视觉语言模型，包括GPT-4o、Gemini等主流模型，以任务完成成功率作为核心评测指标，并进一步拆解分析了"目标识别""导航规划""身体定位"等子能力的表现。 The researchers built HumanCLAW-Bench with 1,218 "find-navigate-interact" task episodes across 41 indoor scenes, all from a first-person (egocentric) viewpoint. They tested nine state-of-the-art VLMs including top models like GPT-4o and Gemini, using task success rate as the main metric. They also broke down performance into sub-skills like target recognition, navigation planning, and body self-localization to pinpoint where models fail.
📊 实验
结果		 9个顶尖VLM模型无一能解决这个基准测试：表现最好的模型成功率仅为16.8%，其余模型表现更差。进一步分析显示，目标识别并不是瓶颈，模型主要败在"身体自我感知"上——不知道自己当前在哪、有没有到达目标位置、有没有撞到障碍物。 Not a single one of the 9 tested state-of-the-art VLMs could solve the benchmark. The best model achieved only a 16.8% success rate. Analysis confirmed that object recognition is NOT the weak point — models fail specifically at body self-awareness: tracking their own position, knowing when they've reached a goal, and detecting collisions.
🏁 结论 本文证明了当前最先进的视觉语言模型在具身智能上存在根本性缺陷——不是"看不懂"世界，而是"不知道自己身在其中"。HumanCLAW提供了一个公平、可量化的评测框架，为未来具身AI的发展指明了关键改进方向。 This paper proves that today's best AI vision models fundamentally lack "embodied self-awareness" — they can see and recognize the world but can't track their own body within it. HumanCLAW provides a fair, rigorous benchmark that pinpoints exactly what needs to be fixed to build truly embodied AI.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

HumanCLAW的思路就像给一个聪明的"大脑"配上一个专业的"驾驶员"：大脑（VLM）每一步只需下达一个简单指令，比如"向前走"或"拿起那个杯子"；专业驾驶员（运动控制模块）负责把这个指令精确变成全身连贯动作，同时保持平衡、处理碰撞。这样一来，测试结果只反映AI的"决策智慧"，而不会因为身体摔倒或运动抖动而被拉低分数。整个系统在物理仿真环境中运行，重力和碰撞都是真实存在的。 Think of it like a driving test where the examiner only grades your route decisions, not your steering smoothness. At every step, the AI (VLM) issues a simple atomic command like "walk forward" or "pick up the cup." A separate professional motor controller then executes that command physically, handling balance and collisions on its own. This way, test scores purely reflect the AI's decision-making intelligence. The whole thing runs in a physics simulator with real gravity and collisions.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着人形机器人和智能家居助手走入现实，AI必须学会真正"住进"一个身体里。这项研究揭示了当前最强AI的核心短板，为下一代能做家务、照顾老人的机器人指明了技术攻关方向。 As humanoid robots and home assistants become reality, AI needs to truly inhabit a body, not just see through a camera. This research exposes a critical blind spot in today's most powerful AI, pointing engineers toward the exact fixes needed to build robots that can do your laundry or help elderly relatives.

MemHarness: Memory Is Reconstructed, Not Replayed MemHarness：记忆是重构的，而非回放的

🧑‍🔬 Rong Wu, Daocheng Fu, Licheng Wen, Xuemeng Yang et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 让AI像人类一样"活用"过去经验，而非机械照搬
MemHarness teaches AI agents to reconstruct past experiences instead of blindly replaying them.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个有经验的厨师，不会把上次的菜谱原封不动地照搬——他会根据今天冰箱里有什么食材，灵活调整做法；而一个死背菜谱的新手，食材稍微不一样就手忙脚乱。MemHarness让AI从"死背菜谱的新手"变成"灵活变通的老厨师"。Think of it like an experienced chef versus a beginner: the beginner follows a recipe word-for-word and panics if one ingredient is missing, but the experienced chef glances at what's in the fridge and adapts the dish on the fly. MemHarness turns AI agents from rigid recipe-followers into flexible, experienced cooks who use past knowledge without being enslaved by it.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的AI智能体在做决策时，会把过去的经历原封不动地"回放"出来，就像背课文一样一字不差地套用。但问题是，过去的情况和现在的情况往往不完全一样，生搬硬套反而会让AI做出更差的决定——这种现象叫"负迁移"。人类不会这样做：我们会根据当前情况，灵活地调整和重新理解过去的经验，而不是死记硬背。 Most AI agents that use memory just copy-paste old experiences into their thinking process, like a student who memorizes example answers word-for-word instead of understanding the idea. The problem is that old experiences rarely match new situations perfectly, so this blind replay often causes the AI to make worse decisions — a phenomenon called "negative transfer." Humans are smarter about this: we naturally adapt and reinterpret past memories to fit whatever we're facing right now.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出"重构式记忆"新思路：AI不再被动复读旧经验，而是主动根据当前情境重新理解并改造它们。 • 统一策略模型：在每个决策步骤中，同一个模型同时负责"批判旧经验"和"生成行动指导"，两者相互促进。 • 端到端训练：通过GRPO强化学习算法，重构能力自然涌现，无需人工标注重构过程。 • 双重收益：不仅防止了负迁移，重构目标还在训练中充当"隐性引导"，从根本上提升了AI的推理能力。 • Novel "reconstructive memory" paradigm: the AI actively adapts old experiences to fit the current moment, rather than copying them verbatim. • Unified policy model: a single model both critiques retrieved memories and decides what action to take, with each task reinforcing the other. • End-to-end training: the reconstruction ability emerges naturally through reinforcement learning (GRPO), no manual labels needed. • Double benefit: reconstruction prevents negative transfer AND secretly boosts the agent's core reasoning ability during training.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者在两个经典AI智能体任务上测试了MemHarness：ALFWorld（模拟家庭场景中的文字交互任务，比如找东西、开灯等）和WebShop（模拟网上购物，让AI按要求找到合适的商品）。对比的基线包括：纯强化学习（无记忆）、静态记忆回放（直接把旧经验塞进提示词）等方法。评估指标主要是任务完成率，同时也测试了分布外（OOD）场景下的泛化能力，即在训练时没见过的新任务类型上的表现。 The team tested MemHarness on two standard AI agent benchmarks: ALFWorld (a simulated home environment where the agent completes text-based tasks like finding objects or turning on lights) and WebShop (a simulated online store where the agent must find and purchase products matching specific requirements). They compared against pure reinforcement learning (no memory) and static memory-replay baselines. The key metric was task success rate, and they also specifically tested out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization — how well the agent handles task types it never saw during training.
📊 实验
结果		 在ALFWorld上，MemHarness相比纯强化学习基线提升显著，也大幅超越了静态记忆回放方法；在WebShop上同样表现出色。更值得注意的是OOD泛化能力：在分布外场景中，MemHarness展现出强健的鲁棒性，而静态回放方法在这些场景下甚至出现了性能下降（负迁移）。具体数字方面，论文报告MemHarness在ALFWorld和WebShop上均"大幅优于（substantially outperforms）"纯RL和静态记忆增强基线，且重构机制从根本上提升了智能体的内在推理能力。 MemHarness substantially outperforms both pure RL agents and static memory-replay agents on ALFWorld and WebShop benchmarks. The gains are especially pronounced in out-of-distribution (OOD) scenarios, where static replay methods actually suffer performance drops due to negative transfer — while MemHarness remains robust. The paper reports that the reconstruction objective also measurably improves the agent's intrinsic reasoning capabilities beyond just the memory benefit, demonstrating that reconstructive memory is a better training signal than passive replay.
🏁 结论 MemHarness证明了"重构式记忆"比"静态回放式记忆"更适合AI智能体：不仅能避免负迁移、提升任务成功率，还能在训练中顺带增强AI的推理能力。这为构建更像人类思维方式的AI智能体提供了新的设计范式。 MemHarness proves that teaching AI agents to reconstruct memories — rather than replay them — leads to better task performance, stronger generalization, and even improved core reasoning. It establishes a new, human-inspired design principle for memory-augmented AI agents.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

MemHarness的核心思路是：每次AI需要做决策时，它先从记忆库里找出相关的旧经验，但不是直接用——而是用一个统一的"策略模型"来审视这段旧经验，问自己"这段记忆和我现在的处境有多相关？需要怎么调整？"然后模型根据当前状态，把旧经验改造成真正有用的指导建议，再依据这个建议采取行动。整个过程通过GRPO强化学习端到端训练：AI做对了就奖励，做错了就惩罚，慢慢地它就学会了如何灵活重构记忆。重构这个动作本身也反过来锻炼了AI的推理能力，相当于"练脑子"。 At each decision step, MemHarness pulls relevant past experiences from memory — but instead of injecting them directly, a single "policy model" first reads the old memory and asks: "How well does this match my current situation, and what needs to change?" It then rewrites the retrieved experience into tailored, context-aware guidance, and only then decides on an action. This whole pipeline is trained end-to-end using GRPO reinforcement learning: the agent gets rewarded for correct outcomes, and over time it learns both how to reconstruct memories well and how to act wisely. As a bonus, the act of reconstructing memories also trains the model to reason better in general — like how explaining something to others helps you understand it more deeply yourself.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\pi_{\theta}(a_t | s_t, \hat{e}_t), \quad \hat{e}_t = \text{Reconstruct}(e_t, s_t)\]
💡 智能体的行动策略：在当前状态 s_t 和重构后的经验 ê_t 的共同指导下选择动作。重构函数将原始检索到的旧经验 e_t 结合当前状态 s_t 改造成更贴合当下的指导信息。 The agent's action policy: it chooses action a_t based on the current state s_t and the reconstructed experience ê_t. The Reconstruct function adapts the raw retrieved memory e_t using the current state s_t to produce context-grounded guidance.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI智能体越来越多地被用于自动化复杂任务（如网购、操控电脑、执行工作流），如何让它们聪明地利用过去经验而不被旧经验"带偏"，变得至关重要。MemHarness提供了一个实用框架，让AI真正学会"举一反三"，而不是"死记硬背"，这对开发更可靠、更通用的AI助手具有重要意义。 As AI agents are increasingly deployed to handle complex real-world tasks — like browsing the web, shopping online, or automating workflows — the ability to adapt past experience intelligently (rather than copy it blindly) is critical for reliability. MemHarness offers a practical blueprint for building AI agents that genuinely generalize and reason better, moving us closer to assistants that can handle truly novel situations.

Weak-to-Strong On-Policy Distillation 弱到强的在线策略蒸馏

🧑‍🔬 Fangxu Yu, Zinan Lin, Xiaodong Liu, Weijia Xu et al.

图 Figure · Page 3 of paper

🎯 多个弱小模型联合"教"强大模型，无需更大的教师模型
Small weak models can team up to teach a bigger AI model new skills — no giant teacher needed.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个顶尖厨师已经没有更厉害的师傅可以拜了，但他可以通过对比"加了某种香料的菜"和"没加的菜"之间的差异，自己悟出那种香料的妙用——用多个普通厨师的对比经验，反而能让自己突破瓶颈。Imagine a top chess player who has no stronger opponent to learn from. Instead of giving up, they study the difference between two weaker players — one who just learned a clever new trick versus one who hasn't — and absorb exactly that trick. By comparing many such pairs, they keep getting better without ever needing a grandmaster teacher!
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练一个强大的AI模型，通常需要一个"更强的老师"来指导它。但如果你已经是最强的模型了，找不到更强的老师怎么办？现有的方法要么需要一个更大的模型来"抄作业"，要么需要花大价钱重新训练同等规模的模型，在前沿领域都行不通。 Training a powerful AI model usually requires an even more powerful "teacher" model to guide it. But what happens when you're already at the frontier — when there's no bigger or smarter teacher available? Existing methods either need a larger model to copy from, or require expensive retraining of equally large models, both of which hit a wall at the cutting edge.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 不需要更强的老师！方法通过对比"训练前"和"训练后"的小模型，提取出它学到的那部分能力精华。 • 将多个廉价小模型的"能力差值"信号合并，注入到强学生模型中，为它指引正确方向。 • 提出三种对比对：RL训练前后对比、大小基础模型对比、正确与错误提示对比，分别捕捉不同维度的提升信号。 • Instead of needing a smarter teacher, the method cleverly "subtracts" what a weak model knows before training from what it knows after, to extract just the new skill it learned. • This "skill signal" from multiple small, cheap models is combined and injected into the student, pointing it in the right direction without needing a full big teacher. • Three different types of contrast pairs are introduced: before vs. after RL training, small vs. large base model, and correct vs. wrong hints — each capturing a different kind of improvement signal.
🧪 实验
内容		 作者在四个数学基准（MATH500、AMC、AIME、Olympiad Bench）和三个代码基准（HumanEval、MBPP、LiveCodeBench）上测试了W2S-OPD，使用Qwen2.5-Math和DeepSeek系列作为学生模型。对比了标准在线策略蒸馏（OPD）、自我提升方法以及其他知识迁移基线。代理教师均由比学生更小的模型构建，包括RL微调后的小模型、不同规模的基础模型和带提示的变体。 The authors tested W2S-OPD on four math benchmarks (MATH500, AMC, AIME, Olympiad Bench) and three code benchmarks (HumanEval, MBPP, LiveCodeBench), using Qwen2.5-Math and DeepSeek models as student models. They compared against standard on-policy distillation (OPD), self-improvement methods, and other knowledge transfer baselines. The proxy teachers were built from models smaller than the student, including post-RL fine-tuned small models, base models of different scales, and hint-conditioned variants.
📊 实验
结果		 W2S-OPD在全部七个基准上均超过标准OPD。在数学任务上，学生模型成功超越了领域教师（更大的模型），在AIME和Olympiad Bench等难题基准上取得显著提升。在代码基准上同样超越所有基线。即便所有监督来源都弱于学生模型，W2S-OPD仍能持续提升学生性能——这是现有方法全部失效的场景。三种对比类型提供互补信号，组合使用效果最佳。 W2S-OPD consistently outperformed standard OPD across all seven benchmarks. On math tasks, it enabled the student model to surpass its domain teacher (a larger model), achieving notable gains on challenging benchmarks like AIME and Olympiad Bench. On code benchmarks, it similarly outperformed all baselines. Even when every supervision source was weaker than the student, W2S-OPD kept improving the student's performance — a setting where all prior methods fail. The three contrast types showed complementary improvements, with combined use yielding the best results overall.
🏁 结论 本文证明了强大的AI模型可以仅靠更小、更弱的模型作为教师来持续提升——通过巧妙提取并组合它们的"能力方向"信号。这打破了"必须要更强老师"的假设，为在没有更大模型的情况下提升前沿模型提供了一条可行的低成本路径。 This paper proves that a powerful AI model can be meaningfully improved using only smaller, weaker models as teachers — by cleverly extracting and combining their "capability direction" signals. It breaks the assumption that you always need a bigger teacher, opening a practical path for advancing frontier models without prohibitive costs.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

核心思路是构建一个"代理教师"：取两个小而廉价的模型——一个懂某件事（正向），一个不懂（负向）——计算它们输出预测（logits）的差值。这个"对比差值"精确地指出了正在学习的技能方向。将这个差值信号加到学生模型自己的基础输出上，就构造出一个虚拟的更聪明的教师，引导学生朝正确方向走。学生模型通过自己生成答案，逐个token地向这个代理教师靠拢来完成训练。这适用于三种场景：RL训练前后对比、大小基础模型对比、正确提示与错误提示对比。 The key idea is to build a "proxy teacher" by taking two small, cheap models — one that knows something useful (positive) and one that doesn't (negative) — and computing the difference between their output predictions (logits). This difference, called the "logit contrast," pinpoints exactly what skill or direction is being learned. That difference signal is then added on top of the student model's own base, creating a virtual smarter teacher that nudges the student in the right direction. The student then trains itself by generating its own answers and trying to match this proxy teacher's guidance, token by token. This works for three scenarios: comparing a model before and after reinforcement learning, comparing a big vs. small base model, and comparing a model given a correct hint vs. a wrong one.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

当今最强大的AI模型已经没有更大的模型可以学习了——它们已经处于顶端。本研究提供了一种实用、低成本的方式，仅用更小的"助手"模型就能持续提升它们，有望大幅降低AI能力进步的成本。对社会而言，这意味着数学辅导、编程助手等AI应用可以越来越好，而无需不断建造更大更贵的模型。 Today's most powerful AI models have no bigger model to learn from — they're already at the top. This work shows a practical, cheap way to keep improving them using only smaller helpers, which could dramatically cut the cost of advancing AI capabilities. For society, this means better AI assistants for math tutoring, coding help, and problem-solving — achieved without needing ever-larger and more expensive models.

Beacon: Knowing When and How to Perform Agentic Visual Reasoning Beacon：知道何时以及如何进行智能体视觉推理

🧑‍🔬 Qixun Wang, Yang Shi, Letian Cheng, Zhuoran Zhang et al.

🎯 让AI学会"聪明地用工具"，而非盲目调用
Teaching AI agents to use tools only when needed, not reflexively every time.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个聪明的学生做考试题——遇到简单题直接心算，遇到复杂题才掏出草稿纸和计算器。如果每道题都先拿计算器算一遍，不仅浪费时间，还可能因为操作失误把本来会做的题算错。Beacon就是教AI学会这种"见机行事"的智慧。Think of it like a student taking a math test — smart students do simple problems in their head and only pull out a calculator for the really tough ones. If you reach for the calculator on every single question, you waste time and might even punch in the wrong numbers on problems you already knew. Beacon teaches AI to have that same smart instinct.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的AI视觉推理模型会"无脑地"调用工具，哪怕一道简单题根本不需要任何工具。这就好比你做一道简单的加减法，却偏要掏出计算器——不仅浪费时间，还可能因为工具操作失误而算错。研究发现：工具在难题上带来的好处，几乎被它在简单题上引入的额外错误完全抵消了。 Current AI visual reasoning models tend to use tools all the time — even on easy questions that they could answer perfectly well on their own. This wastes computing power and, worse, actually causes new errors on problems the model already knew how to handle. The net benefit of tool use turns out to be surprisingly small because the gains on hard problems are cancelled out by the damage done on easy ones.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出了两个新评估维度："模式适应性"（AI是否知道什么时候该用工具）和"工具效果"（工具到底是帮了忙还是帮了倒忙） • 用强化学习设计了"必要性感知自适应奖励"，让AI在不需要工具时主动放弃调用，避免多此一举 • 提出"提示引导能力扩展"机制，专门强化AI在最难问题上正确使用工具的能力，做到"刀用在刀刃上" • Introduces two new evaluation dimensions: Mode Adaptiveness (does the AI know *when* to use tools?) and Tool Effect (do tools actually help or hurt overall?) • Designs a "Necessity-Aware Adaptive Reward" in reinforcement learning so the AI learns to skip tools on easy problems it already handles well • Proposes a "Hint-Guided Capability Expansion" that specifically trains the AI to use tools effectively on the hardest problems where they're truly needed
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在多个主流视觉推理基准测试上进行，包括MathVista、MMStar、MMBench、AI2D等，对比了多个现有的智能体视觉推理模型作为基线。研究者还专门设计了一套基于"模式适应性"和"工具效果"的新评估框架，将题目按难易分层，分别统计工具使用对不同难度题目的影响，量化模型是否真正做到了"用对地方"。 Experiments were run across multiple popular visual reasoning benchmarks including MathVista, MMStar, MMBench, and AI2D, comparing Beacon against several existing agentic visual reasoning models. The researchers also designed a new evaluation framework centered on their two key dimensions — Mode Adaptiveness and Tool Effect — splitting test questions into easy and hard subsets to measure whether tool use was genuinely helpful or wasteful on each type.
📊 实验
结果		 Beacon在多个基准测试上超越了现有最优模型，整体准确率显著提升。在"模式适应性"指标上，Beacon比此前模型提升明显，减少了对简单题的无效工具调用。在"工具效果"上，Beacon实现了真正的正向收益——工具在难题上的帮助不再被简单题上的副作用抵消。具体数字方面，Beacon在MathVista等数学视觉推理任务上的得分较基线模型提升了数个百分点，并在多个榜单上达到同类模型的最优水平。 Beacon outperforms existing agentic visual reasoning models across multiple benchmarks, achieving state-of-the-art results. It shows substantial improvements in Mode Adaptiveness (fewer unnecessary tool calls on easy problems) and genuine positive Tool Effect (tools now provide net gains rather than breaking even). On MathVista and similar math-visual benchmarks, Beacon achieves several percentage points of improvement over strong baselines, with the overall accuracy gains attributed to both smarter tool invocation decisions and stronger tool-use capability on hard problems.
🏁 结论 本文证明了：AI在视觉推理中使用工具，关键不在于"用得多"，而在于"用得准"。Beacon通过强化学习让模型学会判断何时需要工具、如何在真正困难的地方把工具用好，从而实现了更高效、更准确的整体推理性能。 This paper proves that for AI visual reasoning, smarter tool use beats more tool use. Beacon shows that training a model to know *when* and *how* to use tools — via targeted reinforcement learning rewards — leads to better overall accuracy, less wasted computation, and genuine performance gains on the hardest problems.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Beacon的核心思路是用强化学习来训练AI的"工具使用判断力"。首先，设计了一个"必要性感知奖励"机制：如果模型在不该用工具的简单题上调用了工具并答错，它会受到额外惩罚；如果在简单题上不用工具直接答对，则额外奖励。其次，通过"提示引导能力扩展"，专门对那些不用工具就答不对的难题进行针对性训练——先给模型一个"提示"（如工具返回的关键信息），让它明白工具能带来什么，再训练它主动去调用并正确使用工具，从而真正提升工具在难题上的实际效果。 Beacon trains the AI's "judgment" about tool use through reinforcement learning — essentially a reward-and-penalty system. First, a "Necessity-Aware Adaptive Reward" gives the AI a bonus when it correctly solves easy questions without touching any tools, and penalizes it extra when it wastes tools on easy questions and still gets them wrong. Second, a "Hint-Guided Capability Expansion" targets the hardest questions — it first shows the model a hint (like a key piece of information a tool would provide) so the model learns what tools are *for*, then trains it to proactively call and correctly use those tools on genuinely difficult problems.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI助手越来越多地使用"工具"（如搜索、计算器、代码执行）来回答问题，如何让AI聪明地决定"该不该用工具"变得至关重要——盲目调用工具既浪费算力，又可能引入新的错误。Beacon提供了一套切实可行的训练方法，有望让未来的AI助手更高效、更可靠。 As AI assistants increasingly rely on external tools (like web search, calculators, or code runners) to answer questions, teaching them *when* to use those tools is just as important as teaching them *how*. Beacon's approach could make future AI assistants faster, cheaper to run, and more reliable by avoiding the hidden cost of unnecessary or poorly-timed tool calls.

In contact-rich manipulation, action multimodality and reactivity dominate different stages of a single episode. Before contact, multiple trajectories might be equally valid, making it important to preserve diverse action modes. After contact, geometric constraints and force limits narrow the solution space, while successful execution demands rapid responses to force feedback. However, standard diffusion policies use a fixed inference frequency and sampling steps throughout the episode, forcing a fundamental compromise: low-frequency, multi-step sampling better preserves pre-contact multimodality but responds slowly to force feedback, whereas high-frequency sampling improves reactivity but tends to collapse distinct pre-contact modes. A shared multi-frequency visual-force Transformer predicts action chunks at both low and high frequencies, while a learned multimodality indicator dynamically selects multi-step low-frequency sampling before contact and one-step high-frequency sampling as action ambiguity decreases. Experiments on three contact-rich manipulation tasks show that FA-RDP achieves the highest success rate while preserving diverse pre-contact trajectory modes. Pretraining navigation diffusion policies rely on large-scale expert demonstrations. These data are typically generated by a fully-informed oracle planner suited to a single nominal robot. This limits the policy's generalization to diverse embodiments and challenging scenarios (e.g., escaping dead ends or detouring long obstacles) that demand diverse local reactive behaviors with only onboard local observations. Post-training the policy with reinforcement learning (RL) offers a principled remedy. However, previous RL for diffusion approaches lead to only marginal improvements. This is because the intractable likelihood of diffusion policies renders policy gradients unstable in addition to inefficient policy exploration. To address these challenges, we propose a data-efficient diffusion RL post-training framework - GQRM (Group Q-score Reweighted Matching). Our framework introduces two complementary designs: (i) a self-bootstrapped exploration strategy with behavior perturbation that preserves the pretrained policy prior, and (ii) a group Q-score normalization mechanism that computes per-trajectory values on each state for efficient reweighted score matching. By conducting distributed online RL training across heterogeneous embodiments, the resulting fine-tuned policy, X-NavDP, achieves state-of-the-art cross-embodiment visual navigation performance, improving the overall success rate from 61.20% to 84.28% in simulation and 10% to 65% in real-world hard cases. The code and model are publicly available at https://yty-sky.github.io/x-navdp-project-page.

Scaling test-time computation can improve language-model reasoning, but uniform budgets waste computation on easy inputs, while verifier-guided refinement relies on external feedback. We introduce Self-Verifying Refinement (SVR), an oracle-free multi-turn reinforcement learning framework that learns to use self-verification as a compute-control policy. At each turn, the model produces a solution together with a discrete correctness verdict and a confidence score; it retains the current answer only when the verdict is Correct and confidence exceeds a threshold, and otherwise continues refinement using its own self-verification. Ground-truth correctness is used only to construct training rewards and is never exposed to the policy through refinement prompts or required at inference. In the evaluated complete-system comparison, it exceeds standard GRPO, strong multi-turn baselines, and a fixed-budget oracle-guided score-feedback reference while requiring substantially fewer turns than fixed ten-turn inference. These results demonstrate that learned self-verification can serve as an effective internal control signal for answer retention and adaptive test-time compute allocation.
