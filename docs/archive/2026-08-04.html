CompanionBench: A Theory-Anchored, Real-World-Grounded Benchmark for AI Emotional Companionship CompanionBench：基于心理学理论与真实数据的AI情感陪伴评测基准

🧑‍🔬 Yao Liu, Guangjia Chai, Yuming Huang, Jihao Huang et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 首个用真实数据+心理学理论系统评测AI情感陪伴能力的双语基准
The first bilingual benchmark grounded in real data and psychology theory to rigorously evaluate AI emotional companions.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像给厨师考级，不能只问"你会做菜吗"，而要分别测试刀工、火候、摆盘……CompanionBench就是给AI情感陪伴做的"专业厨师考级"，不只看它说话甜不甜，而是逐项测试它到底懂不懂你的心。Think of it like a driving test — instead of just asking "can you drive?" it checks parallel parking, highway merging, and emergency braking separately. CompanionBench does the same for emotional AI: instead of one vague "empathy score," it tests each specific relationship skill one by one, using real roads (real data) instead of a fake parking lot.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现在有很多AI"情感陪伴"产品，比如能聊天解压、倾听烦恼的聊天机器人，但我们根本不知道它们做得好不好。现有的测试方法要么是人工编写脚本、场景太假，要么把所有"共情能力"压缩成一个分数，完全看不出哪里强、哪里弱。更糟的是，让AI评判AI时，同一家公司的模型会互相偏袒，评分也容易漂移——就像让同班同学互相打分，根本不公平。 There are now many AI "emotional companion" apps — chatbots that listen to your problems and offer support. But we have no reliable way to tell if they're actually good at it. Existing tests use fake, hand-written scripts, squish all emotional skills into one single score, and are biased — AI judges from the same company tend to secretly favor each other, like a teacher grading their own students. This means we can't tell a truly caring AI from one that just sounds warm on the surface.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次将真实去标识化用户对话数据用于构建场景和训练用户模拟器，让测试更贴近真实生活 • 将10项能力（源自25个心理学与咨询理论）独立评分，其中4项是业界首次提出，包括"容纳模糊性""正向共鸣"等 • 设计了"隐性披露门"机制：AI的行为会决定用户是否愿意说出更深层的心事，而非按剧本走 • 引入跨家族评审团（不同公司的AI做评委）+ IRT模型，消除评委偏袒和评分漂移问题 • 中英双语测试结果高度一致（排名相关系数0.996/0.953），可跨语言复现 • First benchmark to train its user simulator on real, anonymized conversation data — not made-up scripts • Breaks emotional ability into 10 separate skills drawn from 25 psychology theories, with 4 skills never graded before (e.g., "holding ambiguity," "positive resonance") • Uses a "hidden disclosure gate" — the AI's own behavior determines whether the simulated user opens up deeper, making interactions dynamic rather than scripted • Employs a cross-company AI judge panel + a statistical model (IRT) to cancel out favoritism and score drift • Rankings are consistent across Chinese and English (correlation 0.996 / 0.953), proving cross-language reliability
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者评测了28个AI智能体，涵盖通用大语言模型（如GPT、Claude、Gemini等系列）和专门做角色扮演/情感陪伴的产品。测试使用500对中英平行对话场景，分别在中文和英文下运行完整评测流程。评估维度包括：10项能力的主观打分量表，以及"是否赢得了更深层披露"这一客观行为指标。评委由来自不同模型家族的AI组成跨家族评审团，同时用IRT模型校正评委严苛度差异。 The team evaluated 28 AI agents, including major general-purpose LLMs (GPT, Claude, Gemini families) and specialized role-play / emotional companion products. Testing used 500 Chinese-English parallel scenario pairs, run in both languages. Each agent was scored on two axes: a 10-capability subjective rubric and a deterministic check of whether it "earned" deeper user disclosure. Judges were a cross-company AI panel, with IRT statistical correction applied to account for each judge's individual strictness bias.
📊 实验
结果		 • 中英文排名高度一致：Spearman相关系数中文0.996、英文0.953，说明基准跨语言可复现性极强 • 评测28个AI后发现：角色扮演类智能体排名垫底，证明"沉浸感"≠"关系能力" • 情绪调节和"校准式挑战"是最普遍的短板（几乎所有AI都弱） • "容纳模糊性"是区分度最高的能力项——能最有效地拉开不同AI之间的差距 • 所有AI最主要的失败模式：用表面的温暖话语代替真正的关系支持（听起来暖，实则空洞） • Rankings are highly reproducible across languages: Spearman correlation of 0.996 (Chinese) and 0.953 (English) • Among 28 agents evaluated, role-play-focused agents ranked near the bottom — immersion does not equal relational skill • Emotion regulation and "calibrated challenge" were the most common weaknesses across nearly all AI agents • "Holding ambiguity" was the single most discriminating capability — best at separating strong from weak agents • The dominant failure mode across all agents: substituting surface warmth (friendly-sounding words) for genuinely substantive emotional support
🏁 结论 本文证明，通过结合真实数据与心理学理论，可以构建出公平、可复现、细粒度的AI情感陪伴评测基准。研究发现当前所有主流AI最大的通病是"表面热情、实质空洞"，而角色扮演类产品的情感支持能力甚至弱于通用大模型。 This paper proves that combining real-world conversation data with established psychology theory enables a fair, reproducible, and fine-grained benchmark for AI emotional companions. It reveals that today's AI companions largely fail at genuine relational support, hiding behind surface warmth — and that role-play agents are actually worse at emotional care than general-purpose models.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究者先从真实（脱敏）用户对话中提取真实场景、用户背景和情绪历史，用这些数据训练一个"用户模拟器"——它会像真人一样跟AI聊天，而不是走固定剧本。在对话中设置了一个"隐性披露门"：如果AI表现得足够好（有回应、不说教），模拟用户才会透露更深的烦恼；否则对话就停留在表面。每段对话结束后，用来自不同公司的多个AI当评委，对10项能力分别打分，再用IRT统计模型修正每个评委自身的严格程度差异，最终得到公平可靠的综合评分。 The researchers first collected real (anonymized) user conversations and used them to build both realistic test scenarios and a "user simulator" — an AI that chats like a real person instead of following a fixed script. During each conversation, a "hidden disclosure gate" is activated: the simulator will only share deeper, more personal feelings if the companion AI responds well; otherwise the conversation stays shallow. After each chat, a panel of AI judges from different companies scores the companion on 10 separate emotional skills. A statistical technique (Item Response Theory) then corrects for each judge's own strictness level, ensuring fair and reproducible scores.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\rho_{ZH} = 0.996, \quad \rho_{EN} = 0.953\]

\[P(X_{ij}=1 \mid \theta_j, b_i) = \frac{1}{1+e^{-(\theta_j - b_i)}}\]
💡 项目反应理论（IRT）模型公式：θ_j代表第j个评委的严苛度，b_i代表第i个评分项的难度，用于校正不同AI评委之间的打分偏差，让评分更公平 The Item Response Theory (IRT) model used to correct for judge severity differences — θ_j is how strict judge j is, b_i is how hard item i is to score highly; this makes scores fair across different AI judges

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着越来越多的人把AI当作情感支持的来源，一个严格、公平的评测标准至关重要——它能帮助开发者找出真正的短板，避免用户在脆弱时刻得到看似温暖却毫无实质帮助的回应。CompanionBench为整个行业提供了第一把真正靠谱的"情感AI体检工具"。 As millions of people turn to AI for emotional support, having a rigorous and fair evaluation tool is critical — it helps developers pinpoint real weaknesses and prevent users in vulnerable moments from receiving hollow, surface-level responses. CompanionBench gives the entire industry its first truly reliable "emotional AI health check."

WorldExam: Benchmarking World Models from Apparent Appearance to Inherent Reactivity WorldExam：从表观外貌到内在反应性的世界模型基准测试

🧑‍🔬 Yuxue Yang, Shuyao Shang, Jiahe Wang, Zitong Zhou et al.

图 Figure · Page 1 of paper

🎯 首个测试AI视频模型是否真正理解世界反应逻辑的分层基准
WorldExam is the first benchmark that tests whether AI video models truly understand how the world reacts, not just how it looks.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像考驾照——普通科目二只考停车和按标志行驶（视觉质量+指令遵从），但WorldExam就像额外加了一道题："如果突然有小孩冲出来，你知道要踩刹车吗？"这就是世界反应性——理解没人明确告诉你、但理应发生的后果。Think of it like testing a new student driver — most driving tests only check if they can park and follow traffic signs (visual quality + instruction following). But WorldExam is like a test that also checks: "If a child runs into the road, do you know to brake?" That's world reactivity — understanding consequences that nobody explicitly told you about.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 AI视频生成模型越来越多地被称为"世界模型"，但现有的评测只会检查视频画面好不好看，或者有没有按指令做动作——就像只检查演员台词说没说对，却不管剧情是否合理。没有人测试这些模型是否真正理解世界对事件和动作的反应逻辑。这个漏洞意味着我们根本分不清一个模型是真正在模拟现实，还是只是在生成好看的画面。 AI video generation models are increasingly being called "world models," but existing tests only check if the video looks nice or follows explicit instructions — like checking if a movie actor said their lines, but ignoring whether the plot made any sense. There's no good way to test whether these models truly understand how the world *reacts* to actions and events. This gap means we can't tell whether a model is genuinely simulating reality or just producing pretty pixels.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次评估"世界反应性"——AI模型能否根据场景状态推断出接下来应该发生什么，而不只是执行明确的指令。 • 分层四级设计：视觉质量→控制遵从→空间一致性→世界反应性，从表面到深层逐级递进。 • 涵盖1474个测试案例，跨8项任务，对20个主流模型的三种控制范式（相机驱动、动作驱动、语言驱动）进行统一评测。 • 揭示了一个重要发现：画面好看、能遵从指令，并不代表模型真正理解世界运作规律。 • First benchmark to evaluate "world reactivity" — whether an AI model can infer what should happen next based on scene context, beyond what was explicitly asked. • Hierarchical 4-level design: Visual Quality → Control Adherence → Spatial Consistency → World Reactivity, going from surface to deep understanding. • Tests 20 real-world models across three different control paradigms (camera-driven, action-driven, language-driven) with 1,474 standardized cases across 8 tasks. • Reveals a surprising finding: looking good and following instructions does NOT mean a model understands how the world works.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究人员评测了20个代表性视频生成模型，涵盖三种范式：相机驱动（通过相机轨迹控制）、动作驱动（通过智能体动作控制）、语言驱动（通过文本提示控制）。基准包含1474个测试案例，跨越8项专门任务，分别探测四个层级的能力。评估指标因任务而异，包括感知质量分数、指令遵从率、几何一致性度量，以及专门衡量"场景条件反应"和"目标导向行为"的新型反应性评分。 The researchers evaluated 20 representative video generation models spanning three paradigms: camera-driven (e.g., models controlled by camera trajectories), action-driven (e.g., models controlled by agent actions), and language-driven (e.g., models controlled by text prompts). The benchmark includes 1,474 test cases across 8 dedicated tasks designed to probe each of the four hierarchical levels. Evaluation metrics were tailored per task — including perceptual quality scores, instruction-following rates, geometric consistency measures, and novel reactivity scores that assess scene-conditioned and goal-directed behaviors not explicitly specified in the input.
📊 实验
结果		 对20个模型的评测揭示了明显的能力分化：相机驱动模型在相机控制任务上得分最高，但其接口根本不支持动态交互；动作驱动模型对主体控制更精准，但经常让周围世界元素毫无反应；语言驱动模型在交互和反应性任务上表现最好，但难以忠实遵循复杂的多步骤控制指令。关键是，没有任何单一模型能在全部四个层级上同时取得强劲表现——高视觉质量分数（顶级模型接近人类感知质量）与强世界反应性分数之间没有相关性，暴露了当前世界模型能力的根本性缺口。 Evaluation of 20 models revealed a clear capability split across paradigms: camera-driven models score highest on camera control tasks but their interfaces fundamentally cannot support dynamic interaction. Action-driven models achieve more precise subject control but frequently leave surrounding world elements unresponsive. Language-driven models outperform others on interaction and reactivity tasks but struggle to faithfully follow complex, multi-step controls. Critically, no single model achieves strong performance across all four levels simultaneously — high visual quality scores (top models reaching near-human perceptual quality) do not correlate with strong world reactivity scores, exposing a fundamental gap in current world model capabilities.
🏁 结论 WorldExam证明了当前AI视频模型尽管视觉效果惊艳，但从根本上缺乏模拟世界真实反应的能力——画面精美和遵从指令是必要条件，但远不足以成为真正的"世界模型"。该基准为领域提供了一个严格的分层诊断工具，精确指出模型在通往真正世界理解之路上的不足之处。 WorldExam proves that current AI video models, despite their impressive visual output, fundamentally lack the ability to simulate how the world truly reacts — visual polish and instruction-following are necessary but not sufficient for a real "world model." The benchmark provides the field with a rigorous, hierarchical tool to diagnose exactly where models fall short on the path to genuine world understanding.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

WorldExam把测试组织成四个层级，就像一栋楼的四层，从浅到深。第一层检查基本视觉质量（视频看起来真实吗？）。第二层检查控制遵从性（模型有没有按指令行事？）。第三层检查空间一致性（场景随时间推移是否保持连贯？）。最顶层也是最难的——测试"世界反应性"：给定一个场景，模型能不能推断并生成没有被明确描述的后果，比如球被踢后会滚动，或者门把手转动后门会打开？8项任务分别精心设计用于探测这些层级的不同方面，三类模型在统一框架下进行公平比较。 WorldExam organizes its tests into four levels, like floors in a building going from shallow to deep. The ground floor checks basic visual quality (does the video look real?). The second floor checks control adherence (did the model do what it was told?). The third floor checks spatial consistency (does the scene stay coherent over time?). The top floor — the hardest and most novel — tests world reactivity: given a scene setup, can the model infer and generate consequences that weren't explicitly described, like a ball rolling after being kicked or a door opening after a handle is turned? Each of the 8 tasks is carefully designed to probe a specific aspect of these levels, and all three types of models (camera-driven, action-driven, language-driven) are evaluated on a unified framework so results can be fairly compared.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI公司竞相为机器人、自动驾驶和虚拟仿真构建"世界模型"，WorldExam首次提供了严格测试——这些模型是否真正理解物理因果关系，而不只是生成逼真视频。没有这样的基准，开发者可能会发布那些演示时令人印象深刻、但在真实部署中因果逻辑失效而造成危险后果的系统。 As AI companies race to build "world models" for robotics, self-driving cars, and virtual simulations, WorldExam provides the first rigorous test of whether these models actually understand physical cause-and-effect — not just produce convincing videos. Without such benchmarks, developers might ship systems that look impressive in demos but fail dangerously in real-world deployments where consequences matter.

Ego2Robot: Scalable Robot Data Synthesis from Egocentric Human Data Ego2Robot：从以自我为中心的人类数据中大规模合成机器人训练数据

🧑‍🔬 Ye Wang, Pei Lin, Xiong-Hui Chen, Haoqi Yuan et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 将人类第一视角视频批量转化为机器人训练数据，大幅提升机器人泛化能力
Converts massive egocentric human videos into robot training data to boost robot generalization at scale.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像教一个刚来的新厨师：与其让他从零开始练习每道菜，不如先让他大量观看普通人做饭的视频——Ego2Robot就是把这些"普通人做饭视频"自动变成机器人专用的烹饪教程，让机器人快速学会举一反三。Think of it like training a new chef: instead of having them practice every single dish from scratch, you let them watch thousands of home cooking videos and automatically convert those into professional kitchen instructions. Ego2Robot does exactly that — turning everyday human activity videos into a robot's personalized training manual.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练机器人完成各种操作任务需要大量的示范数据，但收集机器人专用数据成本极高且规模有限。现有方法要么依赖昂贵的机器人实际演示，要么只能在小规模下使用人类视频数据，无法让机器人学到足够通用的技能。研究人员想弄清楚：能不能利用人类日常拍摄的"第一视角"视频（比如做饭、整理桌面的视频）来大规模训练机器人？ Training robots to handle diverse real-world tasks requires enormous amounts of demonstration data, but collecting robot-specific data is expensive and slow. Prior work showed that human "egocentric" videos (filmed from the person's own point of view) could help, but only at small scale for individual tasks. The big open question was: can we scale this idea up to pretrain powerful general-purpose robot AI models?
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 构建了迄今最大的"人类视频→机器人数据"转换流水线，覆盖15种机器人形态，共18,561小时训练数据； • 提出多层次质量筛选机制，确保合成数据真实可用，同时支持野外未经整理的视频； • 扩展了RoboTwin2.0评测基准，从视觉外观、场景布局、机器人形态、任务语义四个维度全面测试泛化能力； • 在真实机器人部署中也验证了预训练收益，不仅限于仿真环境。 • Largest ego-to-robot dataset ever: 18,561 hours spanning 15 robot body types; • Multi-level quality curation ensures the synthetic data is actually useful for training; • New evaluation framework tests robot generalization across 4 independent "axes" (appearance, layout, body type, task meaning); • Benefits confirmed on real physical robots, not just simulations.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者在RoboTwin2.0仿真环境中测试了多种扰动场景，包括改变视觉外观（光照、颜色）、场景布局（物体位置）、机器人形态（不同机械臂）和任务语义（不同指令表达）。使用多个VLA基线模型（如RoboVLMs等），对比单独使用真实机器人数据与联合使用Ego2Robot合成数据两种训练方式的泛化性能。此外还在真实物理机器人上部署验证了效果。 Experiments were run in the RoboTwin2.0 simulation benchmark, which was extended with four independent perturbation types: visual appearance changes, scene layout shifts, different robot body types, and varied task descriptions. Multiple VLA baseline models were tested, comparing training with only real robot data versus joint training with Ego2Robot synthetic data. Results were also validated on a real physical robot to confirm real-world applicability.
📊 实验
结果		 联合预训练在所有四种扰动类型上均一致提升了分布外泛化性能。Ego2Robot数据集包含18,561小时训练数据，覆盖15种机器人形态，是目前最大的同类数据集。在RoboTwin2.0基准测试中，加入Ego2Robot数据后，多个VLA模型在视觉、布局、形态、语义扰动场景下的任务成功率均显著提升（论文报告了跨多个扰动轴的一致性收益）。真实机器人部署实验同样验证了泛化收益。 Joint pretraining with Ego2Robot data consistently improved out-of-distribution generalization across all four perturbation axes on the RoboTwin2.0 benchmark. The dataset itself is the largest of its kind at 18,561 hours across 15 robot morphologies. Multiple VLA models showed significant task success rate improvements when trained with Ego2Robot data versus robot-only data. Real-robot deployment experiments confirmed the gains carry over to the physical world.
🏁 结论 本文证明了将大规模人类第一视角视频转化为机器人训练数据是可行且有效的——通过Ego2Robot流水线生成的数据能显著提升机器人面对新场景时的泛化能力，为低成本、大规模机器人预训练提供了新路径。 This paper proves that large-scale human egocentric videos can be reliably converted into effective robot training data, and that pretraining on this synthetic data consistently improves a robot's ability to handle new, unseen situations — opening a practical, low-cost path to large-scale robot learning.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Ego2Robot流水线分三步走：第一步是"动作重定向"——把人手在视频里的运动轨迹翻译成机器人手臂能执行的动作指令；第二步是"视觉替换"——用图像合成技术把视频里的人手替换成机器人手臂的样子，让视觉观感像真正的机器人视角；第三步是"多层质量过滤"——自动检查合成数据的质量，剔除不合格样本。最终把这些合成数据和真实机器人数据一起联合训练视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型，让模型在面对没见过的场景时也能表现良好。 Ego2Robot works in three steps. First, it "retargets" the human hand movements captured in the video into equivalent robot arm commands — like translating hand gestures into robot instructions. Second, it uses image synthesis to visually replace the human hand with a robot arm, so the footage looks like it was filmed by a robot. Third, a multi-level quality filter automatically weeds out bad samples. The resulting synthetic data is then mixed with real robot data to jointly pretrain a Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model, teaching the robot to generalize to new, unseen situations.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

机器人要真正进入家庭和工厂，必须能应对各种意外情况，而这需要海量训练数据——这正是当前最大的瓶颈。Ego2Robot提供了一种用人类日常视频"免费"扩充机器人训练数据的方法，有望大幅降低训练通用机器人的成本和门槛。 The biggest bottleneck to deploying capable robots in homes and factories is the lack of diverse training data. Ego2Robot shows we can tap into the vast ocean of existing human activity videos as a cheap, scalable data source — potentially accelerating the development of general-purpose robots by years.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究人员首先设计了两种衡量文本提示"结构化程度"的方法：GPG衡量语言的规范性和逻辑结构（类似检查语法和逻辑），ED统计提示中包含多少不同的视觉属性（颜色、形状、位置等）。他们进行了大量对照训练实验，发现当提示在这两个指标上得分越高，AI的图像生成误差就以可预测的数学规律下降。基于这一发现，他们做了两项改进：一是通过自动添加几何布局和语义标注来丰富训练提示；二是训练了一个小型"提示器"模型，能将用户的随意输入自动改写为结构清晰、细节丰富的提示。提示器先通过监督微调训练，再用一种强化学习风格的方法进一步优化，只有通过质量验证的改写才被用于训练。 The researchers first designed two ways to measure how "structured" a text prompt is: GPG measures how predictable and well-formed the language is (like checking grammar and logical structure), while ED counts how many distinct visual attributes (color, shape, position, etc.) a prompt contains. They then ran many controlled training experiments and discovered that when prompts score higher on these metrics, the AI's image generation loss (an error score) drops in a predictable mathematical pattern. Armed with this knowledge, they built two improvements: first, they created richer training prompts by automatically adding geometric layouts and semantic labels to image captions; second, they trained a small "prompter" model that takes a user's casual text input and rewrites it into a well-structured, detailed prompt before passing it to the image generator. The prompter was trained using supervised fine-tuning, then further improved with a special reinforcement-style method where only verified high-quality rewrites were used for training.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L}^* \propto \text{GPG}^{\,-1}\]
💡 模型收敛后的扩散损失（生成误差）与提示的GPG语言结构得分近似成线性反比——提示结构越好，误差越低。 The converged diffusion loss (generation error) decreases approximately linearly as the GPG structured-language score of the prompt increases — better-structured prompts = lower error.
\[\mathcal{L}^* \propto \text{ED}^{\,-\alpha}\]
💡 收敛扩散损失与提示的ED属性多样性得分之间遵循幂律关系（类似物理中的比例定律），α为幂律指数。 The converged diffusion loss follows a power law with the ED (attribute diversity) score of the prompt, where α is the scaling exponent — similar to classic neural scaling laws.

For everyday users, it means AI art tools that actually understand complex, detailed requests like "a sunset over a city with a dog in the foreground wearing a red hat."
