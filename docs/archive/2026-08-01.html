Explorative Modeling: Unlocking a Third Pretraining Axis and End-to-End Generation 探索式建模：解锁第三预训练轴与端到端生成

🧑‍🔬 Alexi Gladstone, Heng Ji, Yilun Du

图 Figure · Page 3 of paper

🎯 用"多选探索"训练让AI生成更高效、更端到端
A new "explorative" training trick makes AI generative models sharper, faster, and truly end-to-end.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像参加厨艺比赛时，选手先做K道菜，然后只把最好的那道端给评委打分——久而久之，厨师会越做越精，而不是端出一道"所有菜融合"的奇怪大杂烩。探索式建模就是让AI每次训练都"只提交最优答案"，从而越学越精准。Imagine a cooking competition where each chef secretly makes K different dishes, then only serves their best one to the judge. Over time, the chef gets really good at making sharp, distinct flavors — instead of mushing everything together into a bland stew. That's exactly what Explorative Modeling does: it lets the AI "try K drafts and submit only the best," so it learns to generate crisp, clear outputs instead of blurry averages.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的AI图像、视频和语言生成模型非常强大，但它们有一个共同的缺陷：训练时总是把所有可能的答案"平均"在一起，导致生成结果模糊不清，就像把所有颜色混在一起变成灰色。更大的问题是，这些模型无法做到真正的"端到端训练"——也就是说，它们必须被人为地拆分成很多小步骤来训练，而不能像人一样整体学习。扩大模型规模（用更多参数）或扩大数据量是目前提升性能的两条主轴，但这两条路都越来越昂贵。 Existing AI generative models — the kind that make images, videos, or text — have a hidden flaw: during training, they try to predict an "average" of all possible correct answers at once, which blurs the output, like averaging all possible paint colors into muddy grey. Worse, these models can't be trained "end-to-end" (as one unified system), so engineers must break them into hand-crafted pieces. Making models bigger or feeding them more data are the only two known ways to improve them, but both are getting extremely expensive.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出全新训练范式"探索式建模（XM）"：每次训练时生成K个候选答案，只用最好的那个来更新模型，彻底避免"答案平均化"的模糊问题。 • 开辟了预训练的"第三轴"：除了更多参数、更多数据，增加"探索量K"也能单调地提升性能，且与规模正向叠加。 • 首次实现端到端重构式生成建模：推理步骤减少16到256倍，同时匹配扩散模型在控制任务上的表现。 • 探索带来的收益随规模增长而放大：数据规模扩大时增益从7%升至36%，模型规模扩大时增益从13%升至23%。 • Introduces "Explorative Modeling (XM)": at each training step, the model generates K candidate outputs and only learns from the best one — eliminating the blurry averaging problem. • Opens a brand-new third pretraining axis: besides bigger models and more data, simply increasing exploration K monotonically boosts performance. • Enables true end-to-end generative modeling for the first time, cutting inference steps by 16–256× while matching diffusion-model quality. • Exploration gains compound with scale: data scaling pushes gains from 7% to 36%; model scaling pushes gains from 13% to 23%.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验覆盖图像、视频和语言三大领域。图像生成使用ImageNet基准，与Flow Matching等主流扩散模型方法比较，评估指标为FID（越低越好）。语言生成在标准语言建模基准上测试，评估困惑度（perplexity）。视频生成也纳入测试。还专门测试了端到端控制任务（如条件生成），对比了参数效率、样本效率、FLOP效率等多个维度，并通过系统性改变K值、数据量、模型规模来研究三者的交互关系。 Experiments span three domains: image generation on ImageNet (compared against Flow Matching and other leading diffusion-style methods, measured by FID score), language modeling on standard benchmarks (measured by perplexity), and video generation. The team also tested end-to-end control/conditional generation tasks. They systematically varied K (exploration budget), dataset size, and model size to study how these three axes interact, and measured FLOP efficiency, sample efficiency, and parameter efficiency throughout.
📊 实验
结果		 • ImageNet无引导图像生成FID达到1.43，接近最先进水平。 • FLOP效率提升4.1倍，样本效率提升6.2倍，参数效率提升47%。 • 数据规模扩大时，探索带来的增益从7%升至36%；模型规模扩大时，增益从13%升至23%。 • 计算量增加3倍时，效率增益翻倍以上。 • 端到端模式下推理步骤减少16到256倍，控制任务性能与扩散模型相当。 • 在离散（语言）和连续（图像、视频）域均有单调性能提升。 • Achieves near-state-of-the-art FID of 1.43 on ImageNet without guidance. • 4.1× improvement in FLOP efficiency, 6.2× in sample efficiency, and 47% in parameter efficiency. • Data scaling amplifies exploration gains from 7% to 36%; model scaling amplifies gains from 13% to 23%. • Efficiency gains more than double at 3× the compute budget. • End-to-end mode requires 16–256× fewer inference steps while matching diffusion models on control tasks. • Monotonic performance improvements across both discrete (language) and continuous (image, video) domains.
🏁 结论 本文证明了"探索量"可以作为独立于模型参数量和数据量的第三条预训练扩展轴，且与规模正向叠加。更重要的是，探索式建模（XM）首次实现了真正端到端的生成式模型训练，以更少的推理步骤达到与扩散模型相当的性能。 This paper proves that "exploration" is a genuine third axis for scaling generative AI — independent of model size and data, and complementary to both. Explorative Modeling also unlocks truly end-to-end generative training, achieving competitive quality with far fewer inference steps than diffusion models.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

核心思想很简单：训练时不要只生成一个答案，而是生成K个候选答案，然后找出其中与真实数据最匹配的那一个，只用这个"最优候选"来更新模型参数。这样模型就被迫"选边站"，聚焦于某一种具体的生成模式，而不是把所有可能性混在一起变得模糊。类比来说，就像老师批作业时，学生可以交K份草稿，老师只看最好的那份打分——学生自然会越来越精进，而不是交一份"四不像"的答案。这种方法不需要改变模型结构，只需要修改训练循环，因此可以直接"插入"现有的扩散模型、语言模型等主流框架。此外，XM还允许模型在推理时直接输出最终结果，而无需多步迭代，实现真正的端到端生成。 The idea is elegantly simple: instead of generating just one output per training step, the model generates K candidate outputs. It then picks the one that best matches the real data and only updates itself based on that winner — ignoring the rest. This forces the model to commit to one clear answer rather than averaging across all possibilities into a blur. Think of it like a student submitting K rough drafts; the teacher only grades the best one, so the student quickly learns to produce sharp, focused work. Crucially, this change only touches the training loop — the model architecture stays the same, so it can be plugged into existing diffusion models, language models, and more. At inference time, XMs can skip the usual multi-step generation process entirely, producing results in far fewer steps.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L}_{XM} = \mathbb{E}\left[\min_{k=1}^{K} \mathcal{L}(f(z_k), x)\right]\]
💡 探索式建模的核心损失函数：每次训练生成K个候选输出z_k，只用与真实数据x损失最小（最匹配）的那个来计算梯度更新模型，而不是对所有候选取平均。 The core XM loss: generate K candidate outputs z_k, compute the loss against the real data x for each, and only train on the single best-matching candidate — not the average of all K.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项工作意味着我们不必总是依赖"堆参数、堆数据"来提升AI生成质量——增加训练时的"探索次数"就能带来显著收益，且成本更低。对于图像生成、视频创作、语言模型等实际产品，这提供了一条更高效、更经济的性能提升路径。 This means the AI industry doesn't always have to build bigger, more expensive models to get better results — simply exploring more candidates during training is a cheaper, powerful alternative. For real products like AI image generators, video tools, and chatbots, this offers a more efficient path to quality improvements without ballooning costs.

🎯 AI学会自动改进AI，Medal平均分从39%跃升至71%

❓ 待解决
的问题		 构建一个好的AI模型是极其耗时费力的工作，通常需要大量人类专家反复实验、调参和修复错误。现有的AI系统很难自动完成这整个过程——就像你希望有个助手不仅能帮你写代码，还能自己运行、发现问题、自动改进，形成良性循环。"递归自我改进"就是让AI来干这件事：AI自动改进构建AI的流程本身，但目前还没有一套完整、开放的系统来实现和研究这个目标。 Building good AI models usually requires teams of human experts spending weeks writing code, running experiments, fixing bugs, and tuning settings. 💡 解决
的亮点		 - 🔁 **递归自我改进闭环**：首次构建了完整的开放系统（OpenMLE），让AI能在真实机器学习任务上自动写代码、运行、反馈、改进，形成完整闭环。 - 🧬 **四大原子进化算子**：定义了Draft（起草）、Improve（改进）、Debug（调试）、Crossover（交叉融合）四个操作，训练和推理共用同一套操作，训练与进化深度耦合。 - 🚀 **小模型超越大模型**：35B参数的Frontis-MA1在严格限制（单张RTX 4090、12小时）下，性能超越GPT-5.5+Codex，接近参数量是其80倍的2.8T Kimi K3。 - 📦 **完全开源**：模型权重和全套OpenMLE系统公开发布，支持可复现的RSI研究。 - 🔁 **Full RSI loop**: First open, end-to-end system (OpenMLE) where an AI writes ML code, runs it, reads the feedback, and keeps improving itself — all automatically. - 🧬 **Four atomic operators**: The model is trained around just four actions — Draft, Improve, Debug, and Crossover — used consistently at both training time and search time, creating a tight learning-evolution coupling. - 🚀 **Small beats big**: The 35B Frontis-MA1 on a single consumer GPU outperforms GPT-5.5+Codex and nearly matches the 2.8 trillion-parameter Kimi K3. - 📦 **Fully open-sourced**: Weights and the entire OpenMLE stack are released for reproducible research.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

核心思路是让AI像一个不断进化的工程师团队。研究者定义了四个基本动作：起草（写新代码）、改进（优化现有代码）、调试（修复错误）、交叉融合（把两段好代码的优点合并）。首先，用这四个动作生成大量训练数据，并去除与评测集重叠的部分，然后通过监督微调（SFT）和强化学习（RL）训练模型，让它真正理解执行结果的反馈。推理阶段，模型用同样四个动作构成长期搜索（OpenMLE-Evo），不断迭代优化解决方案，就像进化算法一样，让好的代码"存活"并被进一步改良。整个系统训练和搜索共用同一套算子，保证了一致性和高效性。 Think of it as training an AI to act like an evolving team of software engineers. The researchers defined four basic moves: Draft (write new code from scratch), Improve (make existing code better), Debug (find and fix errors), and Crossover (merge the best parts of two different solutions). The model is first trained on a huge dataset of these moves — filtered so it hasn't "seen" any test problems — using both supervised learning and reinforcement learning with real code execution as feedback. At inference time, the same four moves are composed into a long search loop (OpenMLE-Evo), where good solutions survive and get further refined, like natural selection for code. The key insight is that training and searching use the exact same operators, so the model is perfectly suited for the search it will perform.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{Medal Average} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{1}[\text{solution}_i \text{ earns a medal}]\]
💡 奖牌平均分：在N个任务中，AI方案能够在Kaggle竞赛中获得奖牌的比例，是衡量MLE-Bench性能的核心指标。 Medal Average: the fraction of tasks (out of N) where the AI's solution would earn a Kaggle competition medal — the primary performance metric for MLE-Bench.
\[s_{t+1} = \text{Operator}(s_t, f(s_t)), \quad \text{Operator} \in \{\text{Draft, Improve, Debug, Crossover}\}\]
💡 进化搜索核心公式：当前解s_t经过某个进化算子（起草/改进/调试/交叉融合）和执行反馈f(s_t)，生成下一个更优的解s_{t+1}。 The core evolution loop: a current solution s_t is transformed by one of the four operators using execution feedback f(s_t) to produce a better next solution s_{t+1}.

In the long run, this is a foundational step toward AI that genuinely improves itself — a capability with profound implications for both the pace of progress and AI safety.

🧑‍🔬 Philip Arathoon, Gavin Ball, Matthew D. Kvalheim

🎯 用五个点电荷证伪了麦克斯韦160年前的经典猜想
A 160-year-old conjecture about electric fields is proven false using five cleverly arranged point charges.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像有人声称一张地图上最多只能有16个"死胡同"，但有人实际画出一张有24个死胡同的地图，彻底推翻了这个说法。麦克斯韦当年的猜想就是那个"最多16个"的断言，而这篇论文就是那张让人瞠目结舌的新地图。Imagine someone claimed that in any city with 5 fountains, water currents can create at most 16 perfectly still spots in a pool. This paper is like someone actually building that pool and finding 24 still spots — proving the old rule flat-out wrong after 150 years!
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 1873年，著名物理学家麦克斯韦猜测：如果你在空间中放置n个点电荷，它们共同产生的电场最多只有(n-1)²个"平静点"——也就是所有力相互抵消的位置。这个猜想悬而未决超过150年，没人知道它对所有电荷排列方式是否都成立。 Back in 1873, the famous physicist James Clerk Maxwell guessed that if you place n electric charges in space, the total electric field they create can only have at most (n-1)² "calm spots" — points where all the pushing and pulling forces perfectly cancel out. This conjecture has remained unproven or unrefuted for over 150 years. Nobody knew if Maxwell's rule was actually correct for all possible arrangements of charges.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次给出麦克斯韦猜想的明确反例，仅用五个点电荷就做到了。• 构造的电荷排列产生至少24个非退化临界点，超过猜想上限(5-1)²=16个。• 这是一个严格的数学证明，不依赖数值近似，结论确凿可靠。 • First explicit counterexample to Maxwell's 150-year-old conjecture, using just five point charges. • The constructed configuration produces at least 24 non-degenerate critical points — exceeding the conjectured maximum of (5-1)² = 16. • The result is a pure mathematical proof, rigorously verified and not relying on numerical approximation.
🧪 实验
内容		 这不是计算机实验，而是纯数学证明。作者构造了一个具体的五点电荷配置，用拓扑学和代数几何方法分析其电势函数，逐步验证临界点的数量和性质（非退化性），最终确认反例成立。 This is not a computational experiment but a rigorous mathematical proof. The authors constructed a specific five-charge configuration and analyzed its electrostatic potential function using tools from topology and algebraic geometry. They systematically verified the count and non-degeneracy of all critical points to confirm the counterexample holds.
📊 实验

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Chimera把文字、图片、视频的信息统一放进一个"流水线"里处理，不需要额外的位置标记。它同时使用三种注意力机制：一种像"滚动记忆"一样线性扫描长内容（KDA，复杂度O(N)）；一种像"全局广播"一样让所有内容互相对话（MLA）；还有一种"近邻交流"的局部卷积处理空间细节。此外，稀疏的"专家混合"层让模型容量大幅扩展，但每次只激活一小部分参数，节省算力。为了科学地把小模型的调参经验迁移到大模型，团队提出HeteroP方案，根据每个模块的"功能角色"分别调整超参数。最终用拟合出的最优缩放定律指导训练了一个110亿参数、但每次只激活20亿参数的大模型。 Chimera feeds text, image, and video information into one unified pipeline without needing positional labels. It uses three complementary attention mechanisms working together: a "rolling memory" that scans content linearly (cheap and good at long sequences), a "global broadcast" that lets every token talk to every other (great for coherence), and a "neighborhood chat" local convolution for fine spatial details. Sparse Mixture-of-Experts layers dramatically expand the model's capacity while only activating a small fraction of parameters at any given time — like having a huge team of specialists but only calling in the relevant ones for each task. HeteroP then provides a principled way to transfer tuning knowledge from small models to large ones, accounting for each module's unique role. Finally, fitted scaling laws guide how to optimally allocate compute when training the full 11B-parameter model.
