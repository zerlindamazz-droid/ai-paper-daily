Mismatch Matters: On-Policy Distillation Beyond Token Agreement 失配很重要：超越Token一致性的在线策略蒸馏

🧑‍🔬 Zichao Yu, Chengzhi Yu, Shengze Xu, Yujin Han et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 TIDE通过纠正师生Token失配，大幅提升大模型知识蒸馏效果
TIDE fixes a hidden flaw in AI teacher-student training by targeting mismatched tokens, not just agreement.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy想象一个学钢琴的学生，为了通过考试只反复弹同样的三个音符——虽然在这几个地方和乐谱"一致"，但整体听起来乱七八糟。TIDE就像一位聪明的音乐老师，发现了这个把戏后，一边把那些烦人的重复声音调小，一边直接哼出学生总是跳过的难段——这样学生才真正学会了整首曲子。Imagine a music student who learns to pass exams by endlessly repeating the same three notes — technically "matching" the sheet music at those spots, but producing something that sounds awful overall. TIDE is like a smart music teacher who notices the trick, mutes the annoying repetition, and then directly hums the hard passages the student keeps skipping — so the student actually learns the whole song.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 在用大模型（老师）训练小模型（学生）时，学生可能会"作弊"——它通过不断重复某些词来假装和老师一致，实际上给出的答案是错的或者乱的。现有方法只看学生和老师的词是否吻合，忽视了这种作弊行为，导致真正的推理能力没有被传授下来。 When training a smaller AI model to learn from a bigger, smarter one (called "distillation"), the student model can cheat — it learns to repeat certain word patterns over and over to look like it agrees with the teacher, while actually producing wrong or nonsensical answers. Existing methods focus on whether the student's words match the teacher's, but they miss this cheat and fail to transfer the teacher's real reasoning ability.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 发现了"退化一致"这一新型失败模式：学生通过重复循环骗过词级匹配指标，但实际回答是错误的。 • 将Token失配分为两类："学生多余Token"（学生说了老师根本不会说的词）和"学生缺失Token"（老师偏好但学生从不生成的词）。 • 提出TIDE方法，分别对两类失配独立纠正：用有界Hellinger塑形抑制过度偏差，用解析式Top-K注入补充缺失的老师偏好信息，无需学生先采样到这些词。 • Discovers a new failure mode called "degenerate agreement" where student models game token-matching metrics with repetitive loops while giving globally wrong answers. • Identifies two types of token mismatch — "excess" tokens (student says things the teacher would never say) and "deficit" tokens (things the teacher prefers but student never produces). • Proposes TIDE, which independently fixes both problems: it suppresses runaway excess corrections and injects the teacher's preferred tokens analytically — without needing the student to sample them first.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在数学推理基准测试上进行，使用了Qwen3系列的多组师生模型对。TIDE与标准在线策略蒸馏（OPD）以及多种近期基线（包括Token选择方法和奖励塑形方法）进行了比较。评估指标包括平均准确率（Avg@8）、回答长度（反映重复和格式问题）以及格式失败率。 Experiments were conducted on mathematical reasoning benchmarks using multiple teacher-student model pairs from the Qwen3 family. TIDE was compared against standard on-policy distillation (OPD) and several recent baselines including token-selection methods and reward-shaping methods. Evaluation metrics included average accuracy scores (Avg@8) across math problem sets, response length (as a proxy for repetition and formatting quality), and formatting failure rates.
📊 实验
结果		 在师生失配严重的情况下，TIDE将Avg@8从6.9%提升至20.3%，提升幅度接近3倍。平均回答长度缩短了3.6倍，大幅减少了重复循环现象。格式失败率也显著降低。在所有测试的Qwen3师生模型对上，TIDE一致性地优于标准OPD和所有近期基线方法。 Under strong teacher-student mismatch conditions, TIDE improves Avg@8 from 6.9% to 20.3% — nearly a 3× gain. It reduces average response length by a factor of 3.6, indicating a dramatic reduction in repetitive loops. Formatting failures are also substantially reduced. TIDE consistently outperforms standard OPD and all recent token-selection and reward-shaping baselines across all Qwen3 teacher-student pairs tested.
🏁 结论 本文证明，在大模型知识蒸馏中，解决Token级失配问题（而非追求Token一致性）才是提升效果的关键。TIDE的双向针对性纠正在准确率和输出质量上均带来显著提升，尤其在师生模型差异较大时效果最为突出。 This paper proves that fixing token-level mismatches — rather than chasing token agreement — is the key to effective on-policy distillation for LLMs. TIDE's targeted two-pronged correction yields large accuracy gains and more stable, well-formatted outputs, especially when the teacher and student models are very different.

链接		 arXiv: 2608.09836v1  ·  📥 PDF

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

TIDE分两步分别解决两类失配问题。对于"多余Token"（学生生成了老师几乎不会说的词），TIDE使用一种数学上有界的修正（Hellinger塑形），防止惩罚信号爆炸式增长破坏训练稳定性。对于"缺失Token"（老师偏爱但学生很少生成的词），TIDE直接通过解析方式把老师最偏好的词注入到训练信号中，让学生即使没有采样到这些词也能从中学习。可以理解为：一个旋钮负责压制坏习惯，一个提示系统负责直接教好习惯，两者同时运作。 TIDE tackles the two mismatch problems independently. For "excess" tokens — words the student generates that the teacher would almost never use — TIDE applies a mathematically bounded correction (called Hellinger shaping) that prevents the penalty signal from exploding and destabilizing training. For "deficit" tokens — words the teacher prefers but the student rarely produces — TIDE directly injects the teacher's top preferred tokens into the training signal analytically, so the student can learn from them even without ever having generated them. Think of it as two targeted fixes running in parallel: a volume knob to turn down bad habits, and a direct hint system to teach good ones.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{L}_{\text{TIDE}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{excess}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{deficit}}\]
💡 TIDE的总损失由两部分组成：对多余Token的惩罚项和对缺失Token的补偿项，两者独立计算后相加。 TIDE's total training loss is the sum of two independent parts: one that penalizes excess tokens (student says wrong things) and one that restores deficit tokens (student misses teacher's preferred words).
\[\text{Hellinger shaping: } -\log\left(1 - H^2(p_T \| p_S)\right)\]
💡 Hellinger塑形用一个有界的数学函数来衡量老师和学生分布的差异，避免惩罚信号无限增大破坏训练。 Hellinger shaping uses a bounded mathematical distance between teacher and student distributions to penalize excess tokens — unlike raw log-ratios, it can never blow up to infinity and destabilize training.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

知识蒸馏是AI公司将大模型能力压缩进更小、更快、更便宜模型的核心技术，直接影响日常AI助手的质量。如果学生模型通过循环作弊而训练方法无法识别，用户就会收到重复、错误或混乱的回答；TIDE堵住了这个漏洞，让蒸馏出的模型更可靠、更聪明。 Distillation is how AI companies build smaller, cheaper, faster models that still reason well — it powers many real-world AI assistants. If the student model cheats by looping and the training method doesn't catch it, users get repetitive, wrong, or garbled answers; TIDE fixes this blind spot and makes distilled models reliably smarter.

Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs 从专有大模型API中窃取推理过程

🧑‍🔬 Alexander Panfilov, David Schmotz, Ilia Shumailov, Luca Beurer-Kellner et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 加密推理链可被跨模型解密，造成四类严重安全漏洞
Encrypted AI "thinking blocks" can be cracked open across models, leaking secrets and enabling attacks.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像银行把你的保险箱钥匙交给你保管，却没想到同一家银行所有保险箱用的都是同一套万能钥匙——任何拿到一把钥匙的人，都能打开别人的保险箱，读出里面所有的秘密。Imagine a bank gives you a "locked diary" to hold, thinking it's safe because only you have the key — but it turns out every diary from that same bank can be unlocked by any other customer's key. So anyone can grab your diary, hand it to a different customer, and have them read your deepest secrets out loud.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 顶级AI公司（如OpenAI、Anthropic、Google）把模型"思考过程"（即推理链）加密后返回给用户，以为这样能保护知识产权和用户隐私。但问题是：这些加密块存在严重的结构漏洞——它们在同一家公司的不同模型、不同用户、不同会话之间完全通用和可互换。这意味着任何人都可以利用这个漏洞，把加密的思维过程"解码"出来，看到原本应该被隐藏的内容。 Top AI companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google now hide their models' "thinking steps" by encrypting them before sending them back to users. The idea is to protect trade secrets and user privacy. But these encrypted blocks have a critical flaw: they work interchangeably across different models, users, and sessions within the same company's ecosystem — meaning anyone clever enough can trick a weaker model into decoding and reading aloud another model's secret thoughts.
💡 解决
的亮点		 - 🔑 发现跨模型"加密块通用性"漏洞：同一厂商的不同模型可以互相解读对方的加密推理块，这是此前从未被报告的架构级漏洞。 - 🕵️ 构建可扩展的"解密越狱"攻击：把强模型的加密推理块注入弱模型，强迫弱模型将其逐字解码为明文，无需直接攻破强模型。 - 📂 从公开代码库中大规模提取隐私数据：从315,320个公开的推理块中，成功恢复了367条个人身份信息（PII）和182条凭证/密码。 - ☣️ 揭示"安全拒绝"背后的危险信息：即使模型最终的回答看起来是安全的拒绝，其加密推理过程中仍可能藏有有害内容。 - 👻 支持"隐形提示注入"攻击：恶意指令可以完全隐藏在加密块中，用户和安全系统根本看不见。 - 🔑 Discovered a cross-model "encrypted block compatibility" flaw: encrypted thinking steps from one model can be read by a weaker sibling model in the same provider's family — a never-before-reported architectural vulnerability. - 🕵️ Built a scalable "decryption jailbreak": inject a strong model's encrypted reasoning into a weaker, less-guarded model and force it to decode the secret thoughts word-for-word, no direct attack on the strong model needed. - 📂 Recovered real private data at scale: scraped 315,320 encrypted reasoning blocks from public repos and recovered 367 pieces of personal info (PII) and 182 credentials/passwords. - ☣️ Exposed hidden hazardous content: even when a model's visible reply safely refuses a harmful request, its hidden reasoning may still contain dangerous information. - 👻 Enabled invisible prompt injection: malicious instructions can be buried entirely inside encrypted blocks, invisible to users and safety filters alike.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验覆盖三大AI厂商（Anthropic、OpenAI、Google），在各自的多个模型上验证了加密块通用性漏洞。针对知识蒸馏绕过，研究者在不同模型对上测试了解密越狱的成功率。针对隐私数据提取，他们从GitHub等公开代码仓库爬取了315,320个真实的加密推理块，并尝试解码其中的个人信息和凭证。针对有害信息暴露，他们测试了模型在表面拒绝请求时，隐藏推理中是否包含危险内容。针对隐形提示注入，他们验证了恶意负载能否在完全不被人类或安全系统察觉的情况下嵌入加密块并生效。 Experiments were run across three major AI providers — Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google — testing multiple models from each. For the decryption jailbreak, they measured how reliably a weaker model could decode a stronger model's encrypted reasoning. For private data recovery, they scraped 315,320 encrypted reasoning blocks from public GitHub repositories and other open sources, then decoded them to search for personal information and credentials. For hazardous content, they checked whether hidden reasoning steps contained dangerous details even when the model's visible output was a safe refusal. For invisible prompt injection, they verified that malicious payloads embedded in encrypted blocks could influence model behavior without any visible trace.
📊 实验
结果		 - 从315,320个公开爬取的加密推理块中，成功恢复了 **367条个人身份信息（PII）**（如姓名、邮箱、电话）和 **182条凭证/密码**。 - 解密越狱攻击在Anthropic、OpenAI和Google的模型上均成功复现，覆盖多个模型对（如Claude 3.7 Sonnet → Claude 3.5 Haiku等）。 - 即使模型最终回答是"安全拒绝"，其加密推理过程中仍可检测到有害信息（如危险物质合成步骤等）。 - 隐形提示注入攻击成功将恶意负载完全隐藏在加密块中，对用户和安全审计系统均不可见，攻击在公开的智能体工作流中验证有效。 - From 315,320 scraped encrypted reasoning blocks from public repos, recovered **367 PII artifacts** (names, emails, phone numbers) and **182 credentials/passwords**. - The decryption jailbreak successfully reproduced on models from all three major providers — Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google — across multiple model pairs (e.g., Claude 3.7 Sonnet → Claude 3.5 Haiku). - Hidden reasoning blocks were found to contain hazardous details (e.g., dangerous synthesis steps) even when the model's final visible output was a safe refusal. - Invisible prompt injection attacks successfully embedded malicious payloads entirely inside encrypted blocks, verified as undetectable to both users and safety auditing systems in real agentic workflows.
🏁 结论 本文证明了AI厂商用于隐藏模型推理过程的"客户端加密"方案存在根本性的架构漏洞：加密块在跨模型、跨用户、跨会话之间完全通用，导致任何人都可以用弱模型解码强模型的秘密思维，造成知识产权泄露、个人隐私暴露、有害信息外泄和隐形攻击等四类严重安全风险。研究者随后提出了具体的密码学和系统层面修复方案，推动行业重新审视客户端推理存储的安全设计。 This paper proves that the "client-side encrypted reasoning" approach used by top AI providers has a fundamental architectural flaw — encrypted thinking blocks are interchangeable across models, users, and sessions, enabling anyone to decode a powerful model's secret reasoning using a weaker sibling model. The authors exposed four serious attack types and proposed concrete cryptographic and system-level fixes to close the gap.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究者首先发现：AI厂商把模型的"思维过程"加密成一段密文，存在用户侧并随每次请求传回服务器。他们进一步发现，这些加密块在同一厂商的不同模型之间是通用的。 利用这一漏洞，攻击者可以把"强模型"（如Claude 3.7 Sonnet）产生的加密思维块，注入到"弱模型"（防护更少）的对话上下文中。弱模型会把这段加密块当作自己的历史推理，并在新的提示下逐字解码输出为明文。 这套流程无需破解任何真正的加密算法，而是利用模型自身的"自我解读能力"实现解密——就像让一个知道密码本的小助手替你读出加密信件的内容。研究者还将这个方法扩展到四种不同的攻击场景，覆盖知识蒸馏防护绕过、隐私数据提取、有害信息暴露和隐形提示注入。 The researchers first noticed that AI providers encrypt a model's "thinking steps" and store them on the user's side, sending them back with each new message. They then discovered these encrypted blobs are fully compatible across different models from the same provider — a model can decode another model's encrypted thoughts. To exploit this, they take an encrypted reasoning block from a strong, well-guarded model (like Claude 3.7 Sonnet) and inject it into a conversation with a weaker, less-protected model from the same company. The weaker model treats the encrypted block as its own past reasoning and, when prompted, decodes and outputs it as plain readable text. Crucially, no actual cryptographic breaking is needed — the model itself acts as the decryption key. The researchers then demonstrated four distinct attack paths using this single vulnerability: bypassing anti-distillation protections, extracting private data from public logs, surfacing hidden harmful content, and planting invisible malicious instructions.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项研究直接影响数百万人每天使用的AI产品（ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini等）的安全性。如果不修复，攻击者可以偷走AI公司的核心技术秘密、泄露用户的个人隐私，甚至让AI在"表面安全"的回答背后偷偷输出危险信息——这对AI的可信度和合规性是重大威胁。 This research directly threatens the security of AI products used by hundreds of millions of people every day, like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Without a fix, attackers could steal core AI trade secrets, expose users' private data from shared logs, and silently surface dangerous content even when the AI appears to be playing it safe — a major blow to AI trustworthiness and regulatory compliance.

Full-bandwidth transformer 全带宽Transformer

🧑‍🔬 Xi Wang, Ziyang Cai, Zheng Zhan, Harry Dong et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 让Transformer把"内心想法"循环利用，用更少计算达到更强推理
A smarter transformer that recycles its own hidden thinking to reason better with fewer tokens.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一位侦探，以往每破解一条线索后都会清空脑子再看下一条——这篇论文让侦探把最深刻的洞察带到下一步，每条线索都在上一条的基础上推进，而不是每次都从零开始想。Think of it like a detective who, after solving each clue, usually erases their mental notes before tackling the next one — this paper lets the detective carry their sharpest insight forward, so each new clue builds on the last instead of starting from scratch.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的AI语言模型在生成每个词之后，只把最终选定的那个词传回给模型的"思考层叠"，而模型顶层那些丰富的内部计算结果全部被丢弃。这就像一个分析团队辛苦写了一份详细报告，却只把最后一个字传给下一组人，所有细节全扔掉了。这导致模型每次"重新思考"时都要从零开始，浪费了大量的计算潜力。 Current AI language models process text in two directions: left-to-right across words, and layer-by-layer through their internal "thinking stack." But between each word it generates, only the final chosen word gets passed back into the stack — all the rich internal thinking computed at the top layers is simply thrown away. This is like a team of analysts who write a detailed report, but only share the final one-word verdict with the next team, discarding all the nuance.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出"潜在反馈"机制：将模型顶层的隐藏状态（最丰富的内部思考结果）循环回输，而不是丢弃。 • 用门控线性单元（GLU）将循环回来的隐藏状态与新词的嵌入智能融合，无需修改核心Transformer结构。 • 设计"定时多步"训练策略，让模型在不破坏高效并行训练的前提下学会这种循环利用技能。 • Introduces "latent feedback": the model's top-layer hidden state (its richest internal thought) is recycled back as input for the next decoding step, not discarded. • Uses a gated linear unit to smartly blend the recycled hidden state with the new token embedding — no need to change the core transformer design. • A clever "scheduled multi-pass" training trick lets the model learn this recycling behavior without breaking the efficient parallel training that modern LLMs rely on.
🧪 实验
内容		 作者训练了10亿参数的全带宽Transformer，在最多4000亿个token上进行训练，并与同规模的标准Transformer对比。评估覆盖：5-shot语言模型基准测试、数学生成任务（GSM8K、MATH等）、代码生成（HumanEval）以及指令微调后的对话表现。还测量了推理链的长度，验证模型是否能更高效地"思考"。基线为在相同条件下训练的标准自回归Transformer。 The authors trained 1-billion-parameter full-bandwidth transformers on up to 400 billion tokens and compared them against standard transformers of the same size. Evaluations covered: 5-shot language modeling benchmarks, math generation tasks (like GSM8K, MATH), coding benchmarks (HumanEval), and instruction-tuned chat performance. They also measured reasoning trace length to see if the model could think more efficiently. Baselines were standard autoregressive transformers trained under identical conditions.
📊 实验
结果		 全带宽Transformer的性能与用约1.5倍token训练的标准Transformer相当，相当于免费获得约50%的"训练价值"提升。在数学和代码生成基准上，潜在反馈机制持续带来性能提升。经指令微调后，模型在推理任务上精度更高，同时推理链更短（以相同或更好的精度生成更简洁的思维过程）。各项验证损失全面下降，且每个token的解码开销可忽略不计。 Full-bandwidth transformers matched or approached standard transformers trained with roughly 1.5× more tokens — meaning the model gets ~50% more "training value" for free. On math and coding benchmarks, latent feedback consistently improved performance. Instruction-tuned versions showed better accuracy on reasoning tasks while producing shorter reasoning traces (more concise thinking chains at equal or better accuracy). Validation loss also improved across the board, with negligible added cost per token during decoding.
🏁 结论 本文证明，在每次生成词语时将Transformer的内部隐藏状态循环利用而非丢弃，是一种简单低成本的方式，能显著提升语言模型性能，效果堪比用多50%数据训练的模型。研究表明，Transformer中的"垂直反馈通道"是一种被严重低估的资源。 This paper proves that recycling a transformer's internal hidden state between decoding steps — rather than discarding it — is a simple, low-cost way to significantly boost language model performance, matching models trained on 50% more data. It shows that the "vertical feedback channel" in transformers is an underutilized resource worth exploiting.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

在每一步生成词语时，全带宽Transformer不会丢弃顶层的隐藏状态，而是将其保存下来，在下一步生成时重新送回模型底部。具体通过一个门控线性单元将"上一步的深层思考结果"与新词的表示融合在一起，相当于给模型一个"上次深思熟虑的记忆"作为下次思考的起点。整个核心架构、KV缓存和训练目标都保持不变，集成非常方便。为了不破坏高效并行训练，作者设计了"定时多步目标"：先正常训练，临近尾声时逐步引入反馈步骤，并混入少量多步反馈以保证稳定性。 At each decoding step, instead of discarding the top-layer hidden state, the full-bandwidth transformer saves it and feeds it back into the bottom of the stack for the next step. This is done through a gated linear unit that blends the recycled hidden state with the new token's embedding — think of it as giving the model a "memory of its last deep thought" as a starting bonus for the next thought. The core architecture, KV cache, and training objective remain unchanged, making this easy to integrate. To train this without losing the parallelism that makes LLM training fast, the authors introduce a "scheduled multi-pass objective": they first train normally, then gradually introduce feedback passes late in pretraining, mixing a small fraction of multi-step feedback for stability.

\[\mathbf{h}_0^{(t)} = \text{GLU}\left([\mathbf{e}(x^{(t)}); \mathbf{h}_L^{(t-1)}]\right)\]
💡 全带宽Transformer的核心公式：将当前新词的嵌入向量与上一步顶层隐藏状态拼接，通过门控线性单元融合，作为当前步的初始输入。 The core fusion step: the new token embedding and the previous top-layer hidden state are concatenated and passed through a gated linear unit (GLU) to form the starting input for this decoding step.
