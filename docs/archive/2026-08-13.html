🎯 提出"ComBodied智能体"范式，让AI真正关注人的状态而非仅完成外部任务
A new AI paradigm that puts the evolving human state at the center of every decision and action.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个真正好的家庭医生，不只是在你挂号时开药，而是记得你上次的状态、预测你下次可能的风险、在你还没开口时就知道你需要什么——ComBodied智能体就是要把这种"懂你的医生"变成AI的设计标准，而不是让AI永远只是一个"只会发提醒的闹钟"。Think of it like the difference between a vending machine and a really great family doctor. ❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有AI智能体分两类：一类是软件智能体（比如发提醒短信的App），一类是实体机器人（能帮你拿药的机器人）。但两类都有一个致命盲点：它们只关注"完成任务"，却从不真正理解"人"本身——比如你为什么没吃药？是忘了、搞混了、有副作用，还是主动拒绝？现有AI无法区分这些情况，更无法提供真正合适的帮助。 Today's AI agents — whether apps sending reminders or robots fetching things — only focus on completing a task. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

ComBodied智能体的工作方式就像一个"懂你的私人顾问"，分四步运转：第一步，通过传感器、可穿戴设备等多种渠道持续感知你的生活事件（比如你今天是否起床、吃饭、锻炼）；第二步，用一个可纠正的长期记忆库记录你的个人历史状态，形成时间上下文；第三步，用"个人世界模型"预测不同干预方式（提醒、送药、联系家人）对你未来状态的影响；第四步，选择最小必要、最符合你意愿的干预方式行动，并把你的反馈重新输入系统形成闭环。整个框架明确避免构建"数字孪生"那样的全量数据副本，而是只保留有目的、有边界、用户可纠正的个人模型。 ComBodied Agents work in a four-step closed loop, like a very attentive personal coach who never forgets your history. Step 1: Perception — sensors, wearables, and apps continuously pick up meaningful life events (e.g., did you sleep well? take your meds?). Step 2: Memory — a long-term, correctable personal history gives the AI temporal context about who you are and how you've been changing. Step 3: Prediction — a "Personal World Model" simulates what would happen under different possible interventions, letting the AI reason about consequences before acting. Step 4: Intervention — the AI selects the least-intrusive, most-appropriate action (a gentle reminder vs. calling a caregiver), respecting your consent and control. Your feedback then updates the loop continuously.

随着AI智能体越来越多地进入医疗、养老、教育等与人类福祉直接相关的场景，"只会完成任务"的AI可能造成真实伤害——比如强迫用户服药、忽视用户真实意愿。这篇论文提供了一套既有技术架构又有伦理边界的设计框架，对于负责任地构建下一代AI产品具有重要的指导价值。 As AI agents enter healthcare, eldercare, and mental health support, "task-first" AI can cause real harm — pushing medication on someone who deliberately refuses, or missing a cry for help hidden behind a pattern of behavior. This paper gives designers and researchers a concrete, ethics-aware blueprint for building AI that genuinely serves people rather than just checking off to-do lists.

Why Does CLAUDE.md Keep Growing? Catastrophic Remembering in Agentic Coding 为什么 CLAUDE.md 越来越长？智能体编程中的"灾难性记忆"现象

🧑‍🔬 Kushal Chakrabarti

图 Figure · Page 3 of paper

🎯 AI指令文件只增不减，加入"注释"可解决这一膨胀问题
AI agent instruction files grow forever — adding "comments" can stop the bloat.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像你家冰箱上贴的便利贴——每次出问题就加一张，但从不撕掉旧的，最后冰箱门被贴得密密麻麻，没人知道哪张还有用。如果当初每张便利贴都写上"为什么贴这个"，你就能轻松判断哪些该撕掉了。Imagine your fridge covered in sticky notes — every time something goes wrong in the kitchen, you slap on a new rule, but you never peel off old ones. Soon the fridge is buried under hundreds of notes and nobody knows which ones still matter. If each note had explained WHY it was written, you could easily toss the outdated ones.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 当你使用像 Claude 这样的 AI 编程助手时，你需要给它一个"说明书"文件（如 CLAUDE.md），告诉它你的项目规则和偏好。问题是：这个文件会不断变长，因为每次出现问题，你就往里加一条规则，但从来不删旧的——万一删错了怎么办？研究发现，这个文件平均增长超过 226%，而且越老的规则越难被删除，就像积灰一样越堆越多，最终变成一堆没人看懂的"祖传代码"。 When you use an AI coding assistant like Claude, you give it a "instructions file" (like CLAUDE.md) that tells it your project's rules. The problem: this file only ever grows. Every time something goes wrong, you add a new rule — but you never delete old ones, because who knows if removing one might break something? The result is a bloated, unmanageable file of rules nobody fully understands anymore.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次命名并系统研究"灾难性记忆"现象：与AI容易"遗忘"的问题相反，AI指令文件会无限"记住"所有东西，越积越多。 • 用真实数据证明这一问题：分析了1,867个代码库中近25万条指令的生命周期，发现净增速为每次提交+4.9条指令。 • 提出简单有效的解法：给指令加上"注释"（说明这条规则为什么存在），可以减少99.3%的多余指令，并将现实中的指令遵循质量提升最高23.1%。 • First to name and study "catastrophic remembering": the opposite of AI forgetting — instruction files grow without limit because nothing is ever safely deleted. • Backed by real data: analyzed nearly 250,000 instruction lifetimes across 1,867 real repositories, proving the bloat is systematic and accelerating. • Simple fix with big impact: adding plain-language "comments" explaining WHY a rule exists removes 99.3% of excess instructions and boosts real-world task performance by up to 23.1%.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究分两部分：第一部分是大规模观察实验，从1,867个真实GitHub代码库中收集了247,694条指令的完整生命周期数据，用统计模型（生存分析）分析指令被删除的概率随时间的变化。第二部分是受控实验，将 IFEval 和 WildIFEval 两个标准评测集"反转"使用，构建已知最优答案的提示词优化任务，对比加注释前后的指令数量和任务完成质量。 The study had two parts: First, a large-scale observational study mining 247,694 instruction lifetimes from 1,867 real GitHub repositories, using survival analysis to track how likely instructions are to be deleted over time. Second, a controlled experiment using two standard benchmarks — IFEval and WildIFEval — flipped "inside out" to create scenarios where the ideal minimal instruction set is already known, allowing precise measurement of how much comments reduce bloat and improve performance.
📊 实验
结果		 • 真实代码库中，AI指令文件平均增长 +226%，每次提交净增 4.9 条指令 • 指令越老，被删除的概率越低（每次提交对数危险率下降 0.032） • 加入注释后，IFEval 反转任务中多余指令从 +211.3% 降至 +1.4%，减少了 99.3% • 在 WildIFEval 真实任务中，指令遵循质量最高提升 23.1% • Real repositories show +226% average instruction growth, with +4.9 net instructions added per commit • Older instructions are significantly less likely to be deleted (log-hazard rate: -0.032 per commit) • With comments added, excess instructions dropped from +211.3% to just +1.4% — a 99.3% reduction on the inverted IFEval benchmark • On real-world WildIFEval tasks, instruction-following performance improved by up to 23.1%
🏁 结论 本文证明了 AI 指令文件的无限膨胀是一个真实且系统性的问题（"灾难性记忆"），并提出了一个极其简单的解法：给指令加注释。这一发现呼吁整个 AI 工程领域像对待代码一样认真对待提示词的维护和文档化。 This paper proves that AI instruction file bloat is a real, systematic problem called "catastrophic remembering," and shows that simply adding explanatory comments can almost entirely solve it. It makes a compelling case that AI prompts deserve the same careful documentation practices we already apply to software code.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

核心思路很简单：就像写代码时要加注释一样，给每条 AI 指令也加上一句"为什么要有这条规则"的说明。这样，当某条规则不再适用时，AI 或人类都能判断它是否还有存在的必要，从而安全地删除它。为了验证这个方法，研究者把现有的"指令遵循评测"任务反过来用——不是测试AI能不能遵循指令，而是用这些任务构造"最优指令集已知"的受控环境，来衡量加注释前后指令数量的变化。 The fix is elegant: just like programmers write comments in code to explain WHY something is written a certain way, we should add comments to AI instruction files explaining the reasoning behind each rule. When the reasoning is visible, it's easy to spot and safely delete outdated rules. To test this, the researcher flipped existing AI instruction-following benchmarks upside down — instead of testing if an AI follows rules, they built controlled scenarios where the "perfect" minimal ruleset is already known, so you can measure exactly how much bloat the comments remove.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着越来越多的团队和个人使用 AI 编程助手，维护一个可管理的指令文件变得越来越重要——膨胀的指令会让 AI 越来越难以正确执行任务。这项研究提供了一个零成本的解决方案（加注释），可以立即被所有 AI 工具用户采用，改善实际开发体验。 As more teams rely on AI coding assistants, bloated instruction files make these tools progressively less reliable and harder to maintain. This research offers a zero-cost fix — just add comments — that any developer can adopt today to keep their AI tools working well long-term.

🧑‍🔬 Zihao Liu, Xiaolong Shen, Zhenglin Zhou, Ruijie Quan et al.

🎯 用视频模型的隐藏信号直接生成动态3D场景，无需重建RGB图像
Reuse video model latents to directly generate dynamic 4D scenes without reconstructing RGB frames.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一位翻译官不再等演讲者说完中文再翻译成英文，而是直接读演讲者的草稿笔记就开始翻译——省去了一个中间环节，翻译更准、速度更快，换个演讲者也照样能用。Think of it like a translator who skips waiting for the speaker to finish talking and instead reads their draft notes directly — cutting out a whole step, making the translation more accurate, and working just as well no matter who the speaker is.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有AI模型想要从文字或图片生成会动的3D场景（即"4D内容"）时，通常得先生成一段普通视频，再用另一个模型把视频"翻译"成3D。这样做就像翻译了两次——每次翻译都会出错，误差不断累积。而且一旦换了视频生成器，整套流程就得重来，非常麻烦。 When AI tries to make animated 3D scenes from text or images, it usually generates a regular video first and then converts that video into 3D — like playing a game of telephone where errors pile up at every step. 💡 解决
的亮点		 • 创新性地跳过RGB视频重建环节，直接从视频模型的"内部信号"（潜在编码）预测4D场景，从根源上消除误差累积。 • 设计了一个可复用的"潜在到4D"接口，只要视频模型使用相同的VAE编码器，同一个解码器无需重新训练即可适配多个不同的视频生成器。 • 仅用约1000个重建片段训练，就能在多个视频扩散Transformer上开箱即用，大幅降低训练成本。 • Skips the RGB video reconstruction step entirely — reads the video model's hidden internal signals (latent codes) directly to produce 4D scenes, cutting out a major source of errors. • Designs a plug-and-play interface: as long as video models share the same encoder (VAE), one single trained decoder works across all of them without retraining. • Trains on only ~1,000 clips yet generalizes to multiple video diffusion transformers, making it lightweight and practical.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在两个专门构建的基准上进行：Text4D-200（200个文本提示驱动的4D生成任务）和 I4D-200（200个图像驱动的4D生成任务）。基线方法是将同款视频生成器先生成RGB视频、再用4D重建模型（Wan+4RC级联）处理的传统流程。评估指标包括投影式 DINO-F1（衡量几何质量）以及人类评分（从几何、时间稳定性和整体质量三个维度打分）。 The team tested on two purpose-built benchmarks: Text4D-200 (200 text prompts asking for animated 3D scenes) and I4D-200 (200 image prompts). 📊 实验

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

视频生成模型在生成视频前，会先在一个叫做"潜在空间"的压缩世界里工作，就像草稿一样。本文的方法叫 Latent-to-4D，它不等视频渲染成像素，而是直接拿这份"草稿"来生成4D场景。具体来说，它把视频的潜在编码与一个预训练的4D解码器的网格对齐，然后用逐帧注意力和全局时空注意力对信号进行精炼，最终直接输出动态3D结构。由于不同视频模型只要共享同一个VAE编码器，它们的"草稿格式"就是一样的，所以这个解码器可以跨多个视频模型复用，完全不需要重新训练。 Video generation models work in a compressed "draft" space called a latent space before they paint any pixels. Latent-to-4D intercepts that draft directly — instead of waiting for the final video, it reads the compressed signal and feeds it into a pretrained 4D decoder. The decoder uses two types of attention (frame-by-frame and across the whole video at once) to turn the draft into a full dynamic 3D scene. Because different video models that use the same encoder produce drafts in the same format, the same decoder works for all of them without any extra training.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

StateFlow把场景想象成一个"活的3D舞台"，分三步运作：第一步"状态构建"——把AI生成的2D图像通过双视角初始化和深度估计，拼合成一个一致的3D世界，就像把多张照片拼成立体模型。第二步"状态演化"——用户只需用自然语言描述想改什么（"把那棵树移到左边"），系统把指令翻译成精确的3D状态变化，只修改涉及的部分，其余场景完整保留。第三步"状态访问"——规划摄像机运动路径时，系统先渲染一帧，看看实际效果是否合理，再反馈调整，直到轨迹既流畅又符合物理逻辑，最终可以调用现成的视频生成模型输出高质量视频。 StateFlow treats a scene like a persistent 3D stage with three working modes. First, "State Construction" takes AI-generated 2D images and stitches them into a coherent 3D world using dual-view depth estimation — like assembling a 3D model from multiple photos. Second, "State Evolution" lets users describe changes in plain language ("move the car to the left"), and the system translates that into targeted 3D updates, leaving the rest of the scene untouched — no full regeneration needed. Third, "State Access" plans camera movements by actually rendering trial frames and checking if they look right, then auto-correcting the path — rather than blindly trusting a language model's text-based guess about what the camera would see.

对影视、游戏、建筑行业的创作者来说，这意味着AI终于可以成为真正的"协作导演"——不再是给一句话吐出一段视频就完事，而是能持续理解你的创作意图并帮你精雕细琢。长远来看，这类框架有望大幅降低影视预生产成本，让独立创作者也能实现好莱坞级别的场景预演。 For creators in film, gaming, and architecture, this means AI can finally act as a true collaborative co-director — not just spitting out a video from a prompt, but helping you iteratively refine every detail of a living 3D scene. In the long run, frameworks like this could dramatically cut pre-production costs and democratize high-quality scene previsualization for independent creators.
