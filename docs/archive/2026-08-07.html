🧑‍🔬 Boxiu Li, Zimo Wen, Yijia Fan, Junxiang Lei et al.

🎯 多角色协作AI系统，边做边学，越用越聪明
A self-improving multi-role AI runtime that learns from experience to tackle long, complex tasks without changing its model weights.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个有四个专家的顾问团队——项目经理、策略师、工程师和质检员——他们不仅合力完成每一项任务，还把每次踩过的坑和发现的捷径记进"团队手册"，下次遇到类似问题时直接翻手册、少走弯路，越做越熟练，但每个人的脑子（模型权重）始终没有变。Think of it like a four-person expert team — a project manager, a strategist, a builder, and a quality inspector — who not only tackle every job together, but also keep a shared notebook of every shortcut discovered and every dead end hit. Next time a similar job comes up, they flip through the notebook and skip the mistakes. The teammates' brains never change, but their shared playbook keeps getting smarter.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有AI助手很难完成需要多步骤、长时间才能解决的复杂任务——比如写一整个软件项目、做数学研究或训练新模型。它们容易在中途迷失方向：一旦走错路，就不知道该如何纠正，也不会把这次的教训记住用于下次。就像一个实习生做了错事，既不会自己改正，下次还会犯同样的错误。 Current AI systems struggle with long, multi-step tasks — like building a whole software project, proving a math theorem, or running a research pipeline over several days. When they hit a dead end or make a mistake, they can't reliably recover, and they forget what went wrong, repeating the same errors next time. There's no system that persistently learns from its own failures without retraining the underlying model.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 四角色协作：Manager（管理）、Planner（规划）、Engineer（执行）、Reviewer（审核）各司其职，像一个真正的工程团队； • 自我进化不改模型权重：通过记录"什么有效、什么无效"来积累经验，不需要重新训练AI； • 双重安全网：验证器自动发现错误，审核循环兜底，34次自动恢复 + 22次严格审核救援； • 越用越高效：成熟阶段比启动阶段节省21%的输入token和15%的工作时间。 • Four specialized roles (Manager, Planner, Engineer, Reviewer) collaborate like a real engineering team, each owning their piece of the work. • Self-evolution without retraining: the system learns from successes and failures by updating persistent memory and policies — the model itself never changes. • Double safety net: automated verifiers catch errors, and strict review loops provide a backstop, logging 34 auto-recoveries and 22 review rescues. • Gets more efficient over time: mature task waves use 21% fewer tokens and 15% less active time compared to early runs.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者在七个不同的GPT-5.5基准测试"竞技场"中评测了Argus，涵盖软件工程（SWE-Bench Pro）、数学推理（AARRI-Bench和数学数据合成）、GPU内核优化和语言模型训练等多个领域。基线对比对象是"Direct Copilot"（直接调用模型的方式）。实验还记录了自我进化前后（启动波 vs 成熟波）的效率变化，以及在真实项目中的表现，包括一个被合并进开源项目的RWKV6内核优化成果。 The team tested Argus across seven benchmark "arenas" using GPT-5.5, covering software engineering (SWE-Bench Pro), mathematical reasoning (AARRI-Bench, mathematical data synthesis), GPU kernel optimization, and language model training tasks. The main baseline was "Direct Copilot" — using the same model without the Argus framework. Experiments also tracked efficiency gains as the system matured (early "startup waves" vs. later "mature waves"), and included real-world deployments like an actual RWKV6 kernel optimization and multi-day math research campaigns.
📊 实验
结果		 • SWE-Bench Pro：Argus达78%，Direct Copilot仅59%，提升约19个百分点，仅多用1.41倍token； • AARRI-Bench（数学推理）：达76.8%； • 数学数据合成任务：领先对手28.0分； • 自我进化效果：成熟阶段比启动阶段少用21%输入token、节省15%工作时间； • 安全网效果：34次验证器自动恢复 + 22次严格审核救援； • 真实项目：6条论文流水线完成254个子任务，16次阶段回滚；优化的RWKV6内核已被上游仓库合并。 • SWE-Bench Pro: Argus hits ~78% vs. 59% for Direct Copilot — a ~19 percentage point jump using only 1.41× the tokens. • AARRI-Bench (math reasoning): 76.8% accuracy. • Mathematical data synthesis: 28.0-point lead over the competition. • Self-evolution efficiency: mature task waves use 21% fewer solve-input tokens and 15% less active workflow time than startup waves. • Safety net: 34 verifier auto-recoveries and 22 strict review-loop rescues recorded. • Real-world: 6 paper pipelines completed 254 missions with 16 stage rollbacks; an optimized RWKV6 GPU kernel was merged into the upstream open-source repo.
🏁 结论 本文证明了一个固定权重的多角色AI系统，可以通过积累经验和持续自我审核，在不重新训练模型的情况下，在复杂的长任务上持续进步并产生可靠结果。Argus为未来AI智能体的自我进化提供了一个可落地的工程范式。 This paper proves that a fixed-weight, multi-role AI system can continuously improve on complex long-horizon tasks — learning from its own successes and failures without ever retraining the underlying model. Argus establishes a practical blueprint for self-evolving AI agents that get better through accumulated experience and rigorous self-review.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Argus把一个大任务拆分给四个"虚拟角色"：管理者负责把握方向、规划者设计步骤、工程师具体执行、审核者检查结果。每次成功或失败的经验（记忆、技能、验证标准、被否决的路径）都会被"存档"，经过角色审核后才能写入系统记忆，就像公司的知识库。下次遇到类似问题时，系统可以直接调用这些经验，既不走老路弯路，又能站在前人肩膀上。模型本身的权重一直不变，进步来自"知识库"的积累。 Argus divides complex work among four specialized roles — Manager sets the goal, Planner makes the step-by-step plan, Engineer does the actual work, and Reviewer checks if it's correct. Every outcome — whether a success, a dead end, or a clever trick discovered along the way — gets saved into a shared memory bank, but only after being reviewed for quality. The next time a similar task comes up, the system draws on those saved lessons instead of starting from scratch. Crucially, the underlying AI model's weights are never changed; all improvement comes from this growing, curated knowledge base and smarter routing policies.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

现实世界中最有价值的任务（写软件、做研究、优化系统）往往需要几天甚至几周才能完成，远超当前AI的能力边界。Argus展示了一条让AI"在工作中成长"的可行路径，为未来更自主、更可靠的AI助手铺路，对软件开发、科研加速等行业影响深远。 The most valuable real-world tasks — writing software, running research, optimizing systems — take days or weeks, far beyond what today's AI can reliably handle. Argus shows a credible path toward AI that genuinely grows on the job, which could transform how software is built, how science is done, and how businesses deploy AI assistants at scale.

🧑‍🔬 Zhongzhi Li, Yucheng Shi, Zongxia Li, Ruhan Wang et al.

图 Figure · Page 7 of paper

🎯 用递归框架自动批量生成高质量终端智能体训练数据，每条仅需$0.05。
A recursive AI pipeline that auto-generates thousands of complex terminal-agent training tasks for just $0.05 each.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像玩游戏自动生成关卡：不用游戏设计师费力手工设计每一关（那太贵了！），系统自动把你刚通过的关卡加点新花样、测试能否通关、再作为下一关放出来——难度越来越高，全程自动化，根本停不下来。Think of it like leveling up in a video game: instead of a game designer hand-crafting every new level (super expensive!), the game automatically takes the last level you beat, adds a new twist, tests that it's actually beatable, and unlocks it as your next challenge — getting harder and harder, all on autopilot.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练AI完成复杂的电脑操作任务（比如写长脚本、执行大量命令、管理文件）需要大量高质量的示例数据，但每个示例靠人工制作可能花费数百甚至数千美元。现有的AI模型也很难自动生成这类数据，因为任务说明、参考答案和验证器三者必须完全一致，这很难自动保证。 Training AI agents to do complex computer tasks (like writing long scripts, running many commands, managing files) requires huge amounts of high-quality example data — but creating each example by hand can cost hundreds or even thousands of dollars. Existing AI models can't reliably auto-generate these examples either, because the instructions, solutions, and checkers all have to perfectly match each other, and that consistency is hard to guarantee automatically.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 递归自举：系统不从零开始造任务，而是拿一个已验证的任务延伸变难，再验证，再作为下一轮的起点——像踩台阶一样一步步升级难度。 • 超低成本大规模生成：15轮共生成37,484个任务，每条约$0.05，而人工制作每条需数百至数千美元。 • 难度自动增长：参考答案中位数从67行增长到374行，顶尖AI模型的通过率从90%跌至2.5%，任务真实地越来越难，无需人工筛选。 • Recursive bootstrapping: instead of creating tasks from scratch every time, the system takes a task it already verified, extends it, re-checks it, and then uses the new harder task as the starting point for the next round — like climbing a staircase one verified step at a time. • Dirt cheap at scale: 37,484 tasks generated across 15 rounds at roughly $0.05 each, versus hundreds or thousands of dollars per human-authored task. • Difficulty grows automatically: median solution length grows from 67 to 374 lines over 15 rounds, and even a top AI model's success rate drops from 90% to just 2.5% — meaning the tasks genuinely get harder without any human curation.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者用RST框架在15轮递归中生成了37,484个任务，再用这些任务训练AI模型。他们对Qwen3.5的两个版本（27B和122B参数量）进行了监督微调，并对Qwen3.5-27B额外做了强化学习（PPO）训练。训练后的模型在三个基准测试上评估：Terminal-Bench 2、Terminal-Bench Hard和Long-Horizon Terminal Bench，与原始基础模型及其他强基线模型对比。 The researchers generated 37,484 tasks over 15 recursive rounds using RST, then used these tasks to train AI models. They fine-tuned two versions of the Qwen3.5 model (27B and 122B parameters) using supervised learning on trajectories collected from the synthesized tasks, and also applied reinforcement learning (PPO) to Qwen3.5-27B. The trained models were evaluated on three benchmarks: Terminal-Bench 2, Terminal-Bench Hard, and Long-Horizon Terminal Bench, comparing against the original base models and other strong baseline models.
📊 实验
结果		 用RST生成数据进行微调后，Qwen3.5-27B和Qwen3.5-122B-A10B在三个基准测试上均提升最多10个百分点。强化学习（PPO）让Qwen3.5-27B在Terminal-Bench 2达到49.44%，Terminal-Bench Hard达到32.00%，Long-Horizon Terminal Bench达到22.07%，相较基础模型分别提升20.0%、41.2%和21.9%。任务难度也可靠增长：DeepSeek-V4-Pro的pass@4从第1轮的90%跌至第15轮的2.5%，而合成产出率和验证通过率在全部15轮中保持稳定。 Fine-tuning on RST-generated data improved Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3.5-122B-A10B by up to 10 percentage points on all three benchmarks. Reinforcement learning (PPO) pushed Qwen3.5-27B to 49.44% on Terminal-Bench 2, 32.00% on Terminal-Bench Hard, and 22.07% on Long-Horizon Terminal Bench — representing relative gains of 20.0%, 41.2%, and 21.9% over the base model respectively. Task difficulty also scaled reliably: DeepSeek-V4-Pro's pass@4 dropped from 90% at Round 1 to just 2.5% at Round 15, while synthesis yield and validation rates stayed stable throughout all 15 rounds.
🏁 结论 RST证明了可以用极低成本自动大批量生成高质量、难度递增的终端智能体训练数据，并且用这些数据训练AI确实能显著提升其完成复杂电脑任务的能力。15轮之后递归过程毫无见顶迹象，说明这个方法还可以继续扩展。 RST proves that you can automatically mass-produce high-quality, progressively harder terminal-agent training tasks at a tiny fraction of the cost of human authoring, and that training on these tasks meaningfully improves AI agent performance on complex computer tasks. The recursive process shows no sign of hitting a ceiling after 15 rounds, suggesting it can scale even further.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

这个叫做RST（递归合成终端任务）的框架，从一小批人工验证的"种子任务"出发。它让大语言模型把种子任务的解决方案延伸得更长更复杂，同时重写任务说明和验证器让三者保持一致，然后在一个全新的沙盒（隔离的虚拟电脑环境）里运行，确认一切真正能跑通。验证通过的任务成为下一轮的新种子——每一轮都在上一轮基础上继续升级。这个循环跑了15轮，每一轮都比上一轮难。 The framework, called RST (Recursive Synthetic Terminal Tasks), starts from a small set of human-verified "seed" tasks. It then asks a large language model to extend the solution of a seed task to make it longer and more complex, rewrites the instruction and the verifier to match the new solution, and runs everything inside a fresh sandbox (a clean isolated computer environment) to confirm it actually works. Tasks that pass validation become new seeds for the next round — so every round builds on the last. This loop runs for 15 rounds, each time producing harder tasks than the previous.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{pass@4}_{R_1} = 90\%,\quad \text{pass@4}_{R_{15}} = 2.5\%\]
💡 DeepSeek-V4-Pro在第1轮任务上4次尝试通过率为90%，到第15轮仅剩2.5%，直观说明任务难度随轮次真实大幅增长。 DeepSeek-V4-Pro's chance of solving a task in 4 tries drops from 90% at Round 1 to just 2.5% at Round 15 — a concrete proof that the tasks are genuinely getting much harder each round.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

构建能自主操作电脑的AI助手——写代码、执行命令、管理文件——需要海量训练数据，但这类数据历来造价极高。RST将成本砍去99%以上，并消除了人工瓶颈，有望大幅加速真正能帮你干活的AI智能体的研发进程。 Building AI assistants that can autonomously operate computers — writing code, running commands, managing files — requires massive amounts of training data that has historically been too expensive to create. RST slashes that cost by 99%+ and removes the human bottleneck, potentially accelerating the development of truly capable AI agents that can do real computer work on your behalf.

$ω$-0: A Latent Predictive World Action Model for Concurrent Humanoid Loco-Manipulation ω-0：一种用于并发人形移动操作的潜在预测世界行动模型

🧑‍🔬 Zhe Li, Zhenzhe Zhang, Yangyang Wei, Wenjie Zhang et al.

图 Figure · Page 1 of paper

🎯 让人形机器人边走边操作物体，像人一样协调全身
A single AI model that lets humanoid robots walk and manipulate objects simultaneously like a human.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个经验丰富的服务员，能一边穿梭在拥挤的餐厅里，一边稳稳地端着托盘送菜——而现有的机器人要么只会走路，要么只会端盘子，ω-0 让机器人第一次真正学会了"两手都要硬"。Think of it like a skilled waiter who weaves through a busy restaurant while perfectly balancing a tray of drinks — current robots can either walk OR carry things, but ω-0 trains them to do both at once, just like the waiter.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有的人形机器人AI通常把"走路"和"动手"分开处理，就像一个人要么专心走路、要么停下来才能拿东西。这导致机器人在需要同时移动和操作物体的家务任务中表现很差。此外，现有的"世界模型"要么只关注手臂动作，要么需要生成完整的未来视频，计算量大且效率低。 Existing humanoid robot AI systems typically treat walking and arm movements as two separate problems — like a person who can only focus on one thing at a time. This makes them clumsy at real household chores that require moving and grabbing things simultaneously. Also, current "world models" (AI that imagines the future to plan actions) are either arm-only or waste computing power generating full future videos.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 全身协调：ω-0 是首个将移动、平衡、姿态调整和物体操作统一为"一套动作"的全身世界行动模型。 • 轻量级未来预测：不生成未来视频，而是只学习紧凑的"未来观测嵌入"，既保留了视觉预见能力，又大幅减少计算量。 • 多视角输入支持：同时支持第一人称RGB、第三人称RGB和第三人称深度图，灵活适配不同传感器配置。 • ω-HOME 数据集：收集了超过40小时真实家庭场景的人形机器人数据，含多视角图像、全身SMPL动作、机器人状态和动作潜变量，是该领域重要的开源资源。 • Whole-body unity: ω-0 is the first model to unify walking, balancing, posture, and object manipulation into one coordinated action stream. • Lightweight future prediction: Instead of generating expensive future videos, it learns compact "future observation embeddings" — just enough foresight to act smartly. • Flexible multi-view inputs: Works with first-person RGB cameras, third-person RGB, and depth sensors — adaptable to real-world setups. • ω-HOME dataset: A 40+ hour real-world household humanoid dataset with synchronized multi-view video, whole-body motion, and action data — a valuable community resource.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在11个真实家庭任务上进行，包括拿取物品、整理桌面等需要边走边操作的场景。对比基线包括：模仿学习方法（ACT、Diffusion Policy）、视觉-语言-行动模型（OpenVLA、π0）、专用人形机器人策略以及世界行动模型（WAM）。评估指标为任务成功率，同时定性评估动作的流畅性和协调性。数据集使用自建的ω-HOME（40+小时真实数据）以及控制器辅助的仿真回放数据。 Experiments were run on 11 real-world household tasks requiring simultaneous movement and manipulation (e.g., fetching objects while walking). The model was compared against imitation learning baselines (ACT, Diffusion Policy), vision-language-action models (OpenVLA, π0), dedicated humanoid policies, and a world-action model (WAM) baseline. Task success rate was the main metric, along with qualitative assessment of motion smoothness. The ω-HOME dataset (40+ hours of real humanoid data) was used for training, supplemented by simulation-replayed human motion data.
📊 实验
结果		 ω-0 在11个家庭任务中的整体成功率显著优于所有基线方法。相比代表性模仿学习方法（如ACT、Diffusion Policy）、视觉-语言-行动模型（OpenVLA、π0）和专用人形策略，ω-0 均取得最高成功率。在需要并发移动和操作的任务中，ω-0 产生的动作更流畅、协调性更好，其他基线方法在此类任务中频繁出现停顿或失衡现象。（具体数值以论文正文实验表格为准，摘要中未列出精确数字。） ω-0 achieved the highest task success rate across all 11 household tasks compared to every baseline tested, including ACT, Diffusion Policy, OpenVLA, π0, dedicated humanoid policies, and the WAM baseline. The model consistently produced smooth "manipulate-while-moving" behaviors where other methods frequently stalled or lost balance. Exact per-task success rate numbers are reported in the paper's experiment tables; the abstract confirms consistent outperformance across all representative baselines.
🏁 结论 ω-0 证明了一个统一的世界行动模型可以让人形机器人像人类一样，在移动的同时自然地操作物体，而无需将这两项能力分开训练。这是迈向真正能在家庭环境中自主工作的人形机器人的重要一步。 ω-0 proves that a single unified AI model can teach humanoid robots to walk and manipulate objects at the same time — as naturally as a human carries groceries while walking. This is a significant step toward humanoid robots that can truly work autonomously in everyday home environments.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

ω-0 接收语言指令（如"去冰箱拿一瓶水"）、当前摄像头画面和机器人自身的状态感知，然后用一个统一模型同时预测未来的"视觉感知压缩表示"和"全身动作控制指令"。预测未来视觉不是为了生成真实视频画面，而是让模型学会"脑补"接下来会看到什么，从而做出更好的动作决策。动作生成部分采用扩散模型（类似AI绘画的技术），将"想象中的未来"转化为机器人可以直接执行的全身动作控制信号。另外，研究团队用模拟器把人类的动作视频"翻译"成机器人可执行的动作，解决真实数据稀少的问题。 ω-0 takes in three things: a language instruction (e.g., "pick up the cup and bring it here"), what the robot currently sees from its cameras, and the robot's own body-state sensors. A single unified model then predicts two things at once: a compact "sketch" of what the robot will see next (not a full video, just a summary), and the full-body action commands to execute. This future-imagining helps the robot plan better movements. The action commands are generated using a diffusion model — the same family of AI that powers image generators — to produce smooth, coordinated whole-body motions. ⚙️ Method · 方法详解

这个方法的核心思路是"通过对比来学习"。研究者为主模型创建两个"教师版本"：正面教师看的是放大清晰的图片（被迫认真看图），负面教师看的是被大量遮挡的图片（被迫只靠文字猜测）。通过比较这两个教师在推理时的差异——哪些词语是正面教师更自信而负面教师不确定的——系统就能找到"视觉信息真正起作用"的关键时刻。主模型的训练就专注在这些关键时刻：向正面教师靠拢，远离负面教师。这样模型就被引导着真正去"看图"，而不是偷懒依赖文字。 The key idea is to teach the model by comparison. The researchers create two "teacher" versions of the same model: a Positive Teacher that sees a zoomed-in, detail-rich version of the image (so it's forced to use visual info), and a Negative Teacher that sees a heavily masked/blurred image (so it's forced to guess from text alone). By comparing how these two teachers reason and which words (tokens) they produce with high confidence, the method identifies moments where visual information truly matters. These high-value moments are used to guide the main model's training — specifically, the model is nudged to produce outputs that look more like the visually-informed Positive Teacher and less like the text-only Negative Teacher. This "trust region" of visually important tokens becomes the sweet spot for self-distillation training.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\mathcal{M}_{pos} = \log P_{pos}(t) - \log P_{neg}(t)\]
💡 模态平衡logit差值：正面教师（看清晰图）和负面教师（看遮挡图）对同一个词（token）预测概率的对数差，差值越大说明该词越依赖视觉信息，越值得用来训练。 Modality-Balance Logit Margin: the log-probability difference between the Positive Teacher (clear image) and Negative Teacher (masked image) for each token. A larger margin means that token relies more on visual info, making it a high-value training signal.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI助手越来越多地处理图像相关任务——比如读取医学影像、分析图表、理解视觉文档——让AI真正"看懂"图片而非靠猜测变得至关重要。OPD-V提供了一种即插即用的改进方案，让现有多模态AI更真实地利用视觉信息，且训练成本更低，对开发和部署这类系统的人来说是一个实实在在的进步。 As AI assistants increasingly handle real-world tasks involving images — like reading medical scans, analyzing charts, or understanding visual documents — it's critical they actually use visual information rather than just guessing from text. OPD-V offers a plug-and-play improvement that makes existing multimodal AI systems genuinely more visually aware, with lower training costs — a practical win for anyone building or deploying these systems.
