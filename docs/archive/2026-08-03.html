AskChem: Claim-Centered Infrastructure for Chemistry Literature Synthesis AskChem：以声明为中心的化学文献综合基础设施

🧑‍🔬 Bing Yan, Gregory Wolfe, Stefano Martiniani, Kyunghyun Cho

图 Figure · Page 3 of paper

🎯 将百万化学论文拆解为可溯源的原子声明，实现跨文献精准检索
AskChem turns millions of chemistry papers into searchable, source-verified atomic claims for AI-powered literature synthesis.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy想象一下，你去图书馆查资料，以前图书管理员只给你一摞书让你自己找；现在AskChem把所有书里的每一个知识点都抄在单独的卡片上，每张卡片注明出处页码，你只需说出你的问题，管理员直接递给你最相关的那几张卡片——这就是AskChem的核心思路。Imagine if instead of searching Google and getting a list of websites, you got a perfectly organized index card for every single fact on the internet — each card shows you the exact fact, the exact page it came from, and a direct link back to the original source. AskChem does exactly that, but for chemistry research papers.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 当化学家或AI系统想回答一个研究问题时，现有工具只会返回一堆论文列表，研究人员还得自己一篇篇读，手动找到关键信息，再核实来源。这就像去图书馆查一个问题，图书管理员只给你一堆书，却不告诉你答案在哪一页。 When a chemist or AI agent wants to answer a research question, they currently get back a list of papers — and then have to read through all of them manually to find the specific facts they need. Verifying where each fact came from (its source) is tedious and error-prone. There's no easy way to search for specific findings across thousands of papers at once.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 检索单位从"论文"变成"声明"——每条声明都是一个具体事实，附带原文引用和来源DOI，可直接溯源。 • 构建了三种互补的组织结构：层级分类体系（方便浏览）、证据图谱（连接相关声明）、动态分类体系（将论文与科学原理关联）。 • 支持网页界面、REST API、SDK和MCP接口，AI智能体可直接调用，实现自动化文献综合。 • Instead of returning whole papers, AskChem retrieves individual "claims" — single, verifiable facts each tied to a specific source quote and DOI. • It organizes claims into three complementary structures: a hierarchical taxonomy for browsing, an evidence graph linking related claims, and a living taxonomy that connects papers to scientific principles. • It works not just for humans via a web UI, but also as an API for AI agents — making it plug-and-play for LLM-powered research tools.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队在自建的AskChem-Bench基准上评测，该基准专门测试系统回答化学问题时提供正确引文的能力。将AskChem与另外四种检索系统（以及无检索基线）进行对比，所有系统都以GPT-4.5作为上层阅读模型。主要评估指标是：DOI可解析率（引用的文献是否真实存在可访问）和引文密度（每条回答包含的有效引文数量）。 The authors evaluated AskChem on a benchmark called AskChem-Bench, which tests a system's ability to answer chemistry questions with properly sourced citations. They compared AskChem against five different retrieval systems (including no retrieval at all) by using GPT-4.5 as the reader model on top of each system. Key metrics included the percentage of resolvable DOIs (i.e., citations that actually point to real, retrievable papers) and citation density (how many valid citations appear per answer).
📊 实验
结果		 使用AskChem辅助的GPT-4.5实现了100%的DOI可解析率（每条引文都指向真实可访问的论文），而无检索基线仅为88.3%。在五个对比系统中，AskChem的引文密度最高，即每条回答包含的有效引文数量最多。 When GPT-4.5 was grounded with AskChem, it achieved 100% resolvable DOIs — meaning every single citation in its answers pointed to a real, accessible paper — compared to only 88.3% without any retrieval system. AskChem also achieved the highest citation density among all five systems tested, meaning its answers contained the most relevant, verifiable references per response.
🏁 结论 AskChem证明，将检索单位从整篇论文转变为可溯源的原子声明，能显著提升AI回答化学文献问题的准确性和可追溯性。它为人类研究者和AI智能体提供了一套实用的开放基础设施，可从数百万篇化学论文中高效综合可靠知识。 AskChem proves that shifting the unit of retrieval from whole papers to individual, source-verified claims dramatically improves the accuracy and traceability of AI-generated chemistry literature answers. It provides a practical, open infrastructure that both human researchers and AI agents can use to synthesize reliable knowledge from millions of chemistry papers.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

AskChem先把每篇化学论文"拆解"成一条条最小单位的"声明"，比如"化合物X在Y条件下反应收率提高了Z%"。每条声明都标注类型（实验结果、假设等），附上原文原句和论文DOI，确保可追溯。这240万条声明来自14.7万篇论文，再被组织成三种结构：固定分类体系（按主题浏览）、证据图谱（连接相互支持或矛盾的声明）、动态生长的分类体系（将论文归入更宏观的科学原理）。用户提问时，系统直接返回最相关的声明，而非整篇论文，自动拼出一个有来源的跨文献答案。 AskChem first reads each chemistry paper and breaks it down into "atomic claims" — the smallest meaningful facts, like "compound X increases reaction yield by Y%." Each claim is tagged with its type (e.g., experimental result, hypothesis), linked to the exact quote in the original paper, and stamped with the paper's DOI so you can always trace it back. These 2.4 million claims from 147,000 papers are then organized into three overlapping structures: a fixed taxonomy for browsing by topic, a graph that links claims sharing evidence or contradicting each other, and a growing taxonomy that places papers under broader scientific principles. When a user asks a question, the system retrieves the most relevant individual claims — not whole papers — and assembles a sourced, cross-paper answer.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI智能体越来越多地被用于科学研究，确保其引用真实可验证的来源至关重要——AskChem直接解决了化学领域AI"幻觉引文"的问题。这套基础设施可帮助研究人员和AI工具快速综合全球已发表的化学文献成果，有望加速药物发现、材料科学和化学工程的进展。 As AI agents are increasingly used to assist in scientific research, ensuring they cite real, verifiable sources is critical — AskChem directly solves the "hallucinated citations" problem for chemistry. This infrastructure could accelerate drug discovery, materials science, and chemical engineering by letting researchers and AI tools instantly synthesize findings across the entire published literature.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Qwen-UI-Agent 的核心思路是"全能选手+自我进化"。首先，它把手机、电脑、网页三类操作统一成一套动作语言，让同一个模型可以跨平台工作。其次，它建立了一个"数据飞轮"：用AI自己来生成训练任务、模拟各种场景、诊断失败原因，再把经验反馈给模型，形成不断自我提升的闭环，大大减少对人工标注数据的依赖。训练阶段采用强化学习，支持超长操作序列（100步以上），并用超过1万个并行虚拟环境同时训练来提升效率。最后，一个轻量级的"执行层"负责连接AI决策和真实设备操作，让智能体能在真实手机上稳定运行，还能主动触发有用的服务。 Qwen-UI-Agent works by unifying all device interactions — tapping a screen, typing a command, clicking a browser button — into one shared language that a single AI model understands. Instead of hiring humans to label thousands of training examples, the system builds a "data flywheel": the AI itself generates new tasks, runs them in simulated environments, spots its own failures, and feeds those lessons back into training. This creates a self-improving loop. During training, reinforcement learning is used so the agent can practice on tasks with over 100 steps, running in 10,000+ virtual environments at once to learn faster. A lightweight "harness layer" bridges the AI's decisions to real phones and computers, enabling stable real-device operation and proactive task initiation.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项工作让"AI帮你操控手机和电脑干活"从科幻变成现实——不只是聊天，而是真正替你预订、搜索、发送、填表。对于普通用户，这意味着未来手机里的AI助手将能跨App自动完成繁琐事务；对于AI产业，它提供了一套可扩展、自我进化的智能体训练范式。 This work brings us one giant step closer to AI assistants that don't just chat but actually do things on your devices — booking tickets, filling forms, and managing workflows across apps without you lifting a finger. For the AI industry, it provides a scalable, self-improving training framework that could rapidly accelerate the development of real-world AI agents in consumer products.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

核心思路是让AI像"进化育种"一样改进代码：定义四个基本操作——起草新方案、改进现有代码、调试错误、交叉融合两段代码的优点——然后用真实代码执行反馈来训练模型（先做监督学习，再做强化学习）。推理时，系统把这四个操作组成一棵"搜索树"，AI在12小时内不断尝试不同路径，挑出得分最高的方案。整个训练数据与评估基准严格去重，保证测试公平。模型学到的这些技能还能迁移到全新的任务领域，无需重新训练。 The core idea is to treat code improvement like biological evolution. Four basic moves — Draft a new solution, Improve an existing one, Debug a broken one, and Crossover two good ones — are taught to the AI using real code execution results as feedback (first via supervised learning, then reinforcement learning). At inference time, these four moves are composed into a long search tree: the AI keeps trying combinations over 12 hours, guided by actual scores from running the code. Training data is carefully deduplicated against all test benchmarks to keep evaluation honest, and learned skills transfer to brand-new task domains without retraining.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{Medal Average} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{1}[\text{task}_i \text{ earns a medal}]\]

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

PhiZero分两步工作，就像一个先写故事大纲再写正文的作家。第一步，它观看大量真实世界视频，自动学习一套"物理词汇"——用简短的抽象符号描述场景从一个时刻到下一个时刻如何变化（比如"某物向左落下"、"物体发生碰撞"），全程无需人工标注。第二步，当需要预测未来时，PhiZero先生成一串物理符号序列（推理步骤），再把这个抽象"剧本"交给视频渲染器，转化为真实的画面像素。因为物理规律在画像素之前就已经想好了，生成的视频在物理上更加自洽合理。 PhiZero works in two stages, like a storyteller who outlines the plot before writing the full novel. First, it watches lots of real-world videos and learns a vocabulary of "physical tokens" — short, abstract codes that describe how objects and scenes transition from one moment to the next (e.g., "something falls left," "object collides"). This is done without any human labels. Second, when asked to predict the future, PhiZero first generates a sequence of these physical tokens (the reasoning step), and only then feeds that sequence to a video renderer that turns the abstract plan into actual pixels. Because physics is figured out before pixels are painted, the resulting videos are much more physically coherent.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

AI越来越多地被用于模拟物理环境——从机器人训练、游戏引擎到自动驾驶和科学研究，真正理解物理规律（而非只是复制视觉模式）的模型至关重要。PhiZero的方法能让AI仿真器更加可靠和安全，因为它是在显式推理物理规律，而不是"凭感觉"生成看似合理却违背物理的结果。 As AI is increasingly used to simulate physical environments — for robotics training, game engines, autonomous driving, and scientific research — models that genuinely understand physics (not just copy visual patterns) are critical. PhiZero's approach could make AI simulators far more reliable and safe, since they reason about physics explicitly rather than hallucinating plausible-looking but physically wrong outcomes.
