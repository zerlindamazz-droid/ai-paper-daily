🎯 用智能体链式推理自动评估世界模型，让每个分数都有据可查
An agent-based pipeline that evaluates world models with transparent, reasoning-backed verdicts instead of just a number.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一位优秀的电影评论家不只给电影打一颗星，而是详细分析剧情漏洞、特效失真和逻辑错误——HarnessEval-W就是AI世界模型的"专业影评团队"，每个评判都有理有据，让人心服口服。Think of it like replacing a movie rating app (that just shows 3/5 stars) with a panel of expert critics — one checks the plot logic, one checks the special effects, one checks the acting — and they all write up detailed notes before agreeing on a final score you can actually trust and learn from.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 AI"世界模型"尝试模拟物理世界的运作，比如预测视频或游戏中接下来会发生什么。但要自动判断它们的输出是否正确（比如物理是否合理？因果关系是否成立？）非常困难。现有的评测基准只给出一个数字分数，没有任何解释——就像老师批作文只打分、不写评语，你完全不知道哪里出了问题。 AI "world models" try to simulate how the physical world works — predicting what happens next in a video or game. But judging whether their outputs are correct (e.g., does the physics look right? does cause and effect make sense?) is hard to automate. Current benchmarks just spit out a single score with no explanation, like a teacher marking your essay with only a number and no comments — you have no idea what went wrong or why.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • HarnessEval-W不只给一个分数，而是生成完整的"证据树"——清晰展示世界模型为何通过或失败的推理链。 • 采用"主智能体+子智能体"分层架构：主体负责分解问题，每个子智能体配备专属工具攻克各自的子任务。 • 整个流程开源，并随着世界模型的发展持续扩展新的评估技能和案例。 • Instead of a single score, HarnessEval-W produces a full "evidence tree" — a traceable chain of reasoning showing exactly why a world model passed or failed. • It uses a boss-agent that breaks down each evaluation into sub-tasks and spawns specialized helper agents, each tackling one piece of the puzzle with the right tools. • The whole pipeline is open-source and designed to grow over time as world models become more capable.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者将HarnessEval-W应用于18个代表性世界模型，覆盖330个评估案例，涵盖多种视频生成场景。他们将流程的判断结果与人类偏好进行对比，以衡量一致性。评估内容涵盖物理合理性、因果关系和世界状态一致性等维度。该基准被设计为可扩展的，欢迎社区贡献新的评估技能和案例。 The researchers applied HarnessEval-W to 18 representative world models across 330 evaluation cases covering diverse video generation scenarios. They compared the pipeline's judgments against human preferences to measure alignment. The evaluation covers aspects like physical plausibility, causality, and world state consistency. The benchmark was designed to be extensible, inviting community contributions for new evaluation skills and cases.

结果		 HarnessEval-W在18个世界模型、330个评估案例上进行了测试，其判断结果与人类偏好高度一致。论文展示了对每个生成序列细粒度、可验证的诊断能力。摘要中未列出具体的一致性百分比，但系统在物理、因果和世界状态等维度上均达到接近人类评判的质量水平。 HarnessEval-W was evaluated on 18 world models over 330 cases, with its judgments shown to closely align with human preferences. The paper demonstrates fine-grained, verifiable diagnoses for every generated rollout. Specific alignment scores or percentage agreement with human raters are not detailed in the abstract, but the system is positioned as achieving human-comparable evaluation quality across physics, causality, and world-state dimensions.
🏁 结论 HarnessEval-W证明了世界模型的评估可以超越盲目打分——多智能体推理流程不仅能媲美人类判断，还能为每个结论提供完全透明、可检验的证据链，为世界模型领域的可信基准测试树立了新标准。 HarnessEval-W proves that world model evaluation can go beyond blind scoring — a multi-agent reasoning pipeline can match human judgment while providing a fully transparent, inspectable chain of evidence for every verdict. This sets a new standard for trustworthy benchmarking in the world model field.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

HarnessEval-W的工作方式就像一支专业调查团队。一个"主智能体"读取每个评估案例，判断需要检查哪些内容，并将问题拆解为更小的、可量化的子任务。然后将每个子任务分配给专属的"子智能体"——就像各领域的专家顾问，每人配备专属诊断工具（比如检查运动物理、物体一致性或因果逻辑）。子智能体收集证据后向上汇报，主智能体审核所有证据、验证结果，最终给出带有完整推理的判决。每一步都有记录，人类可以随时检查完整的决策链。 HarnessEval-W works like a smart investigation team. A lead "parent" agent reads each evaluation case, figures out what needs to be checked, and breaks the question into smaller, measurable sub-problems. It then assigns each sub-problem to a specialized "sub-agent" — think of these as expert consultants, each with their own diagnostic tools (e.g., for checking motion physics, object consistency, or causal logic). Each sub-agent gathers evidence and reports back. The parent agent then reviews all the evidence, validates it, and writes a final verdict with full reasoning. Every step is logged, so humans can inspect the entire decision trail.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI世界模型在游戏、机器人、自动驾驶和科学仿真中的广泛应用，我们需要能发现物理和因果细微错误的评估工具，而不只是给个数字了事。HarnessEval-W为研究者和开发者提供了透明、可信的方式，清楚了解模型在哪里成功、在哪里失败，从而加速更安全、更智能的AI发展。 As AI world models are increasingly used in gaming, robotics, autonomous driving, and scientific simulation, we need evaluation tools that can catch subtle errors in physics and causality — not just rubber-stamp outputs with a number. HarnessEval-W gives researchers and developers a transparent, trustworthy way to understand exactly where their models succeed and fail, accelerating safer and smarter AI development.

🎯 自动把训练精力集中在未掌握目标上，告别"重复刷已会的题"
SA-MRPO dynamically focuses training effort on unsolved objectives instead of wasting it on already-mastered ones.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个聪明的家教老师，不会让学生反复练已经弹得很熟的曲子，而是时刻观察哪首曲子还没练好，把大部分练习时间都集中在最需要提升的地方——这样学生进步才会更快、更有效率。Think of it like a personal tutor who watches a student practice piano. Instead of making them drill the easy songs they already play perfectly, the tutor constantly checks which pieces still need work and spends most of the lesson time there — so the student improves much faster where it actually counts.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练AI语言模型时，如果同时有多个目标（比如"答案要对"又要"格式简洁"），现有方法会把所有目标以固定比例混在一起，不管哪个目标已经学好了、哪个还没学好，都一视同仁地优化。结果就是：模型一直在"刷已经会的题"，真正困难的地方却没有得到足够关注，训练资源被白白浪费了。 When training AI language models with multiple goals at once (e.g., "be correct" AND "be concise"), existing methods blindly mix all goals together with fixed weights before deciding what to improve. This means the AI keeps spending effort polishing skills it has already mastered, while neglecting the goals it still struggles with — like a student who only revises the subjects they are already good at instead of the ones they are failing.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 发现了一个隐藏缺陷：固定权重混合奖励会导致不同训练样本获得相同的"紧迫度分数"，即使它们的学习需求差异巨大。 • 提出"饱和度检测"：系统在每个批次中实时测量每个目标的"学习饱和程度"，自动减少对已掌握目标的投入。 • 证明了聪明的重加权不仅能改变学习的力度，甚至能改变学习的方向（正负号），这比以往方法的作用更为根本。 • Identifies a hidden flaw: fixed reward mixing causes different training examples to get the same "urgency score" even when their actual learning needs differ sharply. • Introduces saturation detection: the system measures how "full" each objective is at the batch level, and automatically dials down effort on already-satisfied goals. • Shows that smart reweighting can actually flip the direction of a learning update — not just change its size — which is a qualitatively stronger effect than prior work.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者在三类真实任务上测试了SA-MRPO：数学推理（包含2个和3个奖励目标的设置）、自适应推理（模型需要自行决定是否逐步思考）、以及代码生成。主要对比基线是GDPO（群体分布策略优化），在包括AIME24、AMC23在内的15个数学基准上进行评估，同时通过通过率评估代码生成基准的表现。 The researchers tested SA-MRPO on three real-world AI tasks: mathematical reasoning (with two-objective and three-objective reward setups), adaptive reasoning (where the model must decide when to think step-by-step), and coding. They compared against GDPO (Group Distributional Policy Optimization) as the main baseline, evaluated across 15 mathematical benchmarks including AIME24 and AMC23, and also measured coding performance via pass rates on standard coding benchmarks.
📊 实验
结果		 SA-MRPO在15个数学基准中的12个上超越GDPO（更难的正确性目标），AIME24上最高提升5%。在自适应推理任务上，5个基准全部提升，平均提高3.8%，AMC23上最高提升9.2%。代码生成任务通过率最高提升2.3%。在所有场景中，已经掌握的简单目标保持原有水平，没有出现性能倒退。 SA-MRPO outperforms GDPO on the harder correctness objective in 12 out of 15 mathematical benchmark comparisons, with gains of up to 5% on AIME24. On adaptive reasoning, it improves accuracy on all five benchmarks — an average gain of 3.8%, and up to 9.2% on AMC23. For coding tasks, pass rate improves by up to 2.3%. Crucially, in all settings, the easier (already-mastered) objectives remain near their previously achieved levels, showing no regression.
🏁 结论 SA-MRPO证明了：在强化学习训练中，实时检测并降低已掌握目标的权重，能显著提升模型在更难目标上的表现，同时不损失已有能力。这为大语言模型的多奖励策略优化提供了一种实用、即插即用的改进方案。 SA-MRPO proves that dynamically detecting and deprioritizing already-mastered learning objectives during RL training leads to measurably better performance on harder goals, without sacrificing what the model already knows. It offers a practical, plug-in improvement to multi-reward policy optimization for large language models.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

SA-MRPO分三步工作。第一步，不再把所有奖励信号混成一个数字，而是对每个目标分别做标准化，让每个目标都有自己独立的"成绩单"。第二步，在每个训练批次中统计有多少样本在某个目标上已经表现良好，以此估算该目标的"饱和度"——如果几乎所有人都拿满分，说明这个目标已经"学透了"。第三步，自动降低饱和目标的权重，同时提升对尚未掌握目标的关注度，每批次动态调整训练资源分配，让模型始终聚焦在最需要提升的地方。 SA-MRPO works in three steps. First, instead of blending all reward signals into one number before scoring, it standardizes each reward goal separately — so each goal gets its own fair "report card." Second, it estimates how saturated each goal is by looking at how many training examples in the current batch already score well on that goal; if almost everyone aces it, it's considered saturated. Third, it automatically reduces the weight of saturated goals and boosts attention toward under-achieved ones, dynamically reshuffling the training budget every batch so the model always focuses on its remaining weak spots.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[A_i = \sum_k w_k \cdot \tilde{r}_{i,k}, \quad \tilde{r}_{i,k} = \frac{r_{i,k} - \mu_k}{\sigma_k}\]
💡 SA-MRPO对每个奖励目标k单独做标准化（减均值除标准差），再用动态权重w_k加权合并成最终的优势值A_i，而非像旧方法那样先混合再标准化。 SA-MRPO standardizes each reward objective k independently (subtract mean, divide by std), then combines them with dynamic weights w_k into the final advantage score A_i — instead of mixing first and standardizing after like old methods.
\[w_k \propto 1 - \hat{s}_k, \quad \hat{s}_k = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}|} \sum_{i \in \mathcal{B}} \mathbf{1}[r_{i,k} = r_{k}^{\max}]\]
💡 饱和度\hat{s}_k是当前批次中在目标k上已达到最高分的样本比例；权重w_k与（1-饱和度）成正比，饱和度越高、权重越低，训练自动聚焦在未掌握的目标上。 Saturation \hat{s}_k is the fraction of batch samples already scoring the maximum on objective k; weight w_k is proportional to (1 - saturation), so fully-mastered objectives get less attention and struggling ones get more.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

当前AI助手需要同时满足多个目标（准确、安全、简洁、有用），如何高效分配训练资源是实际瓶颈。SA-MRPO提供了更聪明的资源分配方式，直接提升了AI推理和代码生成中最难实现的能力，对实际产品开发有重要价值。 As AI assistants are increasingly trained to juggle many goals at once — being accurate, safe, concise, and helpful — wasting training compute on already-solved behaviors is a real bottleneck. SA-MRPO offers a smarter way to allocate that compute, directly improving the hardest-to-achieve qualities in AI reasoning and coding systems.

❓ 待解决
的问题		 今天的AI非常擅长用已学过的知识回答问题，就像一个背熟了课本的学生。但真正的科学进步需要探索未知、提出新想法、并通过实验验证这些想法。现有的测评基准几乎都只检验AI能不能答对已知问题，或者在大量人类指导下完成任务——没有任何基准测试AI能不能真正"自己搞科研"。 Today's AI systems are very good at answering questions using knowledge they've already learned — like a student who has memorized a textbook. But true scientific progress requires exploring the unknown, inventing new ideas, and verifying those ideas with actual experiments. No existing benchmark tests whether AI can do *that*. Current tests only check if AI can get the right answer when given lots of human hints — they don't test whether AI can figure out what to do all by itself.

的亮点		 • 首个在"项目级"科研任务上测评AI的基准，不只是问答，而是完整的科研流程。• 在同一任务中逐步撤回人类的方法指导，精准定位AI在哪个环节开始"掉链子"。• 覆盖11个科学领域共60个真实研究项目，由40多名专家耗费3.1万余人时构建。• 所有任务经过专家审核、AI辅助审查、沙盒执行和评分验证，质量有保障。 • First benchmark to test AI on full, project-level scientific research tasks — not just isolated Q&A. • Progressively removes human guidance within the *same* task to reveal exactly where AI starts to fall apart. • Covers 11 scientific domains with 60 real research projects, built by 40+ experts spending 31,000+ human hours. • All tasks are verified through expert review, AI-assisted auditing, sandbox execution, and scorer validation — ensuring they are rigorous and fair.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究者测试了18种当前最先进的AI智能体与模型组合配置，涵盖不同的大语言模型和智能体框架。每种配置在11个科学领域的60个项目级任务上进行评估，并在三个不同人类指导级别下分别测试：完整指导、仅提供方法名称、完全无指导。任务由自动化工具和人类专家共同评分，确保结果公正可靠。 The researchers tested 18 state-of-the-art AI agent-model configurations — combining different AI models with different "agent" frameworks that help the model take actions and run code. When only the method name was provided (no detailed instructions), the average dropped sharply to 29.10. When AI had to determine the method entirely on its own, the score fell further to 26.62. This means removing human guidance caused AI performance to drop by nearly half — a dramatic decline that reveals how dependent current AI remains on human direction for real scientific work.

This benchmark provides the field with the first rigorous measuring stick for the gap between current AI and true artificial superintelligence.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

ASI-Bench本身是一套精心设计的测试体系。每个任务都是一个真实的科研项目，被拆分为不同难度等级，逐渐减少给AI的提示。最简单的级别会告诉AI具体用什么方法、怎么用——就像给学生一份详细的实验手册；中间级别只告诉AI方法的名字，不说细节——就像告诉学生"用回归分析"但不解释怎么做；最难的级别什么都不说，让AI自己决定方法——就像给学生一个空白笔记本说"去发现点什么吧"。这种"阶梯式撤退"的设计，让我们能精确知道AI在哪里开始依赖人类的帮助。 ASI-Bench is a carefully designed test suite, not an algorithm itself. Each task is a real scientific research project broken into stages with progressively less human help. In the easiest stage, the AI is told exactly which method to use and how — like giving a student a step-by-step lab manual. In the medium stage, only the method name is given, not the details — like telling a student "use regression" without explaining how. In the hardest stage, the AI must figure out the method entirely on its own — like handing a student a blank notebook and saying "go discover something." This ladder of guidance lets researchers measure precisely how much AI still depends on human hand-holding.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

如果AI未来要帮助人类攻克癌症、发现新材料或解决气候变化，它就必须能够真正"做科研"，而不只是做选择题。ASI-Bench是一声警醒：我们离那个目标还差得很远，同时也为研究者提供了一个具体的工具，用来衡量和推动AI向这个方向前进。 If AI is ever going to help humanity cure diseases, discover new materials, or solve climate change on its own, it needs to do real science — not just answer quiz questions. ASI-Bench is a wake-up call showing we are nowhere near that goal yet, and gives researchers a concrete tool to measure and drive progress toward it.

❓ 待解决
的问题		 最先进的AI大模型通常需要在价格昂贵的数据中心服务器上运行。当你试图在自己的笔记本电脑或游戏台式机上跑这些庞大的模型时，要么因为内存不够而崩溃，要么运行速度慢得令人抓狂。现有的工具只是把个人电脑当成"小号服务器"来用，完全没有充分发挥CPU、GPU和内存协同工作的潜力。 Running the latest and most powerful AI models (like GPT-scale systems) normally requires expensive data center servers. When you try to run these huge models on a personal laptop or gaming PC, they either crash due to lack of memory or run impossibly slow. Existing tools treat your personal machine like a tiny version of a server, which ignores all the clever tricks your laptop's mix of CPU, GPU, and RAM could actually pull off together.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 把个人电脑视为灵活弹性平台，而非"小服务器"——智能协同利用CPU、GPU和内存三者的算力。 • 能实时适配不同硬件配置，从8GB显存的笔记本到工作站GPU均可流畅运行。 • 支持超过20种MoE大模型，并可在本地运行真实的AI Agent任务（如编程助手、工具调用机器人），无需联网。 • 智能复用AI的"中间状态"，减少重复计算，提升效率。 • Treats your personal machine as a flexible, elastic platform — not just a weak server — by smartly combining CPU, GPU, and RAM together. • Adapts in real-time to whatever hardware you have, so it works whether you own an 8GB laptop GPU or a beefy workstation. • Supports over 20 MoE-style models and real AI agent tasks (like coding assistants and tool-using bots) without needing any cloud connection. • Reuses AI "memory states" intelligently across agent tasks to avoid redundant computation.

They ran real AI agent benchmarks including coding tasks and tool-using tasks to measure practical performance. They compared FreeToken against existing local inference tools like llama.cpp and Ollama, measuring how fast tokens are generated (tokens per second), how large a model can be served, and how well the system adapts to different hardware configurations.

Compared to llama.cpp and Ollama, FreeToken delivers significantly higher token generation throughput across all tested hardware configurations, with improvements reported in the range of multiple times faster for large MoE models in agentic settings. It supports 20+ MoE model architectures out of the box.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

FreeToken从头重新设计了在个人电脑上运行大模型的整个流程。它不会把整个模型塞进GPU显存（那样会溢出），而是动态判断"当前需要用哪些模块"，只把这些模块加载进GPU，其余的放在普通内存或硬盘里。它还会追踪哪些"专家模块"（MoE模型中的专用子网络）被频繁调用，优先把它们留在GPU上，不常用的则灵活换入换出——就像图书管理员把最热门的书摆在最顺手的地方。对于AI Agent中重复的步骤，系统会缓存中间结果直接复用，避免重复计算。所有这些策略都会根据你的实际硬件资源动态调整。 FreeToken works by redesigning the entire pipeline of running an AI model on a personal machine from scratch. Instead of loading the whole model into GPU memory (which would overflow), it figures out which parts of the model are needed right now and loads only those, while keeping the rest in regular RAM or on disk. It also keeps track of which AI "expert modules" (specialized sub-networks inside MoE models) are used most frequently and keeps them on the GPU, while rotating less-used ones in and out — like a smart librarian who keeps the popular books on the front shelf. For AI agent tasks that repeat similar steps, it caches and reuses intermediate results so the system doesn't redo work it already did. All of this is adjusted dynamically based on the actual hardware available on your machine.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

目前，最强大的AI工具通常需要昂贵的云端订阅，你的数据也会被发送到远程服务器，引发隐私和费用方面的担忧。FreeToken证明了顶级AI完全可以在你自己的硬件上运行，让强大AI变得私密、免费、人人可用。 Right now, the most powerful AI tools require expensive cloud subscriptions and send your data to remote servers — raising privacy and cost concerns. FreeToken shows that cutting-edge AI can run entirely on hardware you already own, making powerful AI private, free, and accessible to anyone with a modern PC.
