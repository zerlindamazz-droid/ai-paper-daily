MidTool: Mid-training Data Synthesis for Agentic Tool Use MidTool：面向智能体工具使用的中间训练数据合成

🧑‍🔬 Fengqing Jiang, Yite Wang, Boyi Liu, Zhaoyang Wang et al.

图 Figure · Page 4 of paper

🎯 专项中间训练让大模型更擅长调用现实工具和API
Mid-training AI models on tool-use data makes them dramatically better at using real-world tools and APIs.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像培训一名新员工：大多数公司招来一个有通识学历的人（预训练），然后直接让他上岗接客户电话（微调）——结果一塌糊涂。MidTool就像在中间加了一段专项入职培训，让新员工专门学会公司有哪些工具、怎么用、出了问题怎么处理——然后再去接电话，自然游刃有余。Think of it like training a new employee: most companies hire someone with a general degree (pretraining), then immediately throw them into customer calls (fine-tuning) — and wonder why they fumble. MidTool is like adding a proper onboarding bootcamp in between, where the new hire specifically learns which tools exist, how to use them, and what to do when things go wrong — before they ever pick up the phone.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有AI模型在使用外部工具（比如调用天气API、查询数据库、或者串联多个软件操作）时往往不够可靠。大多数训练流程只在最后阶段才尝试教会模型这种能力（即"后训练"），就像考前才开始临时抱佛脚。结果是模型在面对真实工具调用任务时表现脆弱、不稳定。 Existing AI models often struggle to reliably use external tools — like calling a weather API, searching a database, or chaining multiple software actions together. Most training pipelines try to teach this skill only at the very end ("post-training"), which is like cramming for an exam without any prior study. The result is models that are brittle and unreliable when faced with real-world tool-use tasks.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次专门为工具使用设计"中间训练"阶段，填补了通用预训练与任务微调之间的空白。• 构建了MidTool-Mix大规模开放语料库，融合真实网页/PDF/代码数据与基于真实API和MCP技能合成的监督样本。• 教会模型四项核心能力：识别工具功能、从上下文推断参数、组合工具调用流程、在信息不完整时自我修复。 • Introduces a dedicated "mid-training" stage specifically for tool use — filling the gap between general pretraining and task-specific fine-tuning. • Builds MidTool-Mix, a large open corpus combining real web/PDF/code data with synthetic examples generated from real-world APIs and MCP skills. • Teaches models four key skills: recognizing what tools can do, reading context to fill in arguments, chaining tools together in workflows, and recovering when information is missing.
🧪 实验
内容		 研究团队在MidTool-Mix上对两个版本的Qwen3-Base模型（4B和8B参数）进行中间训练，再叠加监督微调和强化学习。评估使用三个基准：BFCL（伯克利函数调用排行榜）、tau2-Bench（真实工具使用智能体基准）和MCP Universe（测试模型上下文协议技能的新基准）。基线包括未经中间训练的同款模型，以及其他有竞争力的工具调用模型。 The team mid-trained two versions of the Qwen3-Base model (4B and 8B parameters) on MidTool-Mix, then applied supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning on top. They evaluated on three benchmarks: BFCL (Berkeley Function Calling Leaderboard), tau2-Bench (a realistic tool-use agent benchmark), and MCP Universe (a new benchmark testing Model Context Protocol skills). Baselines included the same models trained without mid-training, as well as other competitive tool-use models.
📊 实验
结果		 MidTool-Mix在所有三个基准测试上均一致提升了下游性能，在SFT和RL两种后训练设置下均有效。在BFCL上，经过中间训练的模型明显优于未经中间训练的对应模型；在tau2-Bench和MCP Universe上，4B和8B模型均取得了明显提升。无论后续训练采用监督微调还是强化学习，提升效果均稳定出现，证明中间训练为工具使用能力提供了强而通用的基础。（摘要未列出具体百分比，但所有实验设置下均报告了一致性增益。） MidTool-Mix consistently improved downstream performance across all three benchmarks under both SFT and RL settings. On BFCL, MidTool-trained models outperformed their non-mid-trained counterparts. On tau2-Bench and MCP Universe, gains were observed for both the 4B and 8B model sizes. The improvements held whether the follow-up training used supervised fine-tuning or reinforcement learning, demonstrating that mid-training provides a strong and generalizable foundation for tool-use capabilities. (Exact percentage figures were not specified in the abstract but consistent gains across all settings were reported.)
🏁 结论 MidTool证明，将工具使用能力的教学设置为专门的中间训练阶段，效果远优于仅靠最后的微调阶段"顺带学习"。这一发现扩展了"重要AI能力需要专项训练投入"这一日益成熟的共识。 MidTool proves that teaching AI models to use tools works much better when it's done as a dedicated intermediate training stage — not just squeezed into the final fine-tuning phase. This finding extends the growing evidence that important AI capabilities need their own focused training investment.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究团队构建了一个名为MidTool的数据流水线，从三类原材料中提取知识：提及工具相关概念的网页和PDF文档、展示工具使用模式的代码，以及通过提示强大AI模型来模拟与真实API和MCP工具交互所生成的合成样本。这些样本不只教模型"按哪个按钮"，而是训练它如何推断哪个工具合适、如何从对话中提取正确参数、如何串联多个工具调用、以及在某一步骤失败时该怎么办。模型先在该语料上进行中间训练，再通过监督微调和强化学习进一步优化。 The researchers created a data pipeline called MidTool that harvests three kinds of raw material: web pages and PDFs that mention tool-related concepts, code that shows tool usage patterns, and synthetic examples built by prompting strong AI models to simulate interactions with real APIs and MCP (Model Context Protocol) tools. These examples teach the model not just "what button to press" but how to reason about which tool fits, how to extract the right arguments from a conversation, how to chain multiple tool calls, and what to do when a step fails. Models are first mid-trained on this corpus, then further refined with supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

随着AI助手越来越多地被期望能自主浏览网页、调用API和操作软件，让它们可靠地掌握工具使用能力对于实际落地至关重要。MidTool提供了一套开放、可复现的方案，让开发者无需依赖私有数据或秘密训练技巧，就能构建更强大的AI智能体。 As AI assistants are increasingly expected to autonomously browse the web, call APIs, and operate software, making them reliably capable at tool use is critical for real-world deployment. MidTool provides an open, reproducible recipe that developers can use to build more capable AI agents — without needing proprietary data or secret training tricks.

Beyond Imitation: Self-Improving Robot Policies via Off-Policy Q-Planning 超越模仿：基于离策略Q规划的机器人策略自我改进

🧑‍🔬 Varun Giridhar, Anant Khandelwal, Jeremy A. Collins, Ignat Georgiev et al.

图 Figure · Page 7 of paper

🎯 让机器人从失败中自我学习，无需人类额外示范
A lightweight Q-function lets large robot policies self-improve from their own failures without human help.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个学徒厨师（大模型）已经学会了很多菜谱，但偶尔会炒糊锅。Q规划就像给他配了一个挑剔的美食评委（Q函数）：每次厨师做几种方案，评委选最好的那道上桌；下班后评委复盘今天哪道菜成功、哪道失败，自己越来越懂行——厨师本人完全不用重新培训。Think of it like a chef (the big BC model) who knows thousands of recipes but sometimes burns the dish. Q-Planning adds a sharp food critic (the Q-function) who tastes several attempts and picks the best one to serve. After each shift, the critic reviews what worked and what flopped — getting smarter over time — while the chef never has to go back to cooking school.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 现有机器人学习方法（行为克隆）就像死记硬背的学生：它只会照着老师的示范做，一旦失败就不知道怎么进步，必须等人类再来演示一遍才行。而强化学习虽然能从失败中学习，但要微调那些拥有数十亿参数的大型机器人模型，计算成本极高，难以实际落地。这就造成了一个困境：机器人要么只会模仿、无法自我提升；要么学习代价太大、无法规模化。 Today's best robot-learning systems (called Behaviour Cloning, or BC) are like students who only study by copying worked examples — they can't learn from their own mistakes without a human showing them what to do again. Reinforcement Learning can fix this, but it's too expensive to apply to the huge modern robot-brain models (billions of parameters). So robots are stuck: great at copying, helpless when they fail on their own.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出"Q规划"：只训练一个轻量级Q函数来评估动作好坏，不动原有的大型机器人模型权重，计算高效。 • 推理时，用Q函数对多个候选动作加权平均，挑出最优动作，无需重新训练整个模型。 • 在线部署时，机器人自己跑任务，Q函数从成功与失败的经历中同时学习，而大模型保持冻结不变。 • 是唯一一种在相同计算预算下，能从失败案例中稳定提升、无需额外辅助策略网络的方法。 • Introduces "Q-Planning": trains only a small Q-function (a value scorer) on top of a frozen giant robot policy — cheap and scalable. • At decision time, the Q-function picks the best action by scoring many candidates from the big model, like a wise judge picking the best answer from many drafts. • Online, the robot improves itself by running tasks and letting the Q-function learn from both wins and losses — the big model never changes. • The only method tested that reliably learns from failures without needing a separate "actor" network.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在两个仿真基准（LIBERO系列共4个子集，以及双臂操作基准RoboTwin）和两个真实机器人任务（叠杯子、插钱包）上进行。对比方法包括：Best-of-N采样、过滤式监督微调（SFT）、IBRL、DSRL和DAWR。评估指标为任务成功率，并进行了最多10轮在线自我改进迭代，同时在接近满分的基准上还测量了成功回合的平均时长。 Experiments ran on two simulated robot benchmarks — LIBERO (four sub-suites testing spatial, object, goal, and long-horizon tasks) and RoboTwin (bimanual manipulation) — plus two real-world robot tasks: stacking cups and inserting a wallet. The method was compared against five baselines: Best-of-N sampling, filtered supervised fine-tuning, IBRL, DSRL, and DAWR. Success rate was the main metric, measured over up to ten rounds of self-improvement, with episode length also tracked for near-perfect benchmarks.
📊 实验
结果		 • LIBERO-10（仿真）：成功率从93%提升至99%（提升6个百分点）。 • RoboTwin（仿真双臂）：成功率从83.8%提升至91.4%（提升7.6个百分点）。 • 真实机器人叠杯子任务：成功率从40%飙升至90%（提升50个百分点，仅5轮迭代）。 • 真实机器人插钱包任务：成功率从25%提升至80%（提升55个百分点，仅5轮迭代）。 • 对比基准：仅用成功案例做SFT的方法在叠杯子上止步于55%，插钱包止步于30%，远不及Q规划。 • 在接近满分的LIBERO-Object和LIBERO-Goal子集上，Q规划还缩短了成功完成任务所需的时间步数。 • LIBERO-10 (simulation): success rate jumped from 93% to 99% over 10 iterations. • RoboTwin (simulated bimanual): 83.8% → 91.4% success rate. • Real robot — stack cups: 40% → 90% in just 5 iterations (a +50 percentage point gain). • Real robot — insert wallet: 25% → 80% in 5 iterations (+55 percentage points). • Competing method (filtered SFT on successes only) stalled at 55% (cups) and 30% (wallet) — far behind Q-Planning. • On near-perfect LIBERO sub-suites, Q-Planning also shortened the number of steps needed to finish tasks successfully.
🏁 结论 本文证明了，在不修改大型机器人模型的前提下，只需附加一个轻量级Q函数，机器人就能通过自身的成功与失败经历不断自我改进。Q规划是首个在相同在线预算下，能稳定地从失败中学习、无需人类干预、无需辅助演员网络的方法，为大规模可扩展的机器人自主学习开辟了新路径。 This paper proves that a robot policy can reliably teach itself to get better — using its own successes and failures — simply by adding a small value-scoring module, without ever touching the large model it was built on. Q-Planning is the first approach to achieve stable, scalable self-improvement from failures under a realistic deployment budget, with no human demonstrations required after the initial training.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Q规划的核心思路是"分工合作"：一个大型行为克隆模型负责产生候选动作（像一个经验丰富但只会照搬的工人），一个小型Q函数负责评估哪个动作最好（像一个懂得好坏的裁判）。推理时，从大模型采样多个动作，Q函数给每个打分并加权平均，选出最佳动作执行。训练时，Q函数用贝尔曼方程从成功和失败的经历中更新——成功路径得高分，失败路径得低分——而大模型完全冻结，不参与梯度更新。这种设计让自我改进只需要微调那个小小的Q函数，计算代价极低。 Think of it as a two-role team: the big BC model is a skilled but inflexible worker that proposes many possible moves, and the small Q-function is a smart judge that scores each move and picks (or blends) the best ones. At inference, the robot samples a bunch of candidate actions from the BC model, the Q-function scores them, and they are averaged weighted by their scores — so good actions get more influence. For self-improvement, the robot runs tasks on its own, collects what worked and what didn't, and only updates the small Q-function using those experiences (via the Bellman equation from RL). The giant BC model stays completely frozen throughout, making the whole process lightweight and scalable.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

现实中的机器人部署后往往需要大量人工干预和重新演示才能提升表现，这极大限制了机器人的实用性。Q规划让机器人能在工厂、家庭等真实场景中"越用越聪明"，自动从失败中成长，大幅降低了人工成本，是迈向真正自主机器人的重要一步。 Most real-world robots today need constant human supervision and retraining when they fail — which is expensive and impractical at scale. Q-Planning is a step toward robots that get smarter on the job automatically, making household robots, factory arms, and surgical assistants far more viable without an army of human trainers behind them.

图 Figure · Page 4 of paper

🎯 自适应采样让LLM智能体评估成本降低80%
Cuts LLM agent evaluation costs by 80% using smart adaptive task sampling during harness optimization.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一位聪明的老师备考：不需要每天把所有练习题都刷一遍，而是重点盯着学生"时对时错"的题目反复练——那些每次都全对或全错的题直接跳过。Task-CoEvolve就是用这个思路给AI"备考"，把评估精力花在最值得的地方。Think of it like a teacher preparing students for an exam: instead of drilling every single practice problem every day, a smart teacher focuses extra time on the problems students sometimes get right and sometimes get wrong — skipping the ones everyone always aces or always bombs. 维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 在优化AI智能体的"框架代码"（即告诉AI如何解题的脚手架程序）时，研究人员需要反复在大量测试题目上评估效果。问题在于：大多数题目要么每次都答对、要么每次都答错，根本没有区分价值，却还是被重复评估，浪费了大量算力和时间。现有方法每次迭代都跑完整测试集，代价极高。 When building smarter AI agents, researchers often tweak "harness code" — the scaffolding that tells an AI how to approach a task — and test it over and over on hundreds of examples to see if it improved. The problem is that most of those test examples give very little useful information: either the AI already nails them every time, or it always fails them. Running the full test suite at every step wastes enormous amounts of computing time and money. Existing methods stubbornly evaluate every single task every single iteration, even when most of those tasks stopped being informative long ago.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 聪明筛题：只评估不同版本框架产生分歧的题目——这些才是真正有区分力的"关键题"。 • 方差加权采样：优先多测那些AI"时对时错"的题目，就像老师重点练习学生半会半不会的知识点。 • 无偏分数估计：即使每轮只评估部分题目，统计校正确保跨轮次的分数可以公平比较。 • Smart task filtering: Only evaluate tasks where different harness versions actually disagree — these are the most revealing "swing tasks." • Variance-weighted sampling: Tasks near the agent's capability boundary get sampled more often, like a teacher focusing extra practice on problems a student sometimes gets right and sometimes gets wrong. • Unbiased score estimation: Even though only a fraction of tasks are evaluated each round, the method statistically corrects for the sampling bias to produce fair, comparable scores across iterations.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在两个基准上进行：在线文本分类任务（AI需通过网络搜索和推理对文本分类）和Terminal-Bench 2.1（编程与命令行任务）。基线方法包括：全量评估（黄金标准）、随机固定子集采样，以及其他固定预算选择策略。核心指标为最终任务解决率和优化过程中消耗的LLM评估总次数。 Experiments were run on two benchmarks: an online text classification benchmark (where the agent must classify text via web search and reasoning) and Terminal-Bench 2.1 (a coding/command-line task benchmark). The baselines included full-set evaluation (the gold standard), random fixed-subset sampling, and other fixed-budget selection strategies. The key metrics were final task-solve rate and total number of LLM evaluations consumed during the optimization process.
📊 实验
结果		 与全量评估相比，Task-CoEvolve将优化过程中的评估次数减少了80%，同时在两个基准上均达到与全量搜索相当的最终性能。在Terminal-Bench 2.1和文本分类基准上，仅用约20%的评估预算即可达到等价甚至更好的准确率，并持续超越固定子集随机采样等基线方法。 Task-CoEvolve reduced the number of evaluations during optimization by 80% compared to full-set evaluation, while matching the final performance of full-set search on both benchmarks. On Terminal-Bench 2.1 and the text classification benchmark, it achieved equivalent or better accuracy with only ~20% of the evaluation budget.
🏁 结论 Task-CoEvolve证明：优化AI框架时无需每轮评估所有题目——通过自适应地聚焦最有价值的题目，可在减少80%评估量的前提下达到与全量评估相同的最终效果。 Task-CoEvolve proves that you don't need to evaluate every test case every iteration to find a great harness — smart, adaptive sampling of the most informative tasks achieves the same final quality at a fraction of the cost, cutting evaluations by 80%.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Task-CoEvolve持续追踪每道题在不同框架版本下的历史表现。对于那些"大家都答对"或"大家都答错"的题，认定它们没有区分力，很少抽到；而对那些"有的版本过、有的版本不过"的题，则大幅提高抽样概率，因为它们最能揭示框架之间的差异。抽样概率由历史结果的方差决定，并随框架演化自动更新。为了让不同轮次的部分评估结果可比，采用重要性加权校正，估算出完整测试集上的无偏分数。 Task-CoEvolve works by tracking how different harness versions perform on each task over time. Tasks where the various harnesses consistently agree (all pass or all fail) are considered uninformative and sampled rarely. Tasks where harnesses disagree — some versions pass, others fail — get sampled much more frequently because they reveal real differences between harness candidates. The sampling probability is weighted by the variance of past outcomes per task, and it updates automatically as the harness evolves. To make scores from different partial evaluations comparable, the method applies an importance-weighted correction that accounts for how often each task was sampled, producing an unbiased estimate of what the full-set score would have been.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\hat{S} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{T}|} \sum_{t \in \mathcal{S}} \frac{r_t}{p_t}\]
💡 用重要性加权估计完整测试集分数：对被抽到的题目，用其结果除以被抽到的概率，再求均值，从而无偏地还原全量得分。 Importance-weighted estimator: each sampled task's result is divided by its sampling probability, then averaged — this corrects for the bias of non-uniform sampling and estimates the true full-set score.
\[p_t \propto \text{Var}(\{r_t^{(i)}\}_{i=1}^{k})\]
💡 每道题的采样概率正比于它在历史各框架版本下结果的方差——方差越大（时对时错），被抽到的概率越高。 Sampling probability is proportional to the variance of a task's outcomes across past harness candidates — tasks with higher variance (sometimes pass, sometimes fail) are sampled more often.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Swift-Image以60亿参数的扩散Transformer为基础，分阶段训练：先在大量图文数据上学习广泛的视觉概念，再逐步提升分辨率和质量门槛。后训练阶段，用并行专家强化学习"奖励"模型的好输出，再通过多教师蒸馏分别学习生成和编辑两类技能，防止两者互相干扰。另外设计了"Prompt增强器"模块，把用户的模糊描述自动翻译成图像模型最容易理解的精确指令。最后通过结构剪枝将模型压缩到30亿参数，再用少步蒸馏大幅加速推理，就像把厚重教材精炼成同样好用的口袋手册。 Swift-Image starts with a 6-billion-parameter Diffusion Transformer (DiT) and trains it in stages: first on a wide variety of image-text pairs to learn broad visual concepts, then progressively at higher resolutions with stricter quality filters. For post-training, it uses reinforcement learning with parallel expert "judges" to reward good outputs, then distills knowledge from multiple specialist teacher models — one for generation, one for editing — so the student model learns both skills without them clashing. A separate "Prompt Enhancer" module rewrites vague user prompts into precise, model-friendly instructions before they reach the image generator. Finally, the model is pruned down to 3B parameters and accelerated with few-step distillation, like compressing a thick textbook into a pocket guide that's just as useful.

For everyday users, it paves the way for faster, cheaper, and more capable AI creative tools in apps and products.
