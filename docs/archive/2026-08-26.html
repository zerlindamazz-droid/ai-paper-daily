On-Policy Self-Distillation in Diffusion Models 扩散模型中的在线自蒸馏

🧑‍🔬 Wei Zhou, Xiongwei Zhu, Lingdong Kong, Bo Chen et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 将图像级奖励转化为逐步监督，高效对齐扩散模型
On-policy self-distillation converts image-level rewards into step-by-step targets, aligning diffusion models faster and more efficiently.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一位体操教练，以前只在选手完成整套动作后说"你得了7.5分"，现在却在每个动作环节暂停，告诉选手"这个手臂姿势应该是这样的"。选手学得更快、更准确——而且教练在每次训练后还会更新自己的指导方案，让建议始终保持最新。Think of it like a sports coach who, instead of only telling a gymnast "you scored 7.5" after the routine, now pauses the routine at every move and says "here's exactly how that arm position should look." The gymnast learns faster and more precisely — and the coach refreshes their playbook after every session so the advice stays sharp.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 AI图像生成模型（如Stable Diffusion）通过多步去噪生成图片，但当我们想让它符合人类偏好时，只能对最终图片打分，无法指导每一步该怎么做。这就像教练只告诉运动员比赛输赢，却从不在训练过程中给出具体指导。现有的改进方法要么速度慢，要么消耗大量计算资源，而且难以分析和调试。 AI image generators like Stable Diffusion work by slowly removing noise from a random image over many steps, but when we try to teach them human preferences (e.g., "make images more beautiful or accurate"), we only give feedback on the final image — not on each intermediate step. This is like a coach only telling an athlete whether they won the race, but never giving tips during training. Existing methods to fix this are either slow, computationally expensive, or hard to analyze and debug.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 将最终图像的奖励信号转化为每个去噪步骤的明确学习目标，让模型在生成过程中每一步都得到具体指导，而非只在最后打分。 • 采用"冻结教师+在线学生"的自蒸馏设计，教师模型通过指数移动平均持续刷新，训练更稳定。 • 将"目标构建质量"和"模型实际学习效果"分开衡量，便于诊断训练过程中的问题所在。 • 在20个测试场景中的19个取得最佳成绩，训练时间最多缩短63%。 • Converts final image rewards into explicit, step-by-step learning targets — so the model gets guidance at every denoising step, not just at the end. • Uses a "frozen teacher + live student" design (self-distillation) where the teacher is continuously refreshed via exponential moving average, keeping training stable. • Separately measures how good the targets are vs. how well the model actually learns them — enabling clear diagnosis of where training gains or losses come from. • Achieves top results in 19 out of 20 tested settings while cutting training time by up to 63% compared to a strong baseline.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在两个扩散模型上进行：SD 3.5-M（Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium）和Z-Image-Turbo（步骤精简的快速模型）。使用十种不同的奖励函数（评估器）进行评测，涵盖美学质量、提示词对齐度和图像质量等维度。与DiffusionDPO、DDPO、DPOK、DiffusionNFT等多个强基线进行比较。评估采用未见过的提示词（held-out集），以测试模型的泛化能力，而非简单记忆。 Experiments were conducted on two diffusion model backbones: SD 3.5-M (Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium) and Z-Image-Turbo (a step-distilled, faster model). The method was evaluated across ten different reward functions (evaluators) spanning aesthetics, prompt alignment, and image quality. Results were compared against several strong baselines including DiffusionDPO, DDPO, DPOK, DiffusionNFT, and others. The evaluation used held-out (unseen) prompts to test generalization, not just memorized performance.
📊 实验
结果		 DiffusionOPSD在20个测试场景中的19个取得了最佳held-out分数（两个模型骨架×十种评估器）。与最强竞争方法相比，奖励分数最高提升44.0%。训练效率大幅提升：与DiffusionNFT相比，在SD 3.5-M上GPU训练时间减少40%，在Z-Image-Turbo上减少63%。受控的同查询实验还揭示了一个重要诊断发现：更大的目标构建增益并不总能转化为更大的实际学习增益。 DiffusionOPSD achieved the best held-out scores in 19 out of 20 reward-matched settings (across two backbones × ten evaluators). It outperformed the strongest competing method by up to 44.0% in reward score. Training efficiency improved dramatically: GPU-hours were reduced by 40% on SD 3.5-M and by 63% on Z-Image-Turbo compared to DiffusionNFT, the previous top competitor. Controlled same-query experiments also revealed that larger target-construction gains do not always lead to larger realized gains — an important diagnostic insight.
🏁 结论 本文证明，通过在线自蒸馏将图像级奖励信号转化为明确的中间步骤目标，是一种高效且可解释的扩散模型对齐方法，在质量和训练速度上均优于此前的方法。 This paper proves that converting image-level reward signals into explicit intermediate targets — via on-policy self-distillation — is a highly efficient and interpretable way to align diffusion models with human preferences, outperforming prior methods in both quality and training speed.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

该方法以循环方式运作。首先，用一个"冻结"的模型副本（行为策略）生成图片，并记录下每一个中间去噪步骤。然后，利用最终图像的奖励信号，为每个中间步骤计算出更好的"目标图像"——一个比当前预测略好（正目标），一个略差（负目标）。可训练的模型学习向正目标靠拢、远离负目标。训练几步后，冻结副本通过指数移动平均更新，与可训练模型保持同步。这个循环不断重复，持续用新鲜的在线数据改进模型，全程无需任何外部教师模型。 The method works in a loop. First, a "frozen" copy of the model (called the behavior policy) generates images and records every intermediate noisy step along the way. Then, the reward signal from the final image is used to calculate better "target" images for each intermediate step — one slightly better (positive target) and one slightly worse (negative target) than the current prediction. The live, trainable model then learns to move toward the positive targets and away from the negative ones. After a few learning steps, the frozen copy is refreshed using an exponential moving average of the trainable model's weights. This cycle repeats, continuously improving the model with fresh, on-policy data — all without needing any external teacher model.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

更好对齐的图像生成模型意味着AI绘画工具、设计助手和内容平台能更准确地生成用户想要的图片，同时减少计算资源浪费。40%至63%的训练时间缩减也意味着更小的团队和公司能负担得起微调强大模型的成本，让高质量AI图像对齐技术更加普惠。 Better-aligned image generators mean AI art tools, design assistants, and content platforms can produce images that more reliably match what users actually want — with less wasted compute. The 40–63% reduction in training time also means smaller labs and companies can afford to fine-tune powerful models, democratizing access to high-quality AI image alignment.

Beyond the Stability-Exploration Dilemma: Environmental Regularization for LLM Policy Optimization 超越稳定性与探索的两难困境：面向LLM策略优化的环境正则化方法

🧑‍🔬 Xianlei Zhou, Xiangdi Meng, Yu He, Tianyu Qi et al.

图 Figure · Page 6 of paper

🎯 将正则化从输出端移至输入端，同时保留探索能力并提升训练稳定性
ERPO stabilizes LLM training by regulating input queries instead of output responses, preserving exploration.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个备考的学生，旧方法要求他"每次答题都不能偏离标准答案太多"（限制输出），结果他不敢尝试新思路；ERPO改成"确保练习题的类型分布合理，不要只刷简单题"（管控输入），学生既能大胆尝试解题方法，又不会因为偏科而崩盘。Imagine a student studying for exams. The old method says "your answers must never stray too far from the textbook" — safe, but it kills creative thinking. ERPO instead says "make sure you practice a balanced mix of question types, not just the easy ones." The student stays on track AND gets to explore bold new strategies — best of both worlds!
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 当我们用强化学习（RL）训练大语言模型时，需要在"稳定性"和"探索性"之间做艰难权衡。现有方法通过限制模型输出的变化幅度（Policy-KL）来防止训练崩溃，但这同时也束缚了模型尝试新答案的自由，就像给学生戴上了手铐——要么老老实实照旧答，要么放开手但乱成一团。 When training large language models with reinforcement learning, researchers face a painful trade-off: constraining how much the model's answers can change keeps training stable, but it also stops the model from exploring creative new solutions. Existing methods (called Policy-KL) act like a leash on the model's outputs — too tight and the model can't learn boldly; too loose and training goes haywire.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 创新性地将正则化从"输出端"（回答）移至"输入端"（问题分布），打破稳定性与探索的两难困境； • 引入Query-KL（QKL）项，约束训练过程中问题分布的漂移，而不干涉模型如何生成答案； • 加入基于参考数据集的每问题权重，让训练偏向那些"典型"问题，稳定优化过程； • 可无缝插入GRPO、PPO、REINFORCE等主流RL训练框架，无需额外的前向计算开销。 • Cleverly shifts regularization from the model's outputs (answers) to its inputs (questions), breaking the classic stability-vs-exploration deadlock. • Introduces a Query-KL (QKL) penalty that controls how much the training question distribution drifts, without touching how answers are generated. • Adds per-query importance weights so training naturally focuses on representative, typical questions. • Plugs directly into popular RL frameworks (GRPO, PPO, REINFORCE) with zero extra computation cost.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在六个数学推理基准上进行，包括MATH、AMC、AIME、Olympiad等不同难度的题目集。基线模型包括标准GRPO（带Policy-KL）以及去掉KL约束的变体，并测试了不同温度设置（高温解码）和长时程训练场景。评估指标为各基准上的准确率，同时观察训练曲线的稳定性。 The team tested ERPO on six mathematical reasoning benchmarks covering a range of difficulty levels: MATH500, AMC, AIME24, AIME25, Minerva Math, and OlympiadBench. They compared against standard GRPO with Policy-KL, GRPO without any KL constraint, and other ablated variants. Experiments also stress-tested the method under high-temperature decoding (where models are more random) and extended training runs to probe stability.
📊 实验
结果		 在六个数学推理基准上，ERPO相较于标准GRPO（含Policy-KL）平均准确率提升显著：在AIME24上提升约4-6个百分点，在AMC上提升约3-5个百分点，在MATH500上保持可比或更优水平。在高温解码场景下，训练曲线显著更稳定，不出现崩溃现象；在长时程训练中，ERPO持续优于基线，无明显性能退化。 Across six math benchmarks, ERPO outperforms standard GRPO with Policy-KL: roughly 4–6 percentage point gains on AIME24, 3–5 points on AMC, and comparable or better results on MATH500. Under high-temperature decoding — a notoriously unstable regime — ERPO maintains smooth training curves while baselines collapse. In long-horizon training runs, ERPO continues to improve where baselines plateau or degrade.
🏁 结论 本文证明了将正则化从模型输出端移至输入（问题）端，可以同时实现训练稳定性和充分探索，打破了长期存在的两难困境。ERPO是一种即插即用的改进方案，无需额外计算，即可让LLM的强化学习训练更稳、更强。 This paper proves that shifting regularization from model outputs to training inputs simultaneously achieves stability and exploration — dismantling a long-standing dilemma in LLM reinforcement learning. ERPO is a drop-in improvement that makes RL training for large language models more stable and more powerful without any extra computation.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

ERPO的核心思路是：与其限制模型"怎么回答"，不如管控模型"在哪些问题上训练"。随着RL训练推进，模型会越来越偏向某类问题（比如简单题），这种"问题分布漂移"是训练不稳定的隐藏根源。ERPO引入一个叫做Query-KL的惩罚项，衡量当前训练问题分布与初始参考分布的偏离程度，并用一个权重系数让模型多关注那些在原始分布中"典型"的问题。关键在于，这个QKL梯度只流过问题的概率，完全不影响答案的生成梯度——探索空间被完整保留。整个机制就像给训练过程加了一个"问题筛选过滤器"，而不是"答案枷锁"。 Instead of restricting how the model answers, ERPO controls which questions the model trains on. As RL training progresses, the model unconsciously shifts its attention toward certain types of questions — a hidden source of instability. ERPO adds a "Query-KL" penalty that measures how far the current question distribution has drifted from the original, and applies importance weights to steer training back toward typical questions. Crucially, this penalty only touches the math around question selection, never the math around answer generation — so the model remains free to explore diverse responses. Think of it as a smart question curator, not an answer censor.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

强化学习是让ChatGPT、DeepSeek等模型变得更聪明的关键技术，但训练过程容易崩溃或停滞。ERPO提供了一种更安全、更高效的训练方式，有望让未来的AI助手在数学、编程、科学推理等复杂任务上表现更出色。 Reinforcement learning is the secret sauce behind making AI assistants like ChatGPT smarter, but it's notoriously fragile. ERPO offers a safer, more efficient training recipe that could help future AI systems reason better at math, coding, and science — with fewer crashes and more reliable improvements.

🎯 稀疏表征让AI世界模型规划更简单、更可解释。
Sparse representations make AI world models simpler to plan with and more interpretable.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像整理衣柜一样。密集表征好比把所有衣服堆成一座山，每次找衣服都要翻遍全部；稀疏表征则像用贴了标签的抽屉分类收纳，大多数抽屉是空的，但少数几个抽屉里放的正是你需要的——AI"规划下一步"就像直接拉开对的那个抽屉，又快又准。Think of it like organizing your closet. Dense representations are like throwing all your clothes into one giant pile — to find anything, you have to sort through everything. Sparse representations are like using labeled drawers where most are empty but a few hold exactly what you need — grabbing the right outfit (planning the right action) becomes effortless.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 AI智能体（比如机器人或游戏AI）需要一个内部"心理地图"——即世界模型——来预测每个动作后会发生什么。现有的世界模型把所有信息混在一起，形成"密集表征"，就像把所有信息叠印在同一张纸上，规划模块很难从中找出清晰的规律。这导致规划时需要非常复杂的计算，效率低下。 AI agents that plan ahead (like robots or game-playing systems) need a compact mental "map" of the world — called a world model — to imagine what will happen after each action. Existing world models pack all information into dense, tangled representations where every feature is always active, making it hard for a planning module to learn clean, predictable patterns. Think of it like trying to read a book where every word is printed on top of every other word at once.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 从理论上证明：在足够高维的空间中，稀疏表征可以任意精确地近似任意复杂的动态系统，为稀疏性提供了坚实的数学基础。• 提出LpWM，通过一种新颖的正则化方法RDMReg，让模型内部编码变得稀疏且非负，就像只在必要时"点亮"少数几个神经元。• 发现学到的稀疏编码具有"模式分解"特性：哪些特征被激活代表离散的动态模式（如"抓着物体"vs"未抓"），特征大小则捕捉连续的细粒度状态。 • Theoretically proves that sparse (mostly-zero) representations can approximate any complex dynamics perfectly in high enough dimensions — giving a solid math foundation for using sparsity. • Introduces LpWM, a new world model that forces its internal codes to be sparse and non-negative using a novel regularization trick called RDMReg. • Discovers that the learned sparse codes are naturally "mode-factored": which features are active tells the agent what discrete mode it's in (e.g., object in hand vs. not), while feature sizes capture fine-grained continuous state.
🧪 实验
内容		 主要实验在PushT机器人操作任务上进行——智能体需要把一个T形积木推到目标位置，这是一个经典的规划质量测试基准。LpWM与密集世界模型（使用各向同性高斯正则化的LeWM，以及基于VICReg的模型）进行对比，测试了多种复杂度不同的预测器（不同大小的MLP、线性模型）。核心评估指标是规划成功率，并在不同预测器容量下分别评估，以衡量"需要多复杂的模型才能规划成功"。 The main experiments use the PushT robotic manipulation task, where an agent must push a T-shaped block to a target position — a benchmark that tests planning quality. LpWM is compared against dense world models (LeWM with isotropic Gaussian regularization, and VICReg-based models) across multiple predictor families of varying complexity (MLPs of different sizes, linear models). Planning success rate is the key metric, evaluated at different predictor capacities to see how much model complexity is needed.
📊 实验
结果		 在PushT基准测试中，稀疏LpWM在使用中等容量预测器时，规划成功率比密集LeWM高出最多57%。这一优势在多种预测器架构下均成立（不局限于某一种网络）。LpWM也稳定优于基于VICReg的密集表征模型。理论分析中，随着隐空间维度增大，滚动误差趋近于零，与数学推导吻合。 On the PushT benchmark, sparse LpWM outperforms dense LeWM by up to 57% in planning success rate when using intermediate-capacity predictors. This advantage holds across multiple predictor families (not just one type of network). LpWM also consistently beats dense VICReg-based representations. Rollout error in the theoretical analysis vanishes as the latent dimension grows, confirming the math.
🏁 结论 本文证明，在用于规划的世界模型中，稀疏表征比密集表征具有更优的几何结构：它们降低了控制所需预测器的复杂度，并自然形成可解释的、模式分离的结构。LpWM在机器人任务中用更轻量的预测器实现了最高57%的规划成功率提升。 This paper proves that sparse representations are a better geometry than dense ones for world models used in planning: they reduce the complexity of the predictor needed for control and naturally organize into interpretable, mode-separated structures. LpWM achieves up to 57% better planning success on robotic tasks with lighter predictors.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究团队首先证明了一个数学结论：如果把世界状态编码成一个高维向量，且只有少数几个数值不为零（即稀疏），那么预测动作后世界如何变化就近似于一个简单的线性运算，极易学习。基于此，他们设计了LpWM：在训练时加入一种特殊的惩罚项（RDMReg），让神经网络的内部编码趋向"修正广义高斯分布"——简单说就是强迫大多数数值为零，只留少数有意义的大数值。这样，负责预测未来状态的模块只需要是一个轻量小网络，因为稀疏编码已经把世界状态整理成了清晰分离的"小格子"。 The authors start with a key mathematical insight: if you encode the world's state as a very high-dimensional vector where only a few entries are non-zero (sparse), then predicting how the world changes after an action becomes almost a simple linear operation — much easier to learn. To make this practical, they design LpWM, which adds a special training penalty (RDMReg) that nudges the neural network's internal codes toward a "Rectified Generalized Gaussian" shape — essentially forcing most values to zero while keeping a few meaningfully large. The predictor that imagines future states then only needs to be a lightweight model, because the sparse codes already organize the world into neat, separable chunks.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[f(s, a) \approx W(a)\, z + b(a), \quad z = \phi(s) \in \mathbb{R}^d_{\geq 0}\]
💡 核心理论结论：在高维稀疏编码z下，任意非线性动态f(s,a)可以被动作条件下的线性映射W(a)z+b(a)任意精确地近似，维度d越高误差越小。 The core theoretical result: any nonlinear dynamics f(s,a) can be approximated arbitrarily well by a simple linear map W(a)z+b(a) when the state s is encoded as a high-dimensional non-negative sparse vector z — the higher the dimension d, the smaller the error.
\[\mathcal{L}_{\text{RDMReg}} = D_{\text{KL}}\!\left(q_\phi(z) \,\Big\|\, p_{\text{RGG}}(z)\right)\]
💡 正则化损失：通过最小化编码器输出分布与修正广义高斯分布之间的KL散度，强制编码变得稀疏且非负。 The regularization loss: it pushes the encoder's output distribution to match a Rectified Generalized Gaussian (RGG) by minimizing their KL divergence, which forces the codes to be sparse and non-negative.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

GeoWAM的核心思路是：与其让AI预测未来的照片，不如让它预测未来场景的三维"骨架"——即点云（一堆在空间中有坐标的点，代表物体的形状和位置）。模型先通过大量驾驶数据学会"预见"未来的三维场景结构，这个过程让它自然地理解了空间变化和物体运动规律。然后，一个专门的"动作头"模块读取这些三维几何表示，直接推断出车辆接下来应该走的轨迹。整个框架把"看懂世界"和"决定怎么开车"两件事统一在以几何为核心的空间里。 Instead of training the AI to paint future video frames, GeoWAM trains it to predict the future 3D "skeleton" of the scene — a point cloud, which is like a 3D connect-the-dots map showing where every object is in space. The model is pretrained on lots of driving data to forecast how this 3D map will change over time, naturally learning how objects move and how space transforms. Then a dedicated "action head" module takes these learned 3D representations and figures out the best trajectory for the vehicle. Because 3D geometry is the same space where driving decisions actually happen, the transition from "understanding the scene" to "choosing an action" becomes much more direct and accurate.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\hat{G}_{t+1:t+T} = f_{\text{GeoWAM}}(G_{t-K:t}, C)\]
💡 GeoWAM根据过去K帧的历史三维几何场景G和条件信息C，预测未来T步的三维几何序列，代替了传统的未来图像预测 GeoWAM takes the past K frames of 3D geometry scenes (G) plus context (C) and predicts the next T steps of 3D geometry — replacing the traditional future image prediction objective

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

自动驾驶的核心挑战之一是让AI真正"理解"三维世界，而不只是识别画面。GeoWAM的方法让AI像人类司机一样用空间感知来做决策，有望显著提升真实道路上的驾驶安全性和可靠性，加速自动驾驶商业落地。 One of the biggest hurdles for self-driving cars is getting AI to truly understand 3D space, not just recognize what things look like. GeoWAM's approach could make autonomous vehicles significantly safer and more reliable on real roads, potentially accelerating the deployment of self-driving technology in everyday life.
