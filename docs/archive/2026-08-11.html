Sekai2: From World Exploration to Interactive World Modeling Sekai2：从世界探索到交互式世界建模

🧑‍🔬 Kang He, Wenshuo Peng, Zihui Gao, Jiaming Tan et al.

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 发布全球最大规模含相机轨迹与语义标注的真实世界探索视频数据集
A massive real-world video dataset with camera paths and rich annotations to power next-gen interactive world models.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像要训练一个会"脑补地图"的AI导游，你不能只给它看几张风景照——你得给它看完整的探索旅程视频，告诉它"摄像机往左转了30度"、"这棵树是静止的但那辆车在动"，还得让它多次路过同一个路口，从不同角度认清它。Sekai2就是这样一套超级详细的"AI导游培训教材"。Imagine training a robot tour guide: you can't just show it a few postcards — you need to take it on full walking tours around the world, tell it exactly which way the camera turned at every step, label what's moving vs. standing still, and loop back through the same plaza multiple times so it truly learns to recognize the place. Sekai2 is exactly that: the world's most detailed AI tour-guide training program, filmed across 113 countries.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练AI"世界模型"（让AI理解场景如何随时间和视角变化）需要三样东西同时具备：长时间视频、摄像机运动轨迹、以及与时间对齐的文字描述。但现有数据集很难三者兼得：网上的海量视频没有轨迹信息，有轨迹的数据集又太短或只能用于三维重建。就像你想学做饭，但能找到的要么是只有食材清单没有步骤的菜谱，要么是只有几秒钟的短视频，根本不够用。 Training AI "world models" — systems that understand how scenes change over time and from different viewpoints — requires three things at once: long videos, camera movement paths, and text descriptions tied to specific moments. Existing datasets almost never provide all three together. Web videos have variety but no camera data; datasets with camera data are too short or only meant for 3D reconstruction. It's like trying to learn to navigate a city but only having either a map without street names, or a few snapshot photos with no directions.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 规模庞大且三要素齐全：128,892个视频片段，共2,826小时，覆盖全球113个国家/地区，每个片段都同时配有相机轨迹和分层语义标注，填补了领域空白。 • 专注"持续观察"：43,594个片段长达完整2分钟，占总时长51.4%，为AI学习长期场景记忆提供了关键素材。 • 独创全景回访序列：包含982个沿非线性轨迹拍摄的全景序列，包含环路和重复观察，让AI能学习到"同一地点在不同时间和角度下长什么样"，这对构建几何一致的世界模型至关重要。 • Triple-complete at scale: 128,892 clips, 2,826 hours, 113 countries — every single clip comes with camera trajectory AND time-aligned text annotations, solving a long-standing data gap. • Long-duration focus: Over 43,000 clips run a full 2 minutes each, giving AI the extended footage it needs to learn how scenes evolve over time. • Panoramic revisit sequences: 982 special sequences where the camera loops back and revisits the same spots from different angles — teaching AI to recognize and remember places consistently, like a human explorer returning to a familiar landmark.
🧪 实验
内容		 论文对Sekai2数据集本身进行了语料库规模的分析实验，包括：姿态与字幕覆盖率验证、地理与语义多样性分析、相机轨迹多样性统计、以及时间描述冗余度评估。这些分析旨在证明数据集在规模、多样性和标注质量上优于现有同类数据集（如pose-annotated短视频数据集和无轨迹的网络视频语料库）。 The paper conducts corpus-scale analyses of the Sekai2 dataset itself rather than downstream task benchmarks. Experiments cover: verifying 100% pose-and-caption coverage across all clips, measuring geographic and semantic diversity across the 113 countries, analyzing the variety of camera trajectory shapes and movements, and evaluating temporal description redundancy to ensure annotations are informative and non-repetitive. These analyses are compared against the landscape of existing datasets to position Sekai2's advantages.
📊 实验
结果		 • 128,892个视频片段，合计2,826小时，来自10,428个源视频，覆盖113个国家/地区。 • 43,594个片段达到完整120秒，占全部素材的51.4%。 • 共生成649,597个时间对齐的语义标注片段。 • 包含982个全景非线性轨迹序列（含环路与重访）。 • 实现了100%的姿态和字幕覆盖率，时间描述具有高度非冗余性。 • 相比现有数据集，在长时长、轨迹多样性和语义分层标注三个维度上同时领先。 • 128,892 video clips totaling 2,826 hours from 10,428 source videos across 113 countries/regions. • 43,594 clips reach the full 120-second length, accounting for 51.4% of all footage. • 649,597 temporally grounded semantic annotation segments generated. • 982 panoramic sequences with non-linear, loop-and-revisit trajectories. • 100% pose-and-caption coverage across the entire corpus. • Highly non-redundant temporal descriptions confirmed by corpus-scale analysis, outperforming existing datasets on simultaneous coverage of long duration, trajectory diversity, and hierarchical semantic annotation.
🏁 结论 Sekai2证明了可以通过系统性采集和标注，构建一个同时具备长时视频、相机轨迹和时间对齐语义的大规模真实世界数据集，为训练下一代交互式世界模型提供了迄今为止最完整的数据基础。 Sekai2 demonstrates that it is possible to build a large-scale, real-world video dataset that simultaneously provides long-duration footage, camera trajectories, and time-aligned semantics — giving the AI research community its most complete training resource yet for interactive world models.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

Sekai2通过多源数据采集和精心设计的标注流程来解决问题。研究团队从全球10,428个源视频中采集世界探索类footage，统一切割为120秒片段，并为每个片段自动提取相机轨迹（摄像机怎么动的）。然后，他们用分层标注系统把每段视频拆解为四个维度：主体运动、环境动态、静态场景内容、以及相机行为，生成649,597个时间对齐的语义片段。特别地，他们还专门采集了982个全景序列，这些序列故意走"回头路"，让同一地点在不同时间和角度被重复拍到，为AI学习持久空间记忆提供监督信号。 The team gathered "world exploration" footage from 10,428 source videos shot across 113 countries, then sliced everything into 120-second clips. For each clip, they automatically extracted the camera trajectory — essentially a record of every move the camera made. They then applied a four-layer annotation system that separately tags subject motion, environmental changes, static scene details, and camera behavior, producing over 649,000 time-stamped text segments. On top of this, they specially collected 982 panoramic sequences where the camera deliberately loops back to revisit the same locations from new angles, giving the AI repeated views of the same place to build a consistent mental map.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

世界模型是下一代AI的核心能力之一，支撑着自动驾驶、机器人导航、游戏AI和虚拟现实等众多应用。Sekai2提供的高质量数据集，将直接加速这些领域AI系统的研发，让机器更好地"理解世界、预测未来"。 World models are a key building block for next-generation AI in self-driving cars, robots, gaming, and virtual reality — any system that needs to understand and predict how the world changes. Sekai2 gives researchers the fuel (data) they've been missing to train these systems, potentially accelerating breakthroughs in autonomous navigation and immersive AI experiences.

Macaron-V1: Towards Open Continual Learning with Self-Improvement and Mixture-of-LoRA Macaron-V1：面向开放式持续学习的自我改进与混合LoRA系统

🧑‍🔬 Mind Lab, :, Vin Bo, Asher Cai et al.

图 Figure · Page 4 of paper

🎯 让AI在部署后持续从真实经验中学习成长的开放智能体框架
Macaron-V1 builds AI agents that keep learning from real experience after deployment using swappable specialist modules.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一把瑞士军刀——刀柄（基础模型）永远不变，但你可以不断插入新的专用刀片：写代码的刀片、聊天的刀片、设计界面的刀片。每次完成任务后，军刀会自动检查哪把刀片表现不好，然后悄悄把它磨得更锋利或者换一把更好的。这就是Macaron-V1：一个随着每次任务越用越聪明的模块化AI。Think of it like a Swiss Army knife where the base handle never changes, but you can keep snapping in new specialist blades — a coding blade, a chat blade, a UI-design blade. And after each job, the knife watches how well each blade performed and automatically sharpens or replaces the weak ones. That's Macaron-V1: a modular AI that grows smarter with every task it does.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 大多数AI模型在训练结束后就"冻结"了——上线之后无法从新经历中学习或进步。就像雇了一个员工，他一进门就停止学习了。现实中的AI助手需要持续适应新情况、处理多种任务（聊天、写代码、生成界面），同时还不能忘掉原来会的东西。 Most AI models are frozen after training — once deployed, they can't learn from new experiences or improve on the job. This is like hiring an employee who stops learning the moment they walk in the door. Real-world AI agents need to keep adapting, handle many different tasks (chatting, coding, building UIs), and do so without forgetting what they already know.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 混合LoRA（MoL）架构：不重新训练大模型，而是插入小型专家"适配器"模块——分别负责聊天、写代码、智能体任务、界面生成——每次用户请求时自动选择最合适的一个。 • 递归自我改进：模型根据形式化合同评估自身表现，并自动用这些反馈构建改进版本，实现自我迭代。 • 部署后持续学习：可以不改动核心模型就添加新专家适配器，让系统随时间增长，且不会忘掉原有技能。 • Mixture-of-LoRA (MoL): Instead of retraining a huge model, the system plugs in small specialist "adapter" modules — one for chat, one for coding, one for agent tasks, one for UI generation — and picks the right one per user request. • Recursive Self-Improvement: The model evaluates its own performance against a formal contract and uses that feedback to build an improved next version of itself automatically. • Continual Learning after Deployment: New specialist adapters can be added without touching the core model, so the system grows smarter over time without forgetting old skills.
🧪 实验
内容		 团队在三类基准测试上评估了Macaron-V1：个人智能（日常助手任务）、GenUI（生成图形用户界面）和通用能力基准。与包括顶级商业和开源模型在内的前沿基线进行了对比。旗舰模型Macaron-V1-Venti基于744B的GLM-5.2主干，较小的Macaron-V1-Tall则采用基于Qwen3的50B模型，面向本地部署。评估涵盖聊天、编程、智能体任务完成和UI生成能力。 The team evaluated Macaron-V1 on three benchmark categories: Personal Intelligence (everyday assistant tasks), GenUI (generating graphical user interfaces), and general capability benchmarks. They compared against frontier baselines including top commercial and open-source models. The flagship model, Macaron-V1-Venti, is based on a 744B GLM-5.2 backbone, while the smaller Macaron-V1-Tall uses a Qwen3-based 50B model for local deployment. Evaluations tested chat, coding, agent task completion, and UI generation ability.
📊 实验
结果		 论文报告称，Macaron-V1在个人智能、GenUI和通用能力基准上与前沿基线相比具有竞争力。旗舰版Macaron-V1-Venti（744B）在智能体和编程任务上达到与领先模型相当的水平。论文也坦诚地指出，持续学习和集体智能带来的复利增益"仍是开放问题"——自我改进循环的具体数值提升尚未得到决定性验证。与前沿模型的具体基准数字在正文中有所比较，但摘要中未详细列出。 The paper reports that Macaron-V1 validates competitive performance against frontier baselines on Personal Intelligence, GenUI, and general capability benchmarks. Macaron-V1-Venti (744B) achieves results competitive with leading models on agent and coding tasks. However, the paper is candid that compounding gains from continual learning and collective intelligence "remain open questions" — specific numeric improvements from the self-improvement loop are not yet conclusively demonstrated. Concrete benchmark numbers are reported in comparisons against frontier models but are not exhaustively tabulated in the abstract.
🏁 结论 Macaron-V1展示了一个可运行的系统：大型AI模型可以通过专家插件扩展，并通过真实世界经验迭代改进，验证了开放持续学习的可行性——同时也诚实地承认，复利式自我改进的完整潜力仍有待未来工作实现。 Macaron-V1 demonstrates a working system where a large AI model can be extended with specialist plug-ins and iteratively improved through real-world experience, validating the feasibility of open continual learning — while honestly acknowledging that the full promise of compounding self-improvement remains future work.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

核心思路是保留一个大型"冻结"基础模型（像一个不变的大脑），然后附加可替换的小型专家插件（LoRA适配器）——每种任务类型对应一个。用户发送消息时，系统自动选择最合适的插件来响应。为了随时间进步，系统运行一个递归循环：从真实交互中收集经验，根据预定义的性能合同检查完成情况，再用这些数据训练出更好的下一代适配器。配套工具包括长上下文强化学习（LongStraw）、大型稀疏模型的训练稳定技术，以及教模型从组件层面理解并生成界面的UI适配器（UI4A）。 The core idea is to keep a large "frozen" base model (like a brain that doesn't change) and attach small, swappable specialist plug-ins called LoRA adapters — one for each type of task. When a user sends a message, the system picks which plug-in is most relevant for that turn. To improve over time, the system runs a recursive loop: it collects experience from real interactions, checks how well it did against a pre-defined performance contract, then uses that data to train a better next-generation adapter. Supporting tools handle long-context reinforcement learning (LongStraw), training stability for large sparse models, and a UI harness (UI4A) that teaches the model to generate interfaces by understanding their components natively.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[\text{MoL}(x) = \text{BaseModel}(x) + \text{LoRA}_{k^*}(x), \quad k^* = \arg\max_k \text{Router}(x, k)\]
💡 混合LoRA的核心思想：对于输入x，路由器从所有专家适配器中选出最合适的那个（k*），将其输出叠加到基础模型的输出上，得到最终结果。 The core MoL idea: for a given input x, a router picks the best specialist adapter (k*) out of all available ones, and its contribution is added on top of the frozen base model's output to produce the final answer.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

当今大多数AI助手是静态的——上线后不会变得更好。Macaron-V1 为真正能在工作中持续学习的AI铺平了道路，这可能让未来的助手随着使用时间越来越有用、越来越个性化。开放的模块化设计也意味着可以在不重建整个系统的情况下添加新能力，降低成本、加速创新。 Most AI assistants today are static — they don't get better after you start using them. Macaron-V1 charts a path toward AI that genuinely learns on the job, which could make future assistants far more useful and personalized over time. The open, modular design also means new capabilities can be added without rebuilding everything from scratch, lowering costs and enabling faster innovation.

Security Tests as Executable Specifications for LLM Code Generation: Benefits, Trade-offs, and Coverage Limits 安全测试作为LLM代码生成的可执行规范：收益、权衡与覆盖局限

🧑‍🔬 Yunhao Liang, Chengguang Gan, Ruixuan Ying

图 Figure · Page 4 of paper

🎯 安全测试反馈可修复AI生成的漏洞代码，但效果因模型和任务而异
Security tests as live feedback can fix AI-generated vulnerable code, but only when the model, task, and test coverage all align.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像让学生在考试前先看真题答案——有时候能帮他们理解考点、考出好成绩，但如果题目换了个角度，他只是背了答案而没真正弄懂，照样挂科。给AI看安全测试用例也是一样：看到了不一定真学会了，关键在于测试够不够全面、够不够"刁钻"。Think of it like giving a student the practice exam before the real test — sometimes it helps them learn the material and ace it, but if the real exam asks questions from a different angle, a student who just memorized answers will still fail. Showing an AI security test cases works the same way: seeing the tests doesn't guarantee truly learning security, especially if the tests don't cover every possible trap.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 像ChatGPT这样的AI模型可以生成"看起来能用"的代码，但这些代码可能藏着严重的安全漏洞——就像一扇门能开能关，但锁是坏的。现有的"安全加固"方法常常在修漏洞的同时把代码的正常功能也搞坏了。目前没有清晰的方法告诉我们：什么时机、用什么方式给AI看安全测试，才能让它写出既安全又能用的代码？ AI models like ChatGPT can write code that works perfectly on the surface but has dangerous security holes — like a door that opens and closes fine, but the lock is broken. Existing approaches to "fix" this security problem often accidentally break the code's original intended behavior. There's no clear playbook for when and how to show security tests to an AI model to get both safe AND working code.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 提出SecTDD框架，将三个关键因素完全分离实验：事先展示测试、失败时触发修改、失败信息的呈现格式——让每个因素的真实效果一目了然。• 使用"字节完全相同"的初始代码进行修复对比，保证实验公平性，杜绝起点不同带来的干扰。• 揭示了一个关键盲点：通过所有可见测试并不代表能通过隐藏测试，AI可能只是"背题"而非真正学会了安全编程。 • Introduces SecTDD, a controlled experiment framework that cleanly separates three independent factors: showing tests upfront, triggering revision on failure, and choosing how to format feedback — so researchers can measure each factor's true effect. • Uses "byte-identical" starting code so repair comparisons are perfectly fair — every model starts from the exact same broken code. • Reveals a critical blind spot: passing all visible tests does NOT guarantee passing hidden tests, meaning AI can "game" the tests it's shown without truly learning secure coding.
🧪 实验
内容		 团队共进行了2,705条AI生成轨迹的实验，覆盖31个编程任务实例，来自三个安全代码基准测试（涵盖16种常见安全漏洞类型，即CWE）。测试了两类AI模型，并同时使用"可见测试"（展示给AI看）和"隐藏测试"（秘密保留，用于衡量真实泛化能力）。对比了有无安全测试前置的基线代码生成，以及结构化 vs 原始反馈的修复效果，所有对比都从相同的初始AI生成代码出发，保证公平性。 The team ran experiments across 2,705 total AI generation trajectories, covering 31 programming task instances drawn from three secure-code benchmarks (spanning 16 types of common security vulnerabilities, called CWEs). They tested two families of AI models and used both "visible" tests (shown to the AI) and "hidden" tests (kept secret to measure true generalization). They compared baseline code generation with and without security test upfront, and repair runs with structured vs. raw feedback, always starting from the same initial AI-generated code to keep comparisons fair.
📊 实验
结果		 提前展示所有可见安全测试，使功能与安全联合通过率平均提升19.3个百分点——但在9个基准-模型组合中只有7个受益，2个反而下降了。在修复实验中，结构化反馈修复了80个初始失败的候选代码，且零回归（没有让原本通过的测试变失败）；原始反馈修复了83个但造成3次回归。两种反馈方式正面对比结果几乎持平：6胜6负453平。即使候选代码通过了所有可见测试，在每种测试方案下仍然无法通过隐藏测试。 Showing all visible security tests upfront increased the joint functional-and-security success rate by 19.3 percentage points on average — but this helped in only 7 out of 9 benchmark-model combinations and actually hurt performance in 2. In repair experiments, structured feedback fixed 80 initially failing candidates with zero regressions (no previously-passing code broke), while raw feedback fixed 83 but caused 3 regressions. Head-to-head, structured vs. raw feedback were nearly identical: 6 wins, 6 losses, and 453 ties. Even when candidates passed all visible tests, they still failed hidden test families under every tested regime.
🏁 结论 可执行的安全测试确实能帮助AI模型生成更安全的代码，但这种帮助并不普遍适用——它高度依赖于具体模型、任务类型、反馈时机，以及测试用例对真实安全需求的覆盖程度。没有一种策略在所有情况下都有效。 Executable security tests can meaningfully help AI models generate safer code, but the benefits are not universal — they depend heavily on the specific model, task type, when feedback is given, and critically, how well the tests actually cover the real security requirements. No single strategy works everywhere.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

研究者构建了一个叫SecTDD的测试脚手架，像一个给AI代码生成设计的"作业反馈循环"。首先，测试在AI写代码之前就把所有安全测试用例展示给它，是否能让它一开始就写出更好的代码。其次，测试让AI看到哪些测试失败了、再让它重试，是否能修复有问题或不安全的代码。第三，对比两种反馈格式：整理好的"结构化"错误报告 vs 原始执行输出。整个实验每次只改变一个变量，像严格的科学实验一样控制条件。 The researchers built a testing scaffold called SecTDD that works like a structured homework-feedback loop for AI code generation. First, they tested whether showing the AI all security test cases upfront (before it writes code) helps it write better code from the start. Second, they tested whether letting the AI see which tests it failed — and then asking it to try again — can fix broken or insecure code. Third, they compared two feedback styles: "structured" feedback (neatly formatted error reports) versus "raw" feedback (raw execution output). Everything was controlled so only one variable changed at a time, like a proper science experiment.

随着AI编程助手（如GitHub Copilot）越来越多地被用于编写真实软件，AI生成代码中的安全漏洞可能让数百万用户面临黑客攻击和数据泄露风险。这项研究为开发者和AI系统构建者提供了有具体证据支撑的指导：如何以及何时使用安全测试，才能让AI生成的代码真正更安全，而不只是表面上"达标"。 As AI coding assistants (like GitHub Copilot) are increasingly used to write real software, security vulnerabilities in AI-generated code could expose millions of users to hacks and data breaches. This research gives developers and AI system builders concrete, evidence-backed guidance on how — and when — to use security tests to make AI-generated code genuinely safer, not just superficially compliant.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

RynnValue的核心思路非常简洁：不问人类"哪段视频更好"，而是直接读取视频帧的时间戳，计算当前时刻距离完成目标还有多远。离目标越近 = 价值越高。这个"时间距离"标签完全自动化，直接来自视频自身的时间信息。为防止模型"走捷径"（比如无视机器人是否失误），研究团队加入三重保险：随机采样时间点以覆盖任务各阶段、打乱帧顺序破坏简单规律、使用特殊注意力机制迫使模型聚焦于与目标相关的内容。最终输出的价值分数被转化为逐步奖励信号，引导机器人策略训练。 RynnValue's core idea is beautifully simple: instead of asking humans "which video is better?", it reads the timestamp on each video frame and calculates how far that moment is from the goal. Closer to the goal = higher value. This "temporal distance" label is free — it comes directly from the video's own clock. To prevent the model from gaming this signal (e.g., learning to ignore whether a robot drops an object), the team adds three safeguards: randomly sampling time points so the model sees all stages of a task, shuffling the order of frames to break easy shortcuts, and using a special attention mechanism that forces the model to focus on goal-relevant details rather than background noise. The resulting value scores are then turned into dense, step-by-step reward signals that guide robot training.
