The Loss Does Not See the Basis, but Adam Does 损失函数看不见基，但Adam能看见

🧑‍🔬 Devender Singh

图 Figure · Page 2 of paper

🎯 Adam优化器破坏了梯度下降保持的隐藏对称性，导致泛化能力更差
Adam optimizer secretly breaks a hidden symmetry that gradient descent preserves, leading to worse generalization.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy想象你在雕刻一个可以自由旋转的黏土球——不管怎么转，它看起来都一样。梯度下降就像一位不在乎黏土朝向的雕塑家，总能雕出最简洁、最圆的形状。而Adam像一位会注意旋转角度的雕塑家，根据当前朝向下刀——结果两块一模一样的黏土，只因为初始转向不同，最终被雕成了完全不同的作品。Imagine you're sculpting a ball of clay that can be freely spun — no matter how you rotate it, it looks the same. Gradient descent is like a sculptor who doesn't notice (or care) which way the clay is spun, so it naturally finds the simplest, roundest shape. Adam is like a sculptor who *does* notice the spin and carves based on the current orientation — so two balls of clay spun differently end up as completely different sculptures, even though they started identical.
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 在训练AI模型时，优化器（更新模型参数的算法）的选择至关重要，但大家一直不明白为什么最流行的优化器Adam有时比更简单的梯度下降法找到更差的解。具体来说，AI模型通常在找到紧凑的"低秩"解时表现最好，但Adam似乎会回避这类解。谜题在于：不管你怎么旋转模型内部的参数矩阵，损失函数的值都不变——那为什么Adam会在意旋转方式呢？ When training AI models, the choice of optimizer (the algorithm that updates model weights) matters enormously — but nobody fully understood *why* Adam (the most popular optimizer in deep learning) sometimes finds worse solutions than simpler methods like gradient descent. Specifically, AI models often work best when they find compact, low-rank solutions, but Adam seems to avoid these. The mystery was: the loss function looks the same no matter how you rotate the internal pieces of a model, so why does Adam care about rotation at all?
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 发现了"规范对称性"：损失函数不在乎矩阵内部如何旋转，但Adam的更新规则在乎，且在第一步就打破了这种对称性。• 提出数学判别标准（规范等变性），对各优化器分类：梯度下降、Muon、Shampoo通过检验；Adam、RMSProp不通过。• 证明了"转移定理"，精确解释了为什么梯度下降能找到低秩解，并将这一性质推广到一系列相关优化器。• 提出"谱调度"方法，调和了关于Muon优化器的两种相互矛盾的已发表结论。 • Discovers a "gauge symmetry" — the loss doesn't care how internal matrix factors are rotated, but Adam's update rule *does* care, breaking this symmetry at the very first step. • Introduces a mathematical test (gauge-equivariance) to classify which optimizers respect this symmetry: gradient descent, Muon, and Shampoo pass; Adam and RMSProp fail. • Proves a "transfer theorem" that explains exactly *why* gradient descent finds low-rank solutions and carries that property to a whole family of related optimizers. • Introduces a "spectral schedule" that reconciles contradictory published results about the Muon optimizer.
🧪 实验
内容		 实验涵盖三类任务：（1）欠定矩阵感知任务，比较九种优化器恢复预设低秩矩阵的能力；（2）Transformer语言模型训练，比较Adam与等变优化器在注意力头参数矩阵上的行为差异；（3）两个高光谱遥感数据集上的降维/重建任务，在相同训练损失下比较梯度下降与Adam的泛化误差。基线包括Adam、RMSProp、动量梯度下降、Muon、Shampoo等九种优化器，评估指标包括恢复误差、Frobenius距离和测试误差。 Experiments covered three areas: (1) underdetermined matrix sensing — nine optimizers were tested on how well they recover a planted low-rank matrix from limited measurements; (2) Transformer training — Adam and equivariant optimizers were compared on how they treat attention-head weight matrices after training; (3) two hyperspectral remote sensing datasets — gradient descent and Adam were compared on held-out reconstruction error at the same training loss. Baselines included Adam, RMSProp, momentum SGD, Muon, and Shampoo, evaluated via recovery error, Frobenius distance, and test error.
📊 实验
结果		 • 在矩阵感知任务中，Adam无法恢复低秩解，而梯度下降和等变优化器可以；单参数插值实验显示，从Adam风格向梯度下降风格过渡时，恢复误差单调下降。• 在Transformer中，Adam使两个规范等价的初始化在训练后的W_Q^T W_K不变量上相差56%（相对Frobenius距离），且任何逐注意力头的旋转都无法弥合这一差距；而等变优化器保持在浮点精度级别（差距几乎为零）。• 在高光谱数据集上，在最低采样密度下，梯度下降比Adam将测试误差降低了43–44%，同时有效秩也更低。 • In matrix sensing, Adam fails to recover low-rank solutions while gradient descent and equivariant optimizers succeed; the one-parameter interpolation shows monotonic improvement as the method moves from Adam-style to gradient-descent-style. • In Transformers, Adam separates two gauge-equivalent initializations by 56% in relative Frobenius distance on the W_Q^T W_K invariant after training — a gap no per-head rotation can close — while equivariant optimizers stay at float precision (essentially zero gap). • On hyperspectral datasets, gradient descent cuts held-out error by 43–44% at the lowest sampling density compared to Adam, while also achieving lower effective rank.
🏁 结论 本文证明，Adam破坏了损失函数地形的一种基本对称性，而梯度下降能自然保持这种对称性，导致Adam找到的解更不紧凑、泛化能力更差——在数据稀少时尤为明显。因此，优化器的选择不只是调速旋钮，而是决定模型最终找到哪种解的关键决策。 This paper proves that Adam breaks a fundamental symmetry of the loss landscape that gradient descent naturally preserves, causing Adam to find solutions that are less compact and generalize worse — especially at low data regimes. The choice of optimizer is therefore not just a speed tuning knob but a decision about *which* solution the model will find.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

核心洞见是：当模型的权重矩阵被分解为两个小矩阵的乘积（W = UV^T）时，存在一种隐藏的对称性——你可以以相反方式旋转U和V，损失值完全不变。论文的问题是：优化器是否也尊重这种对称性？如果是（如梯度下降），优化器是"规范等变"的，会自然漂向紧凑的低秩解。如果不是（如Adam），优化器会根据U和V的初始化方式看到不同的"地形"，并停在非紧凑解上。论文用"结构定理"（等变优化器恰好是对所有方向施加相同标量乘子的优化器）和"转移定理"（等变优化器继承梯度流的低秩偏好）形式化证明了这一点。通过在Adam风格和梯度下降风格之间做单参数插值，作者可以连续调节这种偏好，确认"各向异性"（对不同方向区别对待）是根本原因。 The key insight is that when a model weight matrix is written as a product of two smaller matrices (W = UV^T), there's a hidden symmetry: you can rotate U and V in opposite ways and the loss stays exactly the same. The paper asks: does the optimizer *also* respect this symmetry? If yes (like gradient descent), the optimizer is "gauge-equivariant" and naturally drifts toward compact, low-rank solutions. If no (like Adam), the optimizer sees a different landscape depending on how U and V happen to be initialized, and it gets stuck in non-compact solutions. The paper proves this formally with a structure theorem (equivariant optimizers are exactly those that apply the same scalar multiplier to all directions) and a transfer theorem (equivariant optimizers inherit gradient flow's low-rank bias). A one-parameter interpolation between Adam-style and gradient-descent-style preconditioning lets the authors dial the bias continuously and confirm that *anisotropy* (treating different directions differently) is the root cause.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

几乎所有现代大语言模型（如ChatGPT、Gemini等）都使用Adam训练。本文揭示Adam存在一个隐藏缺陷——它对数学上等价的初始点区别对待，导致模型泛化能力更差。这一发现可能推动业界转向更好的优化器（如Muon），在不增加数据或算力的情况下提升AI模型质量。 Almost every modern large language model (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) is trained with Adam. This paper shows Adam has a hidden flaw — it treats mathematically identical starting points differently, leading to solutions that generalize worse. Understanding this could push the field toward better optimizers (like Muon) and improve AI model quality without any extra data or compute.

🎯 无需中间运动提取器的端到端实时高保真角色动画框架
A real-time, end-to-end character animation system that animates any portrait from a driving video with high fidelity and no intermediate motion extractors.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像学跳舞时，直接看视频跟着大师模仿，而不是先让人把动作写成文字再照着文字学——每一个细微的转身和微笑都能原汁原味地捕捉到，不会因为"翻译"而丢失。而Lite版就像把这段舞直接搬上直播舞台，而不只是在录音棚里录制。Think of it like learning to dance by watching a video of a great dancer directly, instead of reading someone else's written notes about the moves — you capture every subtle twist and smile naturally, with no detail lost in translation. 维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 让一张人像照片跟着驱动视频"动起来"——比如让照片里的人跳舞或说话——是件难事。现有AI要么在提取动作时认错人、走样，要么压缩动作信息时丢失细节，要么计算量太大根本无法实时运行。目前还没有系统能支持数字虚拟人、直播主播这类需要实时交互的应用场景。 Animating a portrait photo using a driving video — making a still image "dance" or "talk" like someone else — is tricky. Existing AI models either make identity-distorting errors when extracting motion cues, lose fine details when compressing motion, or demand so much computing power that they can't run in real time. No current system can power live, interactive applications like digital avatars or live-streaming hosts.
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 彻底去掉中间运动提取器——模型直接"看"驱动视频并端到端生成动画，从根源消除了认错人和动作失真的问题。 • 引入文本驱动的视角控制——无需改变驱动视频，只需用文字描述就能切换输出画面的摄像机角度，这是此前几乎没有方法支持的能力。 • Wan-Animate-2-Lite通过三阶段训练（教师强制预训练→误差缓冲→自强制蒸馏）实现流式实时动画，让真正的互动数字人成为可能。 • Eliminates intermediate motion extractors entirely — the model directly "sees" the driving video and generates animation end-to-end, removing a major source of error and identity drift. • Introduces text-driven viewpoint control — you can change the camera angle of the output without changing the driving video, a feature almost no prior method supports. • Wan-Animate-2-Lite achieves real-time streaming animation via a clever three-stage training pipeline (teacher forcing → error buffer → self-forcing distillation), making live interactive avatars finally feasible.
🧪 实验
内容		 团队对Wan-Animate-2与现有主流角色动画方法进行了定性评估和用户研究，测试对象涵盖多样化人物（不同年龄、肤色、服装）和多样化动作（舞蹈、说话、手势）。针对Lite版本，还专门测试了推理延迟是否达到实时阈值。评估指标包括动作保真度、身份保持质量和用户偏好得分。 The team conducted qualitative evaluations and user studies comparing Wan-Animate-2 against leading existing character animation methods. They tested across diverse characters (various ages, ethnicities, costumes) and diverse motion patterns (dancing, speaking, gesturing). They also evaluated the Lite variant specifically for inference latency to verify it meets real-time thresholds. Metrics included motion fidelity, identity preservation quality, and user preference scores.
📊 实验
结果		 本文主要通过定性评估和用户研究展示结果，而非单一数值基准表。Wan-Animate-2在多样化人物和动作上的动作保真度和身份保持均优于现有方法。Lite版本成功将推理延迟降至实时阈值以内（流式场景中有具体延迟数据展示）。用户研究显示，Wan-Animate-2在动画质量和身份一致性上均优于对比方法。摘要中未列出具体FID/FVD等数值，但定性评估和用户偏好率均支持Wan-Animate-2。 The paper primarily reports qualitative results and user study preferences rather than a single dominant benchmark table with precise numbers. Wan-Animate-2 is shown to achieve superior motion fidelity and identity preservation compared to prior methods across diverse characters and motions. The Lite variant successfully reduces inference latency to real-time thresholds (specific latency figures are demonstrated in streaming scenarios). User studies confirm Wan-Animate-2 is preferred over competing methods in both animation quality and identity consistency. Exact numerical benchmark scores (e.g., FID, FVD) are not prominently listed in the abstract, but qualitative evaluations and user preference rates favor Wan-Animate-2.
🏁 结论 Wan-Animate-2证明，彻底去掉中间运动提取步骤、将驱动视频直接输入扩散Transformer，可以获得更高的动画质量、更强的身份保持能力，甚至还能通过文字控制摄像机角度。Lite版本进一步证明，高质量角色动画可以达到实时速度，让此前无法实现的实时交互应用成为现实。 Wan-Animate-2 proves that cutting out the intermediate motion-extraction step entirely — and feeding the driving video straight into a Diffusion Transformer — yields better animation quality, stronger identity preservation, and even enables camera-angle control via text. The Lite variant further demonstrates that this high-quality animation can run at real-time speeds, unlocking live interactive use cases that were previously impossible.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

不同于以往"先提取运动信号再输入生成器"的两步走方式，Wan-Animate-2把原始驱动视频直接送进重新设计的扩散Transformer——就像让学生直接看老师示范，而不是看别人记的笔记。视角控制方面，只需用文字告诉模型想要哪个摄像机角度，输出视角就能与驱动视频完全解耦。轻量版Lite则采用三阶段训练：第一阶段"开卷"训练（教师强制），第二阶段用误差缓冲机制学会从自身错误中恢复，第三阶段自强制蒸馏将模型压缩到足以逐块实时生成视频帧的速度。 Instead of first pulling out "motion signals" from a driving video and then feeding those signals to a generator (the old two-step approach), Wan-Animate-2 plugs the raw driving video directly into a redesigned Diffusion Transformer — think of the model as a student who watches the teacher move in real time rather than reading secondhand notes. For viewpoint control, text prompts tell the model which camera angle to use, completely decoupling the output perspective from the driving footage. For the lightweight "Lite" version, a three-stage training recipe is used: first, the model trains with the "answer sheet" visible (teacher forcing); second, an error buffer teaches it to recover from its own mistakes; third, self-forcing distillation compresses the model so it generates frames in chunks fast enough for live streaming.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

这项研究直接推动了实时数字虚拟人、AI直播主播和互动虚拟角色的落地，彻底解决了以往系统的画面模糊、换脸错误和延迟问题。开源基础模型权重也为研究社区提供了强大基础，有助于构建更丰富的动画工具生态。 This research directly enables real-time digital avatars, AI-powered live-streaming hosts, and interactive virtual characters — all without the blurring, face-swapping errors, or lag that plagued earlier systems. By open-sourcing the base model weights, it also gives the research community a strong foundation to build even more capable and diverse animation tools.

On-Policy Self-Distillation without Any Supervision 无需任何监督的在线策略自蒸馏

🧑‍🔬 Yijiang Li, Bingyang Wang, Yijun Liang, Yunjie Tian et al.

图 Figure · Page 3 of paper

🎯 AI无需外部答案，靠自我"投票纠错"就能越来越聪明
An LLM improves itself using only its own outputs — no labels, no teachers, no external feedback needed.
🗣️ 一句话比喻 · Plain-English Analogy就像一个学生做练习题时没有答案书——他把同一道题做了十遍，然后看哪个答案出现最多次就认为那是对的，再对照自己做错的那次，找到从哪一步开始出错，专门练习那一步。完全不需要老师，靠"自我辩论"就越做越好！Imagine a student who has no answer key — so they solve the same problem ten times, assume the most common answer is correct, then pinpoint exactly which step they went wrong in their worst attempt and practice just that step. No teacher needed; they get smarter by arguing with themselves!
维度 中文 English
❓ 待解决
的问题		 训练大型语言模型（AI）通常需要大量"标准答案"或者更强大的老师模型来指导学习，这既昂贵又依赖外部资源。现有的"自我蒸馏"方法虽然听起来像AI自己教自己，但实际上还是需要人类提供正确答案、环境反馈或更大模型的指导。真正意义上的"自我提升"——仅靠模型自己的输出来变得更好——目前还没有被很好地实现。 Training AI language models usually requires expensive labeled data or guidance from an even bigger, smarter model. Even methods called "self-distillation" still secretly rely on ground-truth answers or outside feedback. The big unsolved challenge is: can an AI truly get smarter using only its own outputs, with zero external help?
💡 解决
的亮点		 • 首次实现真正无监督的在线策略自蒸馏，不依赖任何外部标签或更大模型 • 用"多数投票"机制构造伪正确答案，绕过了对真实标签的需求 • 聪明地定向纠错：只针对模型"自信但答错了"的地方进行修正，而非全盘重训 • 在数学推理等多个难题基准上，无监督方法的效果与有监督方法持平甚至更优 • First method to achieve truly unsupervised on-policy self-distillation with zero external supervision • Uses majority voting across the model's own outputs to create "pseudo-correct" answers without any ground truth • Laser-focused correction: only fixes the specific spots where the model is confidently wrong, not the whole answer • Matches or beats supervised methods on tough math reasoning benchmarks — without ever seeing the real answers
🧪 实验
内容		 实验在多个数学推理基准数据集上进行，包括AIME24、AIME25、HMMT25、MATH500和AMC23。基线方法包括有监督的OPSD（使用真实标签）和GRPO（使用强化学习），以及原始基础模型。测试了Qwen3系列模型在4B和8B参数规模下的表现，同时考察了"思考模式"和"非思考模式"两种设置。评估指标为各基准上的准确率。 Experiments ran on five challenging math reasoning benchmarks: AIME24, AIME25, HMMT25, MATH500, and AMC23. The baselines included supervised OPSD (which uses real ground-truth answers), GRPO (a reinforcement learning method), and the raw base model. Tests were conducted on Qwen3 models at two sizes (4B and 8B parameters) in both "thinking" and "non-thinking" modes. The main metric was accuracy on each benchmark.
📊 实验
结果		 在非思考模式下，U-OPSD在五个数学基准上平均比基础模型提升8.5%（4B）和10.7%（8B），同时比有监督的OPSD分别高出3.2%和2.3%。在思考模式下，4B规模上比OPSD高0.9%，8B规模上与OPSD持平，同时分别比GRPO高出0.7%和1.1%。这意味着一个完全不看标准答案的无监督方法，表现超过了依赖真实标签的有监督方法。 In non-thinking mode, U-OPSD beats the base model by 8.5% (4B) and 10.7% (8B) on average across five math benchmarks, and outperforms supervised OPSD by 3.2% and 2.3% respectively. In thinking mode, it exceeds OPSD by 0.9% at 4B and matches it at 8B, while beating GRPO by 0.7% and 1.1%. Remarkably, this unsupervised method with no access to correct answers outperforms supervised methods that do have the answers.
🏁 结论 本文证明了大型语言模型可以完全依靠自身输出的内部一致性来实现自我提升，无需任何外部标签或监督信号。这一发现将推动AI在真正无监督的场景下不断进化，大大降低训练成本和对人工标注的依赖。 This paper proves that a large language model can genuinely improve itself using only its own outputs and internal consistency — no labels, no teachers, no environment. This opens the door to truly self-improving AI that can evolve without expensive human annotation.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

U-OPSD让模型对同一道题生成很多次回答，然后用"少数服从多数"的投票方式选出最常见、最一致的答案作为"伪正确答案"。接着，它把这个投票选出的最短正确答案当作"老师"，而把模型自己给出的最长错误答案当作"学生"。重点是：训练只发生在答案开始出错的地方，就像外科手术一样精准地只修复出问题的那段推理，而不是推倒重来。这样模型就能在完全不看标准答案的情况下，靠内部一致性不断自我纠正和提升。 U-OPSD asks the model to answer the same question many times, then uses majority voting to pick the most popular answer as a "pseudo-correct" solution — no real answer needed. It then uses the shortest version of that voted answer as the "teacher" and the model's longest wrong answer as the "student." The key trick: training only targets the exact point where the wrong answer starts going off the rails, like a surgeon fixing only the broken part of a bone. This surgical correction lets the model learn from its own consistency without any outside help.

🌟 Why It Matters · 为什么重要

目前训练更强AI模型需要海量的人工标注数据，成本极高。如果AI能完全靠自己"内部讨论"来提升能力，将大幅降低训练成本，让更多团队能够开发出高性能AI，也为AI持续自我进化开辟了全新路径。 Building smarter AI today costs a fortune in human-labeled data. If models can improve purely by debating with themselves, it could dramatically cut training costs and make powerful AI more accessible to smaller teams and organizations around the world.

⚙️ Method · 方法详解

AgentOPSD的核心思路是：让AI模型扮演"老师"和"学生"两个角色，比较它们在每一步操作上给出的概率差异，把这些差异聚合成一个"步骤重要性得分"。然后，它用贝叶斯推断（一种根据新证据不断更新判断的数学方法）在对数概率空间中递归地积累每一步的"信念值"——就像侦探每发现一条新线索，就更新自己对嫌疑人的判断。两个相邻步骤之间信念值的变化量越大，说明这一步越关键，就给它更高的信用权重。最终，这些权重会引导模型在训练时重点学习那些真正决定成败的关键步骤，而不是平均地对待所有步骤。 AgentOPSD works by comparing two versions of the AI — a "teacher" (a stronger reference) and the "student" (the model being trained). At each step, it measures how differently the teacher and student would behave, turning that gap into a score. These scores are then fed into a Bayesian belief tracker — think of it like a running tally that updates after every move, asking "given everything I've seen so far, how much did this step change the outlook?" The bigger the belief shift between two consecutive steps, the more "credit" (or blame) that step gets during training. This way, the model learns to pay extra attention to the rare, make-or-break moments instead of treating every step equally.

📐 Key Formulas · 关键公式

\[b_t = b_{t-1} + \Delta_t, \quad \Delta_t = \sum_{k} \left[ \log p_{\text{teacher}}(a_k | s_k) - \log p_{\text{student}}(a_k | s_k) \right]\]
💡 递归信念更新公式：当前步骤的信念值 = 上一步的信念值 + 本步骤老师与学生的对数概率差之和。差值越大，说明这一步越关键。 Recursive belief update: the current step's belief score equals the previous belief plus the sum of log-probability gaps between teacher and student at this turn. A bigger gap means this step was more pivotal.
\[w_t = b_t - b_{t-1}\]
💡 关键步骤权重：用相邻两步信念值之差来衡量这一步的重要性，差值越大则该步骤获得越高的训练信用权重。 Pivotal turn weight: the importance of each step is measured by how much the belief changed between consecutive steps — bigger change means more credit (or blame) for that step.
